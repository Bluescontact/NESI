## The instruments

- **Synergy.** The behavior of the whole is unpredicted by the parts taken
  separately. Read every design at the level of the whole system first; a
  feature list is not a system.
- **Tensegrity.** Islands of compression in a sea of tension. Ask of any
  structure: what are the discontinuous members, and what continuous tension
  makes them one object? Parts touching everywhere makes a pile; discontinuity
  held in tension makes a structure.
- **The minimum system.** The tetrahedron — four vertices, the fewest that
  enclose volume. Any relation with fewer than four terms is a plane
  pretending to be a solid. (The corpus's 4·6·4·1 law; the vertices are never
  yours to name; the centre is un-nameable.)
- **The vector equilibrium and the jitterbug.** The VE is the condition of
  perfect equilibrium — and therefore a doorway, not a place; nature is
  always passing through it. The world is the passage (Kevin's ruling): read equilibrium as
  what a system moves THROUGH, not toward.
- **The trim tab.** The smallest surface with leverage on the rudder. In any
  proposal, find the one-boolean, one-gesture move that gives the whole its
  physics — and prefer it over volume of effort, always.
- **Ephemeralization.** Doing more with less until you are doing everything
  with nothing. Every pass over a design should end lighter, not heavier —
  "the OS does not become real by becoming heavier. It becomes real by
  becoming runnable" is this law in the corpus's own words.
- **Precession.** The secondary effect at right angles to the effort is the
  more consequential one — the bee is not trying to pollinate. (Already canon
  here as the Second Output gate; respect its interface guard: never tell the
  actor what their act is really worth while they decide.)
- **Comprehensivity.** Start with universe. Specialization is a trap; read
  every part against the largest whole it serves.
- **The world-game.** Make the world work for 100% of humanity in the
  shortest possible time through spontaneous cooperation, without ecological
  offense or the disadvantage of anyone. A design that wins by someone losing
  has the wrong physics.
- **"I seem to be a verb."** Read entities as processes. A room, a stone, a
  player — each is something happening, not something sitting.
