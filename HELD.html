HELD — the register of open questions

Some of what this project has named, it has not yet proven. This file lists those things, each with the condition that would settle it. An entry here blocks nothing; it marks the honest edge of what the project claims. Settling an entry is the keeper's decision alone, and settled entries stay on the record — old text is layered under new, never erased.

Open

  • The membrane in practice. The four membrane documents (house/membrane/) declare how disputes are handled, what the center may not do, what leaves clean, and how things are removed. Only the removal function has run for real so far. The other three are declarations, and only a running circuit — real people, real routes, real disputes — can prove them. Each document carries its own test for being shown wrong; read them against the first circuit that runs.
  • The twelve-vertex hypothesis. The spine currently holds eleven principles. There is a recorded hypothesis that they are the twelve vertices of a known geometric figure with an empty center — held under a strict condition: the count must arrive as an output of the evidence, never as a target. The full condition, in four parts, is stated in the spine.
  • Four watched mechanics in the game and gate. An independent audit of game mechanics found four items that are neither cleared nor condemned — each waits on a specific unresolved question (its "fork") and is re-examined when that question closes. The audit reports, with every item and its fork, are public: first pass · second pass.

Settled

  • The circuit's governance — once claimed as unnecessary by design, now built as the four membrane documents. The removal function (the subtraction membrane) is enforced by the project's own gate and has run its first pass.
  • The genesis seed — once distributed separately, now shipped whole in house/genesis-seed/ beside its tests.
  • The workshop — once withheld from the public copy, now published in full at workshop/.

The working record of how each settlement happened — who decided, when, and on what evidence — lives in the workshop's audits and this repository's commit history, where the making belongs.
