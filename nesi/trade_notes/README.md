# Trade notes — Kevin's own field-guide drop

**Named in the 2026-07-25 evening session as Tool 4 ("a second watched drop-folder for Kevin's own trade knowledge — elevator-mechanic field guides — kept separate from patterns/"), staged and unbuilt until now. Built 2026-07-26 as the smallest real version: this folder existing, with its boundary stated plainly.**

## What this is

A place to drop your own practical trade knowledge — elevator work, mechanical fixes, field notes, anything you'd otherwise have to hold in your head or dig for later. Plain files. No format required.

## What this is NOT

- **Not `patterns/`.** Nothing dropped here becomes a recognition-canon pattern automatically, or at all, unless you separately decide to develop it that way. This folder is practical, not metabolized.
- **Not `_INTAKE/`.** `_INTAKE` is the general regathering feedstock zone the metabolizer reads. This folder is scoped to trade/field knowledge specifically and isn't watched by that pipeline.
- **Not scored, sorted, or summarized by anything automatically.** It holds what you put in it, in the shape you put it in.

## How to use it

Drop a file. That's the whole mechanism. Come back and read it, edit it, or leave it. If something in here is worth developing into a pattern later, that's a separate, explicit move — this folder isn't a queue for that, it's just where the knowledge lives until you decide otherwise.
