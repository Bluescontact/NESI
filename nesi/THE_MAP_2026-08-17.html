LAYER 3

The solid

WHAT IS UNDERNEATH

Two tables are written down in solid.js — twelve seats, and four closed walks of six. Everything below is derived from those two and nothing else is stored, so a claim here cannot go stale while the shape moves underneath it.

12seats — 6 falling, 6 rising.
24members: 8 fall, 8 rise, 4 turns, 4 returns.
8 · 6triangles and squares. Every member borders exactly one of each.
6mechanisms, against 6 squares — 24 bars, rank 24, 0 redundant. Not one member is spare.
20tetrahedral units, in 14 cells: 8 tetrahedra at 1, 6 pyramids at 2.
0members reach the centre. It sits on all 4 circuits, equidistant from every seat, and every radius equals the edge exactly.

The six antipodal pairs

A seat's opposite shares both its circuits and is not adjacent to it. Derived, never listed — and because the solid is vertex-transitive, all six sit at exactly the same distance.

  • LENS ↔ TANK
  • DAM ↔ HELIOSTAT
  • FILTER ↔ SEATING
  • OVERWINTERING ↔ STATIONS
  • GARDEN ↔ GROUND
  • CAST ↔ DEEP

Two seats carry neither a turn nor a return — GROUND and GARDEN — and they are one of the six pairs. The one place that spends nothing is opposite the one place everything settles into.

The named members the build sites things on

Found rather than written, so that if a circuit ever changes and the member stops existing, the lookup returns null and the caller says so instead of drawing a door onto nothing.

LAYER 4

Where the documents and the disk disagree

MEASURED, NOT RULED

Three claims stated in prose that this build measured differently. None of them is a fault in the thing measured; all three are counts that moved while the sentence stayed still — which is the exact failure solid.js's own header records and the reason this map is generated rather than written.

The instrument count

"Twelve instruments hold or refuse; three more run in the page." — CLAUDE.md, the boot path

Measured today: 18 hold, 3 more wait for a page. Six were registered on 2026-08-17 alone — the three trim-tab surveys, the standing spine, the display law, and the traversal organ. The sentence was right when it was written and the suite grew past it inside a week. A count in prose beside a register that grows is a claim with an expiry nobody set.

Specification against build

"This repo has far more specification than game." — carried forward from the 3D brief

True of nesi/ whole: 382 markdown files against 204 runnable ones. False of the live build, and by a wide margin. game2d/ holds 12,707 markdown lines against 27,039 lines of code — 2.1 to 1 the other way. The ratio inverted when the 3D tree was retired and nobody updated the sentence. The warning it carries is still worth keeping; the measurement it rests on is no longer the live one.

The gate ledger stopped before the marks did

205 opens, 140 closes, 65 still open — OPEN_GATES.jsonl

Its last event is 2026-08-16. MARKS_LOG.jsonl ran on to 2026-08-17, and the six instruments above were registered in that gap. 65 gates stand open and nothing has opened or closed one since the retirement — so the surface that records what is undecided has been still for three days while the build moved. That is not a backlog. It is a ledger that stopped being written in, which is the one state a ledger cannot report about itself.

LAYER 5

Open

NAMED, UNDEFAULTED

Two of these the build carries in its own source, in headers written to be read rather than smoothed. The rest are the corpus's, and none is this map's to close.

  • Is a level an edge or a tetra of four faces? Both models are live. Held together they give 24 × 4 = 96 faces against 17 built mechanisms. solid.js assumes the edge reading and forecloses nothing — MEMBERS carries no face field, so adding one costs nothing. Carried in the file's own header, undecided.
  • What is a set-down's trace? One of every seat's three outputs leaves none by design; whether "none" means invisible-but-recorded or genuinely nothing is unmarked. The build's candidate answer is genuinely nothing — member: null, and no caller stores it. 12 products hang on this.
  • Whether the collapse's price is the mechanic or a wall. 6 display windows sited is 6 mechanisms spent is a world that cannot fold, cannot pack, and cannot be carried out — which lands directly on the last four levels, where the fold is assumed to be available.
  • Density or emptiness at the centre. The deposit's endpoint is a tetrahedron dense with history; the solid's is a centre that cannot be reached, cannot be filled, and has no field to fill. The second is enforced in code by a parameter that does not exist, which is why it is the deepest of the seven seams rather than the loudest.
  • 65 gates in OPEN_GATES.jsonl, unmoved since 2026-08-16, and 21 dormant directories with no terminal mark of any kind.
