# Standing Template — Transmittal Packet

*Pasted into every Field Kit's `templates/` folder. Per-substrate kit fills in adverse party, recipients, enclosure inventory.*

The transmittal packet is the structural leverage move of any post-demand-letter phase. A demand letter alone reaches one recipient (the adverse party) who can absorb silence cheaply. A transmittal packet reaches that party + a set of agencies / regulators / commercial third parties / formal-dispute intermediaries simultaneously, all with the full documentary record. **Silence stops being free once each CC has the file in hand.**

This template formalizes that move so it runs the same way every kit.

---

## When to send

After the demand letter's deadline expires without adequate response. Not before. The packet's leverage depends on the adverse party having had — and failed — the opportunity to resolve at the demand-letter stage. Premature transmittal weakens both letter and packet.

## What it does (structurally)

1. **Multiplies witnesses.** The CC list converts a private dispute into a publicly-documented one.
2. **Triggers parallel enforcement.** Each agency CC starts its own clock and process.
3. **Forecloses the adverse party's retraction option.** Once disclosed, cannot be undisclosed.
4. **Creates settlement pressure.** Each entry on the CC list is a cost the adverse party can avoid only by resolving the underlying matter.
5. **Builds the record for any subsequent civil filing.** Documents that all extra-judicial paths were exhausted in good faith.

## What it is NOT

- Not a substitute for the demand letter (the letter does the substantive work; this transmits the file)
- Not a public-facing communication (still confined to specific parties with legitimate interest — see Communications Discipline standing component)
- Not a place to introduce new claims or evidence (only restates and transmits what's in the existing record)

## Template — cover sheet

Same text works for the printed packet (top sheet) and the email accompanying the digital version.

```

[Primary adverse party — name, title]
[Entity]
[Address]

Re: Documentary Record — [Matter description]
    Demand Letter dated [DATE] — deadline expired [DEADLINE DATE]

[Salutation]:

Enclosed is the complete documentary record referenced in
[Section X] of my Demand Letter of [DATE], transmitted to support
the actions named in [Section Y] of that letter and now commencing:

  (a) [First consequence track and its status]
  (b) [Second consequence track]
  (c) [Third consequence track]
  (d) [Fourth consequence track]

Contents (this packet):

   1. Demand Letter dated [DATE]
   2. Post-Deadline Notice dated [DATE]
   3. [Adverse-party correspondence acknowledging the underlying
      facts, if any — quote-bearing exhibits are highest-leverage]
   4. [Technical / evidentiary documentation — diagnostic reports,
      records, photos, expert opinions]
   5. [Email or other correspondence chain]
   6. [Transactional record — bill of sale, contract, payment record]
   7. [Current status documentation — photos, condition reports,
      location confirmations, anything establishing what is preserved]

[Optional — current status of held property / situation, if
applicable. State preservation posture and any duty-of-care
position. Reference governing statute or rule.]

[Optional — settlement-door-open paragraph. Keep terse:]
I prefer prompt resolution on the terms stated in the [DATE] demand
letter. Nothing in this transmission waives any right, remedy, or
claim, all of which are expressly reserved.

Sincerely,


[Sender name]
[Address]
[Phone]
[Email]


cc (with full enclosures):

   [Regulatory body — name, division, address]
   [Second regulatory body — name, division, address]
   [Commercial third party with common interest — e.g.,
    manufacturer, franchisor, principal]
   [Formal-dispute intermediary — e.g., card issuer, insurance
    adjuster, mediator]
   [Any other recipient with legitimate interest]
```

## CC list construction — the heart of the leverage move

For each substrate, build the CC list by asking:

1. **Which regulators have enforcement authority over the adverse party's conduct?** Usually state AG consumer protection + relevant industry regulator (DMV, insurance commissioner, banking regulator, licensing body, etc.) + federal counterpart if jurisdiction reaches.
2. **Which commercial third parties share an interest in the adverse party's conduct?** Manufacturers if the adverse party is a distributor. Franchisors if the adverse party is a franchisee. Principals if the adverse party is an agent. Membership bodies if professional. Trade associations only if they actually adjudicate disputes.
3. **Which dispute-resolution intermediaries are already involved?** Card issuers (chargeback), insurance adjusters (claim), arbitration administrators, ombuds offices.
4. **Which media or public-record bodies are appropriate?** Generally **not** included in this packet — public-facing communications are reserved per the Communications Discipline standing component. Do not list press, social platforms, or public review sites in CC.

Three to six CC recipients is typical. Fewer than three reduces leverage. More than six dilutes and may invite "vexatious" framing if the matter is later litigated.

## Packet assembly — physical / digital

Send the same enclosure set to every recipient. The cover sheet's *"cc (with full enclosures)"* line establishes that each CC has received the complete file, not just notification.

### Physical packet (recommended for primary adverse party + AG / regulator)

- 3-ring binder with section tabs
- Cover sheet on top (printed on letterhead if available)
- Tab dividers between document categories
- Color-printed photos
- Total binder cost: ~$15 per recipient
- Mailed certified, return receipt requested

### Digital packet (acceptable for commercial third parties + intermediaries)

- Single bookmarked PDF
- Cover sheet as page 1
- Each enclosure as a section
- Sent via email with the cover sheet's text as the email body
- Save delivery confirmations

Cover sheet language adjusts slightly for the agency-facing version (its cover says "Complaint enclosed" instead of "Re: Documentary Record") but the underlying packet is identical. See per-substrate kit's `nm_ag_complaint_outline.md` or equivalent for the agency cover sheet variant.

## After the transmittal

- Diary the dates each recipient should reasonably respond by
- Keep all returned receipts and acknowledgment emails
- If the adverse party reopens communications, *all settlement discussion goes in writing through this same channel — never verbally*
- Update the kit's status report as each track responds
- Reserve public-facing communications (Communications Discipline Rule 3) for late-phase escalation only
