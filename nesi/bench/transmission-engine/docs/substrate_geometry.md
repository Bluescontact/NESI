# The Geometry of Published Substrate

*The generative principle beneath all substrate work — one level above
`visual_transmission_invariants.md`. That doc says what makes a single asset transmit.
This one says how a whole published substrate is created and drafted, and why the assets,
the page, the system, and the framework are the same shape at different scales. Canonized
2026-06-09 at Kevin's mark.*

> **The principle, in one line:** *You don't compose a page. You grow a fractal.* A
> published substrate is the same minimum-system instantiated at every scale, with all four
> cognitive channels served at each scale. Coherence is not styled in — it emerges from
> self-similarity. The glyph is a tiny page; the page is a large glyph.

---

## 1 · Why four — the minimum system

The recurring figure is the **tetrahedron**, and it is not aesthetic. Four vertices is the
**fewest that enclose a volume.** Three points make a plane — open, leaky, no inside. Four
close it. A *minimum system* is the fewest parts that hold a whole **with no gap.** Every
scale of substrate needs that same closure, so the same four-fold figure recurs at every
scale. Below four, something always leaks out an open face.

---

## 2 · The scale-stack — nested tetrahedra

The same minimum-system is instantiated at five scales. Each nests inside the one above;
each is a smaller copy of the whole's structure.

| Scale | The tetrahedron | Its four vertices |
|---|---|---|
| **Framework** | the four facets | Differentiation · Connection · Boundaries · Architecture |
| **System** (the act of transmission) | the transmission tetrahedron | Geometry · Altitude · Stance · Modality |
| **Substrate** (the page) | the cognitive channels | **Word · Image · Shape · Color** |
| **Asset** (a single visual) | the transmission invariants | Structure · Legibility · Guidance · Resonance |
| **Mark** (the glyph) | the recognition figure | the same four, in miniature |

The part carries the shape of the whole. This is the nesting Kevin named: the way you build
an asset is tetrahedral; the system is tetrahedral; they are nested — and they keep nesting
in both directions, up to the framework and down to the mark.

---

## 3 · The substrate tetrahedron — the scale that was missing

Between the asset and the system sits the **published substrate** (the page). Its four
vertices are the four channels through which a mind **metabolizes** meaning. Serve all four
and *any* cognitive metabolic process is supported — verbal, visual, spatial, chromatic.

| Vertex | Channel | Metabolism it serves |
|---|---|---|
| **WORD** | text | propositional, sequential — *read and follow* |
| **IMAGE** | picture | episodic, affective — *see and feel* (picture superiority) |
| **SHAPE** | structure / diagram | spatial, relational — *map it* |
| **COLOR** | signal | categorical, pre-attentive — *sort and scan* |

### The 6 edges — where the craft lives
Each edge is a multimodal fusion; this is the actual design work of a coherent substrate:
- **Word × Image** — the captioned instance (the word names what the picture shows)
- **Word × Shape** — the labeled diagram
- **Word × Color** — the color-named term (the word and its category-hue agree)
- **Image × Shape** — the **composed infographic** (structure riding on illustration)
- **Image × Color** — palette-as-affect (the warm register *is* the gift-frame)
- **Shape × Color** — the color-coded geometry (the vertex system on the diagram)

### The 4 faces — the locked-out reader
Drop a channel and you open a face — a whole class of reader can't enter:
- **No Word** → the wordless poster (felt, unparseable)
- **No Image** → the dry document (parsed, unfelt, unremembered)
- **No Shape** → the flat list (legible, unmapped)
- **No Color** → the monochrome (no salience, nothing to scan toward)

### The fractal closes on itself
The **Color** vertex, when it carries the four vertex-hues
(Differentiation·Connection·Boundaries·Architecture), is encoding the **framework**
tetrahedron *into the substrate.* So color is never decoration — it is the channel through
which the framework's own minimum-system is transmitted at page scale. The smallest channel
carries the largest structure. That is the self-similarity made literal.

---

## 4 · The two laws

1. **Closure** — every scale must serve all four channels, or a reader-type is locked out.
   A complete substrate has no open face at any scale.
2. **Self-similarity** — every part carries the shape of the whole. Coherence emerges from
   recursion, not from styling. A reader can enter at any scale, through any channel, and
   recover the entire thing, *because the whole is present in every part.*

---

## 5 · The drafting method — recursive closure

You do not write-then-decorate. You instantiate the minimum-system top-down and check
closure at each scale:

1. **Find the meaning's geometry** (Stage 1 structural read — the load-bearing distinction,
   the shape of the argument). This is the seed tetrahedron.
2. **Instantiate the page tetrahedron** — express the load-bearing idea in Word *and* Image
   *and* Shape *and* Color, braided so any reader's path is complete (not once-each
   scattered).
3. **Recurse into sections** — each section is a smaller page: its own four channels, its own
   closure. Then each **asset** is a smaller section; each **mark** a smaller asset.
4. **Make color meaning-bearing at every scale** — the same four hues mean the same four
   things from the page map down to the smallest glyph, so Color is a channel, not paint.
5. **Check every face at every scale** — is any channel missing here? That's an open face;
   close it.

---

## 6 · The falsifier — how to tell it worked

- **Take any part out and read it alone.** If it still carries the shape of the whole, the
  self-similarity holds. If it's an orphan, the recursion failed.
- **Enter through each channel in turn** (read only the words; scan only the color; trace
  only the shapes; look only at the images). If the meaning is recoverable through each
  alone, all four faces are closed. If one entry leaves you lost, that face is open.
- The failure smell: *a pile of good assets* — each a closed tetrahedron, but the page-scale
  tetrahedron never imposed, and the scales not self-similar. (This is the current state of
  most pages, including The Model before resynthesis.)
