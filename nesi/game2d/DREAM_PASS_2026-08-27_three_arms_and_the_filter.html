3 · Audit — adversarial pass on the seed

Finding 1 — "the different water filters" overclaims what's built

type: unearned

Read as "several independent filter games already exist," this is false. What exists is one built mechanic — behaviour-typed sorting, three fractions, hand-sorted, ported into ORGANS.json's own words as "the cleanest organ in the tree." A second FILTER organ (the tombstone — deletion recorded as a marker, never an erasure) is named in the same file but marked state: absent. "Different filters" is honestly two things: one built, one not, both at the same seat — not a family of sub-games waiting to be gathered.

Finding 2 — the water-filter and the writing-filter are two different animals wearing one name

type: metaphor doing argumentative work it hasn't earned

THE_FILTER.md (2026-08-14) already opened a real, still-open fork: whether the FILTER organ's fractions stay physical (bedload/suspended/film — safe, built, says nothing about the writer) or become Kevin's own human-side naming (lack, transactionality, emotion — which law 5 explicitly forbids automating: "no automatic sorting, no computed pass, no classifier deciding what a fraction is"). If today's "sub-game" is read as the second kind, it is blocked by a standing law until Kevin resolves that fork himself. If it's read as the first kind (the water, not the writing), it's already-built and safe. The seed sentence doesn't say which one it means, and building the wrong one either wastes work or breaks a law.

Finding 3 — "three arms" has no named content yet, and this corpus already paid for that exact mistake

type: unfalsifiable as stated — no state of the world currently confirms or denies it

The twelve-seat door was retired on 2026-08-26 for being "a bench of links leading out" — Kevin's own words, the day before, named the shape: "menus and tabs and functions that route nowhere... I have no reason to write anything anywhere." A fresh "three arms" structure risks recreating that exact shape if two of the three arms are named before they have real content behind them, the same way the old door did.

5 · Diverge — five real shapes for this material

A · Inversion — the filter feeds the page, not the other way round

Instead of the page opening outward to a filter game, route withdrawn sentences (the × delete already built into index.html, and the tombstone organ named but unbuilt at FILTER) into the water as what gets sorted. The sub-game becomes about the writer's own act of taking things out, not a decorative demo. Gains: the two long-open unbuilt pieces (tombstone, filter-as-game) resolve into one build instead of two. Loses: the "arms" framing entirely — this isn't a destination, it's a consequence of something you already do.

B · Non-sectional — filter as a seventh ground, not a separate game

No new file, no "arm." relationship / time / weave / knowledge / categories / words already exist inside index.html; add filter as a seventh, reading the same sentences, rendering them sorting through water by idle-time or word-count (the two facts the page already privileges) instead of a fourth structural fact nobody asked for. Gains: zero new architecture, zero new "arms" decision. Loses: the sub-game framing Kevin actually asked for — this makes it smaller than he named it.

C · Radical cut — ship only the filter, standalone, decide arms later

Extract ascent.html's already-built FILTER code verbatim into its own small file. Link it from nowhere yet. The smallest possible real, playable, buildable unit — the "never routed" line in the gate goes green without deciding anything about the page's shape at all. Gains: real, shippable today, zero risk of the retired-door mistake. Loses: doesn't answer "the page opens, three arms" at all — defers the actual question.

D · Two arms, not three — name only what's real

TANK (the page, writing intake — already built) and FILTER (the water sub-game — already built, just unrouted) become the two real arms. The third arm is named as an open slot, not filled with invented content, exactly the discipline ORGANS.json already uses for its own open sockets ("the computed cell," "the paired entry" — both marked state: absent, both left honestly empty rather than forced full). Gains: survives the ground pass in §4 by construction — nothing here is a menu item without content. Loses: doesn't give Kevin the "three" he asked for, today.

E · Ask-back — the gesture the page already has IS the filter gesture

The page's existing connect-and-declare flow ("declared" edges, hand-drawn, never inferred) already performs FILTER's own gesture — I TAKE THIS ONE OUT — at sentence scale: the writer is already hand-sorting relationships out of an undifferentiated stream. Rather than building a second, separate game, name this correspondence explicitly in the UI and let one mechanism serve two organs, the way ORGANS.json already does elsewhere (e.g. "the computed cell" serving both a radius and CENTRE.at()). Gains: nothing new to build at all. Loses: it's an argument, not a game — Kevin may have wanted an actual separate playable thing, and this shape gives him a reading instead.

6 · Dream — the one idea worth Kevin's time

Of everything named at the FILTER seat across two sessions, one organ is closer to finished than anything else unrouted in the whole tree — ORGANS.json's own words: "the cleanest organ in the tree... there is no classifier to remove because there was never one to add." It already satisfies both governing laws (5 and 10) by construction, not by care. The cheapest real gain available today is exactly what Kevin named: pull it out, give it its own door, let the page point to it. That much requires no new mechanism — only wiring.

The genuinely new idea — the part actually worth building, not just routing — is Shape A: right now the only water in the FILTER mechanic is synthetic test data, three made-up sink rates. It sorts nothing real. If instead the water is filled from the page's own withdrawn sentences — the ones already removed by the × Kevin asked for on 2026-08-26 — the sub-game stops being a demo and starts being an honest second life for something the writer actually did. Two organs that have sat unbuilt since 2026-08-16 (the tombstone) and 2026-08-26 (a wired sub-game) resolve into one real thing instead of staying two open sockets.
