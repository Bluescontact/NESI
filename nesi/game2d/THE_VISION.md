# NESI — THE VISION

*A knowledge document. Written 2026-08-15 at Kevin's ask: "a fresh articulation
of the vision." It is a synthesis, not a new design — every mechanic, law, and
name in it is quarried from existing files, cited in § 10. Nothing here is a
ruling, and nothing here supersedes anything. Where the record is unsettled,
§ 9 says so rather than smoothing it.*

---

## 1 · WHAT IT IS

> **You write every day. A world underneath receives it as weather. Something
> living at the bottom builds with what you let go, and gives it back as
> ground.**

That is the whole thing. Everything else in this document is how that is kept
honest.

It is a single-player game — a calm god-game — in which your ordinary daily
writing is the weather over a drowned world. The world visibly keeps everything
you give it. It never once knows what you said.

The medium is a 2D game with 3D windows, objects and spaces, and heads-up
displays that show the machine operating in full transparency.

---

## 2 · THE DAILY LOOP

Ten minutes, three beats.

### RAIN — you write · 60–90 seconds

A blank field. You write into it. Nothing reads it, scores it, tags it,
summarises it, or forms an opinion about it — not now, not later, not by any
indirect route such as choosing a colour from what you said. The words are
verbatim and stay verbatim.

What the world receives is the **fact** of writing, never its content. A
completed sentence falls into the valley as visible rain: water rising, streams
finding channels, pools catching light. The world celebrates arrival without
knowing arrival's contents.

The opening screen is an **intake**, and an intake has an intake screen — the
first surface water meets, whose job is not to judge water but to keep out what
would damage the works. NESI's intake screen has **no mesh**. Everything passes.
That is not a missing feature; it is the no-judgment law made physical.

### SHAPE — you play with water and light · 5–7 minutes

This is the part that is actually a game, with hands and skill in it. Two verbs,
both spatial, both fun with no text attached:

- **The dam hand.** Carve channels, place and open dams, flood one region and
  starve another. The terrain remembers — every choice erodes, silts, reshapes.
  **The land is the save file.**
- **The heliostat.** A mirror you physically aim, bouncing light down into the
  valley. Light is the scarce resource, and **where light and water meet, things
  grow.** Aiming past an obstacle into a dark pool is a spatial puzzle. Twelve
  mirrors eventually; each seats into the vector equilibrium.

Neither verb requires the world to know a single word you wrote.

At every station the hand has **three outputs**, always: send it downstream,
sink it to the deep, or set it down and send it nowhere. Setting down has no
destination, no animation, and no confirmation. The absence is the feedback.

### THE DEEP ANSWERS — while you are gone

Whatever you sink goes past the waterline, where NESI lives. You never see the
creature plainly: a wake, a shadow under the surface, bubbles over work being
done.

**The creature builds with what you sink.** Days or weeks later, structures rise
at the shoreline overnight — a stair of composted stones, a reef, an arch —
recognisable in shape, never in words, as made from what you let go.

The game's one promise: **nothing you release is wasted; it comes back as
ground.**

---

## 3 · THE RETENTION ENGINE — the Overwintering

Growth roots only across **returned days**. No volume of writing can force it.

Generalised: **the creature works only while you are away.** Close the game and
the deep begins. Open it tomorrow and the shoreline has changed. The game
mechanically requires absence — the anti-grind.

No streaks, no notifications, no guilt. A missed week means a bigger reveal.

"What did it build overnight?" is the reason to open it in the morning.

## 4 · THE SHAPE OF THE WORLD

### The solid

Twelve seats on a cuboctahedron — Fuller's vector equilibrium.

| direction | medium | seats |
|---|---|---|
| **falling** | water · hydrology | TANK · DAM · FILTER · STATIONS · GROUND · DEEP |
| **rising** | light · optics and growth | LENS · HELIOSTAT · SEATING · OVERWINTERING · GARDEN · CAST |

**Falling is water. Rising is light.**

### The four circuits

The solid has exactly four central hexagonal sections. Each is a closed walkable
circuit of six real edges, and every one runs **three falling, then three
rising, contiguous** — one complete water cycle that closes. That was not
designed in; it fell out of the solid.

Every level lies on exactly two of the four loops. Twenty-four incidences, two
apiece.

### The reveal, without a cutscene

The player is shown true cross-sections and never a lie: a triangular face, then
a square face, then one hexagon — *the whole world, and it closes* — then two
hexagons sharing two vertices, then the solid. Each rung is a real section of
the same body. The player is never deceived, only **under-informed by exactly
one dimension**, which is the only honest way to hide a shape.

The reveal asserts itself from inside the geometry: two loops that both close
share a room, so the player arrives somewhere already furnished, from a
direction that should not exist. The doubt arrives twice before it resolves.

### Two openings, and only two

**One way in — at ↓TANK, where your writing enters. One way out — at ↑CAST,
where a fruit leaves. Everything else recirculates.**

And they are not a mirror pair: ↓TANK's mirror is ↑LENS, ↑CAST's mirror is
↓DEEP. What enters and what leaves are not reflections of each other. The world
is **not conservative** — something is transformed in passage. That is the
argument for the world's generosity, stated as structure rather than sentiment.

## 5 · PROGRESSION — the jitterbug

The world starts as one small tetra valley. As light accumulates — regions lit
and grown — the solid **physically unfolds**. Fuller's jitterbug transformation,
a geometric bloom: new valleys, new water tables, new mirror seats.

Each unfold is a level.

At the final unfold — all twelve mirrors seated, the equilibrium reached — the
water goes glass-clear for the first time, and you see NESI whole. **Once.** The
creature, built out of everything you ever gave it, standing on the bottom.

That is the ending, and the beginning of the invitation.

---

## 6 · THE SECOND PLAYER

The endgame verb: grow a **fruit** — carrying shape, never words — and hang it
on the membrane at your world's edge. Send an invitation.

The invited person walks a bridge into your world's shallows. They see your
terrain, your light, your standing structures. **Never your words.** They may
take the fruit.

When they take it and mark it as a gift, the deep surfaces something for you: a
recognition built from what you had released long before they arrived.

Gift → witnessed → answered from your own past.

## 7 · WHAT NEVER BENDS

1. **Content-blind, permanently.** Nothing reads, judges, rewrites, summarises,
   paraphrases or infers categories from the player's words. Verbatim only.
2. **No number reaches the player.** No scores, counts, ranks, percentages,
   progress bars, streaks or word counts. Nothing compares regions to each
   other, ever, not even as a stub. Progress is **form** — water level, light,
   terrain, the size of what surfaced.
3. **No model call on the core loop.** No network, no LLM anywhere on the path a
   player walks. A feature that needs one is not a core-loop feature.
4. **The operator's hand runs the filter.** No automatic sorting, no computed
   pass, no classifier deciding what a fraction is.
5. **Three outputs at every station** — downstream, to the deep, or set down.
6. **Set-it-down is silent.** No destination, no animation, no confirmation, no
   toast, no chime, no counter.
7. **Held is lawful.** Unprocessed water, an unassigned spire, dry ground, a
   fallow session — all correct states. Never nag, never prompt a return, never
   build a re-engagement hook.
8. **The world runs while you are in it and stops when you leave** — except the
   deep, which is the one designed exception and the whole retention engine.
9. **The deep never renders.** You see hints, never the creature — until the one
   ending.
10. **Colour is never the only carrier of a distinction.** Fractions separate by
    behaviour — sinks fast, hangs, films, clumps — with colour redundant.
11. **Nothing reaches outward.** No telemetry, analytics, sharing, accounts or
    cloud sync. Local files only. Nothing leaves the world but fruit, by the
    hand.
12. **Quitting mid-sentence loses nothing**, and nothing on screen says so.

### THE DISPLAY LAW

The law that keeps § 7.1 from being defeated through the side door:

```text
The machine may never read the words to decide how the words appear.

Every display state — glow, dim, hue, weight, size, face, motion —
must derive from one of exactly three sources:
  1. WHERE the writing sits in the world
  2. WHAT THE HAND DID with water and light at that site
  3. HOW LONG it has been there

Never from a fourth: what the writing says.
```

Two consequences worth stating plainly:

- **Water decides whether you can read it** (submerged · suspended · surfaced ·
  settled). **Light decides what colour it is** (the cast at that site). Two
  independent axes, and neither one opens the text.
- **The machine's own words are never coloured. Only yours take colour.** Law,
  names, prompts, the world's own text: uncoloured, always. This is what stops
  the surface from appearing to have feelings about you.

And the tagging rule that follows from it: **tags are player-applied or
position-derived, never inferred.** A position-derived tag is honest because it
records where you stood, not what you meant.

## 8 · WHAT IS RETIRED

- **Invisibility as a virtue.** The world is lavish in how it responds — it just
  never responds to content. Generosity and blindness at the same time is the
  trick the whole design turns on. Juice is not extraction.
- **Stations, fractions and routing as the core loop.** Writing is the *smallest*
  act in the game; the game is what the world does with it. The three outputs
  survive inside the dam hand as world-acts, not filing acts.
- **Teaching by absence.** The world teaches like Zelda: the first rainfall shows
  the water rising; the first mirror arrives aimed almost right.
- **The 3D tree.** `nesi/world3d/` was retired 2026-08-14 as a dead end and
  composted for skills. The live build is `nesi/game2d/`.

## 9 · WHERE IT STANDS, AND WHAT IS UNSETTLED

*State as recorded on disk. Dated and attributed rather than asserted — a fresh
walk would be needed to confirm any line of it today.*

**Built and walked** (per `BUILD_RECORD.md`, last standing **WALKABLE**): the
twelve seats reachable from spawn through the real map door; the blind intake
field appending verbatim; **the sounding** — TANK → GROUND → DEEP → your own
sentence, verbatim → TANK — the only fully closed, fully built loop in the world,
and the only place a player's own words come back to them. Those two facts being
the same fact is not an accident.

**Half-built or absent** (per `THE_SHAPE_OF_THE_WHOLE.md`, 2026-08-14): the
cast writes a file but the world loses nothing when it leaves; the field between
people has zero implementation; `world.html` (1,495 lines) is unreachable from
the door.

### Four things genuinely open

1. **Is the twelve-level ascent the shape, or the table of contents?** The solid
   is only three members across, and every level lies on two closed loops. The
   geometry offers the ascent as a *reading order* over a shape that permits many
   orders and enforces none. The computation is evidence, not a ruling.
2. **The short circuit.** ↓TANK and ↑CAST are one member apart — a player can
   reach departure from intake in a single step, and nothing structural forbids
   it. Legitimate? Cut? Or permitted at a cost?
3. **What routes a writing to a colour.** The display law forbids the machine
   inferring it. That leaves the hand, or the site, or both.
4. **The missing sideways loop.** Both designed open loops point *outward* — to a
   file, to a person. Neither points *sideways*, between the world's own stores.
   Fuller: *islands of compression with no sea of tension is a pile, not a
   structure.* The sea of tension here would be water leaving one store and
   arriving in another. There is not one. That is the named reason the build
   still reads as parts rather than one body — and a shared store would not fix
   it, because a shared store is one bigger island, not tension.

## 10 · WHY SOMEONE PAYS

A calm god-game where your real journaling is the weather. A world that visibly
keeps everything. A creature that builds you back out of what you let go.
Geometry that blooms. One ending you can only reach by living with it.

Nothing on the market does this. Priced like a premium meditative game —
Monument Valley, Tearaway territory. **Pay once, own your world.**
