## THE FOUR INSTRUMENT RULES — carried out of a retiring gate, 2026-08-12

**Provenance and standing, stated first because they govern how this is read.** These four
lines were written into the retired 3D tree's `sorting_tarp.gd` and proven on exactly one
instrument (`test_tarp` enforced rule 2 mechanically — any `Label3D` under an instrument
turned the harness red). **Kevin has never ruled whether they bind.** They are carried here
because, until now, they existed ONLY inside `OPEN_GATES.jsonl` and a script in a retired
tree — and they are the only written UI grammar this corpus has, while the 2D build now has
instruments. Filed as **candidate grammar, not law.** No seat may promote them.

1. **A worksurface, not a sign.**
2. **Shape carries meaning, never words.**
3. **The control is a physical thing you press.**
4. **The act is visible while it happens.**

## THE INDICATOR REFUSAL — carried out of a retiring gate, 2026-08-12

Filed beside the four instrument rules, same standing: **candidate grammar, unmarked.**
From gate 33 of the nesi-other set:

> **An indicator that quietly encodes a judgment is worse than an NPC** — because an NPC is
> legible as a speaker and can be disbelieved, while an indicator reads as *atmosphere* and
> is believed without being noticed.

The test it implies, and the reason it is worth keeping: **a thing in the world may be alive
and must not be looking at you.** The heron reads depth by standing in it; it does not
report. Any future ambient element — a moving life-form, a sound, a drift — passes only if
it would behave identically for a thousand words of grief and a thousand words of grocery
lists, and if nothing about it is aimed at the player.

**A live seam attached to this, named not smoothed:** the corpus has already flagged that
NESI's own circling is the single thing moving that the player did not cause. A second such
thing is not a free addition — it is a change of kind, and it is Kevin's.
