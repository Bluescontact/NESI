**Standing beneath all six:** the sacred laws (content-blind forever · no numbers reach the player · nothing leaves but fruit, by the hand · quitting loses nothing · absence is productive · blank is complete). ## MANIFEST 1 · THE DAY — the valley learns time
**Stands on:** the fun slice.
**The new beats:** the world gains a felt day. The water stirs while you act and goes still when you stop — the stilling, sited in-world at visible scale. Closing and reopening shows *yesterday's valley*: flood marks, silt where water sat, the channel your sluice cut still cut. Terrain is the save file — every session leaves the land discernibly different, and it stays.
**Sited from corpus:** the stilling mechanic · persistence/pour (kevins-water key) · terrain memory (silt/erosion from the dam corpus).
**Walked when:** two real sessions with a true close between them; the second open shows the first day's marks unprompted; the water still until the hand moves.

## MANIFEST 2 · THE NIGHT — the deep works while you're away
**Stands on:** M1's real day boundary (the creature needs true absence to work in).
**The new beats:** what you sink doesn't vanish — it queues in the dark. On your *next* day, something has risen at the shoreline overnight: a stone stair, a low reef, an arch — built from what you released, recognizable in shape, never in words. Wake-lines and shadow hint at the worker; the creature is never fully shown. A missed week means a bigger reveal, never a penalty.
**Sited from corpus:** the L4 recognition-log grammar (generalized, single-player, no second-person crossing — the gated fork stays gated) · compost→forest link · law 9 (the deep never renders — only what it *makes* renders).
**Walked when:** sink on day one, find the risen structure on day two, and the structure is materially traceable to what sank (count and kind, never content).

## MANIFEST 3 · THE GREEN — light and water make an ecology
**Stands on:** M2's returns (growth is return-made; the Overwintering grammar governs).
**The new beats:** where beam and wet ground coincide across returned days, the world *lives*: shoots root, stand, thicken; a second mirror seats and the aiming puzzle becomes real (two beams, obstacles, dark pools worth reaching). Fruit begins — a stand that has been lit and wet across enough returns hangs one. Bare ground stays bare without representation.
**Sited from corpus:** the Overwintering (verbatim mechanic) · lensWater/growth code · the second heliostat seat (the layered-ascent ruling begins to matter).
**Walked when:** a volume-storm roots nothing; three returns stand a shoot in the beam's wet ground; the second mirror's beam reaches a pool the first cannot.

## MANIFEST 4 · THE HEADWATER — the writing rejoined
**Stands on:** M3's living valley (the writing needs a world worth feeding).
**The new beats:** the writing stops being elsewhere. The tank sits *in* the world as its headwater — the source pool above the valley. Writing falls as rain (already true); but now the worked writing matters: stones worked at the table charge the water; sentences settled in the soil feed which region the rain favors — the hand's work upstream shapes the weather downstream, visibly, never readably. The cast/fruit becomes pullable: the one export, off a grown stand, by hand.
**Sited from corpus:** `nesi.html`'s working faces (blind/sequential/table/soil — sited as the headwater's chambers, not rebuilt) · the landing ruling (the last piece decides) · THE CAST verbatim.
**Walked when:** a stranger can walk from writing a sentence to seeing *which* valley region its water favored because of where they worked it — and pull one ripe cast to a file.

## MANIFEST 5 · THE BLOOM — the jitterbug is the progression
**Stands on:** M4's complete loop (writing → world → deep → growth → export all live).
**The new beats:** accumulated light unfolds the world. When a valley stands lit and grown, the geometry jitterbugs — a full-screen unfolding, Fuller's transformation played honestly — and a new valley opens with its own water table and a new mirror seat. The ascent is the level structure: axes-lit (one tetra) → faces-lit (the star) → all twelve seated (the equilibrium). At the final unfold the water goes glass-clear once, and NESI is seen whole — one time. All-or-nothing motion per Fuller's invariant; no partial jitterbug.
**Sited from corpus:** the layered-ascent ruling (Kevin's mark) · the field/VE geometry (already exact, already corrected) · the vision's 4/8/12 line as world-states.
**Walked when:** the first unfold plays end to end from lived accumulation (no debug trigger) and the new valley is playable with everything M1–M4 gave the first.

## MANIFEST 6 · THE CROSSWIRING — everything touches everything
**Stands on:** all five. This is the integration pass — no new mechanic, only relations.
**The wiring:** rain shaped by worked writing (M4) erodes terrain (M1) that redirects water that feeds growth (M3) that ripens casts whose pulls leave gaps the deep answers (M2) with structures that change the light paths (M3) that fuel the bloom (M5). Every system must be reachable from every other by a chain a player can cause and see. One world, one camera, one store, one file (or one honest doorway between `world.html` and the headwater). The membrane seam is *named at the world's edge* — visible, closed, waiting on Kevin's gated fork — so the second player exists as a place, not a feature.
**The audit:** for each pair of systems, name the wire or the refusal (some pairs lawfully don't touch — say so). Fuller checks the whole as one solid; the island test inverts: nothing may be an island.
**Walked when:** the stranger walk crosses every wire in one sitting's chain — and then the only verdict that has ever mattered: Kevin plays, and answers *do I want tomorrow?* That answer, not this document, decides what the seventh pass is.
