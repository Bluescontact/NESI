## What it authorises

**The counsel may port anything it judges worth porting into
`nesi/game2d/daily.html` without asking per item.** No per-port manifest, no
gate, no return trip for approval. The grant is a batch, and batches are not
re-opened one item at a time.

It covers three kinds of work, and the second and third are named in his own
sentence and are not decoration:

1. **PORTING** — carrying built capacity from where it already exists into the
   daily surface. `nesi/world2d/scripts/*.gd`, `nesi/game2d/world.html`,
   `level_one.html`, `ascent.html`, `nesi.html` (read-only, gated) and
   `crystal.html` are the sources. The salvage list is
   `THE_WRITING_FACES_SALVAGE.md`.
2. **NOTICING** — tensions and dead ends. Not a bonus pass. He asked for a game
   designer's eye on where things pull against each other and where a hand
   arrives somewhere with no way on.
3. **WEAVING** — *"a way to weave things together fluidly for the user."* The
   surface currently has four ways in (the page, the water, the bed, Esc) found
   by clicking around. Making them one continuous motion is the work, not a
   finish on top of it.

## What it does not authorise

Unchanged, and narrow:

- **Sovereignty, body, gift-at-the-gate** still escalate. Those three, and
  nothing else, come back to him.
- **Open forks stay open.** A port that would only work if an open fork resolved
  one way has resolved it. Named ones live in `nesi/game2d/DECISIONS.md`,
  `THE_SHAPE_OF_THE_WHOLE.md` § VII, and the salvage list — including the
  unmerge question, which the source itself preserves in the data precisely so
  it stays his.
- **The twelve laws and six refusals are not portable subjects.** No score, no
  number to the player, no model call, no network, no prompt, no nag, and
  nothing that reads the words — to sort them, colour them, or decide anything.
- **`nesi.html` stays behind its integrity gate.** Read from it; do not write to
  it.
- **`kevins-water.json` stays read-only**, hash-checked, per SEAM S4.

## How it is recorded

Every port is written into the commit that carries it: what was taken, **the
file and line it came from**, and what it cost. A port with no source line is
not a port — it is a rebuild, and tonight already proved what that costs.

## The falsifier for this authorization

If the daily surface ends up with more mechanics and no more fluency — if a
person's first ninety seconds still consists of clicking around to discover what
is clickable — then the grant was spent on the first word of his sentence and
not the last, and the weaving was the part that mattered.
