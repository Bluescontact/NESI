## I · THE SKELETON, AND HOW IT STAYS HIDDEN
The concealment is not a curtain over the geometry. It is **the geometry seen in
cross-section** — every early level shows a true slice, and no slice discloses
the solid.
### The reveal ladder
| shown | what it is | what the player believes | true? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **3** | a triangular face | a room with three walls | yes |
| **4** | a square face | a room with four walls | yes |
| **6** | a central hexagonal section | *the whole world, and it closes* | yes |
| **6 + 6** | two hexagons sharing two vertices | two worlds that impossibly share a room | yes |
| **12** | the solid | — | the end |
Nothing on that ladder is a lie. Each rung is a real section of the same body.
The player is never deceived; they are **under-informed by exactly one
dimension**, which is the only honest way to hide a shape.
### The four closed circuits — verified
The cuboctahedron has exactly **four central hexagonal sections**. Each is a
closed circuit of six real edges — walkable, not merely a cut. And every one of
them, without exception, runs **three falling then three rising, contiguous**:
```
loop 1   ↓DAM → ↓STATIONS → ↓DEEP → ↑HELIOSTAT → ↑OVERWINTERING → ↑CAST →
loop 2   ↓DAM → ↓GROUND → ↓FILTER → ↑HELIOSTAT → ↑GARDEN → ↑SEATING →
loop 3   ↓TANK → ↓GROUND → ↓STATIONS → ↑LENS → ↑GARDEN → ↑OVERWINTERING →
loop 4   ↓TANK → ↓DEEP → ↓FILTER → ↑LENS → ↑CAST → ↑SEATING →
```
**Each loop is one complete water cycle.** Down three, up three, closes. That
was not designed in. It fell out of the solid, and it is why the hexagon is the
right unit for an early level: a player can walk one, feel it close, and have
seen a whole world.
### The reveal trigger
**Every level lies on exactly two of the four loops.** Twelve levels, four
hexagons, six seats each — twenty-four incidences, two apiece. Verified, no
exceptions.
So the moment of reveal is structural and needs no cutscene:
> The player walks loop 2, which closes. Later they walk loop 3, which also
> closes. Both contain **↓GROUND**. They arrive at a room they have already
> furnished, from a direction that should not exist.
That is the end of the game asserting itself from inside the geometry. Two
hexagons share exactly two vertices — so the doubt arrives twice before it
resolves, which is the right number.
### Where level one already sits
The writing tetra you built — three faces read, the centre opens — is rung one.
It is correct and it is already concealment-safe: **a tetrahedron discloses
nothing about a cuboctahedron.** It does not need changing to fit this.

## II · THE ACCOUNTING — capacity, in Fuller's units
Volume in tetrahedra, the tetrahedron as unity. This is exact, not approximate:
```
tetrahedron ............ 1
half-octahedron ........ 2      (square pyramid, base to centre)
8 triangular faces → 8 × 1 =  8      the BRACED · built and walked
6 square faces     → 6 × 2 = 12      the UNBRACED · open forks
                            ────
cuboctahedron               = 20      ✓ Fuller's VE volume
```
**The world is 40% built and 60% open, and those are not estimates.** They are
the volume of the solid partitioned by which faces are braced.
The number to notice: an open fork costs **twice** what a braced one occupies.
A square pyramid is two tetrahedra. Leaving a fork open does not hold a place
the size of a decision — it holds twice that.

## III · INTAKE
### The pun is structurally exact
An intake has an **intake screen**. It is the first surface water meets. Its job
is not to judge water — it is to keep out what would damage the works.
So *"intake is the opening screen"* is not wordplay laid over the design. It is
the design, said correctly, once.
### The resolution of the fork
*Does the map supersede "intake is the opening screen"?*
**No. They are different organs and both are first.**
- The **map** is the headworks: what you see on arriving.
- The **intake** is the first act: what you do.
A headworks has a map on the wall and an intake in the floor. `index.html →
ascent.html` shows the works. The first movement is still the pour. Nothing is
superseded; one was mistaken for the other because both are called "opening."
### The intake screen has no mesh
Everything the player writes passes. Nothing is trimmed, reordered, filtered for
quality, or scored at the door.
**This is not an omission. It is the no-judgment law made physical**, and it is
already built — `S.kept` appends verbatim and is never read for content. The
screen exists; its mesh is infinite. Say so in the world rather than leaving it
looking like a missing feature.
### Intake is not a vertex
This is the finding that matters. Intake is not a thirteenth level. It is the
**only edge in the solid with one end outside it**, arriving at ↓TANK.
And by the same logic the cast is the only edge leaving, departing from ↑CAST.
> **The solid has exactly two connections to anything outside itself: one in at
> ↓TANK, one out at ↑CAST. Everything else recirculates.**
That sentence is the complete open/closed map, and the geometry forced it.
Note what it also says: intake and outfall are **not** a mirror pair. ↓TANK's
mirror is ↑LENS; ↑CAST's mirror is ↓DEEP. What enters and what leaves are not
reflections of each other. **The world is not conservative — something is
transformed in passage.** That is the whole argument for the world losing what
it gives, stated as structure rather than as sentiment.

## IV · THE LOOPS
### Closed — water returns, nothing leaves
| loop | path | state |
|---|---|---|
| the four circuits | 3 falling → 3 rising → closes | geometric, walkable |
| **the sounding** | TANK → GROUND → DEEP → *your own sentence, verbatim* → TANK | **built and walked** |
| S.kept | append-only; drained only by the sounding | built |
The sounding is the only fully closed, fully built loop in the world. It is also
the only place a player's own words come back to them. Those two facts being the
same fact is not an accident and should not be diluted.
### Open — something departs
| loop | departs to | state |
|---|---|---|
| **THE CAST** | a file, outside the world | **half-built and dishonest** — the file leaves, the world loses nothing |
| **THE FIELD** | another person | **absent** — zero implementation |
**There are exactly two open loops in the whole design.** One is broken. One
does not exist. That is the entire diagnosis of why the world feels like a pile.
### Fuller's charge, resolved
> *"Islands of compression with no sea of tension is a pile, not a structure."*
The three stores — `nesiworld`, `nesi.level_one`, `nesi.ascent` — are three
closed systems with no member between them. Both designed open loops point
**outward** (to a file, to a person). Neither points **sideways**.
> The sea of tension is an **internal** open loop: water leaving one store and
> arriving in another. There is not one. That is the missing member, named.
This is not the same request as "join the surfaces." A shared store would be one
island made bigger. An open loop between them is tension.
### The short circuit
**↓TANK and ↑CAST are directly connected by a real edge.** The shortest path
from intake to departure is **one member**. The twelve-level ascent is eleven
steps; the solid permits one.
The whole solid is only **three members across** at its widest (↓TANK to ↑LENS).
It is a very small, very connected world. The twelve-level sequence is a reading
order laid over a shape that permits many orders and enforces none.

## V · WATER AND LIGHT — the display law

<!-- framing: lint -->
Your constraint: appearance may carry feeling, and must never carry judgment.
Here is the rule that makes that hold under engineering pressure.
### THE DISPLAY LAW
```text
The machine may never read the words to decide how the words appear.
Every display state — glow, dim, hue, weight, size, face, motion —
must derive from one of exactly four sources:
  1. WHERE the writing sits in the world
  2. WHAT THE HAND DID with water and light at that site
  3. HOW LONG it has been there
  4. WHAT IT HAS CROSSED - the traversals the hand carried it through,
     and what became of it there: branched, ended, returned, held,
     carried forward unchanged
Never from a fifth: what the writing says.
The moment a colour is chosen by reading the text, the surface has begun
grading the writer, and it does not matter how gently.
```

> **■ AMENDED 2026-08-17 — the fourth source.** Kevin's mark: *"traversal is a
> fourth source — add it to the display law."* The law stood at three from
> 2026-08-14. The fourth admits what a piece of writing has UNDERGONE — that the
> hand offered it to a work surface, that it branched, that a branch ended, that
> it came back unchanged — as a lawful driver of appearance.
>
> **The fourth carries its own lint, because a widening is where a law gets
> defeated.** Source four records the FACT and the SHAPE of a crossing. It never
> records a return's content, and never a judgement made inside one. *Went out
> five times and came back unchanged* is source four. *Came back stronger* is not
> a fact about a crossing — it is a reading of one, and it is the fifth source
> wearing the fourth's coat.
>
> **And the bound sits at the WRITE, not at the render.** Stripping the model's
> output and re-rendering does not test it: source four is stored as flags, and
> deciding that a crossing BRANCHED rather than CAME BACK UNCHANGED is already a
> judgement about what returned. Strip the text afterwards, the flag survives,
> the display does not change, and the test prints green. So the law names who
> assigns the outcome — the hand does — and the only comparison the machine may
> make is byte-identity on a digest. *(Corrected 2026-08-17 after an adversarial
> read found the render-test admitted content laundered through a
> classification.)*

You already enforce this once. `drawKept(i, x, y)` takes an index and never a
string, so machine wording cannot reach the screen through it. **The display law
is that same law extended from wording to appearance.** It is not a new
principle; it is the existing one, applied to colour.
### The two media are independent, and neither reads
| | acts on | the axis | does not |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WATER** | legibility and position | submerged · suspended · surfaced · settled | say what it means |
| **LIGHT** | hue and temperature | colour cast at the site | say whether it is good |
**Water decides whether you can read it. Light decides what colour it is.**
Two independent axes, and neither one opens the text.
This already matches the world: the descent is hydrology (tank, dam, filter,
stations, ground, deep) and the ascent is optics and growth (lens, heliostat,
seating, overwintering, garden, cast). **Falling is water. Rising is light.**
### Black is structural — and it does the heaviest work
Your naming, carried to its consequence:
> **The machine's own words are never coloured. Only yours take colour.**
Law, names, prompts, the world's own text — uncoloured, always. Colour is
reserved entirely for what the hand wrote. This is what stops the surface from
appearing to have feelings about you, which is how judgment gets back in through
the side door after being refused at the front.
### The palette is available and unplaced
Red, deep blue, green, and the rest exist as a palette the world can produce.
**Nothing here routes any of them to any state.** Not anger to red, not grief to
blue. Under the display law that routing cannot be inferred by the machine, so
it must arrive by the hand or by the site — and which of those it is, is yours
to rule. It is at the gate in § VII.

### Tagging — the one rule
A tag applied by reading the words is a judgment wearing a label. So:
> **Tags are player-applied or position-derived. Never inferred.**
A position-derived tag is honest because it records where you stood, not what
you meant. `oldest lies deepest` is already exactly this — derived from arrival
order, never stored as a rank, never displayed. That is the model every future
tag should copy.
### Unrouted — called by nothing
| thing | state |

| `world.html` | 1,495 lines, unreachable from the door |

The five outputs are the largest unrouted thing in the world, and § IV names why:
they are the field between people, and the field between people is an **open
loop**, and there are only two open loops in the design and neither is built.
**They are not unimplemented because they were forgotten. They are unimplemented
because the world has no open loop to carry them.** That is one finding, not two.
