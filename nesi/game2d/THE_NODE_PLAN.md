## THE GAPS, AND WHERE THE LIBRARY ANSWERS THEM

### GAP 1 · The twelve lenses are named and unrun

Twelve seats, six axes, a ten-clause constitution binding all of them — and no
return from any one. **The array is a described instrument, which is the exact
condition L2 exists to report.** It would read itself as still.

**The answer is already written.** The full spec for every lens — what it is,
what it looks at, what it returns, what it may not do, its falsifier, its fallow
return — is in the session at `237e80ec:3`, 29,344 characters, pulled to
`lens.md` during this pass. Nothing needs designing. Twelve cold starts, one
corpus, no lens seeing another's return.

**What running it costs:** twelve independent passes. Clause 1 forbids any
shortcut that lets one lens see another, so it cannot be done in a single read.

### GAP 2 · Nothing has been given to anyone

The node is built to be handed over and has not been. **§ 06 claims completion
atomic with delivery, and that claim is untested by the only test that matters.**
Until one is handed to someone, the file is a description of a gift.

**Looking backwards:** `MARKS_LOG.jsonl:861`, his own mark of 2026-08-07 —
*"THE FRESH DEVELOPMENT — mine, tagged reached, borrowing every gift above and
recreating none of them."* The precedent for handing over exists in his record.

**And the falsifier is already stated in § 06:** if the giver keeps any tracked
relation to what left — a watching, a hoping-it-lands — the internal ledger is
open and Derrida's objection survives. **So the test is not whether it is sent.
It is whether he can stop watching after.**

### GAP 3 · Six sections read, two work

The Structure face of § 00, named in the node's own oversight table: *floor,
seam and witness present, no Gift.* Correct architecture, and only two places
where anything leaves a hand.

**Looking backwards** — `feedback_build_the_thing_not_the_picture.md`:
*"build the smallest usable core first — a real thing the user operates that
persists or does the work — VERIFY it actually works."*

**This is the gap most likely to be a false alarm.** The node is a document with
two live instruments in it, and a document is allowed to be mostly read. The
question is whether the six are load-bearing prose or decoration, and the
interface test in § 04 answers it mechanically, section by section: *if removing
the language leaves the structural fact fully visible, it must not ship.*

### GAP 4 · The junkyard is barely worked

**1,421 turns he typed across 203 sessions. Five bodies pulled.**

`tools/junkyard.js` reads all of them against the container and reports what has
not landed. The five that came in were the top of the pile by signal; the ranker
is in the source and re-weightable, and the material below the fold has never
been looked at.

**One thing the sweep already established:** the law-with-an-instance seam is
close to worked out. Ostrom appears once in the whole junkyard, mutual aid once,
land trust once. **More laws is not what is in there.** What is in there is
whole written bodies — the substack pieces, the extraction sessions, the
reconciled visions.

### GAP 5 · One duplicate the assembly created

§ 03 arrives at *complete at delivery, no return expected* from the
signal-without-organ pattern. § 06 arrives at the same position from Derrida.
**Two roads, one destination, and the node says so** — but two sections holding
one claim is either the strongest thing in the file or a redundancy.

Left as it is deliberately. Independent derivation is evidence, and collapsing
them would destroy the evidence to save 400 characters.
