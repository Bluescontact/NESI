# NESI

*A rearticulation, 2026-08-17, on Kevin's ask. Gathered from the corpus rather
than composed: every mechanic, law, number and name below is quarried from files
on disk or computed from `solid.js` today. Where the record is unsettled it says
so. Nothing here rules anything.*

---

## 1 · IN ONE LINE

**You write every day. A world underneath receives it as weather, keeps all of
it, never reads a word — and hands it back to you changed.**

That is the whole thing. Everything after this is how that is kept honest, and
"kept honest" is not a figure of speech: the honesty is held by a shape with arithmetic in it, checked by a suite that
refuses rather than advises, and written as lints rather than as intentions.

---

## 2 · THE PROBLEM IT IS THE ANSWER TO

Every instrument built to help a person with their writing reads it.

A journalling app surfaces themes. A note system suggests links. A model
summarises, tags, sentiments, extracts. Each one is useful, and each does the
same underlying thing: it converts what you said into a claim about you, and
hands the claim back. The better the tool, the more accurate the claim — and the
more accurate the claim, the more the writing stops being yours and starts being
*material*.

**The extraction is the claim, not the reading.** A tool that reads you and says
nothing has taken nothing. A tool that never opens the file and hands back a
number about you has taken everything.

That distinction is not a nicety, and the evidence for it is published. BiAffect
is a research keyboard whose stated principle is *how you type, not what you
type*: it discards character content by design and keeps keypress timing,
inter-key delay, backspace rate and device motion. From those alone it predicts
depression severity at conditional R² = .63. **Content-blindness is not the
boundary of extraction. It is a validated method of it.**

So the question this design is the answer to has to be put precisely, or it is
answering the wrong thing:

> **Can a system be lavishly, visibly, daily responsive to a person's writing
> without ever once forming a claim about the person who wrote it?**

Most designs answer by going cold. If the machine must not interpret you, give it
nothing to interpret — a blank file, an empty page, silence. That works and
nobody wants it. Silence is not safety; it is the absence of the thing you came
for.

**NESI's answer is that a world can respond to the fact of writing rather than
its content, and that the fact is enough to run an entire world on.** That you
wrote, how much fell, where you stood when you let it go, what your hand did to
it afterward, how long ago. Those are real signals, dense enough to drive weather
and terrain and growth, and not one of them requires opening the text.

**Content-blindness is how NESI gets there. It is not what the promise is.** The
promise is that nothing is ever concluded about you and handed back. Every
instrument in the build enforces that and nothing else: a centre with no `value`
field, a draw call that takes an index and not a string, *verbatim, unlabelled,
no reason given.* Not one of them prevents inference from timing — which is why
§13 carries a thirteenth law that does.

### Why it has to be structural rather than restrained

**Generosity is safe when it cannot vary with you.** Lavishness becomes judgement
at the moment it becomes contingent: when more arrives on a good day and less on
a bad one, the abundance is a verdict wearing a nicer coat. NESI's world cannot
make its gift contingent, because nothing in it is permitted to hold an opinion
to make it contingent with.

Note what this does NOT claim. It does not claim that warmth from something which
has read you is judgement — the psychotherapy literature flatly contradicts that,
and it is the best-evidenced finding in the field: across 295 studies the
alliance-outcome correlation is r = .278, and therapist empathy r = .28 across
eighty-two samples. That warmth comes from a fully-reading party and is not felt
as a verdict. Rogers' word is **unconditional** positive regard, not *uninformed*.

What is true is narrower and it is the whole argument for building it this way: a
person can hold warmth non-contingent, with training and supervision. **A product
with a metrics dashboard has never once managed it.** So the design does not ask
anyone to be disciplined. It removes the thing discipline would have to govern.

### And the mechanism is not new — the refusals are

Stated plainly, because the opposite was claimed in an earlier draft and it is
false. Worlds driven by the bare fact of an activity already exist and some are
a decade old. **4thewords** is a paid role-playing game in which word count and
elapsed time damage monsters. **Forest** grows a world from the fact that you
focused, at sixty million users. **Pikmin Bloom** grows one from step count.

And the closest ancestor is the sharpest warning. **750 Words** began exactly
where this stands — private daily writing, recognition for the fact of writing —
and then added lexical analysis that returns your emotions, concerns and mindset.
It moved because that is what users pulled for.

**So the mechanism is available elsewhere and the refusals are not.** What is
unusual here is not a world that responds without reading. It is a world that
refuses the next step, permanently, with the refusal written as a lint rather
than an intention — and a design whose whole retention engine is absence, in a
category whose survivors run on notification and streak.

The corpus states the discipline in one line — *"NESI never responds to what you
write. It responds to that you wrote."*

## 3 · THE DAY

Ten minutes. Three beats.

### RAIN — you write · sixty to ninety seconds

A blank field. You write into it. Nothing reads it, scores it, tags it,
summarises it, or forms an opinion about it — not now, not later, and not by any
indirect route such as choosing a colour from what you said.

The opening screen is an **intake**, and an intake in hydrology has an intake
screen: the first grating water meets, whose job is not to judge the water but to
keep out what would damage the works. **NESI's intake has no filter.**
Everything passes. That is the no-judgement law rather than an omission, and it
is the reason the surface can be trusted at speed. Trust at speed is the whole
requirement: a page you hesitate over is a page already being read.

When a sentence completes it falls into the valley as visible rain. Water rises,
streams find channels, pools catch light. **The world celebrates arrival without
knowing what arrived.**

### SHAPE — you play with water and light · five to seven minutes

This is the part that is a game, with hands and skill in it, and two verbs that
are both spatial and both fun with no text attached.

**The dam hand.** Carve channels, place and open dams, flood one region and
starve another. The terrain remembers: every choice erodes, silts, reshapes.
**The land is the save file** — not a record of what you did, but the shape the
doing left. The bed is path-dependent by construction, and the world keeps
no account of HOW you cut — only the shape you cut.

**The heliostat.** A mirror you physically aim, bouncing light down into the
valley. Light is the scarce resource, and **where light and water meet, things
grow.** Aiming past an obstacle into a dark pool is a spatial puzzle. Twelve
mirrors eventually, each seating into the solid.

Neither verb requires the world to know a single word you wrote.

And at every station the hand has **three outputs, always**: send it on, drop it
to the deep, or set it down and send it nowhere. The third has no destination, no
animation and no confirmation. **The absence is the feedback.** This is the
build's smallest and most-tested idea: a world in which doing nothing with
something is a first-class act rather than a failure to act.

### THE DEEP ANSWERS — while you are gone

Whatever you sink goes past the waterline, where NESI lives. You never see the
creature plainly — a wake, a shadow, bubbles over work being done. **The deep
never renders.**

The creature builds with what you sink. Days or weeks later, structures rise at
the shoreline overnight: a stair of composted stones, a reef, an arch —
recognisable in shape, never in words, as made from what you let go.

Nothing you release is wasted; it comes back as ground.

## 4 · THE RETURN, AND WHY IT IS THE WHOLE DESIGN

A world that takes and never gives is a hopper. The corpus learned this the
expensive way and wrote it down as a law: **the world must return what it takes.**
It had stored every word verbatim in five separate places and returned none of
them, ever. Writing appeared as a row and never as a column. Not one path ran
world-to-person.

The repair is the mechanic that the rest of the design now hangs from, and it is
worth stating precisely because its precision is what makes it a gift rather than
a feature.

Your sentences settle in the ground along the width, in arrival order, **oldest
deepest**. You cannot place them. Later — on a day you did not come back to check
— you drop a line over the ground where you think you put something. If the line
finds bottom it brings back **one sentence, verbatim, unlabelled, unranked, with
no reason given for that one.** If it finds nothing it runs out, and **nothing
records where the empty places are, because a deep is an absence and an absence
is not written down.**

Every clause there is load-bearing.

*Verbatim* is law: the words are never rewritten. *Unlabelled* is law: no
category was inferred. *No reason given* is the gift: a bill attached to a
returned thing makes it a transaction. *Nothing records the absences* is the
refusal of a map of your failures — the one record a system like this would
naturally keep and the one it must not.

**Recognition without extraction, in a single mechanic.** The world takes the
writing, keeps it whole, says nothing about it, asks nothing for it, and hands
one back.

It is also the precondition for everything else: the sounding had nothing to
surface, the lens had nothing to work, and the gift had nothing to carry, until
the world kept what it asked for.

## 5 · TIME IS THE ONE THING THAT CANNOT BE COUNTERFEITED

Growth roots only across **returned days**. No volume of writing forces it. A
thousand releases in one afternoon root nothing.

Generalised: **the creature works only while you are away.** Close the world and
the deep begins. Open it tomorrow and the shoreline has changed.

This is the retention engine, and it is the inverse of every retention engine in
the industry. There are no streaks, no notifications, no guilt, and no
re-engagement hook — those are forbidden outright. A missed week means a bigger
reveal. **The world mechanically requires absence**, which makes it the rare
thing that cannot be ground.

Underneath it is a law with a wider reach: **held is lawful.** Unprocessed water,
an unassigned route, dry ground, a fallow season — all correct states. The world
never nags, never prompts a return, never suggests you are behind. There is
nothing to be behind.

*"What did it build overnight?"* is the reason to open it in the morning, and it
is the only reason offered.

## 6 · WHY ANYTHING MATTERS

The objection forms early and the design has to answer it: **if nothing is
scored, nothing is lost, and every state is lawful — why does any of it matter?**

Held is lawful. Fallow is lawful. Setting a thing down produces no destination,
no animation and no confirmation. A world in which nothing can go wrong has no
stakes, and a world with no stakes is a screensaver.

The answer is in the build rather than in an argument, and it is one line each.
**Every one of the twelve seats declares what it costs the hand**, in the file
that renders it, beside its gesture:

> *the morning is poured once; what falls here is not in the daily surface any more*
> *held head is not power — nothing downstream moves for as long as you hold*
> *a fraction you catch is out of the water; the separation cannot be walked back*
> *a route spends the water on one destination, and the other two do not get it*
> *a stone set is ground now; the shape you make is the shape that stays*
> *a line brings back one, and which one is not yours to choose*
> *light spent on growth is light not spent on clarity, and the day is one day*
> *reading across all three is what costs — the lens is time, not material*
> *you feed the frame; where it comes to rest is not yours, and it does not move again*
> *the day itself — a thousand releases in one afternoon root nothing*
> *the fruit leaves the world, and NESI keeps no record of it*
> *nothing is spent; it shows what you made and asks for no act*

Eleven of the twelve name a cost. **Six of them name a door that opens one way
only** — what falls here is not in the daily surface any more · the separation
cannot be walked back · the other two do not get it · the shape you make is the
shape that stays · it does not move again · NESI keeps no record of it.

Four name the other currency, which is what a thing costs while you hold it or
what it costs to have chosen this rather than that: held head is not power ·
light spent on growth is light not spent on clarity · the lens is time, not
material · a thousand releases in one afternoon root nothing.

One is neither, and is the strangest cost in the world — *which one is not yours
to choose*. The price of the return is that you do not get to pick what returns.

And the twelfth declares that it costs nothing: the garden, *"nothing is spent;
it shows what you made and asks for no act."* A world in which every act had a
price would be its own kind of accounting. One place takes nothing, and it is the
place where things grow.

**So the stakes are irreversibility, not judgement.** Nothing here tells you that
you did badly. Everything here tells you that you did *this*, and that this is now
the case. The weight is not in being marked — it is in having spent something
that does not come back: attention, light, a day, a route not taken, a fruit that
left.

The distinction has to be stated narrowly to be true, because the wide version is
false. Plenty of things judge without reading anyone — a lap timer, a step
counter, a chess engine. What is true is the narrow case, and the narrow case is
the one this design lives in:

> **To judge a piece of writing, you have to read it. To make writing
> irreversible, you do not.**

So a system forbidden to interpret you can still have stakes, and they have to be
the second kind. That is the trade the refusals actually make. It is not free —
§15 and §16 are the tally of what it has cost so far — but the thing bought is
the thing that works outside a game too. You do not get a score for the morning
you spent. You get a morning you spent.

One edge keeps it honest: **the cost is declared, never derived.** A parser
cannot see what a hand gives up, so each seat says it in the first person and an
instrument checks that it said something.

*(The twelve above are the live declarations in `ascent.html`, read with comments
stripped. An earlier draft of this section counted thirteen and found seven
one-way doors. The thirteenth — "the bed you cut is the bed you keep" — is real
text in that file and is inside a block comment recording it as REMOVED, because
`solid_check` refused the whole solid for it: a costed mechanic cannot sit on a
triangle. A rejected line was quarried out of the comment that rejects it. The
count is corrected here and the way it happened is left on the record, because
the failure was reading a file without stripping its comments — which is the
discipline `refusal_check` states in its own header.)*

## 7 · THE CONTAINER, AND WHY IT IS A SHAPE

The world is a **cuboctahedron** — Fuller's vector equilibrium. Twelve corners,
twenty-four struts, eight triangular faces, six square faces, and a middle.

That is the complete SURFACE inventory. The interior carries more — twelve
radii, six diameters, fourteen cells — and §8 opens it.

**Twelve seats**, six that fall and six that rise:

| | |
|---|---|
| **falling — water** | TANK · DAM · FILTER · STATIONS · GROUND · DEEP |
| **rising — light** | LENS · HELIOSTAT · SEATING · OVERWINTERING · GARDEN · CAST |

Each seat is a place where a hand does one thing, and each declares its gesture
in the first person: *I pour · I hold, then I let go · I take this one out · I
send this there · I let it settle · I let it go down · I work it until it holds ·
I aim · I feed the frame · I come back · I let it grow · I give it away.*

**Twenty-four members**, each one a seam: not a place but a transit, and its
mechanic is what its two ends do to each other.

**And the levels are thirty, not twenty-four** — 24 seams and 6 diameters, since
F10 (marked 2026-08-17) put a root on a circuit *pair* rather than on a seat,
which took the roots from twelve to six. A diameter is a radius and its
antipode's, straight through the centre, and it is the one crossing in the solid
that is not a walk. So a level is a seam **or** a root, and the six roots are the
half of the count that cannot be travelled.

*(**Six roots is what the mark settles. Thirty is not in it.** The mark's own
note reads "moves F2's arithmetic; not resolved by him and not guessed by me" —
and thirty is arrived at by treating a diameter as a level, which is exactly the
guess the note declines. Whether a diameter is a level, and therefore whether the
count is twenty-four or thirty, is open.)*

**Four circuits.** The solid has exactly four central hexagonal sections, and
each is a closed walk of six real edges running **three falling, then three
rising, contiguous** — one complete water cycle that closes. Every seat sits on
exactly two of them. That was not designed in; it fell out of the solid.

### Why a shape at all

Because a shape can refuse things that taste cannot.

Kevin's standing line is *"the geometry decides what works or doesnt,"* and the
reason it can decide is that the shape has arithmetic. Three examples, all
computed rather than asserted:

**Triangles are stiff and squares are floppy.** Three struts with loose hinges
cannot change shape; four can be squashed into a diamond. So the solid can only
move at its six squares. Everywhere else is braced. That movement is the
jitterbug — the shape breathing. **Anything sited in a square glues that hinge
shut.** The corpus computed the container's own freedom from the rigidity matrix
and found **six internal mechanisms — and there are six squares**, one each.
It was then handed to PyRigi, a published academic tool, which returned
the same answer by a different method. **Not one of the twenty-four struts is
spare.** So "keep the squares empty" stopped being a preference and became a
fact with a number under it.

Any design in which a seat delivers something inward is
geometrically false and is refused on sight. What the middle receives, it
receives by being surrounded.

**The whole solid comes to exactly twenty tetrahedral volumes.** Fuller's number,
and it fell out of Kevin's own circuit table without being put there.

None of that is a metaphor. It is the container's own arithmetic, re-derived on
every run by `solid.js`, which is the authority — surfaces read it rather than
copying it, so a claim about the shape cannot go stale in prose while the shape
moves underneath.

## 8 · THE CENTRE IS THE GAME

Kevin's ruling, and it reorganises everything above it: **"the centre is the
game. everything else serves it."**

The twelve seats, the thirty levels, the faces, the instruments — all of it
is **apparatus**. It is not the game.

What makes this a description rather than a mood is that the middle already
behaves that way, measurably:

- it is **one edge-length from every one of the twelve seats** — radial
  equilibrium, the property Fuller singled out and one that only a few uniform
  polytopes have
- it is the **only point lying in all four circuit planes at once** — not on the
  circuits, which are walks of six edges, and no edge reaches it
- **it is the part everything else moves relative to** when the shape flexes
- **nothing can be sent to it**, and nothing names it

In `solid.js` this is structural rather than remembered: the centre object is
frozen, carries no `name`, `value` or `content` field, and has no `receive`,
`send`, `to` or `set`. **There is nothing to fill and nothing to send.** The
standing instruction that the slot stays empty is not a rule a session has to
recall — it is a parameter that does not exist.

And the geometry supplies the reason rather than a mystery: **a thing that is the
game cannot also be a piece of the game.**

Twelve spokes do run from the middle to the seats, each exactly one strut long.
Nothing travels them. They are the holding — the twelve hold the middle steady by
standing exactly as far from it as they stand from each other. That is the word
*held*, as a measurement.

Every piece of apparatus now has a test it did not have before: **does it serve
the centre, or does it serve itself?** Applying that test is Kevin's, and it has
not been applied.

## 9 · DOWN THROUGH THE SORT, UP THROUGH THE FORM

Kevin's definitions, and they are the clearest statement of what a level is for:

> *"the roots are a representation of writing that has survived water, and light.
> the roots represent a level inhabited"*
>
> *"surviving water is the sort, surviving light is the shareable form"*
>
> *"Each level is a seam, hold two faces… the squares become display windows, the
> triangles are the tools"*

**A root is one piece of writing that went down through the sort and came back up
as shareable form.** It is *returned*, not earned. Each root is a level
inhabited, and the figure only becomes visible as roots grow.

The two halves of that sentence are the two directions of the walk. Falling is
intake, holding, separation, routing, settling, the deep. Rising is working the
material, aiming, feeding the frame, waiting out a season, growth, giving it
away. **Down is the sort. Up is the form.**

**But the halves are not a division of the world's physics, and that is his
ruling rather than a nicety.** F1, marked 2026-08-17: *"three equal physics — each
addresses all twenty-four."* Root, water and light are not two halves and a
leftover; **each addresses the whole member set.** What splits six and six is the
*seats* — a seat falls or it rises, and `solid.js` stores that and nothing else
about direction. The circuit's own shape, three falls, the turn, three rises, the
return, is **water's round trip**, not a map of where light is allowed to act.
Light is not a member kind, and any count that reads "water = 12, light = 12" is
reading a superseded division.

Because the solid is quasiregular, **every SEAM holds exactly one triangle and
one square — one tool and one window, always.** No seam feeds two tools or two
windows. **A tool is made from three seams; a window shows four.** Both counts
reach twenty-four, which is the number of seams and not the number of levels.

And the descent has its own arithmetic, computed today. Cut the solid at its
own vertical — the one axis of the three whose two square faces are pure, one all
falling and one all rising — and the twenty-four members part **twelve and
twelve, with nothing on the equator.** The lower twelve are a bowl: eight seats,
five whole panes, four floor struts and eight rim struts, and the four circuits
contribute exactly three levels each. Its floor is FILTER · STATIONS · GROUND ·
DEEP. Its rim runs TANK · DAM · HELIOSTAT · LENS, no two of them touching.

**The twelfth member of that descent, in the circuit table's own order, is
LENS—DEEP.** The last seam of the descent is the lens member — though the
position depends on iterating the circuit table in its stored order, so it is the
array literal that puts it twelfth, not the solid.

## 10 · THE ASCENT, AND WHAT IT COSTS

The world starts small and the solid unfolds as light accumulates. Each unfold is
a level. At the last one — twelve mirrors seated, the equilibrium reached — the
water goes glass-clear for the first time and you see NESI whole. **Once.** The
creature, built out of everything you ever gave it, standing on the bottom.

That is the ending, and the beginning of the invitation.

Three honest complications, recorded rather than smoothed:

**The ascent and the jitterbug run opposite.** Fuller's jitterbug goes
equilibrium → icosahedron → octahedron → tetrahedron: a collapse *away from* the
equilibrium. The ascent climbs the other way, *toward* it. The equilibrium is the
top of one and the first frame of the other. Same point, two directions, and
which way the build travels is open.

**The collapse has a price, and the price is computed.** The six mechanisms live entirely in the six
squares, one localised fold each — solved with PyRigi, and every one leaves the
other five rigid. The jitterbug is one coordinated mode among them, not the
space itself. Bracing them one at a
time takes the count 6 → 5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 → 0, exactly one per window, in any
order. So a display window built is a mechanism spent, and six built is a world
that cannot fold, cannot pack, and cannot be carried out. **The collapse is
available only while a window is still unsited.**

**And that is answered.** F6, marked 2026-08-17: ***"cap at five — one window
stays unsited so the fold survives."***

**And the geometry does not say what an earlier draft had it saying.** That
draft read the cap as preserving portability — five displays built, the sixth
withheld, the fold surviving on the last free hinge. It does not work that way.
Each of the six mechanisms folds ONE square and leaves the other five rigid;
`solid.js` solved that with PyRigi and all six localise. The jitterbug is a
coordinated mode needing all six squares free, so **the FIRST window sited ends
it, not the sixth.** What a cap at five preserves is one localised fold, not the
ability to carry the world out.

So the mark stands and the reason offered for it does not. That gap is left open
here rather than papered: the incompleteness may still be the point — a place the
build structurally cannot fill, kept open on purpose — but the account of what it
buys has to be rebuilt from the geometry rather than from a sentence that
sounded right.

**The reveal needs no cutscene.** The player is shown true cross-sections and
never a lie: a triangular face, then a square, then one hexagon — *the whole
world, and it closes* — then two hexagons sharing two vertices, then the solid.
Each rung is a real section of the same body. The player is never deceived, only
**under-informed by exactly one dimension**, which is the only honest way to hide
a shape. And because every seat sits on two circuits, the second circuit walked
necessarily returns you to a room you have already furnished, **from a direction
that should not exist.** The doubt arrives twice before it resolves, and no
script arranges it.

## 11 · THREE ARMS, ONE WORLD

Kevin's mark: *"theres 3 arms to this... personal writing development. Commons
extraction and development, and a gift library. Each three comes from a work
surface of its own."* And: *"all three surfaces should be inside the final
nesi."* And, load-bearing: ***"its a world."***

| arm | what it does |
|---|---|
| **personal writing development** | the daily page — intake, water, the deep. Sections 3 to 5 are entirely this arm. |
| **commons extraction and development** | reading what the open commons already holds and routing it into the geometry rather than inventing it |
| **gift library** | what leaves by the hand, and the standing library it comes out of |

The commons arm has a key that turns out to have been written in Kevin's own
files: **each seat's first-person verb is what lets unrelated software land in
the right place.** A text-comparison tool, an audio track-splitter and a physics
routine come from different worlds and share no vocabulary — and all three are
doing *I take this one out*. **Sorting incoming mechanisms by the gesture they
perform rather than the field they came from** is what makes divergent code line
up. The unit is a *mechanism*, not a project — small enough to write yourself,
which also dissolves most of the licence question, which reduces the licence
surface, because what is copied is a pattern rather than a file. Twenty-seven are
catalogued.

*"Its a world"* is the load-bearing one. A game is a thing you open, play and
close. **A world is the container all three surfaces are inside at once** — not a
menu of three games.

## 12 · THE OTHER PERSON

The endgame verb: grow a **fruit** — carrying shape, never words — and hang it on
the membrane at your world's edge. Send an invitation.

The invited person walks a bridge into your world's shallows. They see your
terrain, your light, your standing structures. **Never your words.** They may
take the fruit.

When they take it and mark it as a gift, the deep surfaces something for you: a
recognition built from what you had released long before they arrived.

**Gift → witnessed → answered from your own past.**

Two constraints hold this in place. **Nothing leaves the world but fruit, by the
hand** — no telemetry, no analytics, no accounts, no sync. And **taking is not
spending**: the world does not run out of a thing it has already given.

What actually departs when a fruit departs — the fruit only, or the stand and the
ground it grew from — is an open fork and is Kevin's.

## 13 · WHAT NEVER BENDS

Twelve, and they are the product rather than preferences.

1. **Content-blind, permanently.** Nothing reads, judges, rewrites, summarises or
   infers categories from the player's words. Verbatim only.
2. **No number reaches the player.** No scores, counts, ranks, percentages,
   progress bars or streaks. Nothing compares regions to each other, ever.
   Progress is **form** — water level, light, terrain, the size of what surfaced.
3. **No model call on the core loop.** No network and no LLM anywhere on the path
   a player walks.
4. **The hand runs the filter.** No automatic sorting, no computed pass, no
   classifier deciding what a fraction is.
5. **Three outputs at every station.**
6. **Set-it-down is silent.** No destination, no animation, no confirmation.
7. **Held is lawful.**
8. **The world runs while you are in it and stops when you leave.** No offline
   progression, no catch-up simulation on load. *(And this collides with §5.
   `THE_GAME.md`, the design of record, says "the creature works only while
   you're away — close the game and the deep begins." Both lines are Kevin's,
   nine days apart, and nothing has ruled between them. It is called an
   exception in some passes and forbidden here; the collision is real and is
   listed in §16 rather than settled.)*
9. **The deep never renders.**
10. **Colour is never the only carrier of a distinction.** Fractions separate by
    behaviour, with colour redundant. **The fractions are four** — *dissolved ·
    suspended · bedload · contaminant* (F7, marked 2026-08-17). Three of them
    behave on screen today — one sinks fast, one hangs, one films. **The fourth
    is ruled and not built as a behaviour** — `contaminant` is not one of the
    motions in the ascent, though the name is already carried in four other
    files, and the standing register holds it as INTENDED rather than absent.
11. **Nothing reaches outward.**
12. **Quitting mid-sentence loses nothing**, and nothing on screen says so.
13. **The world holds no legible record of the writer's behaviour.** Arrival
    timing is quantised past the point of inference, session cadence is not
    stored, and the hand can destroy the ground and the store at any time by
    an act needing no confirmation. What the world keeps is the writing and
    the shape it made — never the schedule of the hand that made it.

*The thirteenth is Kevin's mark of 2026-08-17 and it is the newest law here.
It exists because content-blindness was doing privacy work it cannot do: the
four display sources are, feature for feature, a published behavioural
instrument. Law 1 stops a claim being formed. Law 13 stops the material for
one being kept. **It is declared and not yet enforced** — no instrument checks
quantisation or the absence of a cadence store, and until one does this line
is INTENDED in the standing register rather than HARDENED.*

### The display law

The clause that stops the first law being defeated through the side door:

```
The machine may never read the words to decide how the words appear.

Every display state — glow, dim, hue, weight, size, face, motion —
must derive from one of exactly four sources:
  1. WHERE the writing sits in the world
  2. WHAT THE HAND DID with water and light at that site
  3. HOW LONG it has been there
  4. WHAT IT HAS CROSSED - the traversals the hand carried it through,
     and what became of it there: branched, ended, returned, held,
     carried forward unchanged

Never from a fifth: what the writing says.
```

**■ AMENDED 2026-08-17 — the fourth source.** Kevin's mark: *"traversal is a
fourth source — add it to the display law."* The fourth admits what a piece of
writing has UNDERGONE — that the hand offered it to a work surface, that it
branched, that a branch ended, that it came back unchanged — as a lawful driver
of appearance. Circulation through models is the case that opened it: a crossing
is a fact about a writing's life, not a claim about its meaning.

**It carries its own lint, because a widening is where a law gets defeated.**
Source four records the FACT and the SHAPE of a crossing, never a return's
content and never a judgement made inside one. *Came back stronger* is a reading
of a crossing and is the fifth source wearing the fourth's coat.

**And the bound has to sit at the write, not at the render — which an earlier
statement of it got wrong.** That statement was: strip the model's output from
the record, and if a display state changes, the state was reading content. It
does not hold. Source four is stored as flags — *branched · ended · returned ·
held · carried forward unchanged* — and deciding that a crossing BRANCHED rather
than CAME BACK UNCHANGED is a judgement about what returned. Strip the text
afterwards and the flag survives, the display does not change, and the test
prints green. **Content laundered through a classification passes a test aimed
at the render.**

So the law names who assigns the outcome, and the answer is the hand. A crossing
is recorded by the person who made it; nothing in the build decides what came
back. The one comparison the machine may make is **byte-identity on a digest** —
identical or not, one bit, no reading in it — which is how *came back unchanged*
is established and the only way it may be. `traversal_check` holds the record to
it: there is no field for a return's content, so there is nothing to classify
from.


Two consequences. **Water decides whether you can read it** — submerged,
suspended, surfaced, settled. **Light decides what colour it is.** Two
independent axes, and neither opens the text. And **the machine's own words are
never coloured; only yours take colour** — which is what stops the surface
appearing to have feelings about you.

### Why they are lints and not sermons

A rule a session reads and then decides about is a rudder against a current. The
refusals here are **filesystem facts**: eighteen instruments run from one
command and refuse rather than advise. The strongest of them do not ask anything to
behave — one of the two paths that draw a stored sentence takes an *index into the
store* and has no string parameter at all, so machine wording cannot reach the
screen through it. The other, the lens path, takes an array of strings — and that
is a gap, named here rather than smoothed.

The corpus paid to learn the failure mode too: **a blank screen passes every
refusal test.** A world with no text primitives satisfies the silence law
vacuously, and a suite once asserted that incapacity as its strongest guarantee.
A test passable by absence tests nothing. So the instruments are
**presence-asserting** — a run that checks nothing must not print green.

## 14 · HOW THE WORLD KNOWS WHAT IT KNOWS

The newest organ, built 2026-08-17, and it governs the documents rather than the
game.

A corpus this size accumulates statements from different jurisdictions that look
identical on the page. A click says a thing binds. `solid.js` says a thing is
geometrically true. A walk says a thing was seen. A hardened convention says the
system behaves as though something binds. **Those are not four ways of saying
true.**

So every consequential claim now answers two questions: **what kind of standing
do I have**, and **what keeps me standing.**

Seven kinds, no generic fallback: **MARKED** (his words admitted it) · **MEASURED**
(a computation over a live authority returns it) · **OBSERVED** (a hand walked it)
· **HARDENED** (the system behaves as though it binds, ratified or not) ·
**DERIVED** (mechanical, and re-runnable by a second hand) · **INFERRED** (a
reading, and never disguised as one).

**A provenance says where a claim came from. A support set says why it is still
warranted.** Those are different things, and only the second survives the ground
moving. So every support is a reference that resolves or fails to — a predicate
re-run, a line present in a file or absent, a mark, another claim. Nothing is
cached; re-evaluation *is* the mechanism.

When a support stops resolving the verdict is **UNSUPPORTED**, which is not
false. It is *something beneath me changed; I no longer know whether I stand.*
Nothing is rewritten and nothing is invalidated. The break is exposed and left
for a hand — because a classifier deciding the answer would be the same organ
law 4 already refuses, one level up.

Its first run caught two live breaks; one has since been repaired by a mark, and
the other stands on purpose. One was a document asserting the build's
creative freeze still binds; the line it stood on had been superseded and the
support no longer resolved. The other was the claim authorising the spine's own
construction — **the spine priced its own build** and reported that it stands on
an unsupported claim.

**The world is thoroughly surveyed and barely inhabited.**

## 17 · WHO IT IS FOR, AND WHAT IS NOT KNOWN

A calm god-game where your daily writing is the weather, which keeps everything
and reads none of it. Bought once, owned outright: nothing leaves it, nothing is
measured, nothing is compared, and nothing is ever explained back to you.

**The appetite it serves is narrow and worth naming as narrow.** The nearest
successes in this register sell the opposite thing. Kind Words is loved because a
stranger reads your actual words and answers them. Rosebud takes subscriptions to
read your journal and hand back patterns, and its paid unlock is memory. People
do not merely tolerate being read — they pay a premium to be *remembered*.

So this does not compete for that appetite and should stop implying it does. It
sells **not being assessed**, which is a different want and a smaller market.

**And no one has been asked.** There is no buyer, no price tested, no stranger
who has walked it. §15 already says the world is barely inhabited; the honest
form of this section is that the commercial claim is the least evidenced thing
in the document, and it stays that way until someone plays it and comes back the
next morning without being asked to.

## THE END IT IS BUILT TOWARD

You will open it one morning months from now, for no reason, and drop a line over
a part of the ground you do not remember making. It will come back with a
sentence you wrote and forgot — verbatim, unlabelled, nothing attached saying why
that one, and no record anywhere of the places where the line found nothing.

That is the only promise the world makes, and it has two halves:

> **Nothing you release is wasted. Nothing you release is read.**

Everything else in this document — twelve seats, thirty levels, six hinges,
a centre that cannot be reached, a bowl of twelve that closes on a lens — is the
apparatus that lets those two sentences stand next to each other.
