# THE WORK SURFACES — twelve seats, what a hand does at each

**The counsel pass, 2026-08-14.** One entry per seat, seven fields. Grounded in
the bodies as they stand in `ascent.html`, not in what they might become.

**STATE: WALKABLE.** The placement it waited on is pinned in `THE_SOLID.md`,
cut by his line *"↓TANK and ↓FILTER are joined"* together with the counsel
brief's own census of the eight crossing edges.

---

## 0 · THE GEOMETRY UNDER THE TWELVE

The four circuit sets are the four central hexagons of a cuboctahedron: every
vertex on exactly two, as the solid requires. Three falling then three rising
holds on all four. The placement is pinned, and the twenty-four members, the four
turns, the four returns and the six antipodes are in `THE_SOLID.md`.

**Nothing in the twelve entries below depends on it.** A seat's gesture, its
material, its work surface, its outputs, what persists, its cost and its held
form are properties of the seat.

---

## 1 · THE TWELVE

Read the fields as: **gesture · material in · work surface · outputs ·
what persists · cost · held form.**

Where a field describes something not yet built, it says so in its own line
rather than in a footnote.

> **RULING, 2026-08-15 — Kevin's mark: *the third output is the stations' work.***
>
> This pass listed **three outputs** as a field of every seat. Law 1 says three
> outputs at every *station*, and the build calls `bays()` from `stations` alone.
> Spec disagreed with spec; the mark settles it. **The three outputs are one
> seat's job, not twelve seats' field.** The heading is `outputs` from here — a
> seat has whatever outputs it has, and only the stations owe all three.
>
> `assertion_audit` F4 now asks that **exactly one** seat carry all three. Not
> "at least one": a second seat carrying them would mean the third output leaked
> back out into the world it was just ruled out of.
>
> The cost line at each seat is **declared** in `ascent.html` beside its gesture,
> not derived — a parser cannot see what a hand gives up.

### ↓ THE TANK — *writing becomes water*

**GESTURE** — **I POUR.** Built, `g:"reach"`.
**MATERIAL IN** — what the hand types, or what arrived at the head of the tank
from the daily surface through the internal open loop.
**WORK SURFACE** — a writing field that opens by itself; and, when water has
already arrived, beads held at the head of the tank that a hand reaches for.
**THREE OUTPUTS** — *commit* (Ctrl+Enter: it falls and pools) · *lay aside*
(Escape: nothing kept, nothing said, re-enterable) · **the third leaves no trace
by design** — a bead reached for and released back is simply still there.
**WHAT PERSISTS** — sentences kept verbatim in `S.kept`, forever, never counted;
the water's level; and, since the rebuild, its load and its clarity.
**THE COST** — the writing is taken. Deleting the page removes nothing that has
banked; the world has it.
**HELD FORM** — **built.** The panel is a held state: everything typed sits
uncommitted until Ctrl+Enter, and Escape sets the whole of it down.

---

### ↓ THE DAM — *power comes at the release*

**GESTURE** — **I HOLD, THEN I LET GO.** Built, `g:"hold"`.
**MATERIAL IN** — the level THE TANK raised. There is only as much head to
gather as was actually poured.
**WORK SURFACE** — a handle on the gate. It rides down while held and back up
when released, and it is the only moving thing in the room before anything else
moves. Mouse or E or space.
**THREE OUTPUTS** — *release* (the wheel turns on the drop) · *let go early*
(under the threshold: nothing releases, nothing is spent) · *leave without
touching it* (the room is left exactly as found).
**WHAT PERSISTS** — `S.released`; and in the water, spent level, raised load,
lowered clarity — the suspension THE FILTER will have to sort.
**THE COST** — the head is spent. What was held is not held any more.
**HELD FORM** — **built, and it is the whole mechanic.** The gathering IS the
holding; the fraction gathered is drawn as form and never as a figure.

---

### ↓ THE FILTER — *separation by behaviour, by hand*

**GESTURE** — **I TAKE THIS ONE OUT.** Built, `g:"reach"`.
**MATERIAL IN** — what is in suspension: what the dam stirred, or since the
rebuild, what the writing itself brought in.
**WORK SURFACE** — bits falling at three speeds — one sinks fast, one hangs, one
films. Separable by behaviour with colour redundant. A hand on one takes it.
**THREE OUTPUTS** — *take it* (into `S.caught`) · *let it fall past* (it leaves
below and is not lost, it is simply not sorted) · *leave the room* (nothing
sorted, and an unsorted water is a lawful water).
**WHAT PERSISTS** — `S.caught`, which is the queue THE STATIONS will route; and
a clearer, lighter water.
**THE COST** — clarity is bought with attention, one piece at a time. Nothing
sorts in bulk and nothing sorts for you.
**HELD FORM** — **■ BUILT 2026-08-14, this doc not updated until now.** A taken
bit rides at the top of the room, held, until a second reach settles it into
the bank; a reach on the water below returns it, still falling. Nothing
settles on one touch, nothing expires — the exact candidate named below,
built. — `ascent.html:1105-1112`, comment cites this file by name.

### ↓ THE STATIONS — *three outputs, always*

**GESTURE** — **I SEND THIS THERE.** Built, `g:"reach"`.
**MATERIAL IN** — the queue of what the filter caught.
**WORK SURFACE** — three bays, drawn as bays, marked ↑ ↓ —.
**THREE OUTPUTS** — *send on to a spire* · *drop to the lake* · *set it down,
which has no destination, no animation and no confirmation.* The absence is the
feedback.
**WHAT PERSISTS** — `S.routed`, permanently, per output. **This is the seat that
proves field five:** send two on and drop two to the lake and the load returns
exactly where it started, while the record of where each went is complete and
permanent. A consequence that persists is not always a consequence in the water.
**THE COST** — routing is irreversible and there is no unroute.
**HELD FORM** — **■ BUILT, this doc not updated until now.** A piece rests on
the lip of a bay (`L.lip`) and only a second reach sends it — the exact
candidate named below, built. — `ascent.html:1205-1206`.

### ↓ THE GROUND — *what it sets down is the ground*

**GESTURE** — **I LET IT SETTLE.** Built, `g:"wait"`.
**MATERIAL IN** — what the released water carries down. Sentences, falling.
**WORK SURFACE** — the bed itself. **You do not place them.** They land where
they land, along the width, in arrival order, oldest deepest.
**THREE OUTPUTS** — *let it settle* · *leave before it lands* (nothing is set
down, and nothing is lost) · *return later and find it already settled* — the
world does this while you are not here, which is the one output that costs the
hand nothing.
**WHAT PERSISTS** — `S.ground`, and it is the only ground there is. There is no
other.
**THE COST** — none to the hand. That is the point of the seat: the one place
where the correct act is not to act.
**HELD FORM** — **built by its nature.** Everything falling is held until it
lands, and the hand cannot hurry it.

---

### ↓ THE DEEP — *what you release comes back as ground*

**GESTURE** — **I LET IT GO DOWN.** Built, `g:"reach"`.
**MATERIAL IN** — what was released and what was sounded.
**WORK SURFACE** — the water above a line, and below it the deep, **which never
renders.** A reach above the line sounds it; nothing below is ever drawn.
**THREE OUTPUTS** — *sound it* · *let it sink unsounded* · *leave the surface
still.*
**WHAT PERSISTS** — `S.sank`; and what went down returns later as ground, on a
day you did not come back to check.
**THE COST** — what goes to the deep is out of reach for a while, and nothing
says how long.
**HELD FORM** — **built by the law.** The deep never renders, so everything in it
is held by definition — it is the one seat whose contents cannot be inspected
before they settle.

---

### ↑ THE LENS — *the hand works the material*

**GESTURE** — **I WORK IT UNTIL IT HOLDS.** Built, `g:"reach"`.
**MATERIAL IN** — his own sentences, standing on three faces.
**WORK SURFACE** — three faces you walk between (WASD or arrows) and read by
holding. When a thing can be held across all three, that is the lens.
**THREE OUTPUTS** — *the lens forms* · *read and move on* (nothing forms, nothing
is lost) · *leave it unread* — three faces that were never all held is a lawful
state and no lens is a lawful outcome.
**WHAT PERSISTS** — `S.lenses`.
**THE COST** — it takes three readings, and two is nothing. The seat cannot be
half-walked.
**HELD FORM** — **built.** Holding IS the read; a face released before it is held
gives nothing and takes nothing.

---

### ↑ THE HELIOSTAT — *light rises from the world below*

**GESTURE** — **I AIM.** Built, `g:"hold"`.
**MATERIAL IN** — light, whose strength is how many times the cycle has run.
**WORK SURFACE** — a mirror aimed by holding: the angle follows the hand while
the button is down, and a seated mirror keeps its own aim.
**THREE OUTPUTS** — *aim and light something* · *aim at nothing* (the beam lands
where it lands, and nothing says it was wrong) · *let go and leave the aim where
it was.*
**WHAT PERSISTS** — where the light falls, and what grows where light and water
meet.
**THE COST** — light is the scarce resource and aiming it here is not aiming it
there.
**HELD FORM** — **built.** The aim is live only while held; releasing settles it.
**The brief's open question sits here:** whether a projection — a writing thrown
to show where it lands, before it lands — is the rise-side held form. *The
candidate is a beam drawn to its landing point before the release commits it.*
**This pass leaves it standing.**

### ↑ THE SEATING — *the frame is fed, never filled*

**GESTURE** — **I FEED THE FRAME.** Built, `g:"reach"`.
**MATERIAL IN** — a lens, cut where the hand wanted.
**WORK SURFACE** — the writing laid out so it can be seen, and the seam nearest
the hand drawn as a rule through the line. No midpoint drawn, no suggested seam,
no default.
**THREE OUTPUTS** — *cut and release* (two parts rise and seat where they belong)
· *cut elsewhere* (the hand cuts where it wants) · **leave it uncut** — an uncut
writing stays uncut and the level stays unfinished and re-enterable.
**WHAT PERSISTS** — `S.seated`: seat indices, one list, two places. Mirror *i* is
lit if and only if seat *i* is fed.
**THE COST** — the hand cannot aim at a socket from either end. Where a lens
comes to rest follows from its drift and from which seats are already fed.
**HELD FORM** — **built.** The seam follows the hand and nothing is cut until the
release; and an uncut writing is explicitly lawful.

---

### ↑ THE OVERWINTERING — *time cannot be counterfeited*

**GESTURE** — **I COME BACK.** Built, `g:"wait"`.
**MATERIAL IN** — a day that actually passed. Nothing else is accepted.
**WORK SURFACE** — the world as it is on a different day. The surface *is* the
elapsed time; there is nothing else to touch.
**THREE OUTPUTS** — *return on a new day* · *return the same day* (the world is
as you left it, and nothing is withheld or scolded) · *do not return* — a fallow
season is a correct state.
**WHAT PERSISTS** — `S.lastDay`, and the strength of everything that needs
returns.
**THE COST** — it cannot be paid with effort. **This is the only seat where the
cost is not the hand's to pay.**
**HELD FORM** — **built by its nature.** Everything waiting on a return is held,
and no act shortens it.

---

### ↑ THE GARDEN — *it fills only from play*

**GESTURE** — **I LET IT GROW.** Built, `g:"wait"`.
**MATERIAL IN** — everything that happened, without being asked for.
**WORK SURFACE** — the growth itself. **It never says what it means.**
**THREE OUTPUTS** — *look and leave* · *look and stay* · *never come* — and the
garden fills the same either way, because it fills from play and not from
attention.
**WHAT PERSISTS** — what grew. It is not a score and it is not a record of you.
**THE COST** — none. The garden is the seat that takes nothing.
**HELD FORM** — **built by its nature.** Growth is held between visits, and
nothing about it can be settled by a hand.

---

### ↑ THE CAST — *the world loses what it gives*

**GESTURE** — **I GIVE IT AWAY.** Built, `g:"hold"`.
**MATERIAL IN** — what is worth giving, which by circuit one means what has
actually wintered.
**WORK SURFACE** — a line drawn back by holding; the pull builds while held and
falls back when let go.
**THREE OUTPUTS** — *cast* (it departs) · *let the pull fall* (nothing leaves,
and nothing is spent) · *leave without drawing* — the door out is never a wall
and never a demand.
**WHAT PERSISTS** — `S.cast`, and the world's `fetch`.
**THE COST** — **this is the seat where the cost is the point.** The corpus calls
it "half-built and dishonest" today: the file leaves and the world loses nothing.
What actually departs is on the decision surface and is not settled here.
**HELD FORM** — **built.** The pull is a held state that decays if the hand stops
— the one gesture in the world that undoes itself when abandoned.

## 2 · WHAT THE TWELVE SHOW WHEN LAID SIDE BY SIDE

**Every seat has a held form, and eight of the twelve already had one.** They
were not designed as a set; holding is what these mechanics do.

**Two seats have no held form and both are on the descent, adjacent in the
built level one:** THE FILTER and THE STATIONS. Both are `reach`, and a reach
settles on contact — which is exactly why the brief's instruction bites hardest
there. *Hold the route before walking it* has no counterpart yet.

**Three seats cost the hand nothing** — THE GROUND, THE GARDEN, and THE
OVERWINTERING — and all three are `wait`. **The gesture that costs nothing is
the gesture where doing nothing is the mechanic**, which is a tautology only
until you notice they are also the three that work while you are away.

**One seat's cost cannot be paid at all.** THE OVERWINTERING takes a day that
actually passed, and no act substitutes.
