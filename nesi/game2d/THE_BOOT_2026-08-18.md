## THE ONE TOOL THAT MAKES THIS BOOT PROMPT WORK: THE PRESTRESS TEST

Before reading anything else in this corpus, know how to read it fast. A
document in `nesi/` is one of three things, and telling them apart in ten
seconds is worth more than reading any one of them in full:

- **PRESTRESSED** — general, would hold against a different situation than
  the one that produced it, and/or re-evaluates against something live
  (`solid.js`, `standing.js`, a running check). Trust it.
- **HELD** — declared, and nothing enforces it yet, and it usually says so.
  Read it as a proposal, never as settled ground.
- **REACTIVE** — shaped to one incident, evidence rather than structure.
  Useful as provenance, not as a rule to extend.

This is not a vibe check. It is the actual test law 3 in `LEARNED.md` was
rewritten around today, and it is how a same-day sweep of ~110 documents got
sorted without reading every one at equal depth. If you need the full sort,
it exists — ask, don't re-run it blind.
