Everything from one session, made runnable. Three parts: a solid you can turn by hand, a gate that works, and the laws underneath both. Nothing here scores you, keeps a profile, or requires maintenance. Reload and it's empty — the node is the file you hold, not the page.

§ 01 The solid

Twelve vertices, twenty-four struts. Drag to turn it. Move the slider and the struts stay rigid while the whole thing contracts — this is the jitterbug, and it is the real transformation, not an interpolation between shapes.

Watch the R / e readout. There is exactly one position where the distance from the center to any vertex equals the length of a strut. That position is vector equilibrium. Everywhere else, reach and hold have come apart.

Solid lines are the 24 rigid struts. Dashed lines are the six square-face diagonals — the gaps. They are the only degrees of freedom in the whole structure, and they are what allows the collapse. At the icosahedral moment they close to strut length and the thing briefly becomes rigid.

Two facts survive falsification, and only two.

R = e. At equilibrium, what you hold and what you extend are the same length. Your reach to a neighbour cannot exceed your own radius. That is not a metaphor about generosity — it is the definition of the solid, and it is checkable above.

Twelve around one. Closest packing of equal spheres puts exactly twelve around a nucleus, and their centres describe this shape. In a packed lattice every sphere is simultaneously the nucleus of its own twelve. Move your attention one sphere over and the identical structure reappears, centred there. There is no privileged position and no view from above.

Equilibrium is not a structure. It bears no load and cannot be built with — the moment force is applied it collapses through icosahedron toward octahedron. So equilibrium is not the goal. It is the resting condition in which contact can be detected without anyone being pushed.

§ 02 The gate

A working membrane, reduced to what actually has to exist.

Something arrives. You are asked one question and only at the moment of asking: can you hold this today? Nothing about you is stored — no radius, no profile, no history of what you declined. There is nothing to keep current and nothing to feed on the day you have nothing.

Silence is legal. An unanswered contact stays unanswered. It is a complete state. No badge, no count, no decay into a debt. You never have to perform a refusal, which is most of what refusing costs.

At the gate — unanswered

Answering I can hold this opens a vector — the symmetry breaks, someone gave and someone received, and the structure has left equilibrium. That is supposed to happen. What matters is whether it comes back.

Open vectors — not yet returned to zero

This is the only thing the node persists, and note what it is not about. Not people, not reputation, not what anyone is owed. Just exchanges that started and haven't closed. That's the smallest possible state, and it happens to be the thing nobody tracks and the thing that actually breaks relationships — not the unmet need, the unclosed loop neither party will name.

No server, no account, no sync. The node is a file you hold and hand to someone the way you'd hand them a key. Reloading this page empties it — that is the design, not a limitation.

§ 03 The laws

Contact precedes model

Responding to your model of someone is not responding to them. Every failure below is a version of this one — the institution responds to its category, the giver responds to an imagined receiver, the withdrawal responds to an assumed capacity.

Response to a projected state is not response to an actual state.

The reciprocity gate

The commons movement hit the problem of free contribution being absorbed and filed, and built a clause for it. Full use by common-good bodies, by self-owned enterprises, by for-profits that contribute. Those who extract without returning, pay. The condition is reciprocity, not money.

the idea is less the money flow, but the recreation of a moral economy and social charter about reciprocityPeer Production License
Circulation is open to whoever returns capacity. Extraction is priced. Giving into a body with no channel for reception is not generosity. It is giving from depletion, which corrupts the gift — it just doesn't feel like depletion when no money changes hands.

Anti-islanding

A grid-tie inverter will not export into a dead line. Not stinginess — a lineman is down there with his hands on the conductor. It keeps no model of the grid at all; it checks continuously, in the moment, and cuts out in two seconds. Its reliability comes from having no memory.

Check whether the line is live before exporting. Enthusiasm is not a live line. It is a voltage reading taken on an isolated segment.

The fork test

Open projects tolerate leadership by demonstrated competence only because exit is free. Remove the fork and the identical arrangement becomes plain authority — same people, same decisions, completely different structure.

No withdrawal without a named fork. Can they continue without you? Not comfortably — at all. If yes, stepping back is construction. If no, it is the collapse of a dependency you built, and the failure happened while you were building something with no second source. Build the fork first. Then step back.

Limit, stated plainly: you can build a fork into a system you control. You cannot build one into a person. Where the thing being withdrawn from is a relationship, this test does not transfer and there is no replacement for it here.

Surplus

Wolfhagen, Germany, 2012. The municipality and an 814-member citizens' cooperative jointly own the local energy utility. The co-op holds 25% of the equity. Member dividends are capped at roughly 4%; everything above the cap routes to efficiency work through member-controlled boards, and the association is required to put surplus toward establishing further partnerships.

Cap the draw. Route the remainder to capacity. A movement's return is spent on the next movement's way in, or it did not return.

One town is not a proof. One town with a capped dividend and fourteen years of operating history is worth more than any amount of theory about circulation.

No score

An open-source value-accounting project built an algorithm to assign equity by contribution, ran it, and retreated — renaming itself contribution accounting on the grounds that value is subjective and cannot be counted. They kept the ledger and abandoned the score. That failure is available for free.

The register logs. It does not score. A metric on capacity is the proving impulse wearing a hat.

§ 04 The twelve lenses

The solid in § 01 has twelve vertices. This is what sits at them — not twelve topics, twelve readings of one corpus, and the geometry is not decoration on the arrangement. It is the arrangement.

Four circuits — four complete, independent readings of the whole pile, each a way of asking one question of everything. Six pairs — every pair of circuits crosses at two antipodal points. Twelve lenses — two per pair. One reads the joint from the side where both circuits are present; its antipode reads the same joint from the side where one or both are absent.

Presence and absence are the two ends of one axis, which is why the negative lens is not a complaint — it is the other half of the same reading.

So L1 reads what runs; L2 reads what is still, and keeps two kinds of stillness strictly apart — a body that performs no act, and an act named with no body anywhere. Neither lens may reach into the other's half. A lens that reports both ends of its own axis has collapsed the axis and produces false volume.

The array constitution — binding on all twelve

1 · Independence is the whole instrument. No lens sees another's return. Overlap means something only if neither could have known what the other found. 2 · Verbatim, always. No paraphrase, no tidying, no normalising of his spelling or his terms. 3 · A lens reports. It does not recommend. The instrument locates; it never steers. 4 · No counting that ranks. "Appears in three places" is a count. "More developed than" is forbidden. 5 · The fallow return is complete. Nothing lit here is a lawful, whole, finished return. 6 · No new naming. Where a lens must point at something unnamed, it points with a quote and says unnamed. 7 · MISSING is never filled with a plausible guess. A path from a summary is not a path. 8 · Every return states its own edge — what it read and what it did not. 9 · The returns are instruments, not documents. Read once, overlap taken, then composted. If they are still being consulted a week later, the array became a library. 10 · The reading is his. The machine never does the recognizing.

Clause 9 is the one that keeps this a gift rather than an archive. Twelve returns is twelve new objects, and the test of any move is whether it adds to the pile or moves it.

§ 05 The aporia the gate answers

The gate in § 02 asks one question and stores nothing. That shape is not a preference. It is a position on a problem Derrida spent a book on, and it is worth stating plainly because the design fails if the position is wrong.

Mauss, 1925. The base unit of the social is the persistent open entry — A gave, B owes. Obligation-to-reciprocate is what makes the gift a gift.

Derrida, 1991. That is exactly the trap. The debt is generated by the gift being seen as a gift. Recognition is the mechanism: the moment either party registers a gift as given, the circuit closes and it becomes exchange. On this reading a pure gift is impossible, because appearing as one destroys it.

Five answers exist in the field, and each keeps something and pays for it — hide the debt in the interval, bracket the terms, purify the exchange, deny it was ever a debt, make obligation itself the freedom.

This node takes a sixth position: relocate the trap from RECOGNITION to ADDRESSABILITY. Completion is atomic with delivery. The giver's relation terminates at the moment of handing over — so there is no open entry, not even on the giver's own books.

And here is where it can fail, stated rather than hidden. Derrida's circuit can close internally, on the giver's side, through self-recognition — the giver knowing they gave and booking the credit privately. So the diagnostic is: is completion-at-delivery load-bearing, or aspirational? If the giver retains any tracked relation to the thing that left — a watching, a hoping-it-lands, a checking — the internal ledger is open and the objection survives at the giver's node. The claim stands or falls exactly there.

Which is why § 02 keeps open vectors and nothing else. Not people, not reputation, not what anyone is owed — only exchanges that started and have not closed, held so they can be closed rather than watched.

§ 06 What stays open

Three, unresolved, deposited as open rather than closed comfortably.

The question the design still asks you to carry. Can you hold this today? has to be answerable on the day it matters most — the depleted day, when the honest answer is hardest to reach and the habit of overstating is strongest. Nothing above solves that. It is a body question, not a design question.

The fork test on a person. Stated above. No replacement offered.

Both bodies of work remain unfalsified in the same direction. Neither the commons literature nor the geometry names a system that lacks the pattern in a way that would test it. If everything that works can be read as a cuboctahedron and nothing that fails can, the mapping carries no information. R = e and twelve-around-one survive because both could be wrong. The rest is decoration.
