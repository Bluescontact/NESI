## CONNECT — the edges, developed (this stage, ready to ratify or refine)

The shared face has a small, closed set of working relationships. Each is one edge:

1. **APPROVE-OUT** (interior → website): only what an owner copies out of their library is on the shared tarp. The larger library never crosses. *This is station-1 load: the owner's no spent at authoring time — only the survivors are offered.* Governed by each owner's own gate.
2. **THE OFFER** (on the face): a copy, immutable, sitting in the library-of-gifts. Not the original. Grabbing it never reaches into an interior.
3. **TAKE** (website → other interior): the visitor copies an offer into their own library **at their own gate** (absorb-in). Bounded only by what's on offer — "take everything" means everything on the tarp, nothing behind it.
4. **THE FRAME** (the face's founding condition): "take everything, no cost, no debt" — station 2. Makes taking and refusing costless, legal, expected, in advance. The login-under-the-frame is where it's declared.
5. **THE WITNESS** (the website): holds the exchange, decides nothing. Not a controller, not a mediator. It displays offers and handles entry + take. Thin.
6. **THE NAVIGATOR** (each local NESI): does the heavy lifting inside each interior — metabolize, sort, find the win-win — before and around the exchange. Never sits in the exchange.
7. **NO-ACK CLOSE** (station 4): the circuit closes without recognition. A visitor can take everything and leave; the gift is received (not debt), and no thank-you is required to close either side.

**The one open CONNECT question for the next session:** whether the website needs any account/identity at all, or whether the emailed login is a bare capability-URL (a secret link = the whole auth). The reframe pushes toward the latter — no identity axis, just a link under the frame. Confirm or refine.

## LIMIT — the hard edges (name before building)

- The website is **thin by law** — display + entry + take. No processing, no accounts, no mediation. If it starts doing heavy lifting, it has drifted into being the navigator (which stays local).
- **No debt instrument may exist** anywhere in the face. If any "owed / reciprocate / balance" field appears, the frame is broken.
- The gift never routes *through* NESI. If NESI ends up mediating the exchange, the reframe failed.

## THE MINIMAL BUILD (what to actually make first)

A **thin shared website face**: a page that (a) shows the library-of-gifts (the offer set), (b) is entered by a capability link under the frame-declaration, (c) offers a "take" that copies an offer to the visitor's side. Each person's NESI stays the local surface already built tonight (`nesi/NESI_surface.html` + `surface_bridge.py` + `surface_app.py`). The website is the ONLY new served surface — and it is thin.
