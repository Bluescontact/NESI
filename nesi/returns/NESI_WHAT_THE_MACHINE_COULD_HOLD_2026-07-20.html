candidate holdings · compiled from the year's record · as of 2026-07-20

A companion to the whole-machine artifact. That one reads what NESI is. This one reads what NESI could hold — the infrastructure, gift patterns, mechanisms, and tools that currently live in your head or scattered across the record, each asked one question: would it serve if the machine held it instead of you? To hold a piece is to move it off head and hand, stand it so it runs beneath the surface, and keep it lawful under staging-only and fail-closed. Nothing here is marked. Every piece below is a candidate awaiting cross / hold / compost.

1 · The Test For Holding

A piece earns a place in the machine's hands only when both hold:

  • It lowers a cost you carry — friction, routing, or coordination. If holding it changes nothing you do, it stays out.
  • It leaves the mark on your touch — holding is not automating the cross. The machine may route, stage, tend, and surface; it may never mark. A piece that would decide for you fails the test regardless of how useful it looks.

Holding is standing, not deciding. The framework doesn't disappear when NESI holds it — it goes under the surface and runs as governance. That's the whole difference between a thing you perform every session and a thing that keeps itself between sessions.

5 · The Coordination Seam

The one open piece that is a decision, not a build. There are two instrument sets — NESI's organs and the DSS gate stack — and one operator running both. Every day they stay two engines, you pay the coordination cost of holding the seam between them in your head.

If NESI held the DSS instruments — or the DSS held NESI, or one inherits, wraps, or composts the other — that cost collapses to zero. Whether they are two engines or one under two names is flagged unresolved in the record, and one live question sharpens it: the interrogator that reaches back and the DSS gate-delta may be the same check specified twice. This is surfaced, not answered. It's a mark, and it's yours.
