## 2 · THE GRAMMAR, RENDERED (from his seeds)

**Axis 1 — coherence (the pattern axis, his quad):** an organ is specified as a pattern before any code: the recurring **problem** it resolves · the **forces** pushing around it · the resolved **form** (including its boundary: takes in, hands off, stops) · the **falsifier** that kills it. An organ that can't state its problem in a breath, or whose falsifier can't be written, isn't ready to raise — the grammar fails it on paper, cheaply.

**Axis 2 — sequence (the elevator axis, his order):** **plumb before load.** The laws beneath an organ must be true before it's raised; it runs as a badged false car before any real motor; its entrance is its own (each floor has its own way in — no shared lobby, no organ reached through another's door); it interlocks against what must wait (nothing outward before THE DOOR); and no stage carries the next until it's signed off with a mark.

**The spec form — 9 fields, one page, fillable at daily capacity:**
```text
ORGAN:      <name>
PROBLEM     the recurring problem it resolves, in a breath
FORCES      what pushes and pulls around it
FORM        the resolved shape · boundary: takes in / hands off / stops
FALSIFIER   the observation that composts it
PLUMB       what must be true beneath it before it's raised
FALSE CAR   its honest badged stub while the motor's out
ENTRANCE    its own way in (each floor has its own entrance)
INTERLOCK   what it must NOT do until a later organ exists
SIGN-OFF    the mark that lets the next stage carry load
```

**The generative sequence (his rule, made explicit):**
1. **Use names the next organ.** Not a diagram — friction in real operation reveals the weakest place (his workbench-last law). 2. **Check plumb** — refuse to raise over un-true ground. 3. **Fill the form** — if PROBLEM or FALSIFIER won't fill, the organ is scaffold or premature; stop on paper. 4. **Raise the false car** — badged stub, work the space. 5. **Sign off, then next** — the mark, then the next center. Each step preserves what's beneath it.
