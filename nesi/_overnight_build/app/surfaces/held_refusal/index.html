Before you enter

Four edges. Not an explanation of what's inside — a description of how this ground behaves.
1
These refusals do not move.
Nothing you do — asking again, waiting, being useful, being close — turns a no here into a yes. That firmness isn't coldness. It's the only thing that keeps whatever you're offered from becoming something you paid for.
2
Entering creates no obligation.
Opening a door costs one click and incurs nothing. You are not now expected to continue, to answer, to thank, or to come back.
3
Stopping changes nothing.
You can leave at any depth. Nothing closes behind you, nothing shifts in how you stand here, nothing is owed for having looked.
4
Each click permits only what opens next.
A click authorizes one more thing to be shown — not a branch, not a commitment. It says nothing about you. The door reports only that it was opened.

What's here

Three things, offered. Open any one. Each ends when it is complete — not when something is wanted from you.
A reading
The house you rest in tonight was built by someone who is not here to be repaid. They laid the floor level so that whoever came after could stop bracing. That is the whole of the inheritance: not the walls, but the fact that you may set your weight down without first earning the right to.
— that is the reading, complete. It ends here.
A tool — the one-currency check
When you feel a no starting to bend, name the currency bending it: is it persistence, standing, guilt, closeness, or usefulness? A no worth keeping holds against all five, separately. Test it once against each. The ones it survives are yours to keep; the ones it doesn't were never refusals, only prices.
— that is the tool, complete. Use it or don't. It ends here.
A thing made
A low bench, cut from a single fallen beam, sanded only where a hand would fall. It sits by a door so that anyone arriving tired has somewhere to be before deciding anything. It was made to be sat on by people the maker will never meet, and it needs nothing back to have been worth making.
— that is the object, complete. It ends here.
