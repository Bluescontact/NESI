THE FIELD

move a thing. that is the whole grammar.
drag — toward you, or away
drag past the rim — gone
double-click the ground — your own mark, unnamed
leave it — a complete state
shift-click two things — name what holds between them
The relation is the naming. No words are offered here on purpose — a menu would be the machine naming the thing this exists to let you name. Leave it blank and nothing is drawn.

what your hand has said — derived from position, never stored

This stages. It writes to no ledger and answers no fork. The lines are a readout of where each thing stands — never of what it is about. Take them or leave them.
