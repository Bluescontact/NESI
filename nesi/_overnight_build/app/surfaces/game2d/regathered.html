a drop, where you stand

Verbatim. Nothing reads it, tags it, or forms an opinion about it. It lands at your feet and becomes ground.

the sorting tarp

The whole pile on one cloth, all at once. A slab's footprint is how big the thing is; its shade is how long it has lain there. There is not one character of text in this instrument, and the world is not told what any of it says. You read the pile the way you read a pile.
The press folds the cloth and gathers what is on it. It stages a sweep and commits nothing — nothing is deleted, moved, marked or composted.

the round

Five moves, dealt from the live field. Play any, skip any — a skip resolves a move the same as a play. The fifth ends the round. Nothing carries over, nothing is owed, and closing the door is a complete ending.
