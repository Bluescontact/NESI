## Transferable form

When a group cannot read an act in a register it hasn't developed, it doesn't just fail to receive — it exports its failure as a verdict on the actor: capacity-data about the receiver gets stamped as character-data about the sender. Consensus adds zero information here, because every member is running the same register-blindness; agreement is correlated error, not corroboration.

## Mechanism

This is `reception_as_territory_map`'s outcome-reveals-receiver-capacity move plus one new step: the inversion-export. Two stages. Stage one: a group encounters an act in a register it cannot read — boundary, refusal, silence — and misclassifies it. Stage two, the mechanically new step: the misclassification is exported outward as a verdict on the actor ("unreasonable"). The data's direction inverts — what the moment actually measured was the receiver's capacity in that register, but it ships as a finding about the sender's character.

The shared-frame clause this borrows from `translated_origin_failure_mode` (cited, not restated): a group's confident, unanimous read is exactly as trustworthy as its members' independence — shared frame produces shared error, not confirmation.

**Timing truth.** The inversion is structurally enabled, not stupid: a held boundary and a reactive refusal share a surface at the moment of delivery. They differentiate only in the tail — a gift completes and releases; reactivity seeks repair or repetition. The group narrates at the moment of surface identity, before the differentiating evidence exists. Judge the tail, not the moment.

**Register specificity.** Groups are not globally low or high. A circle may read labor, money, and food fluently and be blind to boundary and silence; the actor may be blind in the circle's strong registers. A valid instance must name WHICH register the group couldn't read, or the pattern becomes a rank ordering of people that breaks the frame it lives inside.

**Threshold versus immune response.** Non-recognition and active rejection are different organs. A group at threshold produces confusion, silence, misclassification — "I don't understand what just happened." A group that says "they were unreasonable" has metabolized the act into a threat and is running moral expulsion — an immune response protecting a container norm. Conflating them lets the pattern claim territory that belongs to container-ejection mechanics.

**Register and habitat.** This lives in the guest's register — the person already inside a room whose narrative machinery is rewriting them in real time — not the sender's pre-routing register. In-room physics: defense confirms the label at that stage; exit or silence are the only moves that don't feed the story.

**Filing.** The base move belongs to `reception_as_territory_map` (outcome reveals receiver capacity); this is that pattern plus the inversion-export stage. The inversion step is the reusable organ: any diagnostic that detects a verdict pointing the wrong direction — receiver-capacity data stamped as sender-character data — generalizes past this instance.

## Failure modes

**The martyr's mirror.** The pattern's most dangerous false positive is self-application: any rejected act can be retroactively promoted to "clean gift" precisely because rejection now reads as evidence of the group's limit. That makes the pattern structurally identical to a persecution narrative unless gift-status is established by something other than non-reception. Boundary condition: gift-quality must be datable BEFORE the group's narration — a pre-registered read, or a witness outside both the giver and the circle — never after. Before applying "their threshold was below my act," the holder must produce what evidence would show they were being reactive. A pattern that only exonerates its user is a mirror, not a lens; it operates legitimately only where the user can state and check its own falsifier.

## Falsifier

Before calling a rejected act "clean gift," name in advance what evidence would show it was reactive instead (grasping, repetition, seeking repair) — a held boundary completes and releases; reactivity seeks return. If the actor cannot state this falsifier before applying the pattern to their own rejected act, the pattern is not licensed for self-application in that instance.

## Edges

The guest still gets no instrument: as composed, the pattern diagnoses after the wound — it names why the room failed but gives no pre-act move, no test for a circle's current recognition ceiling, no protocol for choosing to act at, above, or below that ceiling. The pre-registration requirement (gift-status datable before the group's narration) is stated but not operationalized — what counts as a valid witness or a valid pre-registered read is undefined. The threshold/immune-response boundary is drawn by narration type, but mixed cases — a group that is confused AND expelling — are unhandled. Whether the inversion step should be filed as a variant of `reception_as_territory_map` or promoted as its own organ remains a membrane decision this file does not resolve on its own.
