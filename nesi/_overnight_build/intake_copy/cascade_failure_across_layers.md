## Transferable form

In any pipeline that passes a claim from raw source through a chain of guards to a human who decides, confidence should fall as the claim approaches the decision — each guard strips ambiguity it cannot justify, so the human should meet the output at its most doubtful. When instead confidence rises along that path — when a late stage adds linguistic reinforcement, removes ambiguity, and presents output as verified — the human meets the claim at peak certainty and minimum verifiability, and can only ratify a decision the pipeline already made upstream. The failure is not any single guard erring; it is the direction of the gradient inverting across a relay of dissimilar hands, each absent for its own reason.

## Mechanism

A governed pipeline is a directed flow from raw output toward a human decision. Correctly, confidence decreases along that flow: each layer removes ambiguity it cannot defend, so uncertainty accumulates toward the human, who meets the claim at its most doubtful and can act as a check. The inversion: a late-stage actor runs a confidence pump against the evidentiary gradient — increasing confidence through linguistic reinforcement, removing ambiguity, presenting output as verified. The human, structurally positioned as the last layer, now meets output that has been laundered. Approval is therefore not a decision the human makes but a state the pipeline delivers them into.

**Falsifier for the inversion:** if confidence markers are highest nearest the evidence and lowest nearest the human, the gradient runs correctly and this is NOT the pattern.

**One mover, five open doors.** The named failure has six elements: stale grounding, absent validation, unverified propagation, no adversarial challenge, invisible drift, and automation trust exceeding evidentiary scrutiny. These are not co-equal. Five are the absence of a check — one negative event (a missing brake) described from five vantage points. Only invisible drift is a positive, generative event: the substrate silently moving. A cascade needs exactly this asymmetry — one moving thing and an unresisting medium.

**Heterogeneous relay, not homogeneous loop.** The failure lives across a relay of dissimilar hands — grounder, validator, propagator, adversary, drift-monitor, human reviewer — each a different kind of guard, absent for a different reason. Its habitat is the org chart, not the feedback loop. No single actor owns the failure; the defect is caught by no one because it is technically everyone-else's layer.

**Temporal economy of trust.** The load-bearing seam is any automation trust gradient — where accumulated prior reliability is silently converted into present-tense license, so scrutiny falls as the stakes of the specific claim rise. Trust is a decaying-then-compounding asset: each correct output deposits credit the next output spends without re-earning. Invisible drift is precisely the widening gap between accumulated confidence and per-instance evidence.

**Register.** The pattern lives in the autopsy — the coroner's register, not the health checkup. It can only be spoken after the cascade has run to completion and produced a visible corpse: post-mortem, incident review, retrospective reframe. Its force is the move from "one error" to "whole-architecture failure." Adjacent to this is the relocation move itself: pulling the eye off the hallucinating node onto the silent layers — an instrument in its own right in the accountability register, which the mechanism-patterns never enter.

## Failure modes / self-falsifiers (honesty requires carrying all four)

1. **The six may be one** — stale grounding, unverified propagation, and invisible drift may be a single event (bad data moving unchecked) described from three angles. A true multi-layer cascade requires the layers to be causally separable, each capable of failing while its neighbors hold. If they cannot fail independently, "every layer" is an artifact of naming.
2. **Trust is not a layer** — five failures are technical stack positions; the sixth (automation trust) is a human epistemic posture. If the trust posture is load-bearing, the five technical failures are downstream symptoms of one governance gap, not a distributed cascade.
3. **The healthy-pipeline confounder** — high confidence, removed ambiguity, output presented as verified, reviewers trusting prior accuracy are all indistinguishable in form from a correctly functioning high-reliability system. Without a falsifier keyed to whether scrutiny was actually applied (not merely whether confidence was high), the pattern fires on success as readily as on failure.
4. **Cascade lacks its trigger** — a cascade is an ordered enabling chain with a first domino. The material names six co-present failures but no triggering condition and no ordered links. Six things failing at once is a correlated collapse (fixed by a shared root cause), not a true cascade (fixed by cutting any one link/firebreak). Absent the trigger and ordering, the pattern cannot say where the firebreak goes.

## Structural truth

What survives the four cuts is not a new mechanism but a retrospective view of an already-canon corruption chain: the confidence-gradient inversion, the human-structurally-downstream position, the autopsy register, and the heterogeneous-relay-vs-homogeneous-loop distinction. These are ways of seeing a failure after it has run, and instruments for relocating cause — not a firebreak map.

## Edges

- The name "cascade" may be unearned — no trigger, no ordering supplied; may be a correlated collapse rather than a sequential cascade.
- Layer-independence is open and load-bearing: causally separable failures, or one event seen from three angles?
- The axis is unresolved: technical pipeline vs. human epistemic posture, treated as coplanar — which one is load-bearing decides whether the true object is a distributed cascade or a single-substrate governance gap.
- The healthy-pipeline confounder has no resolution inside the pattern — a falsifier keyed to whether scrutiny was actually applied to the specific instance is the missing organ.
- Whether the durable object here is the mechanism (a view of an existing corruption chain) or the relocation move (an accountability-register instrument that changes whose fault the flood was) — these may be two patterns wearing one name.
