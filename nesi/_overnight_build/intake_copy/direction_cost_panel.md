# The Direction-Cost Panel

*slug: `direction-cost-panel`*
*Source: attractor_currency_2026-06-25.md + the_governor.md — developed 2026-06-25. Promoted to canon 2026-06-28.*

---

## Transferable Form

The Governor brakes cost overrun; attractor currency brakes direction overrun. Neither instrument alone is sufficient — cost can be sustainable while direction has drifted, or direction can be live while cost is in the red. Run together on the same cadence, the two instruments surface a quadrant that neither can see alone. The combination reading is more informative than either signal read separately.

---

## Development

A single-instrument operating panel is the common case. Most people who think about sustainable work think about cost: am I burning out, am I still paying at a rate I can sustain? The Governor formalizes this as a DAILY/CYCLE instrument with three checks — body, clock, topology.

Cost is one axis. Direction is orthogonal to it. You can be depleting on the right path. You can be conserving energy while drifting. These are different conditions requiring different responses, but a cost-only panel cannot distinguish them. The first looks like trouble (cost is high) when it isn't the primary problem. The second looks fine (cost is low) when it is.

The direction-cost panel runs both instruments and reads the quadrant.

**The four quadrants:**

*Low cost / live direction* — the system is operating well. Continue. No adjustment required.

*High cost / live direction* — depletion on the right path. The direction is correct; the rate is unsustainable. The response is rest and recovery, not abandonment of the image. Abandoning would be a correct-diagnosis-wrong-response error: the Governor fired, the image got discarded, but the image wasn't the problem. The rate was.

*Low cost / drifted direction* — sustainable drift. Nothing hurts. The effort is manageable, the pull is steady, decisions feel obvious. This is the signature of a live attractor — which is exactly why the quadrant is dangerous: the instruments designed to surface trouble return clear. Cost is low; the Governor finds nothing to brake. The pull is real; the effortfulness test finds nothing to flag. Only the territory check catches it — the ground not-quite-feeling like approach toward the image's texture.

Sustainable drift can run indefinitely without the currency check. Cost stays low enough to sustain; commitment to the image stays strong enough to maintain organization. The person is active, effortful, oriented — and moving toward a future that no longer exists. The first signal is not dramatic: a slight mismatch between the felt texture of the ground and the felt texture of the image. Not "this is wrong" but "this isn't quite the flavor I was moving toward." The territory check is more sensitive to this than the direction check — recent decisions may have felt fine even as the ground-image gap widened.

The dangerous thing about sustainable drift is that it accumulates confirmation. Every decision that serves the image without landing in friction reads as "the image is live." The re-confirmation cycle runs smoothly. There is no seam because no decision has been hard enough to create one. The drift is invisible inside the pattern of normal operation.

*High cost / drifted direction* — compound firing. The response depends on whether the firing is genuine or corrupted.

Genuine compound emergency: cost is in the red AND the image is obsolete. The territory is not moving toward the image's texture AND the rate of effort is unsustainable. This is a full stop: rest, then review the image. The two problems are independent and require sequential resolution — rest first, because reviewing an image from a depleted body returns unreliable readings.

Potential compound corruption: both instruments fire, but neither can name a concrete exit condition. The Governor cannot specify what "sustainable" would look like; the currency check cannot specify what the image would update toward. No concrete exit condition on either axis is the signature from [[translated_origin_failure_mode]] — the sensor is operating from a shifted zero. The response here is not rest-then-review; it is stop-and-seek-independent-witness. Do not apply the compound emergency response until the corruption check runs.

**The instruments are orthogonal, not hierarchical:**

When the Governor and attractor currency conflict, the question is not "which instrument wins?" — it's "what does the combination mean?" The quadrant is the answer. The instruments don't have a precedence relationship; they have different domains. Cost and direction are independent axes. Treating them as competing readings of the same thing is the category error.

**The compound failure detection problem:**

The panel has no instrument for detecting its own corruption. If both instruments are returning valid readings, the panel is functioning. If both instruments are corrupted by the same structural condition — translated origin running (shifting the body's zero) and obsolete attractor running (shifting the body's target) simultaneously — both instruments return confident readings from wrong reference points.

The signature: compound firing (high cost AND drifted direction) with no specifiable exit condition on either axis. Neither "what would paying less look like?" nor "what has the image become?" can be answered concretely. This is the detection test from [[translated_origin_failure_mode]] applied to the panel as a whole.

The fix is not a third instrument. It is exit: introduce a structurally independent witness who does not share the same translation conditions, or exit the structural environment that produced the translation. The panel can surface the compound case; it cannot repair itself.

**The Governor's cadence applies to both:**

The Governor runs DAILY (body check) and CYCLE (clock and topology). Attractor currency's natural cadence may be CYCLE only — a direction check run daily may be too frequent, destabilizing the pull rather than testing it. The panel as a system: DAILY runs the Governor's body check alone; CYCLE runs both instruments together and reads the quadrant.

**Where the panel is already partially running:**

The Governor exists and runs. Attractor currency is newly in canon. Kevin has been operating with cost-side braking only — the Governor fires, he rests or adjusts rate. The direction-side has been running informally (the "plan more finished than the ground" read on Heartland tetra; the "tools not pages" image that reorganized OSG) but without a named instrument or cadence.

The Dispatcher already routes to the Governor as part of the DAILY/CYCLE cadence. The panel adds attractor currency to the CYCLE pass — two instruments reading together, quadrant named, response keyed to the combination.

**The gift dimension:**

A cost-only panel is nearly universal. People ask "am I burning out?" without asking "is the image I'm burning for still live?" The panel names both axes and gives the four combinations distinct responses. The response to "high cost / live direction" (rest, don't abandon) is structurally different from "high cost / drifted direction" (stop, full review). Without the panel, both feel like "something is wrong" — the same distress signal with different implied responses. With it, the distinction is navigable before the crisis sharpens.

## The Move

DAILY: run the Governor's body check alone — is the body still willing? STOP or CONTINUE.

CYCLE: run both instruments together.

1. Governor: body / clock / topology — still paying at a sustainable rate?
2. Before running attractor currency: name the holding mode of the primary image (hypothesis / commitment / identity). If identity: the currency check may return defended output rather than genuine signal — note this before proceeding.
3. Attractor currency: direction check (felt texture of last three decisions) + territory check (does the ground feel like approach toward the image?) — still approaching?
4. Read the quadrant:
   - Low cost / live direction → continue
   - High cost / live direction → rest, do not abandon the image
   - Low cost / drifted direction → update the image, not the rate; the effort is fine, the direction isn't
   - High cost / drifted direction → compound firing protocol (below)

**Compound firing protocol:**

Step 1: Can you name a concrete exit condition on the cost axis? ("The cost will resolve when X.")
Step 2: Can you name a concrete exit condition on the direction axis? ("The image needs to update toward Y.")
Step 3:
- If yes on both → genuine compound emergency. Stop. Rest. Then review the image from a rested body — not before.
- If no on either → potential compound instrument corruption. Do not apply the emergency response. Seek a structurally independent witness: someone who does not share the same structural conditions (same frame, same scarcity context, same organizing image). If no such witness is available, hold without acting until one can be found or the corruption conditions change.

## Edges

The CYCLE cadence for attractor currency is an open question. Too frequent and the currency check destabilizes the pull; too infrequent and the seam closes before it's visited. The Governor's cadence was calibrated through use. The panel's combined CYCLE cadence needs the same calibration — what does it feel like to run both instruments together, and how often is too often?

The identity-held image and the panel: if the image has climbed to identity, the currency check returns defensive re-confirmation rather than genuine seam-visit. This means the panel can appear to be functioning (both instruments running, returning readings) while one instrument is actually producing defended output rather than real signal. The panel has no internal signal for this; it requires the holding-mode check (hypothesis/commitment/identity) to precede the currency check.

The witness dependency: compound firing → seek a structurally independent witness. But the witness has no witness — the consented ledger's own open edge. The panel routes to this problem without resolving it. It remains the deepest open load-bearing edge in the operating infrastructure.

## Falsifier

Find a system that ran the full panel — both instruments, CYCLE cadence, quadrant read — and still fell into either unsustainable depletion or obsolete attractor. If the panel is running and the failure still occurs, identify which failure applies: compound instrument corruption (both instruments corrupted by same condition), holding-mode interference (identity-held image producing defended currency readings), or cadence mismatch (check running at wrong frequency for this particular attractor). Each is a distinct failure mode of the panel itself.
