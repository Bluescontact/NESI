# Metabolic Handoff

*slug: `metabolic-handoff`*
*Staged: 2026-06-27 · Source: kevin_uses_ai substrate brief; five-model adversarial audit*

---

## Transferable Form

In any collaboration where volatile context holds the rationale for disposition decisions, disposition is cheapest while that context is live. Therefore, in systems where the working context zeros abruptly at session end, disposition must be bound to the substrate-reset boundary. A session that ends without metabolizing its output does not lose the output — the artifacts remain — but the rationale for disposing them decays to zero at the boundary. The next session must reconstruct that rationale cold, at higher cost. The cumulative cost of undisposed state is compounding: each pile adds reconstruction cost to the next session.

The close-out protocol is not an add-on. It is the mechanism that converts volatile session state into durable, dispositioned system state.

---

## Development

**The cliff, not the slope.** All cognitively intensive work ends by exhaustion or interruption rather than clean completion. That is not AI-specific. What is AI-specific is the shape of the substrate loss at the boundary. Human working memory degrades gradually — a person who stops for the day retains a compressed gist of where they were. The AI context window zeros abruptly and completely at session end. No gist. No residual. The continuity humans get for free, even when sloppy, must be manufactured in the AI case or it is literally nothing. Slopes reward diligence; cliffs force ritualization. The cliff is what makes the close-out a required boundary operation rather than a beneficial habit.

**Disposition, not summary.** A summary answers: what happened? A metabolic handoff answers: what is the current state of the system, now that this session has changed it? The distinction is the state change. A summary preserves content. Disposition changes the status of content: PROMOTE, HOLD, COMPOST, PASS, LOG, STAGE. Without disposition, the next session opens on a pile. With disposition, it opens on a current state. The protocol regenerates the present rather than records the past.

**Undisposed state carries forward cost.** The failure is not information loss — the candidates, decisions, and staged work are still there as durable artifacts. The failure is accumulation of un-adjudicated state. Un-adjudicated output cannot be acted on without first adjudicating it. Each undisposed session adds a reconstruction-and-adjudication step to every subsequent session that touches the pile. The cost is not the lost information; it is the work required to restore the rationale context well enough to make the disposition.

**The context window impersonates continuity.** During a high-context session, the AI holds an enormous working world: unresolved forks, provisional names, candidate promotions, partial decisions, felt rejections, not-yet-canon insights. This produces the phenomenology of a persistent collaborator. But the state has no standing unless it crosses into a durable layer. A session can end with the human feeling "the work exists" when it exists only as volatile context. The false sense of housing is the specific AI hazard — more dangerous than ordinary session abandonment because it is harder to notice.

**Boundedness is structural.** The close-out must be bounded or it becomes another generative session, producing more state while trying to metabolize state. An uncapped close-out makes the problem recursive. The ceiling is the instrument that prevents the boundary operation from becoming its own pile. The specific time value is a calibration zero for the system; the boundedness is the pattern.

**The ceiling as undifferentiated alarm.** A close-out that chronically exceeds its ceiling signals that something in the produce-to-metabolize loop is out of balance. It does not specify which component is the problem. Possible causes: sessions are overproducing (governance problem — reduce scope or frequency); the disposition machinery is clumsy (logistics problem — improve the gate tooling); sessions are genuinely rich and have earned their length (no problem — accept the cost). The ceiling fires the alarm; the diagnosis is a separate step. Pre-assigning chronic overage to governance will cause unnecessary throttling of production.

**Domain of validity.** The pattern holds only when both conditions are present: (1) the working state exceeds the human's residual-memory capacity, so they cannot carry the rationale into the next session unaided; and (2) the disposition rationale lives primarily in volatile context rather than in the durable artifacts. Where the human can carry the gist in their head, the cliff is not load-bearing — the human is the continuity mechanism. Where the artifacts are self-documenting enough that cold disposition costs about the same as warm, the boundary ritual is optional. High-context collaboration under both conditions is the domain where the pattern bites.

**The dissolving architecture.** A collaboration infrastructure that makes its outputs sufficiently self-documenting — where each artifact carries its own disposition logic, rationale, and context — converts the cliff into a slope. Cold and warm disposition approach the same cost. At the limit, the metabolic boundary is no longer needed because the artifacts are the metabolism. This is not a failure of the pattern; it is the alternative architecture the pattern points toward. Whether disposition rationale is irreducibly contextual or can be embedded in durable artifacts is the live design question.

**Failure mode 1 — Hygiene framing.** Treating the close-out as "write good notes at the end of a session." This frames the problem as information loss and the solution as diligence. The structural problem is undisposed state; the solution is a bounded boundary operation. Hygiene framing will be deprioritized under time pressure; structural framing cannot be skipped because the next session opens on the pile.

**Failure mode 2 — The reconstruction stall.** The next session opens and cannot proceed without reconstructing the previous session's rationale from the artifacts alone. This is the cliff cost made visible. The reconstruction stall is evidence that the prior session's close-out either did not occur or did not fully metabolize its output.

**Failure mode 3 — The granularity cliff.** Some high-context work is non-compressible: the working state depends on nuances, adjacencies, and implicit framings that cannot be captured in a bounded close-out artifact. If the next session repeatedly stalls despite a completed close-out, the granularity cliff may be the cause — the snapshot cannot carry what the context held. This is a structural limit of the metabolic handoff pattern; it does not dissolve it but narrows its domain.

---

## Edges

The falsifier: if cold disposition (next session, artifacts only) costs approximately the same as warm disposition (context live), the pattern does not hold. The evidence for whether warm is meaningfully cheaper than cold comes from watching how often next-session reconstruction fails versus succeeds when starting from the close-out artifacts. The pattern's hold condition is: accumulate enough close-out data to compare warm vs. cold disposition costs directly.

The cliff framing assumes a hard reset — zero context at session end. As AI architectures move toward persistent, structured memory that survives session boundaries, the cliff may become a slope and the forced ritualization may become optional. If such architectures become standard, the pattern would be superseded rather than falsified — the mechanism that made it necessary will have been built into the medium.
