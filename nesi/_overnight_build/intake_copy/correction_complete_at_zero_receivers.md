## Transferable Form

When someone's wrong reading of you survives your correction — nothing updates afterward — the only circuit you can actually close runs inward: recalibrating your own model rather than waiting for theirs to move. Being misread is not an incident but a dwelling with rent; the self-aimed "no, you don't understand" is a boundary act, refusing residency in someone else's model of you. Its success condition is accuracy, not their change — the relief it produces is the felt signature of a closed loop, and only the inward loop closes.

## Development

MECHANISM. The correction attempt is a negative-space instrument: its signal is the null result — what fails to happen after you speak. A non-event is only meaningful inside a defined expectation window — duration, attempt count, what would count as update — and that window is a missing component of the instrument itself, not an omission from its description: without a clock and a threshold the probe cannot distinguish "absorbed" from "still metabolizing."

The trigger and the diagnostic live in different bodies. Speaking is governed by a hysteresis mechanism inside the corrector — silence accumulates cost until it exceeds the cost of speaking — while the diagnostic reads the other person's frame. The probe therefore fires when the corrector can no longer afford silence, not when measurement conditions are clean: maximum charge, worst possible condition for reading a null result. As built, the probe is never deliberate; it is pressure-released. The dwelling register explains the timing signature: costs of living inside a wrong reading compound quietly, so speaking arrives late and looks disproportionate.

The sentence "No, you don't understand" runs on two circuits. Outward — typing the other person — is open-ended and unfalsifiable from the only chair available. Inward — recalibrating yourself — terminates: it ends in accuracy, a state verifiable from your own chair. Only the inward circuit closes, and relief is its tell: relief as accuracy, explicitly not vindication. Typing the other person is the same inward move applied to your model-of-them — "my model of their model was wrong" — a second map correction, never a verdict about the receiver. The pattern corrects the corrector's map, twice; it judges no one.

The inward form opens a register with an audience of zero. The move discharges in solitude, after the relationship has closed, or where correction is structurally futile — a correction complete on saying, handleless, the same grammar as a gift complete on giving. Nothing in the neighboring territory (reception_as_territory_map, expertise_conscripted_as_rescue_fuel, keystone_held_by_least_authority) can enter that habitat; all of them require a second party or a system to name into.

FAILURE MODES — carried as the pattern's edges, not objections to it:
1. **The diagnostic absorbs its user.** From the corrector's chair, "they absorbed my correction" and "I am absorbing their correct reading" produce identical evidence; running the outward read is itself an absorb move. The pattern is structurally symmetric with the failure it names. The self-read is the firewall: any other-directed read is derivative and must be gated behind it, or the pattern is unfalsifiable.
2. **Compliance is not comprehension.** "Did they update" measures compliance. A person who fully holds the correction and rejects it on principle leaves the exact trace of an absorber. The pattern functions only where understanding entails updating — fact-shaped content. In value-shaped disputes it becomes a machine for converting opposition into diagnosis; candidate discriminator: can they restate the correction in their own grammar before declining it.
3. **Composting typed as absorption.** Frames update on geological time — correction as seed, restructuring months later. A snapshot diagnostic with no latency window types slow metabolism as frame-intactness; its false positive is precisely the metabolizer.
4. **The untransmissible content.** Some load-bearing content is position-dependent, reachable only from inside a lived situation. "Your model can't reach it from where you're standing" can be true and irreparable at once; the correct downstream move is composting the transmission attempt, not escalating the correction.

STRUCTURAL TRUTH. The pattern governs failed transmissions of crossable content, and it resolves inward. What the instrument actually delivers is a private recalibration whose completion requires no receiver — refusing to let an external frame become internal ground.

## Edges

The expectation window remains unbuilt: no clock, no attempt count, no defined "update" — until those exist, any outward probe is a described instrument, not a runnable one.

The probe is coupled to the hysteresis threshold: it fires only at maximum charge. A deliberate probe — run on your schedule under clean conditions, or an explicit correction factor for reads taken at the threshold — is named as necessary but not designed.

The restate-in-their-own-grammar discriminator for separating principled rejection from absorption is proposed, untested.

The boundary with untransmissible content is unresolved from the inside: content that cannot cross and content that was refused look identical from the corrector's chair, and no instrument here tells them apart.

Deepest open question: whether the other-directed read can ever be run safely behind the self-read gate, or whether the pattern should shed the outward circuit entirely — as promoted, it stands as what it verifiably is: a correction that completes at zero receivers.

## Falsifier

Find one case where the inward recalibration completes — the corrector's model updated to accuracy, verified from their own chair — and the dwelling cost continues unchanged: rent still paid to the other's wrong model despite the closed inward loop. If residency in someone else's model persists after genuine self-recalibration, the boundary-act claim fails and the pattern is consolation, not closure. Conversely, relief produced by vindication (their model moved) rather than accuracy (yours did) is not this pattern operating — it is the outward circuit wearing the inward one's clothes.
