# The Holding Mode Ladder

*slug: `holding-mode-ladder`*
*Source: attractor_currency_2026-06-25.md — branched 2026-06-25. Promoted to canon 2026-06-28.*

---

## Transferable Form

A held image climbs a ladder from hypothesis to commitment to identity without the person noticing the rungs. Each rung raises the update cost. At identity, the image can no longer be updated — only survived. The ladder determines whether the attractor currency check can run. It is the upstream condition on every direction instrument.

---

## Development

An image of desired future begins as a hypothesis: this is where I think I'm going. The holding is provisional. Updates feel like learning rather than loss. The pull is real; the image is porous.

The ladder climbs with organization. Every decision made in service of the image, every person told about the direction, every resource committed toward the destination — each is a quiet rung-step. The person doesn't announce the climb. They wake up at commitment and don't remember passing through it.

**The three rungs:**

*Hypothesis* — the image generates pull but the person maintains implicit awareness it might update. Updates feel like learning. The currency check runs cleanly: friction is received as information, the seam stays open long enough to read.

*Commitment* — the image has been organized around. Updating it means acknowledging the organization was premature: communicating a change of direction to others, repurposing infrastructure, absorbing the social cost of having said one thing and doing another. The update cost is now real. The seam still opens but re-confirmation pulls more strongly. The currency check runs with noise.

*Identity* — the image is no longer about a desired future state. It is about who the person is going. Updating the image is no longer changing direction; it is self-revision. The somatic stake in the image's currency is now indistinguishable from the somatic stake in self-continuity. When the currency check is run at identity, friction is processed as threat rather than information. The seam opens briefly and closes fast — not through re-confirmation but through self-protection. The check appears to run. It returns defended output.

**What moves an image up the ladder:**

- Action organized around it: building toward the image without naming the holding mode as still-hypothesis
- Social commitment: naming the direction to others, which creates an implicit contract around the image
- Survival association: the image becoming associated with how the person made it through a hard period — the direction and the survival fused
- Time: long-held images accumulate confirmation, and accumulated confirmation looks like evidence of identity

No single event moves an image to identity. The climb is gradual and usually invisible. The person experiences each rung as "this is just how committed I am" rather than as "my update cost just rose again."

**What moves an image back down:**

From identity to commitment: a rupture in which the image fails visibly and the person survives. The survival felt as separate from the image is the mechanism — "I tried to go there and didn't, and I'm still here" begins to externalize the image from the self. The failure, paradoxically, is the descent mechanism. Without failure, identity-held images have no natural descent path.

From commitment to hypothesis: dissolving the organization. Lower-stakes than identity descent but still costly. The infrastructure built toward the image must be acknowledged as premature or explicitly repurposed. The social commitments must be renegotiated. This can be done deliberately; it just carries the friction of the cost already invested.

The key asymmetry: the ladder climbs unconsciously and descends only through deliberate action or rupture. This means the natural drift of any long-held image is upward. Without designed porosity — explicit holding-mode naming, built-in revision conditions — all images are slowly climbing.

**The intervention at formation:**

At image formation, name the holding mode explicitly: "I'm holding this as a hypothesis. I'll move to commitment when Y happens. I'll update the image if Z changes."

This does three things. First: it names the current rung, which prevents unconscious climbing — the image can't quietly become a commitment because the person knows they're in hypothesis. Second: it installs a commitment threshold — a named condition that must be met before the holding mode advances. Third: it installs a revision condition — a named trigger that would update the image rather than committing more deeply to it.

An image formed without explicit holding-mode naming defaults to climbing with every decision made in its service. The unnamed image climbs by accumulation.

**The ladder and the instruments:**

The holding-mode ladder determines the effective operating range of [[attractor_currency]] and the direction-cost panel.

At hypothesis: instruments run cleanly. Update is possible. The seam stays open.
At early commitment: instruments run with noise. Re-confirmation pulls harder. The seam closes faster.
At late commitment: the currency check may return genuine signal or re-confirmation depending on the strength of the commitment. Hard to distinguish from inside.
At identity: the currency check backfires. The panel appears to function but one instrument is defended. The direction-cost panel's compound firing protocol routes to an independent witness — but the need for an independent witness is already signaled by the identity rung, before compound firing occurs.

Reading the holding mode before running the currency check is therefore not optional — it is the precondition for knowing whether the check is valid.

**The design move:**

The ladder is not the problem. Commitment and even identity-level holding can be appropriate — some things are worth organizing around fully, and some images are genuinely structural to self. The problem is unconscious climbing: arriving at identity without knowing you climbed, without having chosen it.

Designed porosity — explicit holding mode + named revision conditions at formation — doesn't prevent commitment or identity. It makes the rung visible when you step on it. That visibility is what the ladder currently lacks.

## The Move

At image formation: name the rung, name the condition for advancing, name the condition for updating. "I'm at hypothesis. I'll move to commitment when X. I'll update the image if Y changes." Three sentences at formation prevent unconscious climbing across the entire life of the image.

Before running the attractor currency check: read the current rung. Hypothesis → run the check normally. Commitment → run the check, hold the seam longer against re-confirmation pull. Identity → seek an independent witness before running the check alone, or recognize that the check result may be defended.

---

## Edges

The identity descent problem: the only natural mechanism for descending from identity is rupture. This means that deliberately-designed images which have climbed to identity have no designed descent path. The ladder has an asymmetry: managed ascent (via named thresholds) but only rupture-descent or deliberate dissolution from commitment. Designing a non-rupture descent mechanism for identity-held images is the open edge.

The survival-fusion case: when the image is associated with survival ("this direction is how I made it through"), updating it carries a different weight than normal identity descent. The update isn't just self-revision — it's revising the story of how the person survived. This is the hardest holding-mode case and may require a different approach entirely.

The observer problem: the person cannot read their own rung accurately from inside a strongly held image. The identity rung, in particular, cannot be self-diagnosed because the diagnostic instrument (the felt sense) is the thing that's been fused with the image. Reading the rung accurately may require the independent witness for the same reason the currency check requires one at identity.

---

## Falsifier

Find an image that climbed to identity without any organization, social commitment, or survival association — where the identity fusion arose without the accumulation mechanisms the pattern names. If found, the ladder's causal account is incomplete: there is a rung-climbing mechanism not yet named. More useful: find an identity-held image that remained genuinely updateable without rupture — where the currency check continued to return authentic signal. Identify what structural condition preserved the porosity despite identity-level holding. That condition is the real pattern.
