## Transferable Form

The act of bringing something into focus from a position is the same act that distorts it for anyone in another position. Distortion is not error, inattention, or bad faith to be corrected — it is what occupying a position consists of. Two parties examining the same object each make it harder for the other to read, and increasing either party's clarity increases the other's distortion. There is no configuration in which both look and neither casts.

---

## Development

**Constitutive, not incidental.** Most treatments of perspective difference frame the distortion as a defect with a remedy: look harder, compare notes, get a better vantage. This pattern denies the remedy exists in that direction. The cone that illuminates *is* the cone that shadows; they are one operation seen from two positions. A party could only stop distorting the other's view by stopping looking.

**The two readings can both be complete.** The strong form is not "each sees part of the truth" — that is the partial-data case. It is that a fully attentive, fully honest, fully competent read from one position and an equally fully attentive read from another can return **contradictory and both-correct** results about the same object. The object genuinely has the property from one position and genuinely lacks it from the other.

**Three consequences follow directly:**

1. **Convergence is not available by effort.** Effort applied by either party increases the total distortion in the field. Anyone measuring progress by how close the readings are getting will read good-faith effort as failure.
2. **Neither reading can be corrected by the other.** Correction presumes a position from which one is more right. No such position exists inside the pair, and a third party arrives with its own cone.
3. **This is a precondition, not a duplicate, of `merge_requirement_makes_worth_negotiable`.** This pattern says the readings cannot merge; that one says requiring them to merge converts the difference into a contest over standing. They can be adopted separately — but this pattern with a merge requirement still in place produces a permanent unwinnable contest, which is the worst configuration of the two.

**Diagnostic.** Applies when: both parties can describe the object in detail; the descriptions conflict on a checkable feature; neither is withholding; and handing each the other's description produces recognition of a different view rather than an update. **If handing over the other's data produces a good-faith update, the situation is partial data and `symmetrical_blindness_from_asymmetrical_visibility` governs instead.**

## Why this is in the library: it stops a live misapplication

`symmetrical_blindness_from_asymmetrical_visibility` is the nearest neighbour and is **structurally opposed**, not overlapping:

| | symmetrical blindness | this pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Data each holds | honest but **partial** | honest and **complete from that position** |
| The conclusions | mirror-image, both **wrong** about the ledger | different, both **correct** about the object |
| Cause | a visibility regime suppressing information | the geometry of having a position at all |
| Remedy | change what the norm makes visible | none available — more light adds shadow |

That last row is the load-bearing one. The neighbouring pattern's fix is to raise visibility. Here, a party raising their own illumination *increases* the distortion in the other's view. **Adding light is not a convergence operation.** Any remedy imported from the neighbouring pattern makes this situation worse.

Checked and distinct from `shadow_in_witnessing` (the witness carries the pattern they name — a claim about one's own position, not a two-position geometry) and `three-states-one-fog` (shares only the metaphor).

## Edges and open questions

- **The commensurability gap is inherited and unclosed.** As with its neighbour, "both readings are legitimate" can be invoked to flatten a real asymmetry — where one party's position is structurally privileged, or where one is simply wrong about a fact the object determinately has. The pattern has no built-in check separating a genuine two-position geometry from a mistake wearing its clothes.
- **Where the object really is determinate.** Some objects have position-independent properties. The pattern gives no criterion for which properties are position-dependent, and applying it to a determinate property licenses a false symmetry.

---

## Falsifier

If a case is found where both parties' readings are complete and honest and additional illumination from either side **converges** them, the constitutive claim fails and this reduces to `symmetrical_blindness_from_asymmetrical_visibility`.
