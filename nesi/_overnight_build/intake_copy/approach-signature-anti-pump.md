# Track the Approach-Shape, Not the Settling

*slug: `approach-signature-anti-pump`*
*Promoted: 2026-07-21 · Source: The Membrane — human-gate pattern family, repair pass same day*
*EXTENDS `dread_of_sufficiency.md`*

---

## Transferable Form

When a held decision cannot be examined directly because the reason for holding lives below language, audit the shape of its returns instead: every approach-then-pullback, did it name a specific missing element or not? A hold is legitimate exactly as long as it can name its own gap; when it can't, it's a pump, and the object earns the right to demand disposition.

---

## Development

The gate judges the residue at pullback, not the felt state. Legitimacy is coextensive not with nameability-as-assessment but with reproducibility: a gap counts as named iff it binds to a concrete missing element that can be re-run as a check at the next approach — the identity key, borrowed from the sibling pattern `gap-persistence-not-presence.md`. This dissolves the judge problem: no one assesses whether a naming is "specific enough." The object's own prior binding is the standard, and the test is mechanical re-run, not subjective judgment. The renderer re-runs the bound check and shows the raw result; it verifies nothing.

Gap-lineage check (displacement mechanism): each named gap is keyed to its bound failure at naming time. At the next pullback, either the same bound element still reproduces un-closed (legitimate hold), or a fresh element is minted while the prior bound gap was never re-run or closed (displacement — gap-churning, counted as un-named).

Trip count: three — inherited directly from `dread_of_sufficiency.md`'s own calibrated threshold ("three or more approach-followed-by-modification without passage"), not invented fresh.

Reconciliation with `feedback_body_read_is_his`: the valve has exactly one power and two hard prohibitions. It may surface the approach-record — silently, as a work-object. It may never execute a disposition (renderer, not verifier), and it may never prompt the holder to articulate the gap or the body-read. Pre-verbal ripening is protected: a legitimately ripening hold that cannot yet name its gap is neither force-released nor force-named — the holder sees the record and holds if the body says hold. The named-forever gap resolves too: a gap bound to a real but not-yet-closable failure (dependency on an outside event) is nameable and reproducible, a legitimately blocked hold, and the valve correctly does not fire.

---

## Edges

The re-run must present the raw reproduction result, not a conclusion drawn from it — a rendering-discipline constraint, not an unresolved fault; drifting into conclusion re-opens the evidence-arrangement leak named in the sibling pattern `externalize-container-not-verdict.md`. The gap-lineage "structurally isomorphic" boundary remains orderable-not-formalized, inherited honestly from `gap-persistence-not-presence.md`, but does not gate this valve, which keys on a same-vs-different-bound-element binary.
