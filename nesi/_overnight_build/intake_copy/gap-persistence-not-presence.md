# Track Gap Persistence, Not Gap Presence

*slug: `gap-persistence-not-presence`*
*Promoted: 2026-07-21 · Source: The Membrane — human-gate pattern family, repair pass same day*
*EXTENDS `veto_pattern_drift_detection.md`*

---

## Transferable Form

In any culture where naming an open gap earns credit at the moment of naming, the naming can quietly substitute for the closing: each pass re-names its deficiencies in fresh wording while the operative hole never moves. The remedy is longitudinal, not attentional — key each gap to the concrete failure it would produce, and at the next pass re-run that failure rather than re-read the sentence. A gap's obligation discharges only when the failure stops reproducing and no structurally isomorphic gap opens elsewhere.

---

## Development

**Gap identity via bound concrete failure.** At naming time, bind the gap to its load-path address — the concrete claim, remedy, or carving that breaks without it. At the next pass, re-run that failure, not the sentence. Worked example, using this pattern's own sibling `cold-start-needs-outside-zero.md` as the test case: its edges "per-type calibration model unevidenced" and "type-taxonomy regress untouched" read as two gaps in fresh wording, but the identity-key resolves both to one load-path address — the type-carving is itself uncalibrated — with one reproduction test. They are one gap, not two.

**Displacement, not closure.** Three bands, keyed to address/symptom: unmoved (same load-path address, failure still reproduces — naming substituted for closing); displaced (original address's failure gone, but the same failure-symptom now reproduces at a different address — motion, not discharge); closed (failure gone at its address and no isomorphic symptom anywhere else). "Structurally isomorphic" = same failure-symptom, different address.

**Discharge condition.** A gap's tracking loop terminates on two consecutive passes showing closure with zero displacement. Without this exit, the ledger itself becomes a cost pump.

**Organ site, decided.** The second-party check is hosted at the Converger, not a same-culture second reader — a same-culture reader is itself a translated zero, confirming closure with the same confident indistinguishability the pattern exists to break. The Converger is the one organ structurally motivated toward subtraction; that bias is the structural motivation the pattern requires. Any HOLD→PROMOTE re-screen at the Converger must re-run the prior pass's bound failures and route to COMPOST/HOLD if any still reproduces at any address. The Dispatcher's ghost-gate reconciliation supplies the cross-pass seam; the Converger supplies the adversarial motive.

Habitat. Gap-churn is invisible in any single pass; it exists only in the diff between the gap-list at pass N and pass N+1 — the between-passes seam. This demands a longitudinal instrument, not better per-pass discipline. It bites hardest at handoff: agent A names a crisp gap, agent B "addresses" it, no one owns the check that the operative gap was the one addressed.

---

## Edges

Whether two gaps share a load-path address is itself a carving that can drift — the same regress this pattern names, one level up. The failure-reproduction key can't be written for gaps that are genuinely pre-articulate (adjacent to `approach-signature-anti-pump.md`'s pre-verbal-ripening edge). Displacement bands are ordinal, not metric — no formalized "how far did it move."
