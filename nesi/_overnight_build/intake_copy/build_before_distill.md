## Transferable Form

Building a reception medium is an epistemic act, not a display act. The act of rendering source material into form creates structural clarity that is unavailable from the unrendered source. Distillation of that structure requires the build to precede it — not as a prerequisite for transmission but as the event that makes distillation possible.

---

## Development

The pattern rests on a distinction between potential structure and legible structure. Raw source — a note, a sketch, an idea, an observation — contains potential structure. It is not structureless. But potential structure cannot be accurately distilled because it has not yet been forced into a form that externalizes and stabilizes it. The act of building into form does three things simultaneously:

1. **Forces resolution of ambiguity.** You cannot render what is unresolved. The act of building requires decisions at every point — which element goes where, what relationship holds, how the constraint manifests — and each decision resolves structure that was latent in the source.

2. **Externalizes partially-formed structure.** The unbuilt source lives inside the maker's processing. Building it into form creates an external object the maker can now observe from outside the process of making it. This shift from inside-the-making to outside-the-made-thing is the epistemic event.

3. **Creates the object distillation will describe.** Distillation is not a summary of the raw source. It is a description of the built form — which now exists as a stable, perceivable, manipulable object. Distillation without a built form to describe produces a summary of the pre-build state, which may not correspond to the post-build structure.

The sequence constraint is strict in one direction: build can precede or follow distillation chronologically (you can build and never distill), but distillation that precedes building produces a description of something that does not yet exist in legible form. That description is not wrong — it is premature.

---

## Across Domains

**Architecture:** An architect cannot write an accurate structural brief from a sketch. The drawing set must be built first — floor plans, sections, elevations, details. The drawing IS the cognitive event that reveals load paths, spatial conflicts, proportion relationships, material logic.

**Music composition:** A composer cannot accurately describe the harmonic structure of a piece from an initial melodic seed. The piece must be worked into form. Theoretical distillation requires the passage and section to exist as audible, scored objects first.

**Prototype before product requirements:** A PRD written before prototyping describes what the team imagines. A PRD written after a working prototype describes what the product actually is. Building into form (the prototype) created structural clarity about what the requirements actually are.

**Writing as thinking:** The insight does not precede the writing. The act of writing into form creates the thought. Summarizing before writing produces a summary of what was imagined; summarizing after produces a summary of what was found.

---

## Edges

**The premature distillation trap.** Distilling before building produces a distillation that feels complete but captures the pre-build state. The failure is invisible until the post-build structure diverges from the pre-build distillation, which it frequently does.

**The build-and-never-distill trap.** Building without subsequently distilling produces reception-medium richness with no transmissible spine. Related to `sensed_but_unshown`.

**The wrong-form trap.** Not all forms make all structures legible. Building into a mismatched form produces resolution of the wrong ambiguities.

**The form-is-enough trap.** The built form becomes the final artifact rather than the substrate. Distillation is skipped. Widget as deliverable rather than substrate.

## Relation to configure_before_demand

Both patterns are about sequencing — what must precede what. They operate on different axes:

- **configure_before_demand**: the *system's* reception capacity must be in place before demand arrives. The system's state determines routing quality. Configuration is about the receiving end.
- **build_before_distill**: the *builder's* epistemic state changes through the act of construction. The build creates clarity the builder did not have before building. Construction is about the producing end.

They can co-occur: a cascade system requires both configure_before_demand (the receiving models must be in a state to receive structured input) and build_before_distill (the spine passed to the cascade must be distilled from a built form, not from the raw source). The failure modes are distinct: configure failure produces routing failure; build-before-distill failure produces distillation of the wrong thing.

---

## Falsifier

If a builder can produce a structurally equivalent distillation from an unbuilt source as from a built form — same structure captured, same accuracy, same completeness — then the build added no epistemic value. The pattern fails.

The pattern further implies a categorical difference between pre-build and post-build distillation, not merely a quantitative one. If the built form only adds detail (more of the same structure, not different structure), the pattern is weakened.
