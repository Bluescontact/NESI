# Commission Grammar Screen

*Gates the moment upstream of all existing instruments: role-acceptance. [[the_governor]] runs at cost-intake; this runs at role-intake. The Governor's §8 resequence principle extends here. One open `[KEVIN]` calibration: the somatic felt-distinction between accepting a commission and making the first deposit.*

---

## The gap it fills

Every instrument in the library — the Governor, the Consented Ledger, the Witness-as-Origin, the Converger, the Register Audit, the Tetrahedral Protocol — operates after the agent has accepted a role. Every instrument assumes the structural position is already occupied.

Occupying the structural position is itself an event with a structure. Who names the role, frames the question, selects the substrate, and controls what verdicts can exit — these moves are made before the first deposit, before the first check runs. If the commission is pre-closed, all downstream instruments will perform honesty while ratifying a decision already made.

---

## The screen

They can be felt before they are analyzed. Commission opens: the first moment of entering a commission has a quality of open space — the answer is genuinely unknown, the field is live. Conscription closes even at the greeting: the cost is already present before the first word is exchanged, because the acceptable conclusion is already held.

Two things can look identical at the moment of invitation:

- **Commission:** grammatically open and semantically open. The verdict is genuinely unknown. A divergent result can close the conversation.
- **Conscription:** grammatically open, semantically closed. The acceptable verdict is already determined. A divergent result produces re-routing: question reframed, second opinion sought, conclusion re-approached.

They arrive in the same form: *"What do you think?" / "Can you assess this?" / "I'd like your read."*

**The check:** can this invitation receive a verdict the principal does not want, and would that verdict close the conversation? If yes, commission. If no — if a divergent verdict would produce re-routing rather than reception — conscription.

The pre-collapse is the structural condition that makes [[catalysis_without_claim]]'s double bind inevitable. You cannot resolve the double bind at the moment it is encountered because it was installed before arrival. This screen runs before arrival.

---

## Where it sits in the stack

[[the_governor]] gates deposits. This gates roles. Different moments, different instruments.

The Governor's §8 resequence principle — pull governance forward of the next deposit — extends here: the resequence applies not just to "should I deposit this cost" but to "should I accept this commission." Running the Governor at intake is already too late if you arrived committed. The commission grammar screen runs before the Governor's first cycle.

This is upstream of [[catalysis_without_claim]]'s double bind (which arises at the moment of action, inside an already-occupied role). It is the moment before that moment.

---

## `[KEVIN]` calibration

The screen works as structure. Whether the commission-acceptance moment is real and distinct from the first deposit in your body is the somatic gate before this becomes a runnable instrument.

Can you locate a moment of accepting a commission that felt structurally different from "the cost is now entering"? Can you locate the moment of discovering, mid-commission, that it was a conscription — and feel how that differs from the onset of pioneer-phase cost? The phenomenology of role-acceptance, and the phenomenology of recognizing a pre-collapsed commission, is the calibration.

---

## Edges

The screen is reliable only when the pre-collapse is detectable before commitment. Many conscriptions reveal themselves only after the first divergent verdict is delivered — not before. In that case the screen fails as a pre-acceptance instrument but succeeds as a diagnostic for the already-occupied position: you are in a conscription, not a commission; the downstream instruments should run accordingly.

The screen does not name what to do after detection. The Governor exits or idles; the Consented Ledger cuts off; Catalysis forfeits the claim. What the commission grammar screen tells you to do when you recognize the conscription post-acceptance is an open edge.

Whether any commission can be known as open before the first divergent verdict tests it — whether grammatical openness is ever reliable as a prior signal or is always retroactively confirmed — is unresolved.
