# Transition After Successful Departure

---

## The claim

Transition after genuine completion is the correct metabolic response. The ground is internal. The next container does not have to be named immediately. Silence after successful departure is the system working correctly.

---

## Mechanism

Completion produces transition — not as aftermath but as the correct next state. The arc closed; therefore there is silence. The silence is not emptiness that requires filling. It is the appropriate interval between one completed structure and the next one beginning to form.

---

## Differentiation

**From failure:** failure produces disorientation. Transition after completion produces orientation without a named destination.

**From grief:** grief signals that something ended before it was complete. Transition signals that something ended because it was complete. Different metabolic signature.

**From urgency:** the pressure to name the next thing immediately is a performance reflex — the system should appear visibly continuous. Transition permits discontinuity. The discontinuity is not a gap; it is the correct interval.

---

## Falsifier

The person in transition names the next container immediately and confidently. If the next thing is fully formed before the previous one is fully metabolized, the completion was probably incomplete.

---

## Prescriptions

1. After a genuine departure, do not rush to name what comes next. The interval is structural, not a gap.
2. Distinguish transition from emptiness by the felt signature: transition has orientation without destination; emptiness has neither.
3. When someone frames silence after completion as failure, name the metabolic state: transition is the system working correctly.
