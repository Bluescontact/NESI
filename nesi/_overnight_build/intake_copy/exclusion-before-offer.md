## Statement

An offer arrives condition-free at the moment of encounter because the giver's refusals were already discharged at loading time. The excluded things never arrived, so there is nothing present to negotiate over. Unconditionality is not a stance held live — it is a temporal product: the conditioning ran at T-minus, and what the receiver meets is only the survivors of a no already spent.

## Mechanism (as load_to_form's extension)

load_to_form owns the enforcement machinery: a rule paid for at authoring time so no one holds it at execution time. What this pattern adds is the machinery's downstream artifact and its habitat — the interpersonal handoff, two bodies at an exchange, a register the infrastructure patterns (load_to_form, structural_incapacity, pre_clearance_class) never enter.

The structure does not enforce the boundary — it remembers it. The giver could in principle reopen the no, but the negotiation is empty because the complement set is elsewhere. This separates the pattern from the structural-incapacity family: those make the conditional act impossible by construction; this makes it objectless by subtraction.

**Receiver side.** The offer-set is the receiver's whole visible world; the no lives entirely in the invisible complement set. The receiver never faces a decision point where a boundary would activate. This inverts the moral register of gift language: unconditional is not a virtue — it is a manufactured artifact, designable by splitting the timeline, and it releases the giver from the impossible demand of being unconditional live.

**Contrast signature.** Where the no does not precede, conditionality surfaces at the encounter as hesitation, terms, and monitoring — the boundary-holding cost lands in a nervous system instead of a structure.

## Live instance

The gift pile, Mendocino, 2026-07-04: tarp + sign, "Free Gifts. Take what you need, leave what you don't." Load-time exclusion (what is not on the tarp was never offered) makes the encounter unconditional; nobody holds a boundary at the tailgate because the structure already holds it.

## Boundaries and failure modes

1. **Stale no, novel encounter.** A load-time no excludes only what the giver could imagine when loading. The pattern works only where the encounter space is roughly enumerable in advance; retained veto and spent veto are responses to different novelty rates, not rival doctrines.
2. **Avoidance impersonating exclusion.** A giver who never owned any no, but avoids situations where conditions would surface, presents identically. Discriminator: can the giver name what was excluded and when? Without this test the pattern is unfalsifiable.
3. **Unilateral deployment.** Requiring the other party's boundary before trusting their yes reinstates conditionality at the doorway. That move is not this pattern — it is its receiver-side limit, developed separately as **the boundary-ask** (`2026-07-04-the-boundary-ask.md`): you cannot pre-spend a no that is not yours to spend.

## Falsifier

A verified instance where the no failed to precede and the conditionality-at-encounter signature did **not** appear — or a load-time-excluded offer that still generates live negotiation — weakens the pattern. The contrast case is currently predicted, not observed; that is carried face-up.

## Open

Whether "nobody has to hold it" founds a subtraction family (objectless) distinct from the structural-incapacity family (impossible) is an open taxonomic axis. Whether the pattern requires receiver-visibility of the spent no is undecided by the material.
