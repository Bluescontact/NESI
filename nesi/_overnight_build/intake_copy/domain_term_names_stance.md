# domain_term_names_stance

**The pattern in one line.** A literal term of art from your own trade turns out to be the exact structural name of how you stand in the world — not by metaphor, by identity — and the recognition lands as a felt shock because the name was load-bearing in your hands years before it was understood in your life.

*Extends [[body-as-zero]] on the naming axis. body-as-zero names the felt read as the origin of all checking — "the body is already sorting before the mind has words." This pattern adds the two things that file does not reach: (1) a naming-**event** mechanism — a specific recognition where a literal, operational craft term is discovered as the precise structural name of the self, which body-as-zero never addresses (it holds where checks are measured from, not where a name comes from or the shock of one landing); (2) the **zero-translation-distance** claim — the trade term is not a metaphor for the stance, it **is** the stance's definition, the same words specifying the craft act and the self with nothing in between. Clean distinctions: this is the inverse of [[composting_a_situation_into_the_commons]] (which abstracts the instance **away** until only the shape remains; here the literal instance-term **is** the shape, un-abstracted) and orthogonal to [[witness_as_origin]] (the geometry of why an origin has no witness; this is the epistemics of a craft-name becoming a self-name).*

## The mechanism

A term of art is a word a trade has machined down to one unambiguous referent. "Setting the floor," in the elevator trade, names a physical calibration: establish and mark the landing height so the car arrives perfectly level with where people step off. It is a spec, not decoration — it admits no slack, and everyone who uses it points at the same operation.

The pattern fires when the practitioner notices this maximally-literal term is also the precise name for their whole way of standing — and the technical definition transfers intact. Not analogy: identity of structure. A term of art is already an abstraction the trade performed under pressure; it survived because it isolated a structural invariant (establish the reference plane; make the arriving thing level with where people step off; mark it so it holds). That invariant is substrate-independent the moment it is named with that much precision. The practitioner doesn't stretch the word to fit the life — the word was always describing a structure the life also instances.

This is why recognition lands in the body, not the analysis. The ordinary path is concept-first: form a stance, then hunt approximate language for it, the language always a translation with loss. Here it runs backward — the hands knew the operation cold, pre-verbal, for thousands of repetitions, for years before the life was understood as having a stance at all. So when the term snaps onto the life it does not feel like finding a good metaphor; it feels like recognizing something already true and already mastered. The felt-shock ("I felt that") is the signature: the somatic system confirms a match the discursive system is only catching up to. The name is regathered, not constructed.

## What the collapse makes available

1. **Inherited rigor.** The term's operational constraints transfer free, already disciplined by the trade's failure history: there is one correct height; level is checkable, not asserted; the mark must hold under load and after you leave; a floor set wrong is not opinion but a hazard the next person falls through. A self-authored stance-word would have to earn that rigor; a term of art is born with it.
2. **A demonstrable, not declarative, claim.** The stance becomes provable the way the trade move is — by checking level, not by being believed. "I am principled" (contestable, soft) becomes "the floor is set at this height; verify it" (checkable, hard). The claim exits self-description and enters measurable fact.
3. **A gift in the form of disclosure.** The terminal move is publishing the coordinate: announce exactly where the floor is set, independent of whether any external authority sets, lowers, or removes its own. The technical act (mark the height so the next car lands level) becomes an ethical one (mark where I stand so the next person doesn't fall through the hole). The gift is not the floor; it is the public marking of its height, which others can now navigate by.

## How you'd know it's operating

- The recognition arrives as a somatic snap ("I felt that"), needing no argument — a real instance needs no paragraph defending why the metaphor works.
- The claim it produces is checkable against an external landing, not held as identity.
- The term carries its trade constraints intact, not just its poetry.

## Failure modes

- **Forced fit / costume.** Draping a trade word over a stance it doesn't structurally instance. The tell is the absence of the somatic snap and the need to argue the connection. Real instances arrive as recognition and need no defense; costumes need a paragraph. If you have to sell the fit, the structure isn't there.
- **Aestheticizing the term, dropping the constraints.** Keeping the term's poetry while discarding its rigor — "setting the floor" used as a vibe rather than a spec. This forfeits the whole value of the move and is worse than a plain phrase, because it borrows authority it no longer carries.
- **Declaring instead of demonstrating.** Naming the stance with the term and then defending it as identity rather than checking it as level. This throws away the term's gift — checkability — and reintroduces the contestability the structure was about to dissolve.
- **Floor set wrong, held with conviction.** The shock of recognition can authorize a miscalibration. A floor confidently marked at the wrong height is more dangerous than no mark, because others measure from it and step off into the gap. The term's own discipline is the corrective: level is checkable against the actual landing, not against how certain you feel. Recognition validates that the name fits; it does not validate that the height is right.

## The falsifier / open edges

- **Somatic gate — resolved 2026-06-30:** The naming-event snap is structurally distinct in the body from the ordinary body-first read. Kevin's felt-read at canon gate confirmed: a different event, not the ordinary check noticing a word land. Genus status stands.
- **Right fit, wrong height:** the snap confirms the name *fits*; it says nothing about whether the floor is set at the *right* height. A wrongly-calibrated stance produces an equally strong felt-recognition. The form of checkability transfers; the referent to check against — other people's actual ground — may go missing exactly when conviction is highest.
- **The private mark when the public floor is removed — resolved by authority-altitude:** publishing the coordinate "regardless of regulatory intervention" is also a claim on a shared reference plane. The legitimacy condition ported from [[extend_dont_invent_name_the_wall]]: the private floor is legitimate **exactly when apparent height = true height** — you mark at the altitude your authority actually reaches, not above it. Marking where you stand, at the height your authority actually reaches, is legitimate whether or not the public datum is set, lowered, or removed; the public floor's absence does not raise or lower yours. The illegitimate move is the false-height mark — claiming a floor at an apparent height the actual authority does not reach, so the next party steps onto it expecting solid floor and falls through. Two honest floors at their true heights do not collide, because each is marked at the altitude its authority actually reaches.
- **Body-to-mind only:** available to recognize a stance already lived through the hands, never to acquire one. A mind-first stance reaching for a trade word will always be in costume. The pattern cannot be used to get a stance, only to recognize one already running.
