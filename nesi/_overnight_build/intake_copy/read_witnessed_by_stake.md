## Statement

A felt-read cannot be validated by another read. Every second read available from inside the same frame — a peer who shares your history, the same body at the same moment, an AI compressing you back to yourself — is a second instance of the same translation, not a second origin. The only thing that validates a read is a **stake**: the read put into the world in a form that will cost you if it was wrong. An unstaked read is unfalsifiable, and unfalsifiable is the same as unwitnessable. Validation does not come from a better witness; it comes from a consequence you did not author and cannot revise.

## Mechanism

The witnessing coordinate system fails closed on a somatic zero ([[witness_as_origin]]): the body is trusted-or-fail-closed, never checked from outside. [[consented_ledger]] demands "a second sensor that does not share the first sensor's failure mode" — and [[translated_origin_failure_mode]] proves you cannot recruit one from inside the frame (a shifted coordinate system cannot be calibrated from within itself). So the second sensor cannot be another *reader*. It has to be something that responds to the same input **without running your translation.**

Four things do, and none of them is a mind:

1. **Material consequence.** The world does not share your coordinate system. Load the beam — it holds or it doesn't. File the record — the chargeback processes or it doesn't. A consequence you did not author and cannot revise is an origin outside every frame. (Kin: [[the_daylight_test]] — a found failure is a successful measurement, read after the stake, not before.)
2. **Time / drift.** Your own body at a later state is *partly* outside your present frame; the translation has moved. A read sealed now and re-tested cold recruits a near-second-origin — divergence between the two reveals the frame. ([[proximity_instrument_horizon]], [[live_hands_ratification]]'s recorded instance.)
3. **Structure (needs less witnessing).** Does not close the edge — shrinks its surface. Move load-bearing checks off the felt-read and into form that fails closed by construction, so fewer things terminate on the unwitnessable zero. ([[structural_incapacity_over_prohibition]], [[load_to_form]].)
4. **Frame-independent divergence.** A second origin defined by *divergence, not agreement.* Agreement can be shared translation (the echo chamber's false confidence); a reader with a different history whose read **surprises** you is outside your frame — the surprise is the signature, not the concurrence.

Carrier for 2 and 4: the durable, uneditable record neither party controls — the drifted future self cannot reconcile it, an outsider can diverge against it ([[the_open_ledger]] Topology A; "history, not instance, carries the signal," [[source_check_before_giving]]).

The load-bearing inversion: **validation is not a reading operation, it is a betting operation.** The felt-read is complete on the giving side, but its *correspondence to the world* is knowable only by staking it and reading what comes back. This is why confidence-without-stake is structurally indistinguishable from a well-translated error — and why fluency ([[pressure_masquerade_detection]]) can counterfeit any unstaked read.

## What use cannot ratify — the boundary set

1. **Stake without monitoring.** The stake recruits the witness; it does not force you to read its testimony. You can stake a read, pay when it is wrong, and refuse to look — the disconfirmation channel goes unmonitored ([[instrument_as_self_portrait]]: "an extinction event could already have occurred unseen"). Staking is **necessary, not sufficient.** The real instrument is two-part: stake it, then monitor the consequence you would rather not see.
2. **The illegible stake.** Some stakes return no legible signal — a relational gift given may cost you invisibly, off any channel you can read ([[sensed_but_unshown]]). Where the consequence is real but unrepresentable, the stake does not witness; it only spends.
3. **Catastrophic stakes.** "Cost you if wrong" fails as a validator when the cost of being wrong is unrecoverable. A read you can only stake once, fatally, is not made witnessable by staking — it is made terminal. The pattern holds only where the stake is survivable and the loop can run more than once.
4. **The rigged consequence.** If you control the surface the stake lands on, the consequence is inside your frame after all — you authored it. A stake witnesses only to the degree its return is *not yours to revise.* Grading your own homework is an unstaked read wearing a result.

## Falsifiers (attached)

- **Core falsifier:** a read validated *durably and correctly* with no stake — no cost-on-wrong, no exposure to a consequence outside the frame — weakens the pattern. (Prediction: what looks like such a case is either a stake unnamed, or an unfalsified read that has not yet met its consequence.)
- **Sufficiency falsifier:** if staking *reliably* corrected reads without a separate monitoring act — i.e. paying the cost forced the correction on its own — then the two-part instrument is wrong and staking alone suffices.
- **Divergence falsifier:** if agreement from a frame-sharing reader corrected a read as often as divergence from a frame-independent one, then "divergence, not agreement" is not the signature and mechanism #4 collapses into ordinary second-opinion.

- **No stake-sizing rule.** How much must a stake cost to witness? Too small and it is consent theater (a stake you can shrug off witnesses nothing); too large and it becomes the catastrophic case. The proportion between the read's claim and the stake's cost is unspecified.
