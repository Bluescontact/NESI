## The action

Take the undefined condition — the success criterion, the discharge condition, the boundary, the gap — and make it resolve into exactly one of two states: written into shared text, or visibly refused. Then bind whatever remains open to the concrete failure it produces, and at the next pass re-run that failure rather than re-reading the sentence.

## What didn't survive

Patience as a stance toward ambiguity. Under compression, no pattern survived that permitted an undefined condition to simply be tolerated. Every one of them converted waiting into either a write or an exposure.

## Falsifier

If forcing a definition reliably produces a written definition and never a visible refusal, the organ is a negotiation technique, not a diagnostic act. If it reliably produces refusal and never a write, it is an exit engine wearing a diagnostic's face. It is one organ only if both branches actually fire in the field.
