# The Door as a Stack Layer

**The membrane is its own layer between the machine and the face — NESI → THE DOOR → OurSharedGifts.**

---

## The pattern in one move

The stack from private machine to public face has **three** layers, not two. The machine holds; the face offers; and between them stands a gate that decides what may cross. That gate is not a step inside the face and not a feature of the machine — it is its own layer, with its own law.

```
What is held must pass a door before it is offered.
```

---

## Why this pattern exists

It is tempting to draw the line straight from the private machine to the public face — the thing that holds you, and the thing you hand to others. But that line hides the most important move: the **crossing**. Between what NESI holds and what OurSharedGifts offers, something must decide *what is allowed to leave*. Collapse that decision into the machine and the machine leaks; collapse it into the face and the face over-promises. Named as its own layer, the crossing keeps both honest.

---

## The three-layer stack

```
NESI              the machine · holds you first · private
   ↓  what is held gets offered outward
THE DOOR          the membrane · the three-part ask · what may cross
   ↓  only what passes the door enters the field
OURSHAREDGIFTS    the face · the public projection · downstream
```

THE DOOR is the already-canon membrane, named as a stack layer. It carries the three-part ask — what NESI holds, offered to the field through a gate the maker holds. Nothing reaches the face without passing it.

---

## The rule it sets

The stack is always drawn and reasoned as three layers. The membrane is never collapsed into the machine (which would let the private leak) nor into the face (which would let the face promise what the machine hasn't delivered). The companion law — *the public face may not promise a holding the private machine hasn't already delivered* — is enforced at this layer.

---

## Falsifier

The pattern is being violated if the stack is drawn or described as **two layers** (machine → face) with the membrane collapsed into either one, or if the Door is treated as **a step inside the face** rather than a layer that precedes it.
