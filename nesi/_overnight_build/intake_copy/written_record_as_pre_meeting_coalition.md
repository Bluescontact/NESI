# Written Record as Pre-Meeting Coalition

---

## The mechanism

Serial isolation is the precondition for the private-resolution move. The informal-pressure actor operates by catching each dissenting party separately — each party believes they are alone, each is addressed privately, each can be absorbed or pressured without the others present. The sequencing is load-bearing: serial isolation requires that no two dissenting positions are simultaneously visible in the same channel.

A position placed in a shared medium simultaneously with another position collapses the sequencing serial isolation requires. Two positions in the same channel before a decision event constitute a coalition of record without coordination. The second documenter did not have to request backing; the first did not have to grant it. The coalition exists because both positions are visible simultaneously in the same medium — not because the parties communicated with each other.

---

## Simultaneity in medium

Visibility is load-bearing. An undocumented position has no structural weight because it can be denied, minimized, or absorbed into the dominant narrative after the fact. A documented position in a shared medium is fixed — it cannot be retrospectively ungiven.

The mechanism requires:
1. A shared medium where both positions are simultaneously legible to all parties
2. Placement before the decision event (before = constitutive; after = reactive)
3. Both positions documented, not merely stated or implied

When these conditions hold, a party who has been documenting the same facts privately acquires structural backing they did not have to request.

---

## The diagnostic

The private-resolution invitation ("let's take this offline," "we can talk about this in person") is the signature of the isolation-restoration move being attempted. Its function is to pull the dissent off the record, restoring the pre-record asymmetry. When two documented positions exist in the same channel before the decision event, the invitation is diagnosable as an attempt to restore what the shared medium removed — rather than as a neutral preference for direct conversation.

## Relationship to Catalysis Without Claim

[[catalysis_without_claim]] covers operating inside isolation once it has been enforced — how to deposit accurate signal when the structure has already isolated the agent. This pattern covers pre-empting isolation before it can be enforced — changing the topology before the pressure move is attempted.

CWC: solo agent inside isolation, absorbs the cost of depositing signal.
This pattern: shared medium before the meeting collapses the isolation geometry before any cost needs to be absorbed.

---

## Edges

The pattern assumes the shared medium is neutral or semi-neutral. Where the channel itself is controlled by the pressure actor, the written record's structural backing can be nullified before the meeting.

How many independent documented positions are required before the pressure actor's cost exceeds the benefit of the private-resolution move? Two positions may collapse the isolation framing but not prevent a fiat move from an authority-holder who can accept the record cost.

Timing failure mode: a position placed in the channel after the meeting has been opened is reactive rather than constitutive — it documents an existing position but does not pre-empt the isolation geometry.
