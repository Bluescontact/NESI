## The action

At the moment a cost is generated, name where it lands — circulated forward as substrate someone draws on, or felt and refused before it is incurred — and if the only available landing is unpriced (the body, the soil, the next person, the future), stop the move.

## What collapsed into it

`consented_ledger` (three destinations; the third is the only forbidden one) · `the_governor` (a stack that improves with cost comes to require cost; deposit-first architecture has no termination condition) · `direction_cost_panel` (cost and direction are orthogonal axes; a one-axis panel cannot see the quadrant) · `thermal_mass_effect` (recovery is the structural completion of the transmission, not optional rest) · `system-completes-carrier-pays` (correctness is not a partial refund) · `cost_externalized_through_delegation` (authority is positional, cost is physical) · `externalized_boundary_cost` (transfer by default, no handover event, no ledger) · `sensed_but_unshown` (non-representability is what stabilizes the externalization) · `deferred_verification_as_borrowed_speed` · `metabolic_handoff` (disposition is cheapest while the context is live; the cliff, not the slope) · `unholdable_delegation` · `scarcity_interface` · `scarcity_loop_generates_rescue_frame` · `expertise_conscripted_as_rescue_fuel` · `cost-converts-to-field-legibility` (against power the cost does not settle where you aimed it; it changes form) · `symmetrical_blindness_from_asymmetrical_visibility` (both parties honestly conclude they are the net carrier)

## What didn't survive

Sustainability as a felt state. The organ never asks whether the pace feels bearable; it asks where the invoice goes. The felt question survives only as an input to the ledger, never as the ledger.

## Falsifier

If a cost with no named landing reliably surfaces on its own before it compounds, the naming step is redundant and this is bookkeeping rather than an organ. Also fails if "circulated forward" and "externalized" cannot be distinguished at the moment of the move — if the difference is only visible in retrospect, the organ cannot fire when it needs to.
