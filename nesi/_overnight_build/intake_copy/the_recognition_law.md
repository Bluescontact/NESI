# The Mechanic Never Does the Recognizing

*Adopted to canon 2026-07-24 on Kevin's mark and confirm. The one law the outward recognition-engine hangs on — Kevin's own law ([[renderer_not_verifier]], [[the_governor]]) pointed at a product.*

---

## The pattern in one move

A recognition mechanic must **never do the recognizing.** No worth-score, no value-rating, no skill-rank. The felt-read belongs to the player; the mechanic only **surfaces and reflects** — it holds the tension while the player does the seeing. The moment a system returns *"your worth: 7"* it has broken the law it claims to serve and become **extraction wearing recognition's face.** Same shape as the tension-table: the board holds the tension, it never tells you what you feel. You pull your own nodes until something lights, and only you can register that it lit.

---

## Why this is the load-bearing law, not a preference

Recognition is non-templatable. The instant it is generic it stops being recognition — worth cannot be handed, only generated. So the move is never "systematize what you do." It is the [[deletable_keystone]] / Genesis-Seed move: **you cannot hand a person their recognition, but you can hand them the surface and sequence by which they generate their own.** The content stays theirs and never leaves their hands. The mechanics only hold the tension while they see.

This makes every mechanic in the engine a **surfacing-and-reflecting** mechanic, never an assessment mechanic. The win condition is the membrane-seed lighting — and only the player can register that it lit ([[witness_as_origin]]: the felt-read is the origin, not the last sensor in a chain; a score measured from outside is no longer the origin).

---

## The four mechanics, held under the law — three already have organs

The four things worth-apps collapse into one are not one thing. Under this law each is a distinct surfacing mechanic, and three already exist as NESI organs:

- **Worth** — the non-comparative core, and the dangerous one to gamify (streaks/points/badges make worth *conditional*, and conditional worth is anti-worth). Its only honest mechanic is **surface-and-mark**: lay the raw material out, let the person mark what carries charge, never assign it. No leaderboard; the only comparison is to your own past, and even that is a **witness, not a score.** *(The one mechanic still needing its own organ.)*
- **Value** — **circulation-witness pointed at the self.** Value isn't self-claimed; it's what went out and was met. The mechanic runs *backward*, fruit→root: don't assess value forward ("am I valuable?"), look at what landed and let the landing surface the value that grew it.
- **Skill** — the **floor-pattern turned inward**: *what can I transfer that someone couldn't do before I showed them?* Skill you can teach is skill you can see. The mechanic surfaces the teachable, because the teachable is the legible.
- **Capacity to share** — **margin; the HEARTWOOD felt-read**, built as a **gate, not a demand.** It reads margin *before* it ever invites sharing, and sharing is drawn only from surplus — the guard against the "you should give more" pressure every contribution-gamification quietly runs. Steward law: *you can only give freely what you're free to withhold.*

---

## The win condition — deletable on purpose

The engine is real only if it makes the person able to recognize themselves **without** it. An engine checked daily to feel worthy is a **leash** — a worth-slot-machine with your name on it. The one this law protects wins at the moment the person outgrows it and writes their own recognition unaided. Same floor-not-leash invariant as [[deletable_keystone]], now as the product's win condition: **it succeeds when they don't need it.**

---

## Falsifier

If any mechanic in the engine ever emits a number, rank, or verdict the player did not themselves feel, the law was decorative. It earns its place only by **forbidding a real score someone would otherwise ship** — the "your worth: 7" output that every self-worth app converges on by default. If adopting this law changes no build, it was already implicit and the adoption was ceremony.
