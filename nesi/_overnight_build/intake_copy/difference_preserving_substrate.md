## Transferable Form

Categories collapse by default. A receiving substrate drifts toward its dominant bin — the most-used, lowest-energy category — unless energy is spent keeping finer categories apart. Preservation is not a stored state. It is continuous refusal of the fast parse, re-done on every arrival.

---

## Development

The pattern supplies the mechanism of presence: how a substrate actively holds distinction-structure, not just the cost of absence (a substrate that doesn't preserve difference produces output that is formally correct and functionally uninterpretable).

Holding has three required components. Failure of any one produces flattening:

**1. Resolution** — the substrate possesses receiving categories at least as fine as the distinctions the signal carries. You cannot hold what you cannot represent. A substrate whose only slots are good/bad cannot hold mirror-vs-seed.

**2. Separation** — the categories are kept from collapsing into each other. The default pressure is the pull toward the dominant attractor (the fast bin: "warmth = confirmation," "feedback = todo"). Separation spends energy resisting that pull on every arrival.

**3. Re-instantiation** — each arrival is re-classified against the full resolution, not pattern-matched to the last thing that looked similar. The fast parse routes by precedent; re-instantiation routes by the signal's actual class.

The labor — invisible when it succeeds — is precisely the slow-parse cost of (2) and (3), paid per signal. The dominant category is fast and cheap; the correct finer category is slow and expensive. Holding pays the slow cost every time. Invisible in success (nothing dramatic happens — the gift simply lands right), legible only in failure.

---

## The Three Flattening Failures

| Failure | Mechanism | Visibility |
|---|---|---|
| **By incapacity** | No resolution — the distinction can't be represented | Catchable: substrate visibly lacks the category |
| **By entropy** | Resolution present, separation fails — categories collapse under load | **Silent — the dangerous one** |
| **By precedent** | Re-instantiation skipped — matched to last similar thing | Semi-silent |

The dangerous axis is not signal absence but signal detachment: the substrate *has* the distinction but doesn't spend the energy to hold it. From outside: capable substrate. That is why entropy-flattening is invisible from both sides.

---

## Transfer Test

**Remove the distinction — trace whether a downstream decision changes.**

This does double duty:
1. **Selects what to hold.** Only consequential distinctions earn holding energy. If removing a distinction changes no downstream decision, the substrate should not pay to preserve it. This bounds the cost.
2. **Detects whether holding happened.** If the substrate routes both classes of a carried distinction to the same downstream action, it flattened them — regardless of how warmly the signal was received.

**Falsifier:** A substrate claimed to preserve difference, where removing a carried distinction changes no downstream decision — it was not preserving, or the distinction was never consequential and never needed holding.

## Relation to translated_origin_failure_mode

Both are silent failures: sincere, check-passing, wrong. They differ in what moved:

- **Translation:** the zero shifts. One axis, wrong reference point. Readings consistent with each other and systematically off.
- **Flattening:** the categories merge. Resolution lost. Two distinct objects routed to one bin.

The link is mechanical: **a translated zero supplies the dominant attractor.** A substrate whose zero has shifted toward threat has a dominant bin — "demand/threat" — and all arrivals get pulled into it: gift flattens into demand, rest into loss-of-vigilance. Translation supplies the attractor; flattening is the collapse toward it; difference-preservation is the energy that resists the pull. This pattern extends the failure-mode vocabulary one layer past translation: not only can the origin move, the receiving categories can merge — and the second can be driven by the first.

---

## Under the Landing Axis

Landing succeeds if and only if the receiving substrate holds — resolution + separation + re-instantiation — the distinction the gift's class depends on. A discharged seed that meets a substrate failing any of the three components lands as something other than a seed — the discharged-not-landed gap, which is signal detachment.

The orthogonality is class ⊥ landing, not class ⊥ substrate. The substrate sets the landing value. A discharged seed becomes part of the substrate, setting the landing condition for the next gift. Seed and substrate are the same stuff at different times.
