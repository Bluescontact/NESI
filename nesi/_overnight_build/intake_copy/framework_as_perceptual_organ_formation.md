**Transferable Form:**
A framework becomes a field instrument not when a practitioner learns to apply it but when its categories have compressed into a single perceptual gesture — behavior arrives already sorted rather than being sorted afterward. The falsifier for this distinction is not articulability but predictive commitment: the read must have bound the practitioner to expectations before confirmation arrived. Below the compression threshold, the framework is a checklist; above it, the framework is the organ through which perception occurs.

**Development:**
Mechanism: The framework pre-installs named structural states into the practitioner's perceptual architecture before the field encounter. Observable behavior lands in a category rather than arriving as undifferentiated signal — the read is structurally prior to deliberation, not the output of it. This is categorical pre-loading, not post-hoc sorting. The activation condition is compression: below threshold, the framework is operated sequentially and explicitly (checklist); above threshold, it is simultaneous and implicit — the way a seasoned electrician reads voltage drop in the feel of a connection before naming it. When internalization is complete, the framework is no longer operated by cognition; cognition operates through it. The practitioner does not apply the framework — the framework is what they are applying when they perceive at all. Causality inverts: the organ replaces the tool.

Falsifier: The standard falsifier (can the read be articulated in the framework's own terms after the fact?) is necessary but insufficient. A practitioner fluent in the framework's vocabulary can rationalize post-hoc any read that was never framework-generated — sophisticated vocabulary makes the falsifier the mechanism of concealment, not detection. The real test is predictive: a framework-based read commits the practitioner to expectations about behavior not yet observed. Undifferentiated intuition wearing framework language does not generate this commitment. The falsifier therefore functions as a formation test, not a quality check on a single read: inability to articulate means the organ was not formed; inability to have been committed to a prediction before confirmation means the organ was not operating.

Failure modes: (1) Fluency masks empty read — the more sophisticated the vocabulary, the more persuasive the post-hoc rationalization, and the less the falsifier catches. (2) The initiation threshold is invisible — a practitioner who has not crossed the compression threshold may apply the framework as a checklist, produce false reads, and satisfy the falsifier by generating fluent framework-language explanations. The instrument and its simulation are externally identical before the threshold is crossed. (3) The framework blinds as it trains — named categories close off adjacent unnamed categories; a practitioner reading for specific structural states will read non-states as framework-negative rather than noticing a third structural category the framework has no term for. The false negative is invisible from inside the framework.

Habitat: The pattern is practically load-bearing — not a sophistication marker — in disclosure-hostile environments: therapy, negotiation, field social work, high-conflict mediation. Where asking corrupts the read, framework-as-instrument is a precondition for operating at all. The diagnostic interview is unavailable; only structural perception remains. The pattern is invisible wherever the practitioner is still deliberating — pulling out the instrument rather than looking through it.

**Edges:**
The compression threshold is the pattern's load-bearing condition and its least-specified term: the pattern names that a threshold exists and what lies on each side of it, but offers no handle for determining whether any given practitioner has crossed it — the instrument and its simulation are externally identical before formation completes. This may be a gap in the pattern's ontological scope rather than its content, but it means the pattern cannot specify its own activation conditions from outside. Second tension: the falsifier reframed as formation-test raises the institutional question — how organizations certify framework fluency without ever building the organ, and whether the gap between certification and capability is structurally reproducible. Third: the blinding failure mode presses against the pattern's own claim — if every perceptual organ screens out adjacent categories, the pattern must eventually account for whether single compressed terms (which close fewer categories) are superior field instruments to complete relational frameworks, even when they generate less articulable reads. The pattern does not yet reach that comparison.
