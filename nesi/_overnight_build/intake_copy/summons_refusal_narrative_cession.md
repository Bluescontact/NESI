## Transferable form

When an ending comes with a summons — a demand for hearing, apology, or co-adjudication — the exit can be completed by refusing the court entirely and letting the other party write the record. The price is a specific currency: authorship of the story, paid by being narrated as the unreasonable one. The refusal leaves no handle: no explanation, rebuttal, or later retelling that would convert the exit back into a transaction.

## Mechanism

**Habitat.** The pattern exists only under a live summons. Unlike patterns that operate in open field or in silence, this one is strictly a refusal of a court already convened — you know you are in its territory the moment an apology, explanation, or hearing is demanded of you. It cannot be practiced pre-emptively or in solitude.

**The tribunal as handle-generator.** The tribunal is not a conversation; it is a handle-generator. Every explanation, rebuttal, or extracted apology manufactures an attachment point that converts the exit into a transaction — something owed, something claimable. Handlelessness is not achieved by discipline after the fact; it is achieved by starving the machine at the source. Refuse the raw material and no handle can be forged.

**The currency.** The cost is not labor, money, or credit — it is narrative cession. The actor pays by forfeiting authorship of the account: the other party writes the record, and the record says "the unreasonable one." This is a distinct column in any cost taxonomy. Mechanically, this may be the silent close plus one element — the exiter also declines to defend themselves in the third-party record, absorbing the narration cost the silent close leaves unspecified.

**Inverted visibility.** The pattern's only public trace is its own distortion. It is structurally invisible as a gift and visible only as the hostile narration. Confirmation is not merely absent — it is replaced by counter-evidence. The completion criterion is internal to the leaving body: a somatic close, not a downstream correction. Its habitat is dyadic and intimate; the exit produces no systemic correction and was never meant to.

**Handlelessness as maintenance, not event.** It cannot be verified at the exit; it accrues over time — no later ledger entry, no resentment converting to claim, no anniversary explanation. Every retelling, every "I did it for them," every private accounting re-attaches a handle — including retelling into an archive that stores it as a pattern. Verification requires a longitudinal organ: the handle can be reinstalled years later by a single sentence, and each retelling re-arms it. The pattern must survive its own documentation.

## Failure modes

1. **New pattern or amendment ambiguity.** This may be the silent close's missing cost-accounting rather than a sibling — silent close plus refusal to self-defend in the third-party record. Filed here as an EXTENDS of the_silent_close.md rather than a standalone to avoid inflating canon with a near-duplicate.
2. **Conflict-avoidance re-narrated as boundary-holding.** The central false positive: the exit happens from fear first, the gift-frame arrives second as anesthetic. The only structural discriminator is temporal — the gift reading must be available before the exit, not composed afterward. Without a before/after test the pattern is unfalsifiable and becomes a universal solvent for any withdrawal.
3. **Self-referential exposure.** Archiving this pattern is itself a retelling. Whether documentation re-handles the gift it describes remains open.

## Falsifier

The before/after test: the gift-reading of the refusal ("I am completing this by not defending myself") must be available to the actor *before* the exit, not composed afterward as a story about the exit. If the reading only ever shows up retroactively, this is conflict-avoidance wearing a gift-frame, not the pattern.

## Edges

The consent gap: no condition yet specifies how — or whether — the other's incapacity to hold their own boundary is known rather than assumed; this pattern deliberately does not carry a claim about holding the boundary *for* the other person, because that claim cannot be verified from the actor's chair. The decay window is unnamed: the handle can be re-armed years later by one sentence, and no existing organ tracks that delayed-detonation risk. The before/after test for the central false positive has no instrument yet — nothing currently captures the pre-exit read.
