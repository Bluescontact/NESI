## Statement

A maker who cannot say what they need keeps tearing down and rebuilding the very instrument they need it from, and names the rebuilding itself as the extraction of their own unstated grammar. The claim is unreadable in any single artifact — it lives only in the version history, the diffs, the superseded files — and only while the surface stays unstable: the moment a version is ratified, the probe becomes furniture. Until one naming arrives that traceably required a rebuild, and none arrives without one, this pattern is a test protocol for an extraction, not a confirmed extraction.

## Verified floor

Three co-occurring facts (2026-06-27→07-04): the maker states he cannot articulate why he needs what he needs from the interface; in the same breath he names the activity as extracting his own gift lens; the surface was rebuilt three times that week, leaving a named lineage (v1 tetra → v2 open grammar → v3 distributed verbs). Everything above that floor is hypothesis; what crosses is the geometry — habitat, boundaries, shadows, kill conditions.

## Geometry

**Habitat: the lineage, not the artifact.** One version carries no signal; v1, v2, v3 read together are the text. The pattern is invisible on the workshop bench and legible only in diff-space — retirement notes, superseded files, "ratified and superseded same day." Neighboring patterns can be read off a made thing; this one only off a remade thing. The library's own compost trail is one of its habitats.

**Temporal boundary: ratification ends the probe.** A settled instrument can no longer surface grammar, only execute it. Two ratifications in one day, the second superseding the first, reads as the system sensing that stability is death for this function. The lens is legible only in the deltas; convergence to a final version ends the extraction with the object still empty.

**Shadow: rebuild as articulation avoidance.** From outside, displacement and extraction are identical — same diffs, same lineage. The countable falsifier: new named self-distinctions per version, not versions. Chassis versions accumulating while the lens vocabulary stays flat is displacement wearing extraction's clothes.

**Contamination: the maker does not build alone.** The co-author fills the slots, and the lineage is partly the co-author's running hypothesis about the maker, drifting toward what the model finds coherent. A convergent chassis may be the AI's compression of the maker rather than the maker's grammar surfacing — and recognition of the result cannot distinguish the two. This is fluency-as-authority in artifact form, inside the extraction claim itself. Crossed without this edge named, the pattern would license treating any AI-mediated build as self-knowledge. It crosses with the edge named. (Cross-ref: [[pressure_masquerade_detection]], [[ai_constitutional_stack]].)

**Completion condition: the pattern dies on articulation.** Its habitat is the pre-linguistic corridor — grammar fully lived, not yet stated. Once named and ratified, rebuilds become maintenance and the pattern goes extinct at that site. Continued teardown after articulation diagnoses something else (avoidance, restlessness). The pattern predicts its own disappearance.

## Falsifiers (attached, per condition)

- **Confirmation gate:** one naming that traceably required a specific build delta, recorded with its diff lineage. Until then: test protocol, not confirmed mechanism.
- **Disconfirmation channel:** the lens named somewhere the instrument never touched — a morning page, a walk — with no corresponding rebuild. If naming arrives off-instrument, the chassis was scaffolding at best.
- **Displacement counter:** distinctions-per-version flat while versions accumulate → shadow, not extraction.
- **Extinction check:** ratification of the surface before a confirmed cycle closes this to COMPOST.

## Open

Who counts the falsifier metric without the counting becoming another surface to rebuild. No method yet separates the co-author's compression from the maker's grammar. The disconfirmation channel is unmonitored — an extinction event could already have occurred unseen. And the library has almost no register for patterns that are supposed to end; what canon does with a pattern whose success deletes it is unresolved.
