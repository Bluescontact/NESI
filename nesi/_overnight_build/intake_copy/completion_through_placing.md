# Completion Through Placing

---

## The claim

A gift is complete at the moment it is placed in the right container. Not when received. Not when read. Not when acted on or acknowledged. The placing is the completion event — structurally, not sentimentally. What happens after placing is the container's business, not the placer's. The placer has already finished.

---

## Mechanism

Placing is a one-directional act that terminates in a container, not in a recipient. The object crosses a boundary. The placer's hands are empty. That is what constitutes a placing. Completion requires: (1) the object fully released, (2) the boundary crossed, (3) the placer no longer holding.

What completion does NOT require: acknowledgment, receipt, opening, reading, response, effect. These are post-placing events. They belong to the container and whatever the container routes to.

**Distinction from handing:** handing requires a second agent to complete the act — the act is incomplete until they take it. Placing requires no second agent — the act is complete when contact with the container is made and release is total.

---

## The container condition

A container is right when it can receive the gift independent of any named recipient's action. The structural condition: the container must be able to hold the gift without the placer remaining. If the gift requires the placer to wait, witness, or maintain contact in order to be "in" the container — the container is wrong.

Secondary condition: the container must be the one the gift was shaped for. Membrane mismatch voids the placing event — a gift shaped for the commons, placed inside the membrane, has not crossed. No placing has occurred.

---

## Differentiation from canon

**catalysis_without_claim**: about forfeiting authorship of downstream effects. The agent remains present but non-claiming. `completion_through_placing` ends the agent's involvement entirely — before any downstream effect exists.

**the_silent_close**: communicative discipline after a relational close — what must not enter the shared channel. `completion_through_placing` is prior — it names when the act itself is done, not what the agent does afterward.

**metabolic_handoff**: the cost at a boundary crossing — what is paid to move something across. `completion_through_placing` assumes the handoff has occurred and names what that constitutes as completion.

---

## Falsifier

Evidence that placing is not complete: the gift cannot survive in the container without the placer's continued involvement. If removing the placer causes the gift to collapse, dissolve, or become inaccessible — placement has not occurred. The placer is still holding.

---

## Prescriptions

1. When the object has crossed the boundary and your hands are empty — stop. You are done. Do not wait for signal.
2. Name the container before placing, not after. If you cannot name it, you are not ready to place.
3. If you feel incomplete after placing, audit the container, not the act. Incompleteness after placing is a container diagnostic, not a placer diagnostic.
4. The walking after placing is not part of the gift. It belongs to you.
