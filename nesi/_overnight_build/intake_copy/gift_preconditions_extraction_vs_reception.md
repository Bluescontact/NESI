## Transferable form

Before the next unit of labor leaves your body, a gift requires a counterpart who can receive — and reception has cheap observable traces, the small unconditioned reciprocities (a shared meal, a table, hospitality with no toll) whose sustained absence marks a one-way channel. When the preconditions fail, the move is not accusation but reclassification: this outflow was never a gift, so the gift channel closes — routing changes, history stays unlitigated, the relation itself remains undefined. The gate only holds if the precondition-failure is read prospectively, mid-outflow; invoked backward after a souring, it becomes an exit-justification engine, not a detector.

## Mechanism

The pattern is a giver-side gate that fires in first-person present tense, mid-outflow, at the moment before the next act of giving — deciding whether that unit of labor is gift or extraction. Retrospective patterns can only name losses; this one can stop labor still in the body.

Detection runs on the cheapest observable act that cannot be extracted, only co-performed: shared consumption. Meal, beer, coffee, bowl, broken bread — table-facts, not accounting. Sustained absence of zero-cost reciprocity events over time is the signal for a one-way channel. This converts a felt sense of extraction into a checkable count, and it is falsifiable: one genuine unconditioned shared meal breaks the read.

The sharpest extraction signature is not the absent route but the **tolled route**: the only reception offered runs through holding the counterpart's wound as price of entry — a gift channel running the wrong direction, reception available only as tribute. This is what distinguishes the pattern from simple bilateral-absence: not no route, but a route with a toll.

On precondition failure, the operation is **channel-scoped closure**: subtract one channel-type (gift) from one flow (labor to property) without adjudicating history. The mechanism operates on routing, not on story — which is why it need not relitigate. The enabling register is administrative, not grievance: "the preconditions for gift weren't met" is a category correction, a mislabeled transaction being refiled, not an accusation being pressed. Accusation invites defense; reclassification does not.

**Native habitat:** gift-framed settings — communes, land projects, care circles — whose vocabulary of generosity pre-classifies all outflow as gift, so the extraction is performed AS reception and is invisible from inside. A pattern invisible in its own habitat needs an external test to fire; the hospitality probe supplies it.

## What actually holds (structural truth, resolved in-text)

Not "preconditions for gift" as a bilateral fact — that claim is unilateral, retrospective, and unfalsifiable as stated. What holds is a **giver-side detection register**: hospitality-absence as probe, reception-only-as-toll as signature, reclassification instead of grievance as the closing move. For any economy built on labor-as-gift, a live prospective gate at the point of outflow is load-bearing infrastructure.

## Failure modes (the ratchet-risk travels inside the pattern as its own falsifier)

1. **The proxy break** — hospitality-absence is a cultural proxy for reception, not reception itself. False-positives on any counterpart whose reception grammar isn't commensal — the introvert host, the different-class host, the one who receives by lending tools or leaving you alone. Must be one signal among several or the pattern misfires across cultures and temperaments.
2. **The ratchet** — named only after the labor was given, the precondition claim can always be invoked backward to reclassify any completed gift as extraction once the relationship sours. Nothing internal distinguishes "preconditions were never met" from "I have stopped wanting them to have been met." The gate is trustworthy only when the failure was noted contemporaneously; without a timestamp it is unfalsifiable.
3. **The evidenced boundary** — a close announced with its grievance inventory attached (no meal, no beer, no bowl) is already a litigation. The detection layer's output must be discarded at exit or the reclassification collapses back into complaint and loses its non-relitigating property.
4. **The unlit exit** — closure ends at the channel and says nothing about what the labor becomes. If the gift channel closes while extraction continues under a wage, the boundary is not clean — it is a rate change.

## Edges

- The downstream fork is unbuilt: after the gift channel closes, the labor's fate (convert to paid, withhold, depart) is undefined; the handoff to `the_silent_close` (detection output discarded at the threshold) is named only as a risk, not a protocol.
- The prospective test lacks instrumentation: what does contemporaneous noting look like in practice — a timestamped mark, a running count of reciprocity events — such that the ratchet can be caught before it turns?
- The proxy problem is open: hospitality is one reception grammar among several, and the pattern has no method for discovering a counterpart's actual grammar before scoring its absence.
- The gate has not yet been turned on its own holder: what does the giver's side look like when the giver is the one converting inbound channels to inbound-only?
