# Externalize the Container, Never the Verdict

*slug: `externalize-container-not-verdict`*

---

## Transferable Form

Any relief structure a maker builds — a process, a person, a machine — may carry the whole container: routing, holding, record, and the arrangement of evidence. The one thing that may never move outward is the verdict — the settling-read, in the maker's body, that a thing is ready to cross — nor the clock that would time it, because a structure that decides when the read fires has already decided whether. The container is licensed in full; the verdict and its timing stay at the origin.

---

## Development

State may externalize; the event may not. Routing, holding, and record are all container functions — they carry the situation to the read without performing it. The verdict is the single non-externalizable act: the body's settling-read, the origin in `witness_as_origin.md`'s sense — the zero from which readiness is measured, never measured from outside.

The one container function that leaks is evidence-arrangement, because arranging evidence toward a conclusion is verdict-shaped work wearing container clothing. The mechanism that keeps it presentational rather than conclusive is **verdict-liveness preservation**: an arrangement is presentational if, after it, both dispositions remain reachable by the body from what is presented — "not ready" is at least as surfaceable as "ready" — and the settling-read still has to be performed rather than merely ratified. It becomes conclusive the moment it filters, buries, or pre-weights the disconfirming evidence so that only one verdict is mechanically implied; then the body's "read" degrades into rubber-stamping the conclusion the arrangement already built.

The operational test, borrowed from `gate_as_f4.md`'s incapacity move and run on the arrangement instead of the veto: could the body, from this arrangement alone, still reach the opposite verdict? If no, the arrangement has absorbed the read.

## Edges

The clock/timing sub-claim leans on `gate_as_f4.md`'s discharge-condition mechanism from the container side and should not be advertised as this pattern's own distinctiveness — it is inherited, not original. The verdict-liveness test's threshold ("at least as surfaceable") is still a felt-read at the margin, not a paper-check — consistent with `witness_as_origin.md`'s hard limit, not a defect, but worth naming plainly.

**Named risk, carried forward at crossing, not resolved:** the pattern's entire surviving distinctiveness rests on the verdict-liveness mechanism holding up to a cold felt-read over time. If it doesn't land on repeated use, the pattern folds into `witness_as_origin.md` rather than standing alone. See `membrane/WATCHERS.md`.
