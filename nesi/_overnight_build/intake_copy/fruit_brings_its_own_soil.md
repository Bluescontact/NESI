## The invariant

> **A fruit is a seed — the whole tree-pattern, compressed — plus enough metabolic reserve to compost its own soil and fund its own rooting. A made thing is a *gift* (a fruit) only when it can root in soil that is not yours: carrying its own ground so it draws nothing from the one who receives it.**

Anything that must be received into ground the recipient has already prepared — that needs the reader to already hold your framework — is not a fruit. It is a seed without reserve, and it dies in foreign soil.

## Mechanism — the three functions of the reserve

A fruit does three things a bare seed cannot:

1. **Composts its own soil.** It carries the conditions of its own reception — enough context that it lands as legible, not as raw material the recipient must first make sense of. It creates the ground it needs.
2. **Funds its own rooting.** It carries the establishment-energy to take hold, so the *recipient's* metabolism is not spent installing it. This is `load_off_the_vulnerable_node` applied to the receiver: the load of establishment rests on the fruit's reserve, never on the recipient's ground.
3. **Carries the whole pattern, once.** It holds enough of the entire tree — form, forces, falsifier — to regrow the whole in new ground. One generation of recursion, which is all propagation requires.

## The crossing bar (what this gives the DOOR)

`the_door_as_stack_layer` asks "what may cross?" and answers only "what the machine has delivered." This sharpens it to a single checkable question: **cross only what can self-root.** The DOOR's check, and the reader's second-read, run the same test one layer apart.

## Falsifier

**Can it root in soil that isn't yours?** Hand the fruit to someone who does not hold your library, your context, your prior marks. If it takes hold and can regrow the pattern there, it was a fruit. If it needs you standing beside it to explain, translate, or defend it, it was a seed — return it to grow. (The test is receiver-facing and, in its strong form, may need a real reader to run — see the live edge.)

## What it does NOT claim

- **Not every made-object is a fruit.** This is a high bar by design; most made things stay seeds or compost, and that is correct. The pattern is the crossing bar, not a demand that everything ripen.
- **Not fractal-to-infinity.** "Contains the whole at every scale, all the way down" is true poetically; the load-bearing claim is *enough to regrow the whole once*. Building it as infinite recursion is mythology.
- **Self-rooting may not be deterministically measurable.** Deterministic instruments (find-kin, read-lineage, name-the-gap, gather-falsifiers, check-coherence) can flag a seed that is obviously context-locked, but judging genuine self-rooting-capacity may require a real reader — an engine edge, named not hidden. The bar is crisp; its automated measurement is not yet.
