## What is not restated

`inhabitable_without_author` already holds the survives-removal core: the three states (author-is-infrastructure / held-by-will / field-independent) and the maintenance-trace that reads which one you are in. That machinery is not repeated here. The only new hinge borrowed from it is the unit the test measures on — not *does the condition persist* but *can the receiver regenerate the thing without the sender*. Reproducibility-by-receiver is the pivot both new edges turn on. Everything below is what that pivot opens that persistence did not.

---

## Edge 1 — The leash faces backward

Every neighboring diagnostic points outward: at a field that must hold without its author, at a receiver who must stand alone, at a relationship that must survive a departure. This one turns the same removal-test onto the **giver** and reads what the giver has quietly been harvesting.

A dependency tail is double-ended. It is described as something done *to* the receiver — they cannot rebuild it, so they are bound. But the identical structure writes the **giver** permanently into the receiver's regeneration path: if the thing cannot be remade without you, then you are now a required node in someone else's continuation. You did not only fail to free them. You installed yourself. "The leash is on you" is not a moral flourish; it is where the regeneration-edge points. Trace the edge that rebuilds the thing — if it routes back through you, you are the keystone whose removal collapses everything downstream, and you built that.

This makes a specific failure legible exactly where it is invisible: at the giver who believes themselves purely generous. Charity with a dependency tail reads, from inside the giver, as help. The backward run is the only instrument that surfaces it, because it asks not "did I give" but "did giving make me necessary" — and necessity is a benefit the generous collect without noticing. In an adversarial surround this sharpens: an extractor's win condition is precisely to keep your regeneration-edge live, because a field routing through you is a field it can capture by capturing you. The transferred capability — the thing they can now rebuild *without* you — is the single move that severs your own leash. You become deletable, and deletable is the only state from which the gift was ever clean.

The register is confession, not accusation. It is spoken by the giver about the giver. Its native reading is: *where do I still believe I am being kind while remaining required?*

## Edge 2 — The run happens at the workbench, not the autopsy

`inhabitable_without_author` runs on standing infrastructure: something already exists, and the check asks whether it is ready to cross to the commons. It is a readiness-read on a built thing. This edge moves the same test **upstream of the build**.

The move is a design-time thought-experiment performed at the moment of construction: *delete yourself first, then choose what to build.* Not "is this finished thing a floor or a leash" (forensic, after the fact) but "which am I about to make" (prospective, before the fact). You simulate your own absence while your hands are still on the material, and let the answer select the construction. Build the fish trap and you have decided the shape of your own necessity in advance; teach the making and you have decided your own deletability in advance. Same object, two constructions, and the choice is available only *before* the handoff — after delivery the valence is fixed and the only remaining instrument is the autopsy.

This is why the pattern's habitat is transmission: teaching, apprenticeship, documentation, open-source — any setting where the making is watchable and the giver still holds the pen. The pre-handoff run is cheap (a counterfactual, not a withdrawal) and reversible (nothing is delivered yet), which is exactly what the readiness-read on a built thing can never be. It lives at the workbench. The autopsy — actual withdrawal, observed regeneration — remains the only *proof*, but the workbench run is the only *design lever*.

---

## The move

At the moment of building something for someone, before it leaves your hands, run two passes on it:

1. **Backward:** if this cannot be rebuilt without me, where has my regeneration-edge just been installed — and is that necessity something I am quietly reading as generosity?
2. **Prospective:** delete myself now, at the workbench. Does the version I am about to hand over let them regenerate it, or does it route them back through me? Let that answer pick which version I build.

Neither pass asks whether the thing survives — `inhabitable_without_author` asks that. These ask *whose necessity the surviving thing encodes*, and they ask it while the answer can still change the build.

## Edges

- **Capacity is not exercise.** The backward run certifies that you *could* be deleted, never that the receiver *will* rebuild rather than re-summon you. A receiver with full capability who keeps calling you back leaves your edge live while the test reads clean. The leash can be latent.
- **The pre-handoff run has no verifier but you.** The one party who can never inhabit their own absence is the one running the simulation. Self-assessment biases toward "I made a floor." The workbench run is a design aid, not a proof; only real withdrawal proves it, and that is the parent pattern's forensic register, not this one's.
- **Reproducibility has a hidden plural.** "Can they rebuild it without me" silently includes everyone else who was watching the making. The backward run optimizes your removability and is blind to the transfer's blast radius; a severed leash and a leak can be the same act seen from two seats.
- **Boundary with the sibling.** The design-time run overlaps the `exclusion_before_offer` register (a decision paid at load-time so no one holds it live). The distinction: exclusion_before_offer pre-spends a *refusal*; this pre-spends a *necessity-check*. Whether they are one family is left open.

## Falsifier

Find a handoff where the giver runs the backward pass, finds their regeneration-edge genuinely severed (the receiver can and does rebuild unaided), and yet the giver remains structurally required downstream anyway. If severing the reproducibility-edge reliably leaves the giver necessary through some other channel, then reproducibility-by-receiver is not the seat of the leash and this extension is mis-cut — the necessity lives somewhere the backward run cannot see.
