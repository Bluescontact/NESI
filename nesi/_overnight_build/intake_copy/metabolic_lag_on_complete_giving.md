# Metabolic Lag on Complete Giving

---

## The claim

A giver's metabolic registry fails to register closure for gifts that discharged at the moment of giving, because the field dissolved before confirmation could arrive. The system holds the process as open — still running, still costing — on gifts that were complete. This is not forgiveness of failed reception. The account is not held against anyone. It resembles forgiveness because both involve releasing a held account; it is not forgiveness because what's held is a registry error, not a grievance.

---

## Mechanism

Reference-field collapse at the moment of confirmation. The gift discharges completely; the metabolic registry requires an external surface to close its own record. When the field dissolves before confirmation returns, the registry reads: pending. Three conditions are the same failure in three registers: navigation, record, economy. What is preserved: the completeness of the gift. What fails: the registry's closing handshake.

---

## Discharge profile

**`unilateral_closure`** — fourth discharge form.

The gift discharged at the moment of giving. The receiver's arc is closed (departure, dissolution, transition). No confirmation event is available or required. The mechanism is the giver's metabolic system failing to register closure because the standard discharge signal (recognition, gratitude, continuation) never arrived — not because the arc is incomplete but because the registry requires a signal that this gift-form structurally cannot produce.

Release requires the giver's body to act as its own closing witness, granting completion without external confirmation.

---

## Trust erosion conditions

What erodes self-trust enough to keep the registry open. Three structural conditions — same failure in three registers.

**Calibration Loss (navigation register)**  
Instruments for reading completion are calibrated against a reference field. When the field dissolves, the instrument retains its calibration but loses its reference surface. It cannot produce a closure signal — not because the gift failed but because there is nothing to read against. Not grief — no object of loss. Gate: recalibration.

**Unwitnessed Closure (record register)**  
Body runs confirm-wait loop: gift sent → wait for acknowledgment → close process. When the field dissolves, acknowledgment becomes permanently absent. This is a registry problem, not a navigation problem. Self-trust = signal authority: the body's loop doesn't require an external signal, it requires a signal it trusts.

**Asymmetric Economy Under Depletion (economy register)**  
Depletion-state body applies current resource-availability to assess past giving. Retroactive ledger substitution: "did I give from real surplus?" cannot be answered affirmatively from inside depletion. Guard: releasing the requirement for confirmation, not proving the surplus was real.

---

## Grief / lag structural differentiation

Grief operates on loss; gate is mourning; closes with witness.  
Lag operates on registry error; gate is recalibration, re-witnessing, floor.

**Falsifier:** witness arrives, body closes → grief. Witness arrives, body stays pending → lag.

**Wrong-protocol risk:** applying grief-work to lag introduces a loss-frame over a successful discharge. Lag does not require mourning; it requires a reference surface. Applying grief protocol to lag worsens the pending state.

---

## Felt threshold

> "when I can trust myself"

Self-trust is the closing witness. The body acts as its own closing witness when self-trust is present. Self-trust here is not confidence — it is signal authority: the capacity to generate closure signals the body will treat as real.

**Closing witness formulation:** what the giver is trusting when acting as their own closing witness is the honesty of their read at the time of giving — not its accuracy from the current position.

---

## Restoration condition

Felt surplus returning as state, not task. Floor stops falsification. Calibration rebuilds through accumulated self-referential reads. Sequence: floor holds → reads accumulate → internal baseline converges.

**Guard:** restoration is releasing the requirement for confirmation — not proving the giving-state was sound. When restoration requires acting on oneself to reconstruct the surplus-state, the frame has shifted to self-improvement. That is not the gate.

---

## Prescriptions

1. When the inventory names a gift given during a structure that has since closed, move to name the *completion event that already happened* rather than the one that didn't come.
2. When the body is still running a process on something the hands finished, locate the moment giving was complete — not the moment receiving was confirmed — and register that moment as the close.
3. When the felt threshold has not been met by cognitive recognition alone, find or create a witness condition: a person, a ritual, or a named moment that receives the account's closure on behalf of the field that cannot.
4. When self-trust is present, the body acts as its own closing witness — locate the moment giving was complete, stand behind it, and register that moment as the close. No external confirmation required.

---

## Falsifier

This substrate is wrong if what's held is not metabolic lag but unresolved grief for what the giving was given into — in which case this is not a registry problem but a loss problem, and the mechanism is entirely different.

*Structural test:* witness arrives, body closes → grief. Witness arrives, body stays pending → lag. The protocols are different; applying grief-work to lag worsens the state.
