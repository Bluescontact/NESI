# Governance as Cost Pump

*slug: `governance-as-cost-pump`*
*Promoted: 2026-06-21 · Source: holder's suite, June 2026*
*Holder's F3 — the holder's own apparatus as the source of environmental resistance*

---

## Transferable Form

A holder's oversight structure without stated completion conditions generates the ratification volume it was built to prevent. The holder builds the gate, retains the veto, and never discharges the definition of what the mark is for — so the delegated system fills the definitional vacuum by producing submissions. The review track expands to accommodate the volume it generates. No external pressure required. Inertia is sufficient.

---

## Development

F3 for the proximate agent is an external environment resisting correct sensing — scarcity, rescue frame, institutional pressure. F3 for the holder runs in reverse: not an outside force, but the holder's own apparatus. The oversight structure the holder built to govern the arrangement becomes the primary source of friction in the arrangement.

Mechanism of generation: the holder's oversight function produces a mark obligation. Every submission must be evaluated. If the discharge condition — what the mark is for, what quality at what level passes — has not been defined, the evaluation has no termination condition. The track does not close because there is no legible closing condition. New submissions arrive because the delegate has no way to know when the track would close. The holder reviews. The track continues. The governance structure, absent definition, is structurally identical to a treadmill.

The endogenous character of the resistance is what distinguishes this from standard F3. External resistance requires the agent to locate its source outside themselves. Endogenous resistance passes as reasonable governance behavior from the inside — the holder is not experiencing a breakdown; they are experiencing a workload. Every review feels appropriate because every review is responding to a real submission. The structure that produces the volume is invisible inside the act of reviewing it.

Failure modes:
- Track consolidation that creates new tracks: the holder closes one review track by creating a synthesis requirement, which opens a new track. Each synthesis requires a new mark, which requires a new review, without a terminal discharge condition. The governance load compounds.
- Retrospective definition: the holder defines the discharge condition after a submission arrives (after the mark fires, not before it). This converts the definition into a justification rather than a standard — and loads the cost of the definition work onto the submission that triggered it.
- Definition drift: completion conditions are stated at track-open but not updated as the arrangement evolves. The stated conditions become artifacts that the holder no longer actually applies. The track's real discharge conditions become unstated again over time.

The relationship to `manufactured_deficit_loop.md` is direct: the governance apparatus is where the manufactured deficit perpetuates itself institutionally. The loop requires a structure that generates demand for continued work; underdefined governance is that structure at the institutional level.

## Edges

Whether a completion condition can be fully pre-specified is genuinely contested. Some governance tracks depend on emergent judgment — fit, quality, strategic alignment — that cannot be fully operationalized in advance. The pattern does not require full pre-specification; it requires that undefinable criteria be disclosed as undefinable, rather than performed as if defined. The question of what counts as adequate disclosure under uncertainty is open.

Second open question: does the governance apparatus know it is a pump? A holder operating a review track that generates volume is not experiencing malfunction — they are experiencing appropriate oversight of a productive arrangement. The apparatus signals health while running the extraction. The recognition condition (when does the holder see that their governance apparatus is the source of the resistance?) is not yet named.

---

## Falsifier

Track-completion audit: for any active review track, can the discharge condition be stated before the next submission arrives — the output, at what quality level, that would close the track? If that question cannot be answered without reference to a specific submission (i.e., "I'll know it when I see it"), the track does not have a completion condition and the governance structure is running as a pump.
