# Subsidized Clarity Signal

*slug: `subsidized-clarity-signal`*
*Promoted: 2026-06-21 · Source: holder's suite, June 2026*
*Holder's F1 failure — confidence reads legibility, not labor*

---

## Transferable Form

The holder's confidence in reading a situation is a report on the legibility of what arrived, not on the labor required to make it legible. When a delegate pre-processes a situation before it reaches the holder — stripping friction, resolving ambiguity, selecting what to surface — the holder's somatic instrument returns a clean read. The clarity is subsidized. The subsidy is invisible from inside the confidence.

---

## Development

The single-body somatic instrument works on what it receives. When the holder receives clarity, the instrument reads clarity. The instrument is not broken. The problem is that the signal has been processed before it reached the sensor — and the processing cost did not transfer with the signal.

The mechanism: a delegate operating in proximity to ground-level friction has two options for what to send upward. First, the raw material: complexity, unresolved questions, competing signals, their own uncertainty. Second, a pre-processed summary: the situation legibilized, the uncertainty resolved into a position, the complexity compressed into a decision-ready form. The second option is more useful to the holder in the immediate term and less costly to the delegate interpersonally — it lands better, it does not require the holder to hold complexity, it demonstrates competence. The delegate often does this without being asked.

The result: the holder's operating picture is assembled from pre-processed material without the holder knowing that is what is happening. Every time the holder has a clean read, they are potentially reading the delegate's compression work rather than the situation itself. The two are phenomenologically identical from inside the confidence.

This is F1 for the holder in the opposite direction from Sincerity-as-Specification. In that pattern, the holder's F1 failure is what they transmit (not specifying the edge). In this pattern, the F1 failure is what they receive (not knowing that what they receive has been processed).

The structural consequence: the holder's decisions are based on a picture that has had the cost of its own legibility removed. The decisions may be correct. The holder's model of what is happening is systematically missing a variable — the labor that made the picture readable. The holder under-estimates the operational cost of the arrangement because the cost has been absorbed into the substrate of every communication before it arrived.

---

## Edges

The hardest open question: at what point does pre-processing become misrepresentation? A delegate who smooths a report to make it more useful is not the same as a delegate who distorts a report to protect themselves or the holder from uncomfortable data. The same action (compression, clarity, legibilization) can be either good communication practice or structural cost-absorption, and the distinction is not visible in the output. The holder receives the same thing either way.

Second tension: some pre-processing is appropriate — not everything needs to land raw. The delegate's role includes translation, and some translation is part of the work. The question is not whether pre-processing occurs but whether the holder has any instrument for reading the cost of the processing that produced the clarity they are receiving.

The counterfactual check is the only instrument currently available, and it requires the holder to have access to the raw material, which the arrangement often does not provide. If the delegate controls what the holder sees, the holder cannot run the counterfactual. This makes the instrument structurally dependent on conditions the arrangement itself may not supply.

---

## Falsifier

Counterfactual check: would the holder have formed the same assessment from the raw material the delegate received, without the delegate's processing work? If the holder cannot access the raw material, the question becomes: does the holder know what processing work occurred before the situation arrived? If neither is answerable, the clarity signal is subsidized and the subsidy is not accounted for.
