# Expertise Conscripted as Rescue Fuel

---

## The mechanism

When a system is locked in a scarcity-rescue frame, it pulls in expert knowledge not to evaluate whether the path is sound but to generate the next attempt. The expert's competence and care become motive force for the loop rather than signal that could break it.

The coupling: scarcity activates rescue orientation; rescue orientation requires progressively more sophisticated attempts to maintain credibility; sophisticated attempts require expert capacity; the expert, once engaged, is inside the loop's logic and their competence is reinterpreted as loop-serving rather than loop-evaluating.

The structural tell: in a decision-open frame, expert input can produce a stop. In a rescue frame, expert input can only produce a next attempt. If no input can terminate the loop, the loop is closed and expertise is fuel, not signal.

---

## Three failure modes

**The expert cannot exit cleanly.** Exit reads as abandonment rather than accurate assessment. The more competent the expert, the more their departure is interpreted as failure of will rather than honest signal. Social cost of exit scales with expertise.

**The loop self-selects for experts who share the rescue orientation** or cannot distinguish their care from the system's need. The rescue frame filters for proximate, invested experts — exactly the ones most susceptible to fuel-capture.

**Information produced inside the loop is loop-contaminated.** The expert generates analysis oriented toward "how do we continue" rather than "should we continue." This is not dishonesty; it is frame capture. The analysis is sincere. The frame has already absorbed the expert's evaluative function.

---

## The named stop-signal

The evaluative function re-seats outside the loop the moment the expert stops generating next-attempt fuel while still returning the accurate assessment.

**The move:** accurate verdict, silence on next steps. The assessment stays; the "here's what to try" does not enter the channel. The loop needs the expert's knowledge to generate its next move; the expert who returns the assessment without producing next-step fuel starves the loop at its fuel source without misrepresenting the system state.

This is the application of [[the_silent_close]] in the rescue-fuel context: the close holds because nothing enters the channel to reopen it. The expert has done the diagnostic work and delivered the finding. What does not follow — the workaround, the next attempt, the continued engagement — is the structural completion of the close.

The contrast with soft-pedaling: soft-pedaling returns a qualified verdict that can be quoted as permission. The named stop-signal returns the unqualified verdict and then goes silent. The difference is not in the truth-content of the assessment — it is in whether next-step fuel is produced.

---

## What this requires

The move requires that the expert's care and the loop's need for continuation can be separated. Proximate experts are most susceptible to fuel-capture precisely because their care is real. It is recognizing that more care deposited as fuel does not help the system; it extends the loop.

---

## Edges

The positive account: what structural conditions allow expert knowledge to function as genuine decision-signal rather than fuel? The inverse — a frame where expertise can produce termination — requires naming. A decision-open commission, a principal who has not pre-collapsed the verdict, an explicit authorization for the expert to return a stop: these may be the preconditions, not the outcome, of the move.

The structural tell (can this input produce a stop?) is observable in retrospect, not necessarily in real time.
