## Transferable form

A public ask from a position of undefended need can invert the charity geometry: the asker becomes the witnessing surface on which givers are visibly made whole, so the material transfer is only the carrier signal. The asker pays in exposure and gains witness; the giver pays in goods and gains completion — two currencies flowing in opposite directions, deliberately non-convertible. The mechanism is single-shot: it works only while the ask is unrepeatable and nothing persists — no ledger, no role, no standing channel.

## Mechanism

A person makes a public ask involving real need; people respond with kindness; the asker reports the givers appeared made whole; the asker absorbs the cost "because it had meaning in the moment." The material transfer is the carrier; the payload is the asker witnessing the givers' wholeness becoming visible. The ask manufactures an occasion for that visibility — the asker functions as witness-infrastructure, not recipient. This puts the witness vertex inside the point of maximum need, a location witnessing was assumed to require stable ground to occupy.

**Route geometry.** Both parties act from their own ground — ask made, gift given — but the ledger is asymmetric: giver pays in goods, gains wholeness; asker pays in exposure, gains witness. The two flows are incommensurable by construction. If they could be priced against each other the circuit would close into exchange; non-convertibility is the moving part. This is the human-scale, no-tooling instance of the structural incapacity the Open Ledger enforces by form: debt unrepresentable because the currencies do not convert.

**Expiry as load-bearing member.** "Meaning in the moment" is a time-bound valuation — the cost is payable only while the moment is live. The mechanism works precisely because nothing persists: no ledger, no relationship obligation, no standing role of the-one-who-begs. The pattern requires its own evaporation to function.

**Structural truth.** The name "infrastructure" is self-contradictory for this mechanism: infrastructure must be repeatable, and this is single-shot by construction. What transfers is not a standing channel but the conditions for fresh asks — one-time crossings, not routes. The seat inversion stands independent of the failure modes: witnessing is available at the bottom of scarcity, not only from stable ground.

## Built-in limits (carried as constitutive, not pending — per re-screen)

**Limit 1 — the giver-side falsifier does not exist.** "Made whole" is unverifiable from the asker's seat, indistinguishable from pity, social pressure, or performance for third parties. There is no giver-side signal to distinguish these. This pattern must carry this as a permanent unknown, not a gap awaiting a future test.

**Limit 2 — cost-absorption cannot be sorted from outside.** Absorbing the cost can be genuine unilateral closure, or the asker pre-paying shame so no one can call the ask extraction — a defensive move disguised as a gift move. Structurally identical from outside; opposite in load path. The pattern applies only where the asker has already made peace with not knowing which one it was.

## Failure modes

1. **Rehearsal kills it.** The second identical ask to the same witnesses converts vulnerability into a role and the givers' wholeness into obligation-discharge. The pattern is load-bearing only while unrepeatable — single-shot by construction. Any attempt to make it recurring or institutional creates exactly the standing identity (dependent, beggar) the momentary form structurally avoids.
2. **The wholeness read is unfalsifiable** (see Limit 1 above) — licenses continued asking on the strength of a projection, extraction wearing the costume of gift-completion, unless the boundary condition lives on the giver's side or nowhere.
3. **Cost absorption forks** (see Limit 2 above) — performed vulnerability with self-financed absolution is structurally identical to genuine closure from outside.

## Edges

Everything infrastructural in the original framing — persistence, setting, mechanism, third parties, scale — remains imported frame the source material does not support; what "infrastructure for one-time crossings" would concretely be is unnamed. The connection to the floor condition is a live tension, not a resolution: whether witnessing from inside maximum need is sustainable, or only visible once, is exactly the single-shot limit turned back on the pattern's own habitat.
