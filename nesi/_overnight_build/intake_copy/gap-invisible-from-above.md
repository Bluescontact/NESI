## Transferable form

A defect that is point-local and only registers on the body passing through it stays invisible to every position that never stands there — and the system's aggregate self-report is genuinely, defensibly accurate for everyone on level ground. Reports from the affected position cross upward intact but re-type into complaints, because the receiver above has no shared datum to cross-check against and their own correct floor is the reference. Visibility is indexed to a single moving observer: it can be reached only by descending into the specific position, never by better reporting, more authority, or higher vantage.

## Mechanism

The pattern lives in figure-ground: the defect is observer-relative and point-local — only the crosser, standing on the misset landing, registers it — which is exactly why the aggregate self-report is true and useless at once. Honesty of the aggregate and reality of the point-local wound coexist without contradiction. The ledger is not wrong or missing a column; it is right, and the harm is still real.

The distortion runs through no mediator. The converting apparatus IS the receiver's own floor: standing on a correctly-set landing, their body is the reference calibration, so any report of a gap is measured against a self that registers none. A report crossing upward with no shared referent is genre-sorted at intake into "complaint," because complaint is the pre-existing bucket for from-below transmissions the above-floor cannot cross-check. Nothing actively converts — the re-coding is the receiving apparatus itself. **The signal reaches the top fully intact and is re-read, not absorbed: distortion, not loss.**

Verticality is not spatial, hierarchical, or temporal. Two positions are on different floors when neither has stood where the other stands; "above" is simply the position from which the reporter's ground is invisible. The moment grounds overlap — the above-person has once stood below — the invisibility collapses. Visibility from above is reachable only by descent, never by better reporting.

**Habitat.** The pattern lives at crossings — doorways, handoffs, transitions between owned zones — not inside any territory. First-person transit only: it cannot be seen by watching, only by walking. This is why surveillance, dashboards, and audits structurally cannot find it — they are fixed vantages, and the pattern is legible only to a body in motion through the specific join.

**Enforcement geometry.** A self-sealing loop: the very fact that a report originates from a misset landing marks it as unreliable to the floor above — origin-location is read as bias, so the closer the reporter is to the defect, the more their proximity is heard as motive. Accuracy and dismissibility rise together — a competence-to-credibility inversion.

## Failure modes

1. **The symmetric false positive.** A genuinely misset landing and a resentful worker on a correct landing emit the identical signal from above. The pattern has no built-in discriminator between defect-data and grievance, so it can become its own unfalsifiable shield. Valid only where an independent verification of the landing exists that does NOT route through the vertical axis.
2. **Frame-collision, not height.** The conversion is caused not by vertical distance but by two incompatible reference frames meeting at the transit point. "Above" is a proxy for "holds the building's canonical measure." Where height and measurement-authority come apart, height stops predicting the distortion — "invisible from above" is a special case, not the law.
3. **Reporter's fate inverts the object.** If naming-from-below is costless, the pattern is a perception-property. If costly — the reporter is marked, discounted, eventually silent — the invisibility is an active immune response, not passive blindness.

## Falsifier

Loss vs. distortion is resolved in-text: "the signal reaches the top fully intact and is re-read, not absorbed" — distortion, not loss. Height vs. frame-collision is resolved via failure mode 2: the real mechanism is incompatible reference frames; height is the common case where frame-authority correlates with vertical position, not the general law.

## Open edges

- The weaponizable false-positive (failure mode 1) stays carried as an honest limit, not resolved: without an independent, non-vertical verification of the landing, privileging from-below reports as suppressed-data destroys the possibility of a report simply being wrong.
- The cost-regime question is unsettled: whether the pattern is perception or suppression depends entirely on whether the reporter pays a price.
