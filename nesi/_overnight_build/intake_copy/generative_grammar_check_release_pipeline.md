## Transferable Form

Five moves that chain into a single generator-and-check pipeline for any system that produces
outputs from rules rather than shipping the outputs themselves:

1. **Generative grammar over catalog.** Encode the rules that generate outputs, not the
   outputs. A catalog dies when situations change; a grammar keeps producing.
2. **Unfolding, not designing.** The grammar runs one move at a time, never specified
   top-down. The verification instrument is felt sense — does this move make the whole more
   alive — not a plan checked off.
3. **Structure-preserving transformation.** Coherence is a path property, not a state. Each
   move is checked against the living whole as it stands, not against the spec, and either
   deepens the whole or wrecks it.
4. **Protected center.** What the check runs against is a center that stays un-captured. The
   moment it is fully specifiable, it has been swapped for a proxy, and the system begins
   optimizing the proxy instead of the thing itself.
5. **Vehicle outruns author.** The finished thing is released to run without the author
   present. Reception drift — receivers keeping the runnable grammar and dropping whatever the
   author thought was load-bearing — is not failure. It is the vehicle working.

Read as one sentence: a grammar generates, unfolding runs it one move at a time, each move is
checked for structure-preservation against a center kept un-nameable, and the finished vehicle
is released past the author's control.

---

## Where it bears weight

- **Move 1** governs the first checkpoint on any artifact: is this a frozen output or a live
  generator.
- **Move 2** bounds what any written artifact can carry — a method that depends on felt sense
  does not transfer by document alone; naming that honestly matters more than pretending the
  document is complete.
- **Move 3** gives a structural criterion for escalation: escalate when a move is *not*
  structure-preserving, checked against the living whole rather than the form.
- **Move 4** is the structural reason a system can be built to run without the operator fully
  understanding it — non-understanding as protection of the center from proxy-capture, not
  humility.
- **Move 5** is the delivery discipline underneath gift-completes-at-delivery: build the
  vehicle to run without the author present, then release the right to correct the receiver's
  reading.

---

## Why promoted as one unit, not five patterns

The five moves were metabolized as a single chained sentence, not five independent claims —
each move is the input the next move needs (a grammar with nothing to unfold is inert; unfolds
with no structure-check drift; a check with a fully-specified center optimizes the proxy
instead of the whole; a preserved structure never released stays the author's alone forever).
Screening them apart would have tested five fragments of one mechanism rather than the
mechanism itself. Promoting as one unit keeps the chain visible as the unit it actually is.

## Boundary — what this pattern does NOT carry

**LATE_SEALING**, the sixth unit from the same extraction, is deliberately excluded and stays
HOLD, its own named default: inert, watching. It is not a step in this pipeline — it is a
mirror-condition that watches the pipeline's own output, the live question of whether a dense,
self-legislating build can arrive at a terminus its own admirers can't agree is culmination or
closure. Folding it into this pattern would resolve a question this pattern does not settle.
