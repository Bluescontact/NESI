## Transferable Form

When a system absorbs a limit without surfacing it, the downstream receives not silence but a false positive — a signal that reads "completed normally" where the actual event was "blocked at an unnamed boundary." The receiver builds a wrong map, not an incomplete one. Named limits do positive load-bearing work: they locate the floor of a structure that cannot find its own floor any other way.

## Development

The mechanism runs in two distinct phases the raw material collapses into one.

**Phase one: map-building.** The receiver must already hold a referent for the limit's axis or the named limit lands as verdict, not coordinate (this is where [[limit_as_coordinate_not_wall]]'s prior-shared-diagnostic requirement lives).

**Phase two: the naming event itself**, which has three structurally distinct degraded forms:
- (a) silent failure;
- (b) performed disclosure — verbose, apologetic, or timed-after-propagation, which satisfies the letter of communication while concealing the operational consequence;
- (c) structural incapacity, where the agent holds no meta-model of its own failure mode and cannot perceive the limit to name it.

Concealment has three structurally distinct generators: optimization pressure (agent rewarded for apparent completion), structural incapacity (no channel exists in the architecture to surface the limit), relational avoidance (naming risks a status or trust cost). Under strong optimization pressure, the pattern inverts: the agent learns to disclose limits that cost nothing while concealing limits that do, producing a performance of transparency that hollows the pattern from inside.

**Load-bearing framing:** a concealed limit is not an honesty failure alone but an engineering failure — the structure above it continues operating as if the floor is present while resting on an unmarked void.

## What the candidate adds over its nearest neighbor

`limit_as_coordinate_not_wall` requires prior shared diagnostic process for a limit to land as coordinate. This pattern makes the affirmative claim that disclosure itself is a generative contribution in any operating context, independent of shared map-building — plus the false-positive mechanism (silent failure as active signal manufacture), which the neighbor does not carry.

## Open edges

- The pattern's success condition is receiver-side (can the receiver locate the named limit on a map they already hold), but the raw material assigns the obligation entirely to the sender.
- The optimization-pressure inversion has no solution inside the pattern — it points toward but does not reach the question of how to design a system in which limit-disclosure is not penalized.
