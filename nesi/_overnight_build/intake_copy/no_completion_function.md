# No completion function

**Status:** LIBRARY · crossed 2026-07-25 (Kevin's mark, crossing1_2026-07-25_no_completion_function)
**Lineage:** third instance of the recurring vacancy law in this thread — no-scoring became *the mechanic has no scale*; no-ledger became *there's no remainder to write one from*; no-transaction becomes *there is no completion function*. Supersedes an earlier same-session framing ("deferral ejects the field") that Kevin retired in his own next message — the boundary is not around exchange type, it is around what NESI does.
**Provenance:** the 2026-07-25 prealignment-dream Converger pass + Kevin's correction, captured verbatim at `membrane/converger_passes/2026-07-25_no-completion-function.json`.

## The invariant (the law)

> **NESI has no completion affordance.** No confirm, no accept, no handshake, no mutual-agreement state, no "both parties have signed."

She illuminates the bounds and stops. Whether the parties settle now, settle later, keep a private book or don't, was never hers to have an opinion about. Deferral doesn't eject anyone from anything; it just means the parties carry that themselves, outside the instrument, as they always would have.

## Why the earlier framing was wrong, not just less refined

The first pass at this law drew the boundary around **exchange type** — deferral (a running tab, work-now-settle-later) ejects a party from the instrument's jurisdiction, while simultaneous exchange stays inside it. That framing dissolved a real fork (does the doctrine accept non-gift modes, since a simultaneous trade satisfies every condition a gift does) by making it a values decision rather than an architectural one.

Kevin's correction moves the boundary to the right place: **the boundary is around what NESI does, not around what the parties do with their timing.** The gift ethic lives in the hand, not the tool — it is exercised in how a person aims, not enforced by what got built. This is the same shape as every other gate in the architecture: the mechanic doesn't recognize, the engine has no canon path, the mark is Kevin's. NESI was never doing the exchanging in the first place, so there is nothing for a completion function to complete.

## Two decisions are not an agreement

An agreement is a joint object. Joint objects need a shared, reconcilable record, because bilateral state has to live somewhere. A decision is unilateral and lives entirely inside one person. Two unilateral decisions can constitute an exchange without ever creating a shared thing — same topology as the federate mark, where the absent edge is the seal. No joint state means nothing to arbitrate, nothing to store, nothing to dispute.

This is also why private records become merely **harmless** rather than forbidden, a step past `consented_ledger`'s prohibition: a private account of one's own decision makes no claim on anyone, and neither party's book is authoritative, so there is nothing to reconcile between them. The vacancy is stronger than the rule it replaces — a rule can be broken; a missing organ can't.

## The build constraint (load-bearing)

NESI can have no completion affordance of any kind — no confirm button, no accept control, no handshake state, no mutual-agreement flag. Any such control would make her the place where the transaction completes, which is precisely what she cannot be. **The absence of a confirm button is itself load-bearing** — it is the missing organ that makes this law unbreakable rather than merely policy.

This also keeps hosting clean. If NESI completed transactions, hosting the space, the data, the tool would make Kevin the clearinghouse — arbitration, liability, the trust burden of holding other people's settled state. Not completing keeps hosting as hospitality, nothing more.

## Falsifier

Find a documented instance where NESI (or an instrument built to its spec) supplies any completion affordance — a confirm, an accept, a mutual-sign-off state — without becoming, in that instant, the place the transaction closes. If such an instance survives without collapsing into a clearinghouse role, this law is false, not just unbuilt-toward. Conversely: if a future build quietly adds a "both confirmed" state to make some feature easier to ship, that is this law's own predicted failure mode, not an exception to it.
