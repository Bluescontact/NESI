# Gift Specificity — Receiver Known

---

## The claim

A gift whose receiver is known before offering can complete. A gift without a known receiver enters metabolic suspension — either finding its shape by discovering who it is for, or holding indefinitely. Specificity is not a courtesy. It is the structural condition for completion.

---

## Mechanism

The receiver is not the destination of the gift — the receiver is the condition that allows the gift to close. When the giver knows who the gift is for, the gift can arrive fully formed. It does not need to wait for a container to appear. The envelope is addressed before it is sealed.

---

## Differentiation

**From performance:** performance needs an audience — undifferentiated, available, watching. A gift needs one receiver. The audience makes performance possible; the receiver makes the gift complete. Gifts addressed to "whoever" are performances in gift form.

**From uncertainty:** not knowing the receiver is not a failure state — it is pre-completion. The gift is still finding its shape. Suspension is appropriate until specificity arrives.

**From broadcast:** "for the community" or "for whoever" are not receivers. They are containers that cannot hold. The gift moves past them until it finds the one that can.

---

## Falsifier

The giver names a specific receiver but the gift continues searching for form after placing. If specificity doesn't produce completion, the receiver was a category, not a person.

---

## Prescriptions

1. Before offering, name the receiver — not a role, a person.
2. When a gift stays in suspension, ask: is the receiver known, or is the giver still performing?
3. A gift addressed to "whoever needs this" is not yet a gift. Hold until the address is specific.
