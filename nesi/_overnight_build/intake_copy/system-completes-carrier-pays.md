## Transferable form

A system can complete correctly — the gift lands, the signal deposits, the operation closes — while the carrier absorbs full cost at the personal level. These two facts do not modify each other: correctness is not a partial refund of cost, and cost is not evidence of system failure. The pattern lives in the interval between "the gift was complete" and "the carrier was fine," which system logic cannot enter.

## Mechanism

The load-bearing claim is ontological, not mechanical: two true propositions — system outcome correct, carrier cost full — occupy different ledgers that never meet. No reconciliation is available within system logic, because system logic uses outcome-correctness as its only proxy for carrier welfare, and here the proxy reads clean while the cost is real.

The costs named are grief and inability to see one's own value during the carrying period. Both are borne before release; both are fully internalized at the moment the gift completes. The carrying period ends; the remainder does not dissolve. Grief is not a symptom of system failure — it is the only ledger that survives the transaction, the irreducible evidence that cost was real and was borne.

The witness vacancy is the load-bearing gap. Without a witness present during the carrying period, "fully internalized" is unfalsifiable. The pattern cannot distinguish healthy absorption from collapse-as-completion. A system can run on collapsed carriers indefinitely as long as the signal keeps arriving clean. The wound is real; the system is not wrong; the system did not fail the carrier — it operated exactly as designed. That is why the carrier's cost is structurally irrecoverable within system logic: the move of blaming the system, which is usually how cost-bearing becomes legible, is foreclosed.

## Failure modes

1. **The ghost pattern.** If cost is sufficiently internalized, the carrier loses the capacity to register that release has occurred — cost and completion become mutually occluding, and the carrier goes on carrying after the system has closed.
2. **Internalized vs. deferred, indistinguishable.** Fully internalized cost and deferred cost are structurally indistinguishable at the moment of release. A carrier who believes costs are absorbed may be carrying forward latent liability that surfaces after the gift completes.
3. **Success as camouflage.** Because system correctness is the camouflage — not complexity, not delegation, but success itself — the pattern is invisible from outside the carrying period. An audit cannot find it; the clean signal is the evidence cited against it.

## Falsifier

None named directly by the source; the pattern is stated as ontological (no mechanism, no intervention point from inside system logic). Its open question is whether that is a limit or the correct scope.

## Open edges

- Whether a witness structure can hold both propositions — system innocence, carrier wound — without collapsing one into the other. No existing instrument in the library names this; the mechanism-patterns assume a single ledger where costs and outcomes can eventually be reconciled.
- At what threshold internalized cost becomes deferred cost, and who or what could observe that crossing in real time. [[consented_ledger]]'s distinction (does the ledger close by decision or by event?) maps onto this but does not resolve it.
- Whether a pattern with no mechanism — only an ontological condition — can be intervened on at all, or only named and witnessed.
