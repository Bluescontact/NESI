# Veto Retention Owns the Definition

*slug: `veto-ownership-defines-the-pump`*
*Promoted: 2026-07-14 · Source: Substack deletion, batch 2 (39 articles) — full close-out*
*Lineage: new registers on gate_as_f4's conceded mechanism — a naming pass, not a freestanding discovery. Promoted carrying that admission.*

---

## Transferable Form

Wherever one party keeps final rejection power over another's output, the labor of defining what counts as acceptable gets shed onto the party without that power — they define first, and discover the real criteria only when a veto fires against them. The party holding veto pays nothing up front and bears no cost for holding the position; the defining party pays twice — once to produce the definition, once to absorb whatever the fired veto costs. This is not automatic: a veto that sits unexercised is a held option, not yet a toll, and a veto that fires against a standard stated in advance is a different, non-extractive structure.

---

## Development

MECHANISM. Two roles, owner and delegate. Owner sheds defining-labor onto delegate; delegate does the defining. Payment is sequential, not doubled evenly: first payment (defining labor) is certain and priced, lands regardless of outcome. Second payment (absorbing the fired veto) is contingent — occurs only if the veto fires, and its size/nature is set entirely by how the owner exercises it. No risk-premium is named for delegate carrying that tail; the owner pays nothing and keeps everything, which is what makes the structure extractive rather than merely unequal.

METER. The veto-fire event is the only point where the debt becomes visible and gets charged. Nothing in the source specifies its trigger — no threshold, no criterion, disclosed or otherwise. This is the pattern's central unfilled variable: an open, pre-disclosed firing criterion produces a materially different (non-exploitative) structure than a hidden or retroactive one.

PERSON-SLIDE. The source states the pattern in third person (owner/delegate) then restates it in first person ("I keep," "I owe") with no bridge. Left open whether the pump is fractal — identical whether owner/delegate are two people or one person's present veto-holding self versus their own past- or agent-self who did the defining. If fractal, the pattern predicts self-extraction: shedding defining-labor onto a delegate (human or AI) while retaining veto, charging the delegate full cost and oneself none. Unconfirmed, uncollapsed.

REGISTER DELTA — the actual contribution. Three sibling patterns (gate_as_f4, cost_externalized_through_delegation, veto_pattern_drift_detection) all speak diagnostically, from outside, in continuous terms. This pattern shifts register twice: (a) **property** — "owns" recasts an unstated edge as a retained possession, not a gap, opening title-dispute vocabulary (inverted adverse possession: not acting retains the claim, acting forfeits it) for relationships with no formal contract; (b) **confession/prescription** — "if you keep the veto, do the defining" is self-addressed, naming what the holder must do to discharge the debt, where siblings gesture at "a stated discharge condition" without assigning its authorship to the vetoer.

FAILURE MODES. (1) Latent-veto false positive: holding veto without firing it is extraction of an option, not a toll; left unguarded, the pattern over-diagnoses ordinary asymmetric-authority structures (editor/writer, reviewer/coder) whose real cost is uncertainty, not withdrawal. (2) First-person collapse: if the pump describes one person's own owner-function versus execution-function, the interpersonal-exploitation frame (redistribute power, renegotiate) is the wrong remedy for what is actually intrapsychic friction (define before delegating to your future self) — applying the pump's moral charge here manufactures self-blame. (3) Unfalsifiable as stated: no named circumstance where retaining veto does NOT create debt — without that boundary the pattern pattern-matches onto any owner/delegate pair and cannot be distinguished from siblings.

GOVERNOR'S READ, held as floor: this is a naming pass. The contribution is where the mechanism becomes visible and checkable (property register) and where a remedy gets assigned an author (confession register) — not a new mechanism.

## Edges

Firing criterion unstated: whether veto-fire is retroactive/undisclosed (extraction) versus pre-stated (ordinary governance) is the single fact that would most change how the pattern reads, and the source does not supply it.

Fractal question unresolved: two-party exploitation and one-person self-management may be the same structure or two structures requiring opposite remedies (renegotiate power vs. define earlier).

Currently unfalsifiable: no instance is given of a veto firing that did NOT create debt. Locating the boundary requires that counter-case — e.g., a pre-committed acceptance criterion that lets a fired veto land without generating an owed definition.

Remedy untested: "do the defining if you keep the veto" is asserted as the discharge action but never shown to clear the debt in an instance — a prescription, not yet a demonstrated resolution mechanism.

---

## Falsifier

Find one veto that fired against a criterion fully stated before the work began, where the delegate still paid a second, unpriced cost. If such a case exists, "pre-stated criterion" does not discharge the debt and the pattern's own boundary (open criterion = non-extractive) is wrong. Conversely, if the pattern is applied to a held-but-never-fired veto as though the toll were already charged, that application is the latent-veto false positive — the pattern misfiring, not confirming.
