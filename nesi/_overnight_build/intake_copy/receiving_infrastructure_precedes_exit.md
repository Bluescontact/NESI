## The mechanism

A receiving channel is not proven by willingness to accept help or by any declared openness — it is proven only under load, when a public refusal passes through the relation and the relation survives. The aperture for receiving opens not by asking but by refusing where the refusal can be witnessed and fully felt; reception flows toward the visibly sovereign no. Solicitation is not the aperture; witnessed refusal is. A relation that can hold a public no has demonstrated capacity to carry receiving; a relation that has only carried acceptance has demonstrated nothing.

"I would accept help" is unverifiable until it fails or holds under an actual no-and-be-felt event. Without a load-test protocol the pattern is untestable at the only moment it matters — before the crossing — and degenerates into retroactive biography, where every failed exit gets explained after the fact as "the infrastructure wasn't real." The test event, not the intention, is the unit of construction.

The same infrastructure arrives by two opposite vectors. **Forward:** the channel is built from surplus before the crossing, receiving preceding exit. **Downward:** exit comes first and the infrastructure is excavated afterward through a lowered floor — need-compression substituting for reception. These are not one law with exceptions; they are a trade menu whose failure modes mirror each other. The lowered floor can be the failure (receiving never built, floor keeps dropping); the receiving can be the failure (dependence disguised as infrastructure). Which arm is pathological is decided by the case, not the pattern. Any sequencing claim ("receiving precedes exit") holds only in the forward habitat and inverts in the downward one.

For involuntary crossings — eviction, collapse, income loss — the pattern is diagnostic, not prescriptive. It names what is missing, not what to build first, because receiving must be improvised under load when no threshold could be sequenced toward.

## Failure modes

**Willingness masquerading as infrastructure.** A relation untested by refusal has demonstrated nothing, regardless of how much acceptance it has carried. Only the load-test event distinguishes real capacity from declared openness.

**Confusing arrival vectors.** Publishing a sequencing claim without naming which habitat it came from projects one instance as a universal model. What works built-forward can fail found-downward and vice versa.

**Dormant channel.** Existence is not function. A network, listing, or membership roll built but never yet carrying a need is indistinguishable from a live receiving channel by inspection alone — both look built. The only real test: name one need that has already routed through the channel, unsolicited, before the exit. If none can be named, the channel is not proven and the sequencing claim gives false safety regardless of what stands built. This is the load-test protocol (above) applied specifically to channel-existence rather than to relation-quality — a second, narrower instance of the same "test event, not declared intention" requirement.

## Edges

The mechanism requires a witnessing membrane. Tears and a public refusal register as infrastructure only where a village can feel you; in atomized or hostile contexts, the identical act is exposure, not infrastructure. The pattern silently imports a village-container precondition and cannot transfer to isolated, post-exit, or system-container territory — it may fail precisely where it is most needed.

Whether need-compression and receiving-construction are truly substitutable, or one is always the disguised failure mode, is unresolved — the raw material contains both and does not adjudicate.

Whether a witnessing membrane can be assembled post-exit, in the isolated habitat where the mechanism currently cannot reach, is named as a boundary with no bridge offered across it.

The library has no vocabulary yet for act-as-infrastructure — a performed vulnerability functioning as structure rather than event. This pattern opens that register without fully building it out.

Connection to ask-and-receive infrastructure: if the aperture is the witnessed no rather than the routed ask, a commons relay may need to be a witnessing surface for refusals, not a routing surface for requests — unnamed and unbuilt.

**Survivorship caveat.** The forward-habitat sequencing claim ("network → visible offer → exit → scoped re-entry") rests on documented cases — people who exited and stayed exited long enough to write or be written about. Anyone who tried exit-first and failed left no Tauschring, no book, no record; the corpus cannot contain its own counterexamples. The claim therefore carries prudence-heuristic authority, not proof — consultable as "what did the documented cases actually do," never promotable past precedent, because the pattern cannot be tested against the failures it structurally excludes.

**Scoped re-entry — named, unbuilt.** Returning partway into the money system as a designed, bounded move — rather than a collapse — is a state the pattern's own vocabulary (configure_before_demand ends at routing; this file ends at the crossing) has no name for. Without one, the only visible outcomes are "out" and "fell back in," and every future re-entry reads as failure by default. This is flagged as an open question, not a folded pattern member: zero documented cases exist yet of a designed, bounded return. It may earn its own entry once one does.

## Falsifier

If a receiving channel proves reliable without ever having carried a witnessed refusal — if acceptance-only relations hold under real load as well as refusal-tested ones — the load-test claim is false and willingness is sufficient evidence after all. The pattern requires that the untested relation and the refusal-tested relation behave differently under actual load.
