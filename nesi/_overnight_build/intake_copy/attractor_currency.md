# Attractor Currency

*slug: `attractor-currency`*
*Source: trap phenomenology edge in future_image_as_pull.md — developed 2026-06-25. Promoted to canon 2026-06-28.*

---

## Transferable Form

A felt image that was built under one set of conditions continues generating pull after conditions change. The image doesn't alarm when it becomes obsolete — the somatic signal of a live attractor and an obsolete one are identical. Attractor currency is the standing practice of visiting the seam between image and territory before re-confirmation closes it.

---

## Development

The primary diagnostic for whether a felt image is operating as genuine pull is effortfulness: decisions that serve the image feel obvious; decisions that don't feel forced. This instrument is reliable in most cases. It fails in one specific case: when the image has become obsolete but remains somatically active. An obsolete attractor generates genuine pull — following it is effortless — and nothing in the felt sense distinguishes this from a live one.

**The trap structure:**

Conditions shift. The image doesn't register the shift immediately — there's a lag. During the lag, the image continues pulling. First signal: peripheral decisions that previously felt obvious begin to feel slightly off. This is the seam — the earliest visible divergence between image and territory. The seam closes fast: the system moves toward re-confirmation. Slight friction is read as normal variation; the image is reaffirmed; the pull resumes. Once re-confirmed, the trap deepens — the image is now more strongly held than before the friction surfaced.

Attractor currency is the practice of visiting the seam before re-confirmation closes it. Not an alarm (the alarm doesn't exist at the somatic level) but a designed cadence that makes seam-visits regular rather than accidental.

**The two checks:**

*Direction check* — what is the felt texture of the last three decisions in this domain? If they felt obvious and aligned, the image is live. If any felt forced, muted, or slightly off — not "wrong" but "hm" — that's the peripheral signal. The check doesn't ask "is my image right?" (too cognitive, too destabilizing to the pull) but "what did my body do on the last few decisions in this direction?"

*Territory check* — the image has a sensory texture: what the future feels like to inhabit. The territory check asks: does where I actually am feel like it's in the direction of that texture? Not "have I arrived?" but "is the felt quality of my current ground moving toward or away from the felt quality of the image?" This is a texture comparison, not a location check.

**What determines update speed:**

Some images update when conditions change; others calcify and pull as obsolete attractors. The difference isn't time or importance — it's holding mode.

An image held as *hypothesis* ("this is where I think I'm going; I'll know more as I move") updates when the territory doesn't match. An image held as *commitment* ("this is where I'm going; I've organized around it") raises the update cost — updating means acknowledging the organization was premature. An image held as *identity* ("this is who I am going") resists update as threat: the image's obsolescence would mean the self's obsolescence.

Hypothesis → commitment → identity is a holding-mode ladder. Each rung raises the update cost. The trap is most severe when the image has climbed to identity: the body's somatic stake in the image's currency is now indistinguishable from the somatic stake in self-continuity. See [[holding_mode_ladder]] for the full mechanism.

The practical implication: image formation includes naming the holding mode and naming revision conditions at build time — "I'm moving toward X; I'll update this if Y changes." A revision condition built into the image at formation maintains porosity without requiring constant monitoring.

**The Governor parallel:**

The Governor is the brake on cost overrun — it asks "still paying?" on a DAILY/CYCLE cadence. Attractor currency is the brake on direction overrun — it asks "still approaching?" on the same cadence. The two together bracket the runaway from both sides: cost overrun and direction overrun. Neither check is designed to stop movement. They're designed to surface the seam before re-confirmation closes it.

**Where this is already running:**

The Heartland tetra read — "the plan is more finished than the ground, close the lid, land first" — was attractor currency running. The territory (actual ground) didn't match the texture of the image (the finished plan). The seam surfaced before it could be re-confirmed.

The OSG "tools not pages" move: an image of how a visitor uses the site reorganized all design decisions and remained live because it was built as a direction (felt texture of use) not a destination (specific page). Direction images have natural porosity; destination images calcify faster.

## The Move

At CYCLE cadence: run the direction check (what did my body do on the last few decisions in this domain?) and the territory check (does where I am feel like it's moving toward the image's texture?). If either returns friction — not "wrong," just "hm" — hold the seam open rather than re-confirming. The seam is the only diagnostic moment; re-confirmation closes it.

At image formation: name the holding mode (hypothesis, commitment, or identity) and name at least one revision condition. The revision condition is the porosity mechanism — it keeps the image updateable without requiring the currency check to catch every drift.

## Edges

The check cadence question: how often is too often? A currency check run too frequently destabilizes the pull — the image can't generate effortless movement if it's being questioned constantly. The Governor's DAILY/CYCLE cadence emerged from the cost instrument's natural signal frequency. The attractor instrument may need a slower cadence, perhaps CYCLE only. What determines the right frequency is unknown.

The identity-held image: for images that have climbed to identity, the currency check may generate defensive re-confirmation rather than genuine seam-visit. The peripheral friction will be read as threat. The check requires minimum holding-mode flexibility — capacity to receive friction as information rather than danger. Without it, the instrument backfires.

Open question: is there a detectable signature for when an image has climbed from commitment to identity? That signature would be more valuable than the currency check itself — it would flag the case where the check will fail.

The compound case: [[translated_origin_failure_mode]] and obsolete attractor running simultaneously. Translated origin corrupts where you're reading from; obsolete attractor corrupts where you're moving toward. The currency check addresses the second. If both are running, the direction check may be run from a corrupted zero, returning a false reading even when the image is genuinely obsolete. The two-instrument panel (Governor + currency) doesn't resolve this — it may surface the compound case by returning contradictory readings, which is itself a signal.

---

## Falsifier

Find a system that performs regular attractor currency checks and still falls into the obsolete-attractor trap. If the checks are running but the seam doesn't surface, identify which failure applies: check cadence too slow, check run cognitively rather than somatically, or image climbed to identity and is generating defensive re-confirmation. Each failure mode requires a different response. If none of these apply and the trap still runs, the currency check is insufficient as designed.
