## The four faces

### THE GIFT
The player-facing game itself — The Warm One. Five moves played with pieces
of a person's own life: empty your pockets, feel for the warm one, pull the
thread, hand it over or keep it, let go.

**Sourced from:** `nesi/NESI.md §FACE 1 (lines 36-55)`, `nesi/warm_one_card.html`, `nesi/warm_one_walkthrough.html`

**Patterns most directly supporting this face** (gift-completion mechanics, receiver-side conditions, single-round felt-seeing):
- `completion_through_placing.md` — a gift completes at the placing, not at reception or effect
- `gift_specificity_receiver_known.md` — a gift needs a known receiver or it enters metabolic suspension
- `gift_preconditions_extraction_vs_reception.md` — the giver-side precondition gate
- `boundary_ask.md` — the receiver's refusal-capacity as precondition for legibility
- `the_recognition_law.md` — "the mechanic never does the recognizing"; four surfacing mechanics named
- `instrument_witnessing_a_gift_without_merging.md` — the four-check receiver-side discipline
- `place_it_and_close_your_own_record.md` — complete at the placing, close your own record

### THE ENGINE
The load-bearing machinery that makes recognition run without ever becoming
a transaction — the recognition law, the geometric foundation, the boundary
contract, the ledger/gate, board.html as entry point.

**Sourced from:** `nesi/NESI.md §FACE 2 (lines 58-77)`, `patterns/the_recognition_law.md`, `nesi/spec/boundary.md`, `nesi/board.html`

**Patterns most directly supporting this face** (geometry, gate mechanics, verification, tetra-vertex protocols):
- `tetrahedral_agent_protocol.md` — the generic four-vertex/six-edge development engine
- `the_governor.md` — the Governor vertex made standing; cost-intake discriminator
- `setting_the_floor_container_minimum.md` — the explicit 4-vertex/6-edge/4-face/1-volume container, named convergent with the tetra-body and position-board
- `gate_as_f4.md` — definition + held control = gate; undefined + delegated = pump
- `consented_ledger.md` — every generated cost circulated, felt/refused, or consented-to-leak
- `route_every_cost_to_a_named_landing.md` — the 16-pattern organ behind the ledger
- `structural_incapacity_over_prohibition.md` — the strongest rule makes harm unrepresentable, not forbidden
- `_folded/catalysis_without_claim.card.md` — the 4-vertex/6-edge Recognition Infrastructure tetrahedron (vertex map incomplete on disk)

### THE INFRASTRUCTURE
How a recognized gift moves between people without becoming a debt — the
Held Refusal / the Field: the four-edge refusal map, the single-binary door,
gift/mutual-practice/exchange never blended.

**Sourced from:** `nesi/NESI.md §FACE 3 (lines 81-100)`, `held_refusal/index.html`, `held_refusal/DOCTRINE.md`

**Patterns most directly supporting this face** (circulation, refusal-as-write, non-transactional return, doctrine boundaries):
- `surface-without-no.md` / `surface_without_no.md` — refusal as a write operation (NO writes to a SINK, DEFER writes to a SOURCE)
- `the_door_as_stack_layer.md` — NESI (machine) → THE DOOR (membrane, three-part ask) → the public face, as three distinct layers
- `precesse.md` — non-transactional return arrives orthogonal to the giving act, by geometry not surprise
- `asymmetric_valve_pricing_refused_reception_open.md` / `valuation_refused_reception_held_open.md` — outbound pricing refused, inbound reception held open
- `no_completion_function.md` — NESI has no confirm/handshake affordance; the absence is load-bearing
- `stigmergic_deposit_practice.md` — deposits complete at release; the field routes without a director
- `refuse_where_it_can_be_seen.md` / `the_silent_close.md` — how a no is placed into the shared channel and then left alone

### THE SELF (Heartwood) — derived
What a person's own recognitions add up to across every round, over time —
the accreted self, rendered as growth rings. Built already (`heartwood.py`,
called live from `board.html`), but never named as its own face until
derived: capacity is the one recognition-law mechanic (worth, value, skill,
capacity) the other three known faces jointly leave unclaimed.

**Patterns most directly supporting this face** (accretion, self-witnessing over time, capacity as the unclaimed mechanic):
- `same_quartet_assembly_standing.md` — NESI as tensegrity, four members held simultaneously; the assembly-order/standing-structure recursion this derivation itself follows at a second scale
- `read_witnessed_by_stake.md` — a felt-read is validated only by what it costs across time, not by a second in-frame read
- `origin_vs_origin_maintenance.md` — maintenance disciplines that hold a generative zero face inward and accrete no inheritors, by design
- `metabolic_lag_on_complete_giving.md` — the giver's own registry closing (or failing to close) a discharged gift
- `stake_the_read.md` — a read staked so its accretion is real, not merely felt
