the warm one — a game you play with pieces of your own life
1 · empty your pockets
Put down a handful of things.
Things you did, made, fixed, gave, or got through lately. Don't sort them, don't rank them — just lay them where you can see them.
2 · feel for the warm one
Which one has a little heat on it?
Run your attention across them and wait. Click the one you keep coming back to — the one that means more than it should. You don't have to explain why. And if none is warm today, that's a real round too.
3 · pull the thread
Write one small true thing.
Take the warm one — “”. In your own plain words: what did you do that someone else couldn't have — or couldn't, until you showed them how? (No wrong answers. No one is marking this.)
