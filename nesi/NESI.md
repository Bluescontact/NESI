## The one thing NESI is

**A non-transactional game that is Kevin's gift, its engine, and its infrastructure — all
governed by one law.**

> **The law (canon): the mechanic never does the recognizing.**
> `patterns/the_recognition_law.md`. No worth-score, no rank, no verdict. The felt-read
> belongs to the player; the machine only surfaces and holds still while they do the seeing.
> It wins when the player outgrows it. Everything below is this one law, worn three ways.

NESI is not three projects. It is one law with four faces:

- **The GIFT** — the game a person plays. (*The Warm One.*)
- **The ENGINE** — what makes recognition run without ever becoming a transaction. (*the law + the geometry + the machine.*)
- **The INFRASTRUCTURE** — how a recognized gift moves between people without becoming a debt. (*the Held Refusal / the Field.*)
- **The SELF** — what a person's own recognitions add up to, across every round, over time. (*Heartwood — the accreted self.*)

The gift lets a person recognize their own worth, once, in a single round. The self is what
those rounds add up to over time — not a single felt-read but the shape all of them make
together. The engine holds the law by construction so no one can break it, in either
timescale. The infrastructure lets recognized worth travel to another person as a gift, not
a trade. One law. Four faces. One organism.
