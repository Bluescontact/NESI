## §7d · WATER IS THE OPERATING MEDIUM (Kevin's mark, 2026-08-02 — AMENDMENT, adopted)

His words: *"Kevin wants to be able to operate up stream... The visual representation of that is a dam that restricts flow and generates power when it releases water. We use water as the operating medium throughout the world itself."*

**The law:** water is not scenery, not one place among seven, and not a theme. It is the medium every organ acts *in*. Every mechanic in the world is something being done to water — restricting it, releasing it, drawing from it, returning to it, letting it carry something, letting it wash something clean.

**THE DAM — operating upstream, made standable.** Upstream position is not an abstraction or a preference; it is a place in the world. A dam restricts flow, accumulates head, and generates power **at the moment of release** — never at the moment of restriction. Three consequences, stated so they cannot be softened later:

1. **Holding is not producing.** A dam that never releases generates nothing, however much it holds. This is the compost/outflow distinction already standing in the position board, now with a physical body.
2. **Power comes from the drop, not the water.** The same volume released at no height does no work. What generates is the *difference in position* — which is what "operating upstream" means and why it is worth the walk.
3. **Restriction is legitimate and visible.** A gate held closed is a normal state of the world, not a failure or a backlog. Open gates in `OPEN_GATES.jsonl` have a physical face here: held water, not unpaid debt.

**What this reconciles, already on disk:** the lake (SOIL / the Deep), the deep-water refusal, the vortex (THE PULSE — the lake's one motion), NESI mounted in the water, the fog→rain→wash→reliefs cycle of §7c, and stones washing on the Shore were all built as separate features. This amendment names them one system: a water cycle with a single medium. Nothing built is invalidated; what changes is that new mechanics must now answer *what does this do to the water*.

**Honest edge:** the dam does not exist in `nesi/world3d/` yet. This is the naming; the build is separate and unpriced.

## §7e · THE WORLD IS A TETRAHEDRON (Kevin's mark, 2026-08-02 — AMENDMENT, adopted)

His words: *"the world world is a tatrahedron itself. The character can moving in 3d. The entry point is the apex of the tetra... The physical world is the opposite face. The world is procedurally generated each time the lake is procedurally updated every 1000 words into the intake. The lake is the center of the map. I'm imagining being able to rotate a vertex into the apex to shift the expression of the ground. kind of like a spirit mode, or stacked multi dimension."*

**The geometry, as law:**

- **The world itself is a tetrahedron.** Not a tetra-shaped menu inside the world (that is the `[Q]` surface, a different object) — the world's own form. Movement inside it stays fully 3D; the tetrahedron is the container, not the controls.
- **The apex is the entry point.** One vertex is always uppermost, and that vertex is where you come in.
- **The opposite face is the physical world.** The base — the face furthest from entry — is the ground you actually walk. Entering is a descent from a point toward a plane.
- **The lake is the center of the map.** Not one place among seven: the center. This confirms §7d — the medium sits at the middle of the geometry, and every place stands in relation to it.
- **The lake updates every 1000 words into the intake, and the world regenerates on that update.** Writing is the clock. The terrain answers to accumulated words, not to elapsed time and not to decisions. (This supersedes the built world's current seed, which derives terrain from the `MARKS_LOG` line count — a decision-count, not a word-count. Named as a supersession, not smoothed: the built behavior and the named law now differ, and the build follows the law.)
- **Rotating a vertex into the apex re-expresses the ground.** Four vertices, so four apex states. Same coordinates, same world, different expression — *"a spirit mode, or stacked multi dimension."* Stacked, not swapped: the other expressions do not stop existing when one is uppermost.

**What this resolves, and what it does not.** The seven-organs-vs-six-edge-tetra frame question (flagged open in §6 amendment 1) is **not** closed by this. That tension was about organs mapping to tetra edges. This amendment is about the *world's* form, which is a separate object from the organ map — a tetrahedron world can hold seven organs without any of them being an edge. The frame question stays open, and is now visibly a different question than it looked like on 7-31.

**Honest edge:** nothing here is built. The tetra on `[Q]` is a decision surface inside the world, not the world's geometry. The terrain seed is still marks-count. This is a naming, adopted; the build that follows it is separate and unpriced.
