## 1. The opening is at the apex — the world inverts

> "The three spires connect the edges of the world to the apex of the tetra where a user
> stands in the threshold."

> "The game opens in this upper space in the world."

> "Water comes from this place, and flows down each spire. a round space with a glass
> bottom."

> "Each spire is an edge of the whole world tetra. The control/ writing room at the top of
> the world, and the 3 ways of generating water."

> "Each spire has a dam... the outputs send water into the world below. The dams have their
> own game and interface embedded within."

**What this changes against what is on the ground now:** the built world (world3d) is
terrain-first — the player spawns on the ground, the river was sited on the terrain, the
dam has no site. This says the *spawn is the apex*, the glass-bottomed round room, and the
ground is what you look down into and descend to. Three spires = three edges = three ways
of generating water. A dam on each spire, each dam its own embedded minigame/interface.

**Already on disk, unsited:** `nesi/spec/THE_RIVER_AND_THE_DAM_2026-08-05.md`,
`scripts/dam.gd`, `scripts/sweep_dam.gd`. The open gate "THE DAM HAS NO SITE" (08-05T19:45)
gets an answer here: *the dams are on the spires, not on the terrain river.*

> "A Tetra is a held world. One generated from everything everywhere in the system... and as

> "I might start a new world.. and fill it with lots of reference material on a subject, and

## 5. The workshop — tension membrane as a routing computer

> "The workshop, and tool space/ board... i'm thinking about tension mechanics, and water
> routing, and having a tensioned membrane as a routing computer that users could combine
> both light, lens and heliostat dynamics as a tactile user modified surface that directs an
> AI through a build process."

> "I'm thinking of tools and ways i've built things... the templates, and the patterns for
> how i set up win win win arrangements through value first."

The membrane is not a metaphor here — it is **the AI control surface**. Kevin deforms a
tensioned surface; light and lens and heliostat combine on it; the deformation is what
directs an AI through a build. This is the tactile version of the position-board and the
tetra menu — steering by position, never by articulation
(`feedback_steering_without_articulation`).

> "That mountain is the soil that contains every articulation for what the workshop needs to
> be able to do and function as."

The mountain under the workshop = the accumulated articulation. Soil, not scenery.
