# THE WATERSHED BUILDERS AGREEMENT — v0

**Status: DRAFT v0, for Kevin's clause-by-clause mark. Nothing here is standing.** Kevin, 2026-08-04: *"Lets start building a builders agreement."* This is the start of it, not the thing.

**What it is for.** The Tetra Builders Agreement (signed 2026-08-01) governs **how a build pass runs** — scope, laws, proof, authority. It is not replaced and not amended here. This agreement governs a different question that tonight made urgent: **how a naming becomes a build, and in what order.**

**Why it is needed, in one line.** Kevin's read: *"the past nesi builds have been scattershot deviations trying to wrangle the ai into alignment.. it hasn't been a whole ecosystem in convergence, and structural alignment."* The namings have been converging. The builds have been deviating around them. **This is the agreement that puts the second in service of the first.**

**Shape.** Nine clauses and one slot left open, following the precedent of the Tetra agreement's UNNAMED. Each clause is stated as a commitment — what a builder *does* — rather than as a prohibition, wherever the thing can honestly be said that way.

---

## C1 · THE NAMING COMES FIRST

**A thing is named before it is built. The naming is Kevin's.**

A builder who finds an unnamed thing in the way of a build **names the gap and stops there**, offering the naming rather than filling it.

*Provenance: taught 2026-08-04, when the AI answered "there is no build, only lint" by building `nesi/THE_DOOR.html` and was stopped. The instruction was exact — "you must dream and name the object... not yet building... naming."*

*The dreaming itself is machine labor and always available: a builder may dream freely, propose whole namings, and put them face-up. What it may not do is build one.*

---

## C2 · A BUILD FOLLOWS ITS NAMING, OR IT FLAGS AND HALTS

**Where the build and the naming disagree, the disagreement is recorded the day it appears and the build does not proceed on the deviation.**

A deviation is not a small thing to be tidied later. **It creates a second, competing account of what the thing is** — and two accounts is exactly how an ecosystem stops converging.

*Two are already on the record and both are live:*
- *the world's terrain derives from a mark count while §7e names a word count*
- *the tetra was built as a decision surface while §7e names the tetrahedron as the world's own form*

*Both were recorded rather than smoothed, which was right. Neither has been resolved, which is the cost.*

---

## C3 · THE BUILD ORDER FOLLOWS THE WATER

**Upstream before downstream. A place is built after the water that reaches it.**

The Headwaters and the Draw before the Shoals. The Shoals before the Stacks. The Stacks before the Lock. **You cannot build a lock for water that has not arrived**, and every attempt to build a downstream place first has produced a beautiful thing with nothing flowing through it.

*This clause is the one with teeth against the record. The world was built from the terrain down and the result is a gate open since 08-02: the world is built and unvisited. The upstream end — the writing that fills it — was never built at all.*

*Consequence, stated plainly: under this clause the next build is the Headwaters and the Draw. Nothing else is upstream of them.*

---

## C4 · A PLACE IS FINISHED WHEN IT NEEDS NO SENTENCE

**A place ships when a person who has never seen it can act in it. If it needs a line of explanation, it is not finished — the place is, not the documentation.**

This is a build standard and it is checkable: hand it to someone, say nothing, and watch.

*Provenance: "nothing that lets a person play this game without needing a tutorial." The whole medium was chosen to satisfy this — everyone already knows what deep means, what a gate does, what happens when a boat has too little water under it.*

---

## C5 · THE BUILD REMOVES THE ABILITY RATHER THAN ADDING A CHECK

**Where a law says the system must not do a thing, the build makes it unable to.**

A guard that is restraint needs a checker, a reviewer, and someone remembering. A guard that is incapacity holds because of how the thing is made.

*This is already the strongest pattern in the naming and it was arrived at three separate times:*
- *the system cannot point at a puzzle, because **it has no line** and takes no soundings*
- *the Range cannot recommend, because **in line or not is its entire vocabulary***
- *nothing crosses the membrane unilaterally, because **the far gate opens from the other side***

*Where a build can only manage restraint, it says so in its report rather than claiming the stronger guard.*

---

## C6 · WHAT MUST SURVIVE THE SESSION IS WRITTEN BEFORE IT IS SHOWN

**Anything offered for a decision goes to a durable ledger before the surface showing it exists.**

*Already built and enforced tonight: `DECISIONS_OFFERED.jsonl` and `tools/decisions.py`, with ordering checked against file mtime rather than trusted. This clause makes the existing mechanism binding on every future surface rather than on the ones that remember.*

*Provenance: five decisions stranded in widget files on 07-30, and eight candidate sentences lost on 08-02 because they existed only in a transcript.*

---

## C7 · NOTHING IS ADDED THAT ASKS A PERSON TO COME BACK

**The build ships no streak, score, reminder, unlock, notification, completion percentage, or acknowledgement.**

The system will never contact anyone. Its only claim on attention is that it is a good place to be.

*This clause is aimed at craft, not at obvious mechanics. Almost every technique that makes a thing feel good was developed in service of retention, and some carry the contract inside them structurally: a satisfying chime **is** an achievement, a gently rising line **is** a score. The test is not whether it feels transactional but **whether the machine is doing the recognizing.***

---

## C8 · THE BUILDER SPLITS THE TWO CLAIMS AND NEVER MAKES THE SECOND

**"The mechanism works" is verifiable and statable. "This does what you needed" is only ever Kevin's.**

Every report states what actually ran, what did not, and where verification stopped. *Carried whole from the Definition of Done and the Tetra agreement's V3/V4; restated here because it is the clause most likely to erode under enthusiasm.*

---

## C9 · A BUILD THAT WOULD HAVE HAPPENED ANYWAY IS CEREMONY

**This agreement earns its place the first time it stops a build, reorders one, or forces a naming that would otherwise have been invented.**

If a year of passes would each have run identically without it, it was decoration and should be cut rather than amended.

*Applied honestly to tonight: **C1 and C3 would both have fired.** C1 would have stopped `THE_DOOR.html` before it was written. C3 would have put the Headwaters ahead of the terrain months ago.*

---

## C10 · UNNAMED

**Held open, on the precedent of the Tetra agreement.** A tenth clause will be needed and it is not yet known which. The slot stays visible so the agreement does not read as complete before it is.

*My own candidate, offered and not claimed: something about how a second person enters. Every clause above is written for one builder and one keeper, and the Range needs two, the Lock needs someone on the far side, and the Given Stack is made of what somebody else carried in. The agreement has no account of that and it is the largest thing missing.*

---

## HOW IT IS INVOKED

If signed, one line ends every build manifest, beside the Tetra line:

> *"This pass runs under the Watershed Builders Agreement, all clauses."*

**The two agreements do not overlap.** Tetra governs how a pass runs once it has a go. This governs whether the pass should exist yet, and in what order. A pass carries both.

---

## WHAT I WOULD CHANGE IF I WERE MARKING THIS

Stated so the draft is not presented as better than it is:

- **C3 is the strongest and the most expensive clause.** It says, in effect, that most of the world3d build was out of order. That is a real claim about work already done and it deserves to be argued rather than nodded through.
- **C7 is the hardest to verify.** There is no test that catches a satisfying chime; it needs a felt-read, which means it is not really a builder's clause at all — it is a Kevin clause wearing a builder's clothes.
- **C5 may be too strong.** Some laws genuinely cannot be made into incapacity, and a clause that demands the impossible gets quietly ignored, which is worse than a weaker clause that holds.
