## 2 · THE DAM — the mechanism, as it follows from the words

A dam holds water back so it rises. That is the whole reason the thresholds can exist: without something holding, water written into a valley runs out and the level never climbs.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | somewhere in the mountains — the mountains being, per THE WHOLE NAMING, the writing-terrain's home |
| **What it holds** | the day's writing, as it arrives |
| **What rises behind it** | the reservoir — and the reservoir's height is the four thresholds |
| **What it releases** | the river, downstream into the world |

**The dam never decides anything.** It holds and it releases. It does not read, score, summarise, or judge what is in the water — the same guard the morning-pages channel already carries, and the same reason D6 responds to volume only.

▢ **open:** whether the dam can be opened by hand, or only fills and spills on its own.

## 3 · THE RIVER — what carries

The river runs from the dam down through the terrain to the lake. It is the **path deposit takes**, made visible: sentences enter upstream, are carried, and settle where the ground lets them.

This is not new mechanics. §D5 already seeds particles upstream and lets them run down into the world; `THE_WORLD.html` already does exactly this and it was verified running. **The river is the name for what was already happening, plus a channel to happen in.**

▢ **open:** whether the river's course is derived from the grain (valleys already cut along bedding) or is its own feature.

## 4 · BORN NEW EACH DAY — the two sources

> *"the world is born new each day.... it's built from the deep waters of what came before and was composted, and from the daily weather."*

**Two inputs, and only two.**

**THE DEEP WATER — what came before and was composted.** Not everything that was ever written. What was *composted* — metabolised, let go of, sunk. It is the reservoir under the reservoir: the world's inherited depth, there before today's first word. This is why a day never starts from nothing.

**THE DAILY WEATHER — today.** Fog off the lake, rain, wash. Already built (`weather.gd`, §D9), already internal and recirculating and NESI's jurisdiction. **She can never make it rain on the catchment**; new material arrives only by writing. The weather works what is already there; the writing brings what is new.

**What "born new" does and does not mean.** The world is *generated* fresh each day from those two sources — not *erased* each day. Deep water is precisely the part that persists. The composted material is the ground the new day stands on.

▢ **open, and load-bearing:** what exactly *composted* points at on disk. `patterns/_folded/` exists and folding is not erosion. The compost pile is 162 files and the container work found that "compost" is three unrelated jobs wearing one name. **A day that is born from compost cannot be built until compost is one thing.**

| Words | Kevin's words | What that is |
|---|---|---|
| **200** | *"opens up a limited world"* | you are somewhere. Small, real, walkable — not a menu. |

**The shape of it, stated once:** the world does not ask you to write. It is simply smaller until you do. Nothing is withheld as a reward and nothing is dangled — the water is low, so less is above it. That is a fact about water, not an incentive.

**And it is volume only.** Two hundred words about nothing opens exactly as much as two hundred words about everything. Under k6 — marked today — the world may never respond to what the words say.

▢ **open:** whether the thresholds are per-day or cumulative. *(Under "born new each day" they read as per-day; under the joined store the count is cumulative. This is the first real tension between the two, and it is Kevin's.)*

## 6 · THE TWO INTAKES — and the quarry

> *"Two intakes on for copy and past external... That comes in as quarried rock that gets broken into gravel."*

**Intake one is the pen** — what you write, here, now. It arrives as water and settles as deposit.

**Intake two is external material** — pasted in from elsewhere. **It does not arrive as water.** It arrives as **quarried rock**: cut somewhere else, by someone else, and carried here. Then it is **broken into gravel** before it can join the ground.

This is the sharpest distinction in the naming and it is doing real work:

- **Your writing is water.** It carries, it settles, it finds its own level.
- **External material is rock.** It was already shaped when it arrived. It cannot settle until it is broken down.
- **Gravel is what rock becomes when it can finally move with the river.** Broken, not dissolved — it keeps its edges.

The guard this builds in structurally, without any rule being written: **material from outside cannot enter the ground whole.** It has to be broken first. Nothing pasted in ever becomes bedrock by arriving.

▢ **open:** what "broken into gravel" is, mechanically — who or what does the breaking, and whether it happens on arrival or over time.
▢ **open:** whether gravel can ever become bedrock, or is permanently a different material.

## 8 · FALSIFIERS

- **If the world ever opens further for better writing rather than more of it**, k6 is broken and the thresholds became a judge.
