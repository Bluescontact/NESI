## 4 · THE ONE THING THAT WOULD NOT COME

**The dam has no site.** Not at any of 25 throat definitions. Not under any of 20 incision laws. Every combination returned **exactly three eligible steps** — a number that will not move under twenty different grounds is not measuring the ground.

The diagnosis, offered and unmarked: the incision is computed from flow over the whole map; the course is steepest descent from one summit. They are two different derivations, so the river is not required to run in the channels the water cut. **The fix is to make the course follow the accumulation, not to loosen a threshold** — and the difference between those two is the whole ruling.

Kevin's ruling said the dam sits where the grain makes a throat. It does not say what to do when there is none, and inventing one would be placing it by hand. **Nothing was placed.** The measurement prints every time the world loads.
