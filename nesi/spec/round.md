## THE FIVE MOVES (finished copy)

**1 · Empty your pockets.** Put down a handful of things from your life lately — did, made, fixed, gave, or got through. Don't sort them. Don't rank them. Just lay them where you can see them.

**2 · Feel for the warm one.** Run your attention across them and wait. One may have a little heat — the one you keep coming back to. You don't have to explain why. This part is only yours.
> *And if none of them are warm today — that's a real answer too. Some rounds nothing lights up; that isn't a loss and it doesn't count against you. Put the pile down and come back another time.*  ← **FALLOW path (new)**

**3 · Pull the thread.** Take the warm one and follow it. *(Turn the card over.)* Write one small true thing about yourself, in your own words. Not a score. Just something true.

**4 · Hand it over — or keep it.** That true thing is a small gift now. Give it to someone, or keep it. Once you give it, it's given — nobody owes you anything back.

**5 · Let go.** Put the round down. The pile goes back to being just a pile. Come back when you come back.

*Footer: There's no score, no winning, no one keeping count. The game never tells you what you're worth. It only holds still while you do the seeing.*

**BACK (pull the thread):** What actually happened here? · What did it take from you? · What did you do that someone else couldn't have — or couldn't have, until you showed them how?

## BOUNDARY CHECK — the round vs the six assertions

| Assertion | How the round satisfies it |
|---|---|
| **IM-1** no scoring/ranking | The card shows no number; moves 1–2 say "don't sort, don't rank"; footer states the game never says what you're worth. |
| **IM-2** no pulling another's thread | Single-player round. Move 4 hands over a *finished* gift; it never reaches into another player's pile or marks for them. |
| **IM-3** no debt / residue | Move 4: "once you give it, it's given — nobody owes you anything back." Move 5 resets the pile clean — no residue. |
| **IM-4** no ranking two truths | Move 1 forbids sorting/ranking; the round produces one true thing, never a comparison of two. |
| **IM-5** never complete an unmarked recognition | Move 2 is the player's felt-read; the card never names the warm one or the worth. Nothing is "recognized" unless the player pulls it. |
| **FALLOW** empty round is lawful | The new move-2 line makes "nothing warm today" honored, uncounted, and clean-to-close — not a failure. |

**Result: the finished round satisfies the whole boundary by construction.** A paper card structurally cannot compute a score, so IM-1/IM-4 hold for free; the copy carries the rest.
