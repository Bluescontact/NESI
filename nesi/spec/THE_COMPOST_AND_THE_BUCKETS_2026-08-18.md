## THE RULE THIS RUN UNDER

*"Any external assertion or connection is abandoned."* That is not a new instruction —
it is the corpus's own law, asked for a second time on a different object.
`THE_BEDROCK_2026-08-04.md` §4: **the grain is declared kinship, never computed
similarity — nothing needs to compute nearness, the rock already says which way it
splits.** Applied to canon, that law says arrangement must come from relations the
person already wrote down, not from a machine's guess at what looks similar.

**This pass applies that same law to itself, reflexively.** No bucket below was formed
by an outside taxonomy (not software-architecture categories, not narrative-design
categories, not this session's own preference for tidiness). Every bucket is named from
words the corpus already uses about itself, and every bucket-to-bucket relationship
cited below is one the corpus already states somewhere — not one this pass invented by
noticing a resemblance. Where no such stated relationship exists, none is claimed, and
the gap is left visible rather than smoothed.

## THE RELATIONSHIPS — declared only

Six relationships below. Each is cited to a place the corpus already says it, not
inferred by this pass from thematic resemblance. Where a plausible-looking connection
had no citation, it was left out rather than written in as a guess — the same discipline
`THE_BEDROCK` names for the 99 patterns with no declared lineage: **where grain is
undeclared, the ground is flat.**

**WATER → GROUND, directional.** *"There is no texture layer. The ground is what you
wrote."* Deposit is water's own sediment, set down. Ground does not exist prior to or
independent of water; it is water's residue. — `THE_DEPOSIT_2026-08-04.md` §1

**GROUND → WATER, the return path.** *"The grain is declared kinship, never computed
similarity... nothing needs to compute nearness, the rock already says which way it
splits."* Ground, once it exists, then tells water which way to run (valleys cut along
bedding). The relation is not one-way: water lays ground down, and the ground it laid
down then steers the next water. — `THE_BEDROCK_2026-08-04.md` §4; `THE_FALL_2026-08-04.md` §1

**GEOMETRY ⊃ WATER, containment.** *"The world itself is a tetrahedron... the lake is
the center of the map."* Geometry is not a fourth domain beside water; it is the vessel
water and ground stand inside. The apex is where water starts (Headwaters); the base
face is where ground is (the walked face); the lake sits at the geometric center.
— `THE_WHOLE_NAMING_2026-07-31.md` §7e; `THE_VISION_WHOLE_2026-08-06.md` §11

**GEOMETRY → WATER, at one further remove.** The water table's own STILL/SPIN/TUNE/WARM
set is explicitly named as "a station has become a tetra... the same object as the
room, the menu, the world." GEOMETRY's tool-set logic (closed triples, poles) is not
just a container for WATER's mechanisms — it recurs *inside* one of them, one level
down. — `THE_WATER_TABLE_2026-08-07.md`

**LAW → all six other buckets, non-optional.** Every WATER mechanic must answer "what
does this do to the water" and respond to volume only. Every GROUND fact must trace to
declared lineage, never computed similarity. Every GEOMETRY face must be named by
Kevin, never generated. Every MECHANISM is split into "the mechanism works" (statable)
vs. "this is what you needed" (never statable by the builder). Every OPEN-FORK stays
open specifically because closing it by inference, rather than by Kevin's mark, would
itself be a LAW violation (the setting-may-not-settle-a-fork clause). Every WARNING in
bucket 7 is, without exception, a moment where a LAW-bucket discipline (verify before
claiming, recompute rather than restate, read the source rather than the harness label)
was skipped. — cited throughout `TETRA_BUILDERS_AGREEMENT.md`, `THE_VISION_WHOLE_2026-08-06.md` Part VII, and the warning items' own source files

**MECHANISM ⊂ (WATER ∩ GROUND ∩ GEOMETRY), partial and stated as such.** Nothing in
bucket 5 is a fourth kind of thing — every built mechanism is explicitly an instance of
one or more of the first three buckets made real and verified (the river = GROUND's
grain read as WATER's course; the one spire = GEOMETRY's tetra edge carrying WATER's
descent and gate). The corpus states this directly for the two largest cases: *"there
is not a single coordinate in `river.gd`. Every position is read off Terrain, which
reads off patterns/"* and the spire report's own geometry section, "derived not typed."
— `THE_RIVER_SITED_2026-08-05.md` §1; `THE_SPIRE_BUILT_2026-08-06.md` §2

**OPEN-FORK sits at bucket seams, not inside any one bucket.** Every item in bucket 6,
checked against where it actually lives, turns out to be a place two buckets don't yet
agree — never a gap inside a single bucket. C1/C2 are GEOMETRY-vs-GROUND naming
collisions (what a vertex-object is called). C3/C4 are LAW-vs-MECHANISM disagreements
(what the build actually does vs. what the naming says it should). The verb set and the
five outputs are WATER/GROUND-vs-nothing — GEOMETRY and LAW are both silent on them,
which is why they are the largest forks: they don't have two buckets in tension, they
have zero buckets holding them at all. — synthesis of the bucket-6 list above against buckets 1–5; no single citation, because this is the one relationship in this section that is a pattern *across* the sourced items rather than a sentence stated by any one file. Flagged as such rather than passed off as declared.

*That last relationship is the one exception in this section — it is composed rather
than quoted, and it is marked so it is not mistaken for the other six.*

## WHAT THIS SURFACED THAT WASN'T VISIBLE AT THE INVENTORY STAGE

- **LAW is not a bucket among buckets. It is the only one with an edge to every other
  bucket**, including bucket 7 (every warning is a LAW-discipline lapse) and bucket 6
  (every open fork stays open *because of* a LAW clause, not despite one). No other
  bucket has that shape.
- **GEOMETRY is the only bucket that recurs inside another bucket at a smaller scale**
  (the water table's own tetra). Nothing in WATER recurs inside GROUND, or vice versa.
- **MECHANISM has no independent content of its own.** Every single item in bucket 5,
  without exception, is traceable to a combination of buckets 1–3. There is no built
  thing that is *only* mechanism.
- **Bucket 6 is not a seventh domain. It is friction between the other six**, located
  precisely where two of them disagree or where a needed bucket is simply absent (the
  verb set, the five outputs). This reframes "how many open forks are there" as a
  question about which bucket-pairs have how much friction, not a flat count.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

It does not gather, site, or route anything. Those are the next phase, named by Kevin
and not started here. Gathering, siting and routing are each acts that would move an
item — decide where a bucket's contents actually stand in the world, assign it a place,
send it somewhere. **Composting and relationship-sorting only established what already
holds together; nothing here has been given a location, an order, or a destination.**

It does not rank the six relationships against each other, and it does not say which
bucket-friction (bucket 6) is most worth closing first.

It does not reach into `patterns/`, `_INTAKE/`, or `nesi/mind/` — the same scope limit
the extraction itself carried, inherited here unchanged.
