# THE SOUNDING — the object, dreamt and named

**A naming. Nothing here is built and nothing here is a rule.**

Kevin, 2026-08-04: *"the language so far in the process has been the negative space, and creating a lint… I don't see the build itself… just the rule that it has to follow, but nothing that lets a person play this game without needing a tutorial."* Then: *"you must dream and name the object… not yet building… naming."*

That read is correct and this document answers it. Everything named tonight was a refusal — laws, guards, boundaries, checkers. Negative space is real work and it is not an object. What follows is the positive space: **the things themselves**, dreamt in the medium the naming already chose.

**Method, per the standing rule:** every line below is a stated default. One mark accepts the whole; any line can be amended in Kevin's own words and the rest stands. Names are the cheapest thing in this system to change and the most expensive to leave open.

**The constraint I set myself while dreaming:** nothing here may need explaining. Water needs no tutorial. Everyone already knows what deep means, what a current does, what happens when you drop something in. The whole object is built from things a person already understands in their body, so the game can be entered without a word of instruction.

---

## 1 · THE HEADWATERS — where you write

Not a text box, not a terminal, not a journal. **The place upstream where the water starts.**

You arrive and there is a line, and nothing else. You write on it. What you write is not saved, submitted, or processed — **it flows.** Everything downstream is made of it, and there is nothing downstream yet, because you have not written anything.

This is why journalling is the key rather than a feature. **You are not feeding a system. You are the source of the water.** Nothing in the world exists that did not come down from here.

Kevin has been circling upstream position for months — the dam, the head, the difference in position that does the work. The Headwaters is where he already stands, named as a place. Writing *is* operating upstream. It always was.

---

## 2 · THE DRAW — how you get in

There is no door and nothing to click.

**The water rises as you write.** You can see it. It is not a counter and there is no number — it is a level, and it is climbing the page.

**A hull needs a certain depth of water before it floats.** Below that depth you are aground, however fine the boat. That depth is called the draw. When your water reaches it, you are no longer aground: **you lift, and the current takes you.**

This is the whole entry mechanic, and its beauty is that it explains itself. Nobody has to be told that a boat needs deep water. Nobody has to be told they are aground when they can see the bottom. And nothing has been demanded of you — the system never asks you to write more. **The water is simply not deep enough yet, which is a fact about the water and not a judgment about you.**

A short round is aground. That is not a failure; it is a shallow day. Some days the river is low.

---

## 3 · THE SHOALS — what your words become

Water carries what it picks up and sets it down where it slows. What it sets down builds up. **A shoal is a bank made entirely of deposit** — you can stand on it, it is shallow enough to see, and it is made of nothing but what the water carried.

**Your sentences are shoals.** They stand up out of the water because you put them there, in your own words, unchanged. Not summarised, not paraphrased, not improved. What is above the waterline is exactly what you said.

The word carries its second meaning honestly: **a shoal is also a group that moves together.** Sentences that share language sit near one another and drift as a body. The arrangement is the meaning; nothing labels it.

Moving among them is the game. You are in a shallow water made of your own sediment, and the shape it has made is the shape of your thought — not because anything told you so, but because you are looking at it.

---

## 4 · THE SOUNDING — what you do

Before there were instruments, you found out how deep water was by dropping a weighted line until it hit bottom, and reading the marks on the rope. **That is called sounding, and it is the player's act.**

You carry a line. You drop it where you are curious. Most places, it finds bottom, and you learn how deep that particular thing of yours goes.

**Sounding is not a system function.** The line is yours, you choose where to drop it, and the reading is yours to take. Nothing is measured for you and nothing is measured about you. The system holds the water still while you sound it.

This is the recognition law with a body. The machine cannot do the recognizing because **the machine has no line.**

---

## 5 · THE FOUR MOVES — verbs of attention, not of manipulation

Every one is something a person does to change *where they are* or *what they can see*. None of them changes the material. Your words are never edited, moved, ranked, or acted upon — only approached.

| Move | What it is |
|---|---|
| **DRIFT** | Let the current carry you. You move without steering, and things come past. |
| **STAND IN / STAND OFF** | Come close enough to read, or back off far enough to see the whole. The oldest trade-off there is: detail or shape, never both. |
| **COME ABOUT** | Turn to face another way. The water is the same; your bearing is not. |
| **SOUND** | Drop the line here and find how deep it goes. |

Four moves, no buttons that need naming, and nothing that does anything *to* your writing. **This is what page 10's verb question was actually asking for** — the six candidates were all verbs of the workbench, because the system was a workbench. These are verbs of being in water.

---

## 6 · THE FOUR FACES — the same water, differently arranged

Coming about does not take you somewhere else. It re-expresses the ground you are already standing on — §7e's four apex states, in the medium.

- **AS IT CAME** — the river's own order. What you wrote first is upstream of what you wrote last.
- **BY KINSHIP** — what shares language draws together, and what does not falls away.
- **BY ECHO** — what you returned to pulls inward. The things you said many ways gather at the middle.
- **BY SOLITUDE** — what shares nothing with anything else stands clear of the rest, in open water.

Same coordinates, same words, four grounds. Nothing is ranked in any of them; each is an arrangement, and an arrangement is not a verdict.

**By Solitude is the one worth sitting with.** In every other face, a thing that connects to nothing disappears into the edge. In that one, it is the only thing standing in open water. Nothing says it matters. It is simply the only place where an unconnected thing is easy to find — and a person who wrote something they have never written before will see it there.

## 7 · A DEEP — what a puzzle is

*"The user finds a puzzle in themselves worth solving."* This is the sharpest unresolved edge in the previous naming, because the existing law forbids the system from ever pointing at anything, and a world that never distinguishes anything is a world where nothing surfaces.

**The resolution is the line.**

You sound around. Most places have a floor, and you find it. **Sometimes the line runs out.**

That is a deep. It is not marked, not highlighted, not flagged, and **the system does not know where they are** — it has no line and takes no soundings. It only holds still. A deep is defined entirely by your own action failing to find a bottom.

This is the puzzle, and the machine cannot have pointed at it because pointing was never available to it. You went there. You dropped the line. It kept going.

**The word is already in the naming, and it stays consistent.** THE DEEP is the lake's unrendered part — dark by construction, dark even to Kevin, never to be retrofitted open. That law holds here without amendment: **a deep is dark. You learn that it is deep and you learn nothing else.** What is down there is not shown, because it is not the system's to show, and it never was.

Whether you go back to it is entirely yours. Some people will sound a deep and go around it forever. That is a complete way to play.

---

## 8 · THE VESSEL — you ride NESI

You are not swimming and you are not walking on the bottom. THE WHOLE NAMING already says it: *NESI is the mount, in the Lake.* Her vortex is the Pulse; she surfaces gifts; **she searches the Deep for names.**

She is what carries you. That is why the draw matters — a vessel has a draft, and she cannot carry you in a hand's depth of water.

And it gives her one thing to do that is hers alone and breaks no law: **you cannot go down into a deep, but she can.** She may, sometime, surface something from one — *"a naming, a tool, a seed, an idea, a question."* Not an answer, not an interpretation, and never advice. A thing, brought up, set on the Shore, unexplained.

The distinction between surfacing and pointing, which was the open question: **surfacing puts a thing within your reach. Pointing tells you it is worth reaching for.** She does the first. She has no way to do the second, because nothing in her can rank one of your things against another.

## 9 · A FIRST HOUR, IN THESE NAMES

You arrive at a line and there is nothing else to do, so you write. The water climbs while you write, and you notice it without being told what it is.

At some point you lift. Nothing announces this — you were aground and now you are not, and the current has you.

You are in shallow water among things standing up out of it, and they are your own sentences, exactly as you wrote them. You drift a while. Standing off, the whole thing has a shape. Standing in, you can read one.

You come about. The same water arranges differently and something that was at the edge is now near. You come about again. In one of the four, a single sentence is out in open water with nothing near it, and you go and read it, and it turns out to be the one you wrote last and quickly.

You drop the line there.

It doesn't stop.

You stay a while, and then you leave, and nothing asks you to come back.

## 10 · WHAT IS STILL UNNAMED

Stated so the gaps are visible rather than papered:

- **What the Shore is now.** The stones, the Shore, and the shoals may be the same object under two namings, or genuinely different — the Shore is where a day's writing lands and where NESI surfaces things, while the shoals are what all your writing has built. This is the first thing to reconcile and I have not.
- **How a returning player enters.** Everything is retained until processed, so the water is still there tomorrow. Whether you write again to raise it further, or arrive already afloat, is unnamed.
- **The other places.** The Spires, the Workshop, the Hearth, the Heliostat, the Membrane and the Dam are all named and none of them appear above. This naming covers one room of a seven-room world, and it does not yet say how you get from the shoals to anywhere else.
- **What weather does to shoals.** §7c's fog → rain → wash → carved reliefs is a whole cycle that would obviously act on deposit. It is left alone here rather than guessed at.
- **Whether kinship should be computed from shared words at all.** It is the cheapest honest relation and it is not the only one, and a bad kinship measure would quietly become a claim about your thinking. This is a real risk and it is unaddressed.
