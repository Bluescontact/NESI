## WATER AS INSTRUMENT — the reading that makes the table irreplaceable

> *"The table isn't where you process water — it's where you shape water so the other three tools can act. A basin of water is a lens, a medium, a coupling. Nothing else in the room can be a medium."*

**Depth is focal length.** The heliostat's beam rises through the basin before it does anything. Fill deeper, the focus moves. **So you cannot aim light without shaping water first** — which makes one edge of the stations tetra literal, and neither tool works alone.

**The basin rings.** Strike it and the note tells you the load — heavy with bedload it thuds, clear it sings. **A non-numeric readout you hear before you see, and it works with the screen off.**

**Standing waves sort.** Tune the basin and nodes form; sediment migrates to the nodes and stays. **You never sieve anything.** You set a frequency and the material arranges itself — the structure producing the arrangement, hand on the dial, no machine at the sorting.
