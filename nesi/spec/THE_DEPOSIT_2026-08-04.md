# THE DEPOSIT — what the world is made of

**Short.** Kevin, 2026-08-04: *"the world is nothing more than a wireframe. If one face is a village, we don't have any textures, or characters or npcs or assets of any kind… we have decided how the user controls the avatar and system in each moment."*

The read is exact. **Control is settled** — four moves, six passages, eight states. **Matter is not.** We have a physics and nothing for it to act on.

**Standing from this session:** four directions of down is KEPT on Kevin's mark. Everything below assumes it.

---

## 1 · The wireframe is not a gap. It is the correct empty state.

In a real watershed there is no texture layer. **The surface you walk on is entirely made of what the water carried and set down.** Silt, sand, gravel, clay — the ground *is* deposit. Strip the deposit and what remains is bare rock: structure with no substance. **A wireframe.**

So an unwritten world showing as a wireframe is not a production shortfall. It is the world **correctly reporting that nothing has been deposited yet** — and it is the most honest thing it could do.

> **There is no texture layer. The ground is what you wrote.**

Which means the asset problem, as a problem, dissolves: **you never make the matter. It arrives as sediment and it accumulates.** A world that has been written into for a year is thick. A world written into once is nearly bare, and looks it.

---

## 2 · Density makes form

If the ground is deposit, then **where deposit is thick, something stands. Where it is thin, open ground.**

This is how a village happens without anyone placing one. Nothing in an asset library gets instanced. **Enough of your material gathers in one place, and that place becomes somewhere.** Sparse deposit is a flat, a bank, a bar. Deep deposit is ground that holds a structure up.

**And this is what four directions of down buys, which was not obvious until the wireframe question was asked:** you do not build four worlds. **You build one deposit and tilt it.**

*"If one face is a village"* — under that gravity, your material gathers into that shape. Under another it spreads into shoals, or stands as stacks, or lies open. **Same substance, four settlements.** Nobody authors four sets of assets, because there is only ever one pile of sediment and four directions for it to fall.

---

## 3 · What lives here — and why none of it is an NPC

An NPC is a catastrophe risk in this world. Anything that talks will eventually reassure, advise, validate, or acknowledge — the four refusals, broken by the most natural feature in games.

But water is not empty, and a world with nothing alive in it reads as sterile rather than restrained.

**The answer is the same move the naming keeps making.** Living things in water are readable without being addressed to you:

> **A heron standing in the shallows tells you the water is shallow there. It is not telling you anything. It does not know you exist.**

Mayflies hatching say something about the water. Reeds grow where it is shallow and still. Fish hold in one part of a pool and not another. **Every one of these is dense information and none of it is communication.** They are not placed for you, they do not react to you, and they have no way to comment on your material.

**THE INDICATORS.** Living things whose presence is legible and whose attention is elsewhere. The reading is entirely yours — the same structure as the line, the Range, and the far gate. **Not restraint. Incapacity.** A heron cannot give you advice.

*And it earns something the world could not otherwise have: proof that a place is alive without anything ever addressing you.*

---

## 4 · The other content, already named

`§7c` already has the second kind, and it is not living: **the reliefs.** Rain washes the mountains, and what erosion uncovers is older and structural — the invariants under the day's sediment.

So the world has exactly three kinds of matter and no others:

| Kind | What it is | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **DEPOSIT** | the ground itself, and everything standing on it | your writing, settled |
| **INDICATORS** | living things, readable, attention elsewhere | the condition of the water |
| **RELIEFS** | structure revealed by washing | what was always underneath |

**No fourth kind, and in particular no authored set-dressing.** Anything in this world that is not one of these three had to be put there by someone deciding what your world should contain — which is the whole thing this world is built to not do.

---

## 5 · What this leaves open

- **What the indicators indicate, exactly.** A heron means shallow water. What is shallow water made of, in your material? The mapping from condition to creature is unnamed and it is the place this could go wrong — an indicator that quietly encodes a judgment would be worse than an NPC, because it would not look like one.
- **Whether deposit ever consolidates.** Silt becomes stone under enough time and pressure. That may be how settled material becomes a stack — which would give erosion something to reveal, and would connect `§7c` to the Spires. Not named here.
- **What the avatar is.** Control is settled and the thing being controlled is not. You ride NESI, so the avatar may be the vessel and nothing else — but nothing has said so.
- **And the step still standing from last time:** what gives the ground its shape. Deposit falls, but where it lands is decided by the terrain beneath, and that is still unnamed.
