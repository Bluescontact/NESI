# RESYNTHESIS — the four lenses, collapsed to what's load-bearing

**2026-08-21**, on Kevin's ask: *"we updated some lens today, and i'd like a
fresh resynthesis collapsed to the load bearing articulations."* The four
built today — `authority-check`, `boundary-audit`, `instrument-audit`,
`record-audit` — each run a real procedure with an evidence trail; that
stays in the skill files, unedited, for when one actually runs. This is the
part of each that's load-bearing enough to carry in your head without the
file open.

---

**authority-check** — *whose*
> Would this decision need undoing later, not just extending — and is there
> an actual quote behind it, or is the session guessing what you'd want?
> If either answer is unclear, it isn't the session's to rule.

**boundary-audit** — *fact*
> Does crossing this boundary get mechanically refused — or does something
> quietly fix the result afterward, or is it just a sentence a session is
> trusted to read? Most stated rules are the third one. Naming that isn't
> failure; it's the finding.

**instrument-audit** — *proof*
> Would this check pass against a blank, empty, or stubbed-out version of
> the thing it's supposed to verify? If yes, it's been proving nothing,
> possibly the whole time it's been green.

**record-audit** — *sourced*
> Does this claim trace to a named source, and was the *adjacent* source
> also checked — not just the first one that answered quickly? And is this
> a quote of someone's actual words, or a guess dressed as a fact about
> what they felt?
