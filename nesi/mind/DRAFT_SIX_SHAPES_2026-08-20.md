## Why six, and what kind of thing these are

The question that started this: could Kevin's working process be simplified
into a small set of prebuilt capacities, one per recurring joint or action,
without narrowing what he can reach for. Reading the corpus's own history
against that question turned up a caution before it turned up an answer: the
25 laws in `LEARNED.md`, the tool names under `tools/`, and the texture of
`MARKS_LOG.jsonl` don't cluster into six *actions Kevin takes* — they cluster
into six *questions the work keeps asking of itself*, mostly after the fact,
never in place of his own mark. Law 12 already rules out the doorway version
of this: "every live decision open and markable at depth zero." What follows
names the six questions as they already exist in the corpus, cited to where
each one lives.

## The six

**1. Does this only look verified, or is it?**
The gap between a check passing and a thing being true. Laws 2 (a stranger
must read it), 3 (a check holds because it's structural, not because of whose
hand ran it), 5 (measure what an eye could see), 6 (a check must select the
object, not a neighboring label), 23 (ask the object its state, never infer
it from a symptom). Standing tools: `cold_walk.js`, `refusal_check.js`,
`standing.js`.

**2. Does material conserve, or does it leak, duplicate, or vanish?**
What happens to a unit of the player's — or Kevin's — own material as it
moves through a system. Laws 7 (intake priced at the door), 8 (filter,
never a queue), 10 (a high answer- or close-rate doesn't prove resolution).
Standing tools: `conserve.js`, `conserve_stations.js`, `conserve_seating.js`.

**3. Is this boundary a fact, or just a sentence?**
Whether a rule is something the system cannot cross, or something a session
merely reads and complies with. Laws 11 (one manifest per tetra), 22 (a gate
is a filesystem fact — a missing file, a nonexistent parameter — not an
exhortation), 24 (enforce by refusal, never by silently correcting the
result after). Standing tools: `scope_check.js`, `door_check.js`,
`store_guard.js`.

**4. Is the record honest about what happened?**
Whether the log matches the event, sourced and undistorted. Laws 1 and 9
(the admission rule — every line sourced, none composed), 14 (system facts
and verbatim words only, never a declared inner state), 17 (divergence held,
never averaged), 19 (a name only when it carries load). Standing convention:
the layered-mark, never-silent-erasure pattern used across `PROTOCOLS.md`
and this file's own `CLAUDE.md`.

**5. Who actually has authority to rule this?**
Whether a ruling belongs to the session making it. Laws 20 (the keystone —
no law becomes a lever to give Kevin less) and 25 (a build session doesn't
rule its own base fork, however carefully reasoned). Standing convention:
`MARKS_LOG.jsonl` entries explicitly tagged `"source": "session"` so a
session's own note is never mistaken for his mark.

**6. Is unrouted capacity being found and named, or left dead?**
What already got built and forgotten, still available to reroute. Standing
tools: `junkyard.js`, `organ_audit.js`, `route_map.js`, and the
unrouted-gifts skill.

## What this is, and what it isn't

This is a naming of six lenses the corpus already applies, mostly as
after-the-fact checks — not six capacities to hand Kevin mid-decision. A
version of this that turned into a menu at the point of action would be the
exact thing Law 12 refuses. Where it could have standing use without that
failure: as six questions a build or a session checks *itself* against
before Kevin has to ask them — closer to what `check_all.js` and the 25 laws
already do than to a new interface.

## What it's built on, and what it isn't yet checked against

- Built from one read-only survey of laws, logs, and tool names — not
  checked against session transcripts, nor against whether these six
  actually cover the corpus's full history or just its most recent,
  best-documented stretch (the 2026-08-12 through 08-19 run).
- The six are named from clusters that appeared salient during one pass;
  a different read could group the same 25 laws differently, or find a
  seventh shape this pass missed. Not tested against a second, independent
  read.
- Whether these six are worth keeping as a standing reference, and where —
  if anywhere — they should live, is Kevin's mark to make, not this
  draft's.
