Don't describe what the page already demonstrates. The structure is the move; describing the move is padding.

**Why:** Kevin called this out on 2026-05-11 after reviewing the v3 OSG site pages: "I feel like you're describing rather than demonstrating through the word choices you've made." The pages I built had explanation layers, anti-objection blocks, how-this-works framings, and summary closers that glossed structure the page already showed. Pages that describe themselves drain power from pages that perform themselves.

**How to apply:**

Pages whose register is structural recognition or long-read description (architecture.html, about.html, the Solarium piece) — description IS the assignment. Keep that.

Pages that enact something — refusals, offerings, current-state, the four-vertex homepage — should perform what they are with the fewest words possible. Cut:

- **"Why this matters" / "Why these are structural" blocks.** The list IS the why. Cut the explanation paragraph.
- **"What this page isn't" anti-objection blocks.** Refusals page already handles the global refusal frame. Per-page restating is gloss.
- **"How to respond" / "How this works" framings.** Two lines and a contact. Not paragraphs of explanation.
- **Intro paragraphs that name the page's logic.** "Side A names what's on offer. Side B names the stance." — the page IS Side A and Side B. Saying so is the gloss.
- **Summary closing italics.** "*The refusals are structural. They are not apology...*" describes the closer. The last item in the list, hard-stopped, BE the closer.
- **Stance bullets explaining the gift form.** The card doesn't explain — it IS. Each move as a hard statement; no preamble.

Tests for whether a sentence is describing instead of demonstrating:
- Does it tell the reader what the page is going to do?
- Does it anticipate an objection that's already handled structurally elsewhere?
- Does it explain why the structure works?
- Does it summarize what just happened on the page?

If yes to any → cut. The structure does the work.

This is one level deeper than the existing voice memory (direct, geometric, no padding). The principle is: well-built structure makes its own argument; describing it drains the argument.
