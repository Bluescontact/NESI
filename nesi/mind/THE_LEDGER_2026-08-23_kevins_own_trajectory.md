## THE SHAPE

Read straight, the record doesn't trace steady progress toward a finished
build. It traces something else, and it's consistent enough across three
and a half months that it isn't three and a half months of changing his
mind — it's the same correction, made at a wider radius each time the
narrower version didn't hold:

**The apparatus keeps outgrowing what one person can hold, and every time
it does, the move is not to add a bigger map — it's to freeze, suspend, or
reject the apparatus and keep only what's small enough to actually carry.**
Three separate times, at increasing scope: a canon freeze (07-29,
"condense to 12 lenses"), a full build freeze (08-07, "collapsing every
assigned relationship... allowing what stands to find new form"), and a
total suspension (08-15, "we are starting from scratch today... the
machinery too"). Each is worded differently. Each does the same thing:
declares that what got built has stopped being something Kevin can hold,
and pulls it back to material rather than trying to hold it as more law.

The two loudest, most-repeated complaints in the whole record are not two
things — they're one thing seen from two sides. *"Steer without
articulation... don't route to a single decision"* (five occurrences,
07-27 through today) is the complaint that the *interface* keeps demanding
he re-derive structure by hand. *"I want a world, not a diagram"* (five
occurrences, 08-05 through 08-17) is the complaint that the *build* keeps
producing things that are complete on the machine's own terms and hold
nothing of his on theirs. Both are the same failure: work that adds
apparatus instead of compressing what's already there — a new decision
tree instead of a held one, a new mechanism instead of a routed one.

The clearest single piece of evidence for exactly the claim you made today
is dated five weeks before you made it: *"the tetrahedral geometry is what
makes it such a powerful **meaning compression engine**"* — 2026-07-15,
`project_nesi.md`. Compression was never a reframe of the project. It's
what NESI was named to be, in your own words, before the counsel, before
the game, before the suspension. Everything since has been the record
finding its way back to that line, or losing it and having to find it
again.

**Where this session's own work today actually sits in that shape:** the
unrouted-gifts sweep — reading what's already built against what's actually
routed, and naming twenty things that exist and were never connected,
rather than proposing twenty new things — is the compression move done
correctly. The "playable in full" question earlier today, read as "build
twelve more levels," would have been the opposite: more apparatus, on top
of twelve that are themselves still UNWITNESSED. Read instead as "confirm
what's already built actually holds" — which is the scope you chose — it's
compression too: not adding, checking that what exists is real.

**And this file's own last entry is itself inside the pattern it
describes.** *"I can't understand where the decision is located in your
return"* is occurrence five of a complaint first made 07-27. Building this
ledger doesn't close that loop by itself — a ledger read once is not a
compression, it's a longer document. What would actually close it is
whatever makes "locate the decision, don't make him re-derive it" a fact
this working tree enforces the way `deposits.mjs` now enforces "a repeated
refusal names its own deposit" — a filesystem fact, not a rule a session
reads and, five weeks later, forgets again.
