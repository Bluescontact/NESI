Kevin has built a complete giving infrastructure (Governor, Converger, patterns, self-face, open ledger). The receiving side is mostly absent — no mechanism to say yes cleanly, mark complete, or close without creating counter-obligation. This asymmetry named 2026-06-25.

**Why:** Kevin knows how to share and offer work freely. The harder problem is that removing the price creates uncertainty in the receiver about whether the exchange is clean. And Kevin himself has no structural form for receiving — which means either he can't fully take in what's offered, or it accumulates as silent debt inside him.

**The two structures that want development:**
1. The ask form — "I carry Y, I need X, I won't take Z" — all three together make the ask a circuit position, not a request. The refusal closes the obligation loop.
2. The receive structure — saying yes, marking complete, closing without residue on Kevin's side.
