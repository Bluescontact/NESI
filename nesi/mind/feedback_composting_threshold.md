Named by Kevin 2026-06-07. **A real human situation (a crisis, a relationship, a loss) may be metabolized into the commons — the shared/public/canon substrate — only when it has been abstracted past every ADDRESSABLE trace of the individual — no name, place, or detail that lets a reader route back to the person.** (Scoped 2026-07-19, Kevin's mark, vow/policy audit Finding 3: total trace-erasure — voice, style, life-shape — is the vow; addressability is the commitment that carries the weight.) Two or three layers up, until the person dissolves entirely into structure. Pattern crosses; instance NEVER does. The name, the specific facts, anything identifying — all gone.

The absence of the person is **both** the privacy rule **and** the test that the pattern is real: a shape that still needs the person to make sense isn't a shape yet. When the structure stands on its own with no one inside it, it is safe and true to keep.

Architecture written to `frameworks/composting_a_situation_into_the_commons.md` (the rule + a worked example with no one in it). The worked example that crossed this threshold: **the secured-existence precondition** — forfeiting the claim (giving without seeking authorship/recovery/record) is a privilege of a body whose existence is already secured; below that line, gripping the record is the *correct* survival move, not a failure of the gift stance; you cannot teach a body to forfeit, you can only restore the secured existence that makes it available, or take the draining fight off the body.

**How to apply:** when metabolizing anyone's real situation, abstract until no trace remains outside the architecture, or leave it held — never let a traceable instance cross the membrane (deployed / canon / public). The two held living-person situations in this workspace (the grieving greenhouse-builder; the housing-crisis advocate) were composted at Kevin's instruction once at this threshold (2026-06-07), architecture lifted, traceable raw moved to the compost zone for his delete — **do not reopen them.** Connects to [[feedback_recycled_substrate]] (extract substrate-independently), [[feedback_publish_delete_boundary]], [[feedback_somatic_threshold]].
