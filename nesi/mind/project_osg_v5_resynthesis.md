`OURSHAREDGIFTS_V5.md` (DSS root) — **marked by Kevin with energy 2026-06-07.** The agreed direction for the next version of oursharedgifts.org (currently deployed at **v4**, which is the framework-explaining site — the "half that didn't land").

**The shift:** v4 is a site that *explains a framework*; v5 *is* Kevin's working life, made public. It stops describing and starts being — the living face of one person's free economy ([[project_single_person_economy]]).

**The personal-scale frame (durable, folded in 2026-07-02 from the retired May memory):** *"This is infrastructure built by Kevin to support Kevin, and enable Kevin to share gifts coherently without counting the cost."* — Kevin, 2026-05-11. Not for an audience, not for a movement; the legible surface that makes his specific giving sustainable. The standing test for any page: does it enable Kevin to keep giving without depletion? If not, restructure. Once counting starts (transaction, ledger, reciprocity), the gift collapses — the infrastructure exists so counting becomes unnecessary.

**Three people, three doors:** (1) the neighbor who needs his hands (front door — handles intake so his phone/body isn't the route); (2) the stranger who wants the life (the open playbook — KEVINS_ECONOMY + the guides); (3) the person deciding whether to trust him (proof — his standing work shown as objects in the world: bus, solar, pump-and-tank, the running systems — so he never performs his worth).

**Why one site serves both Kevin and the public:** the same standing work is his credential AND the stranger's proof-of-life; the same guides are his storefront (income) AND the public gift (the free playbook that makes a stranger trust him enough to hire him). No business-side/gift-side split — the economy is one circuit; the site shows the circuit.

**Infrastructure it serves:** the site is where four economy layers meet the world — Face (front door + standing work), Vault (guides storefront), Mesh (serving Heartland), model-as-gift. Ground/Hands/pace stay physical/off-site. The **AI back-office** runs intake, turns 90-second job memos into the guides, keeps the books — so his body is never what the site runs on.

**Build approach (not a rebuild):** promote the existing life-pages (Offerings, Current, Refusals, Request) to the spine; let the framework library (tetrahedron, syntheses, gift theory) recede to a quiet archive. Reuses the deployed v4 ground + core.css; crosses the membrane only on Kevin's push.
