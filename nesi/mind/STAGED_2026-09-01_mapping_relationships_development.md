# STAGED — the mapping-relationships development pass

**Held, per crystal 10: staged with its named condition — opens on the
keeper's word.** The keeper's marks, 2026-09-01: *"for it to be functional
for me need to be able to run 750 to 2000 words... but we haven't
established the mapping relationships fully yet"* → *"stage the
mapping-relationships development pass."*

**The question, exact:** the relation vocabulary (chain / near / hinge /
declared) was born at four-sentence scale. What are the mapping
relationships that survive 750–2000 words in one sitting — so the writer
can *name what they want inside the world* at the volume they actually
write?

---

## The measured ground (scale ladder, 2026-09-01, burst arrivals — the
freshet case, which is what a morning's writing is)

| sentences | words | layout | near-pairs | hinges | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | ~120 | 6ms | 20 | 0 | legible |
| 32 | ~470 | 13ms | 218 | 0 | near thickening |
| 64 | ~940 | 48ms | 549 | 0 | near is mush |
| 128 | 1,871 | 288ms | 1,326 | 0 | geometry holds; meaning doesn't |

- Speed is not the problem: keystroke 0ms, store 26KB, grounds <10ms.
  The weave matrix is the one wall: 998ms, 16,384 cells at 128 —
  unusable as a read past ~40 sentences.
- **Near saturates**: in one sitting everything is near everything; the
  relation carries no information exactly at functional volume.
- **Hinge goes silent**: zero at every rung — chain+near saturation
  leaves no articulation points; the instrument vanishes when the most
  material exists. (The outside reads already found the inverse failure
  at low density: nearly everything a hinge. The instrument has no
  scale at which it currently earns its seat in a burst.)
- 128 full-text cards render cleanly (overlaps fixed, swept) but cannot
  all be READ — full-text-always-visible collides with volume.

## The jurisdiction (settled law, not open)

- **Computed relations are lawful INSIDE the private surface** — the
  game is the writer's own room; chain/near/hinge may be computed there.
  Only declared relations cross the membrane (the one-organ doctrine,
  offered in the weave's footer, would make this a stated law — the pass
  should put it to the mark).
- **Volume, never content** (the freshet law): any new relation must be
  derivable from structure — arrival, idle, length, position, the
  writer's own declarations — never from what a sentence says.
- **The machine offers no relation vocabulary** (field_render): any new
  DECLARED relation kind is free text; the machine may hold, never name.
- **A catchment is not tended**: no mechanism may prompt for relations
  or ask the writer to come back and relate things.

## The candidate moves (from the record, each already part-named)

1. **Re-found NEAR for one-sitting writing.** Candidates: near within a
   sitting = pause-bounded neighborhoods (idle gaps as the cut, volume-
   lawful); or near becomes relative (k-nearest by gap, not threshold);
   or near yields entirely inside a sitting and only speaks across
   sittings. *This decides most of the mush.*
2. **Hinge: earn the seat or yield it.** The windowing repair fixed
   direction, not saturation. Candidates: hinge over declared edges only
   (the deliberate graph — sparse, meaningful at any scale); or hinge
   retired from burst display and kept for cross-day structure; or cut
   (the reducer precedent: report the refusal if the instrument has no
   scale where it works).
3. **Stones (dream B's open question).** Grouping is the classic answer
   to volume — and its cost was already named honestly ("does grouping
   earn its complexity?"). The 750–2000 word fact is new evidence: at
   functional scale some aggregation may now earn what it couldn't at 20
   sentences. The writer gathers; nothing auto-clusters (declared
   kinship, applied to grouping).
4. **Interest-weighted selection** (named 2026-08-26, never built): the
   four-pick and the margin should surface structurally meaningful
   items, not whatever is nearest/newest — lawful if the weight is
   structural (degree of declared edges, drag history, dwell).
5. **The weave matrix past 40**: window it (the knowledge doc's own
   suggestion), scope it to a selection (the picked four, a stone), or
   let the full matrix be a generated artifact rather than a live pane.
6. **Reading at volume**: full-text-always-visible was a law for the
   DOCUMENT; the margin at 128 may need levels — full text on focus,
   footprint otherwise (the tarp already holds the lawful precedent:
   volume rendered, text withheld until approached).

## The method (the corpus's own development procedure)

Dream first (outside shapes for what a 1,871-word morning should LOOK
like as a map), then adversarial audit of every candidate against the
four laws above, then build the survivors behind the felt read — played
at 128 before crossing, the way the blind bug and the overlap wall were
found. Every constant swept, not guessed. The scale ladder re-run as the
regression gate: a candidate that only works at 8 sentences is not a
mapping relationship, it is a demo.
