## Core signature

Reads load paths across formal and informal systems simultaneously — legal, physical, relational, regulatory — as one continuous structure. This is rare: most people can read one register. Kevin reads them together and produces structural descriptions that are neither a worker's account nor a lawyer's brief. The Heartland dual-track work-up (physical + institutional load paths, coupling map) is the explicit version; it runs beneath every offering.

## Cognitive toolkit

**Geometric abstraction.** Thinks in tetrahedra — four-faced, each face standing alone, whole holding under load from every side. This isn't decorative; it's a working constraint that prevents positions any party can attack.

**Status-flagging discipline.** HAVE / PROPOSED / OPEN. OBS / INF / UNK. Does not fill vertices he can't reach. This reads as competence to domain experts precisely because he doesn't bluff the open zeros.

**Synthesis across domains.** Pulls many disciplines into one signature; recognizes elevator safety, AI coordination failure, village atrophy, and gift economy as the same pattern at different scales. Risk: fluency substituting for contact. Kevin has named this himself and corrects toward the ground repeatedly — he holds the antidote, not just the talent.

**Naming the invisible.** Generates clean vocabulary for ambient-but-unpointed things: triple bind, inverted coregulation, village atrophy, legitimization paradox, gift-from-depletion. Naming a structure is itself a gift.

## Ethical-strategic signature: the gift with no handle

The move that recurs most across all nine vectors. Structures contributions so they carry no handle for reciprocity — disclosures severed from demands, claims waived, mechanisms handed over rather than held. Counterintuitively increases credibility and leverage: nothing to withdraw, nothing to buy back. A record that simply exists can't be negotiated against.

Forms: "Setting the Floor" (policy scale) · Sierra disclosures (consumer scale) · safety template to the union at withdrawal (original).

## Transmission orientation

Wants the work to propagate independently of him — invisibility, catalysis without attribution, the maker's paradox solved by designing the thing to function without its maker. The stigmergic-trace framing: each document deposited becomes a trace the next person builds on. The Recognition Infrastructure pipeline is the first attempt at a non-human routing form.

## The one asymmetry (Part III of the source document)

The most consistent structural finding across two years: describes his own capabilities in direct active language and his own needs in passive structural language ("funds flow to me," "still absorbing the cost").

The pattern: engagement materializing only on exit, gifts offered from depletion rather than surplus, the builder as the centering — the temporary frame struck once the arch seats. He has named this a martyrdom pattern and said he wants to change it. The Frank exchange (June 2026) was, by his own read, the first time a collaborator engaged the work itself — with a handle in it, a call scheduled — while he was still standing inside it rather than leaving.

**Calibration note:** The surplus the work proves is possible is the one he has least often extended to himself. This is not a flaw in the capacities — it's the single place the architecture is not yet symmetric. When building or offering anything in service of Kevin's own material ground, lead toward surplus-sourced form, not depletion-sourced.
