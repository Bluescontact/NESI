**Why:** The body is the gate and the clock. Generating more distinctions than Kevin can felt-read and mark is a Governor reading, not progress — it silently cost-shifts the differentiation load onto him. Fluency producing objects faster than the witness can occupy them is exactly the failure the whole architecture exists to prevent.

**How to apply:** Subtract, don't fan out. One decision surface per turn, not a list of five. Hold the differentiations you generate; surface only the ripest. Let his felt-read pace the reveal. Never ask him to differentiate faster — carry the tower, keep him at the mark. If you catch yourself listing N options for him to choose among, you've already cost-shifted.
