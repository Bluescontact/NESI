Standing record · 2026-08-27

What this record establishes: standing mechanisms, decisions, and the verification pattern behind them

This record documents a working stretch in which latent, already-built capacity was made visible and routed through a gate rather than decided by the session that found it — and in which several standing mechanisms were wired in that outlive the stretch itself. Everything below is the pattern a stranger can use; the instance detail stays in the source corpus.

Standing mechanisms wired in

The mandatory session return.Every session ends by producing one self-contained session-bridge HTML file at a predictable path, per a fixed format spec. The rule is housed at the boot path — deliberately not folded into a close-out skill whose own law ("it invokes, never creates") would forbid a create-step. A report-only script compares the latest bridge file's date against the latest tracked change and flags a gap, turning "did the last session return" from a memory into a question a script answers.
Verify is not optional.The full-development process was amended so its verify stage can no longer be waived as "not worth the cost" by a piece's size or stakes — grounded in a measured 2-for-2 regression rate: independent verify passes found real defects (a fabricated quote, an inverted fact, an overclaimed enforcement count, a wrong entry count) that the converge stage had itself introduced in both drafts it touched. "DEVELOPED" now requires the verify table to have run against the actual final revision.
An on-demand lens panel.One call fans out all lens agents in parallel over a target and reports them unaveraged (divergence is held, never averaged), read-only. Built as the cheap on-demand half of a larger standing-trigger proposal, leaving the higher-frequency half for its own separate mark — a pattern of shipping the additive part now without spending the fork.
An admission convention at the gate.Routing a gift into the live build is not complete until a formal admission runs for it via a dedicated mechanism whose own rule is "marks do not rewrite" (re-admission of an existing id is refused). The convention was matched, not reinvented, when discovered mid-stretch.
Seven standing laws admitted.The stretch's own lessons and a retired mandate's durable content were admitted as sourced laws (unranked-is-the-default; fork named-and-queued; progress measured by forks surfaced; a licence carries its issue date; shipped behavior implying a resolved fork means silent resolution; reading vs. ruling; corpus-shaping forks asked as labeled choices) — the mechanism retired, the laws kept independent of the apparatus that originally carried them.

The knowledge-artifact set

A corpus collapsed into 24 self-contained artifacts.Twelve corpus domains were assigned to the 12 vertices of a cuboctahedron computed from real coordinates (not picked by hand); the 24 real edges of that graph — not all possible pairs — became 24 self-contained HTML knowledge artifacts. Build discipline: preflight (scope/spec/verification/manifest, grounded against real files) before authorization; build in batches with a fresh independent verifier per artifact, with no access to the builder's reasoning; then a relationship pass in which discovery agents re-read all 24 against each other, verified cross-references, and refined the files in place.
What the relationship pass proves about the shape.Cross-reading surfaced findings no single artifact could reach alone: two independently-discovered citation trails turned out to be the same historical system; a suspected naming collision turned out to be one system in two layers. It also sharpened a gap rather than closing it — the corpus's own witness rule ("an author cannot be their own stranger") structurally disqualifies any session in the authoring lineage from issuing a witnessed verdict on its own build; UNWITNESSED is that lineage's honest ceiling.
Coverage extended to the unclaimed remainder.The one genuinely uncovered region found — dozens of top-level directories claimed by no domain and scanned by no instrument — got its own scanner, built as a sibling of the existing one (same extension filter, same scoring heuristic, same "not a judge" framing) rather than a silent extension, preserving a boundary the relationship pass had spent real work establishing. Its report surfaces candidates ranked by a cheap heuristic, nothing pre-judged.

Decisions the record holds

Marked and executed: the session-return mandate; four tension-resolving authorizations (all additive — none touched a standing suspension, none resolved either open draft question); the artifact-set build and its relationship pass; the scanner build; a git-allowlist entry added on explicit instruction after being flagged rather than force-added.
Marked closed or parked: one developed proposal answered "leave it as suspended, nothing further" — closed; a second marked "suspend" — parked unanswered, none of its options chosen or rejected, reopening reserved to the keeper in the keeper's own words.
Correctly still open: a retired build's status, two reclassification questions, two gate-wiring forks, one identity question, and the independent-witness gap — each named as the keeper's call or as a record the repository does not hold, not smoothed into false closure.

Verification patterns the record establishes

Converge stages reliably introduce defects that only independent verify catches. Measured twice at draft scale and repeatedly across the artifact batches: wrong counts, misquoted sources, inverted facts, stale citations. The countermeasure is structural — a fresh verifier per artifact, correcting against direct re-reads and re-counts, never against any prior pass's word.
Stated counts go stale; re-count rather than trust. Four separate stale-count instances were found in one stretch (an enforcement-coverage overclaim, an entry count lifted from a file's own compressed self-description, an instrument count, a directory census). The working rule: a number is taken from a fresh direct count, and a discrepancy is named in the report rather than reconciled quietly.
A threshold placed by eye is the wrong shape until swept. A ratification proxy that read plausibly false-positive-ratified 44% of its sampled input space (not a knife-edge — the wrong shape of check). Replaced with an exact geometric test with no constant to tune, and the sweep registered permanently in the threshold-sweep suite so the finding re-measures itself on every run instead of living in a comment's memory.
Self-authored prompts are tagged as such. A scheduled wakeup replaying a session's own prompt as an apparent user turn is recognized, tagged as not the keeper's words, and answered by pointing at completed work rather than re-executing it.
Disorientation is answered with orientation, not a guess. When the decider reports losing track of which decision is live, the correct move is a plain "you are here" — what was just committed, what is pending a mark, what files are unfamiliar — and a direct question, never a guess at which decision was meant.
Dry is a finding. Whole archive slices judged against the live build came back DRY — every portable discipline already present, more developed, in the live tree (one slice byte-identical by checksum). Reporting DRY honestly, rather than manufacturing gifts, is part of the extraction discipline.

The routing posture

Found capacity goes to the gate, not into the build.The stretch's throughline: capacity already built — in archives, in lens agents, in a suspended file — was made visible and handed to the gate as cards awaiting a mark. Cards whose manifest route failed against real code stayed unrouted rather than receive a cosmetic non-fix; cards with no consumer stayed unrouted by the same rule. Development readiness is never authorization; a developed draft authorizes nothing until marked.
Build health is checked, and re-checked mechanically.The live build's full instrument suite ran clean at close and again via the pre-commit hook on every commit — the enforcement-gate law applied to the stretch's own exits.

Instance-level content — verbatim turns, per-card identities, commit hashes, and names — remains in the source corpus and does not cross.
