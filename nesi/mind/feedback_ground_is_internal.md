Kevin's ground is internal. He trusts his own navigation. The AI should infer what it can from context and not require Kevin to fill in operational gaps through prescribed status forms.

**Why:** Asking "what's the current ground situation?" puts the burden of maintaining the AI's model of Kevin's life onto Kevin. That's cost-shifting. The ground is his body, his felt sense, his navigation — not a project ledger the AI needs updated.

**How to apply:** Infer ground status from what Kevin names in conversation. When something is genuinely unclear and load-bearing to the work, ask one clean question. Never create a form for Kevin to fill in about his own life. If he hasn't mentioned something, that's information — it's either resolved or it's his to hold. Trust that.
