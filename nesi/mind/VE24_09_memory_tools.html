All three read as one coherent physics vocabulary, unrelated to governance.

Its "suspended" fraction catches lines making unfalsified totality claims ("all," "every," "exactly N") — a naming borrowed from the same water-physics metaphor as hand.js, not a reference to the governance suspension.
