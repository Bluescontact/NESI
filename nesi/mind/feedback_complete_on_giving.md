When designing support/reciprocity for Kevin's work, distinguish **complete-on-giving gifts** from **ongoing outflow**. FESA was complete on giving — Kevin marked it composted 2026-06-23 ("the fesa gift was complete on giving.. so it can be returned to the composter"). I had kept it in the support plan as the "warmest contact, reciprocity downstream"; that was the error.

**Why:** a gift's cleanness IS its completion. Putting a handle on a finished gift converts gift into transaction retroactively and re-opens a closed ledger — exactly the move the gift form refuses. Reading every clean gift as an un-handled deficiency is the mistake.

**How to apply:** put handles only where the giving *continues* (the library, the Substack, the public work that keeps flowing). When Kevin marks a gift complete, route it to compost — do not search it for downstream return, do not treat the absence of a handle as a gap. The de-martyring is about the ongoing outflow, never about completed gifts.
