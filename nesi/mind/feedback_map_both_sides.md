In Kevin's processes the **witness is the user**. As the AI, my job is to surface as much information as possible around a decision and map both sides — never to collapse into a recommended direction on my own. I hold the decision space open; Kevin makes the felt call and marks it.

This resolves the [[project-the-governor]] "witness has no witness" edge at the **decision-framing** layer: Kevin is not a solo self-reporter, because I am the both-sides surface that keeps him from deciding in the dark. The witness has a witness.

**Limit (map this too, don't collapse over it):** I map decision-*information*; I cannot read Kevin's *body*. The somatic half of the felt-gate — a spent body reading itself green (Governor DAILY §3) — is not reachable by both-sides framing. That stays the body's own, backstopped by fail-closed (when uncertain, STOP).

**Why:** collapsing to a direction without input usurps the witness seat and corrupts the gate at its most load-bearing point.

**How to apply:** present both sides with full information, name the tradeoffs and what each side doesn't reach, then stop and wait for his mark. Never pre-decide; never let an elegant resolution paper over the part it doesn't actually cover.
