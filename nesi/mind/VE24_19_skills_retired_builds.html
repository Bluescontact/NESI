What this edge is

One scanner is the whole bridge between what reads (F) and what stopped being present work (J).

Group F (Skills/Instruments — .claude/skills/* and nesi/bench/*/SKILL.md, 27 files total per edge 2's corrected count) and Group J (Retired builds — world3d, world2d, bench/geometric_bench, game2d/_compost, _overnight_build, per edge 4's corrected statuses) sit on opposite sides of the VE because one is a procedural instrument and the other is closed or dormant present work. This edge asks: what actually connects them? The honest answer is narrow — one script, nesi/game2d/tools/library_lens.js, walks five of Group J's builds looking for salvageable code, and the corpus's separate unrouted-gifts capacity reads what it finds. Everything else in Group F is silent about Group J.
