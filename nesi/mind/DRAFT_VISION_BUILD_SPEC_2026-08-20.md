## 1. WHAT THE GAME IS

The whole game in one line: "You are the weather over a drowned world that keeps what you give it — and something enormous and gentle lives at the bottom, slowly building you back out of everything you ever let go." (THE_GAME.md, line 15)

Diagnosis of why prior work wasn't a game: cost (writing/filing/routing) was made the gameplay and payoff was made illegal to render. Inversion: writing is the smallest act in the game (THE_GAME.md, line 21).

Core loop: RAIN (write, 60-90s, content never read) → SHAPE (dam hand + heliostat, real skill) → THE DEEP ANSWERS, visible only as hints (THE_GAME.md, lines 25-29).

Positioning against competitors: Kind Words and Rosebud sell being read/understood; NESI serves the opposite, smaller appetite — a place where words stay the writer's own (THE_VISION_2026-08-17.md, section 17, lines 816-832).

Whole design's promise, two halves: "Nothing you release is wasted. Nothing you release is read." (THE_VISION_2026-08-17.md, closing section, lines 850-856)

## 2. WHAT IS SACRED / WHAT NEVER BENDS

- Content-blind forever — blind to *meaning*, not to the words as renderable/relational material: nothing reads for meaning, judges, or rewrites what a word says, but the words remain live objects the world's mechanics can render, move, or restructure through geometry and relationship (position, adjacency, arrival order, crossing), never through parsing content. A word's text is never rewritten, even when its structural fate is. No numbers/scores/streaks reach the player; nothing leaves the world but fruit, by the hand; quitting loses nothing; blank is complete (THE_GAME.md, lines 44-48, corrected 2026-08-20 — Kevin, MARKS_LOG.jsonl 2026-08-20T20:15:00 and 20:22:00; THE_VISION.md section 7, corrected the same mark).
- The full "12 laws / What Never Bends" list, restated as thirteen laws in the 2026-08-17 pass with law 13 added on Kevin's own 2026-08-17 mark (THE_VISION.md section 7, lines 385-414; THE_VISION_2026-08-17.md section 13, lines 584-636). **Note:** the fact-lens audit found several of these laws stated as absolute with no enforcing mechanism (e.g. "no number reaches the player," "held is lawful" as a uniform claim) — see DEVELOPMENT for the enforcement-gap list. Included here as the design's own stated intent, not as confirmed-enforced state.
- Law 13 (Kevin's own mark, 2026-08-17): the world is to hold no legible record of the writer's behaviour. As of 2026-08-17, only the quantization clause actually holds; the store is append-only (no destroy act exists) and return cadence is in fact stored — the law is written in INTENDED tense, honestly self-audited as such (THE_VISION_2026-08-17.md section 13, lines 627-636).
- THE DISPLAY LAW: appearance may derive only from (1) where writing sits, (2) what the hand did with water/light there, (3) how long it's been there, (4) what it has crossed — never from what the writing says. Amended 2026-08-17 to add source 4, bound at the WRITE via byte-identity digest (THE_SHAPE_OF_THE_WHOLE.md, lines 155-192). Sources 1-3 and the "never" clause have no independent cited enforcement mechanism beyond source 4's digest.
- Black is structural: the machine's own words are never coloured; only the player's own writing takes colour (THE_SHAPE_OF_THE_WHOLE.md, lines 208-214).
- Tags are player-applied or position-derived only, never inferred from content (THE_SHAPE_OF_THE_WHOLE.md, lines 231-237).
- Time is the one currency that cannot be counterfeited: growth roots only across returned days; the world works only while the player is away; "held is lawful" governs every state (THE_VISION_2026-08-17.md section 5, lines 211-231).
- The weight in a world where nothing is scored is irreversibility: every seat declares in the first person what it costs the hand; cost is declared by the hand, never derived by a parser (THE_VISION_2026-08-17.md section 6, lines 235-296).

What is retired: invisibility as a virtue (world should be lavishly responsive, just never to content); stations/fractions/routing as core loop; teaching by absence (THE_GAME.md, lines 50-54).

## 3. THE GEOMETRY (settled, computed, cross-confirmed)

The container is a cuboctahedron (Fuller's vector equilibrium): 12 seats, 24 members (edges), 8 triangular faces, 6 square faces, 1 centre, 4 hexagonal circuits (THE_VISION_2026-08-17.md section 7, lines 309-346; THE_SOLID.md; THE_CENTRE.md §1,§7,§12). Pinned by Kevin's line joining ↓TANK–↓FILTER, confirmed by two independent derivations plus PyRigi (THE_SOLID.md, THE_CENTRE.md §1,§7,§12).

- 12 seats: 6 falling/water (TANK, DAM, FILTER, STATIONS, GROUND, DEEP) and 6 rising/light (LENS, HELIOSTAT, SEATING, OVERWINTERING, GARDEN, CAST) (THE_VISION_2026-08-17.md section 7).
- Every seam holds exactly one triangle (tool) and one square (window); a seam completes when both are inhabited by anything, whether or not it was walked itself — F5, Kevin's mark (THE_BOOT_2026-08-18.md; ALIGNMENT.md F5).
- Triangles (8) are structurally rigid. Squares (6) are the only places the solid can flex (the jitterbug) — confirmed by rigidity-matrix computation and independently by PyRigi (6 mechanisms, 6 squares, no strut spare); nothing may be sited in a square without gluing that hinge shut (THE_CENTRE.md §5,§12; THE_CENTRE_IN_PLAIN_WORDS.md §2).
- The centre is one edge-length from all twelve seats (radial equilibrium), the only position on all four circuits at once, and no edge reaches it — any design where a seat delivers something to the centre is geometrically false and refused on sight (THE_VISION.md section 4, lines 179-211; THE_CENTRE.md §3,§12). The centre stays unnamed by design — in solid.js it is a frozen object with no name/value/content field and no receive/to/send/set (THE_VISION.md, lines 204-209).
- Solid interior: 13 points (12 seats + centre), 36 lines (24 members + 12 radii), 14 faces, 14 volumes — 8 tetrahedral cells under triangles (rigid) + 6 pyramid cells under squares (deforming). Total volume 20 tetrahedral units (Fuller's VE number) (THE_CENTRE.md §13-16). Each pyramid cell is a TRADE — folding one square brings two seats together while pushing the other two apart by a fixed amount, measured via PyRigi (THE_CENTRE.md §14).
- The 12 radii are the world's only per-seat reading (distance from centre), free to change; at equilibrium every radius equals the edge length (THE_CENTRE.md §16).
- Root, water, and light each address all 24 members — not a 12/12 split. Direction (falling/rising) is a property of seats, not of light as a member kind — F1, Kevin's mark (THE_VISION_2026-08-17.md section 9, lines 442-449; THE_BOOT_2026-08-18.md; ALIGNMENT.md B6/F1).
- A level is one EDGE (member). 24 members + 6 diameters = 30 total levels (F10, Kevin's mark, 2026-08-17) (MARKS_LOG.jsonl 2026-08-19T00:50:00; THE_BOOT_2026-08-18.md line ~162; THE_GROWTH.md §2). **Note:** F10 settled six roots on circuit pairs but explicitly declined to resolve whether a diameter counts as a level — carried as open (see DEVELOPMENT).
- Water, light, and growth (root) are mechanics WITHIN every level, not three separate cycle-routes (MARKS_LOG.jsonl 2026-08-19T00:45:00).
- 2026-08-19 ruling: day one is the bottom SQUARE of the solid (FILTER·STATIONS·GROUND·DEEP), not a tetra cell as an earlier 2026-08-18 answer had it. TANK is an outside entry point via FILTER (direct member) or DAM (one step out) — Kevin's own words, quoted (MARKS_LOG.jsonl 2026-08-19T00:00:00; THE_BOOT_2026-08-18.md, 2026-08-19 amendment). This supersedes the earlier "one tetra cell" answer (ALIGNMENT.md B2, THE_BOOT_2026-08-18.md pre-amendment).
- ascent.html's ROOMS system (gesture-grouped, 5 rooms) is superseded in favor of the edge-by-edge/30-level shape, per Kevin's mark; code was left in place, flagged, not torn out blind (MARKS_LOG.jsonl 2026-08-19T00:50:00).

## 5. WORLD-GIVES-BACK / GIFT MECHANICS

"The world gives back" law: sentences settle in the ground in arrival order (oldest deepest); dropping a line over ground brings back one sentence verbatim, unlabelled, unranked, with no reason given, or runs out over open water (which stays open) — "recognition without extraction" (THE_VISION_2026-08-17.md section 4, lines 170-206; THE_GIFT.md).

S.kept stores every sentence committed at THE TANK verbatim, appended, never trimmed/reordered/read for content/counted at any door — distinct from the cast, which only carries counts/tallies never the writing itself (THE_GIFT.md 'What changed').

THE DEEP's sounding: a dropped line reads settled ground and returns one of the player's own sentences verbatim if it hits settled ground, or "runs out" if it doesn't — nothing records where the deeps are, because absence is not written down (THE_GIFT.md).

Kevin's naming of the writing-filter's three fractions — lack, transactionality, emotion — falling away leaves recognition as residue, not product: "a gift falls out; what remains is recognition." (THE_FILTER.md, Kevin quoted directly.) Whether the built water fractions should stay physical or become these three named fractions remains an open fork, not resolved (THE_FILTER.md 'THE FORK THIS OPENS').

## 6. SECOND PLAYER / GIFT LIBRARY

Second player mechanic: grow a fruit, hang it on the membrane, invite someone who walks the shallows (never sees words), takes the fruit and marks it a gift, which surfaces a recognition from the host's own past (THE_GAME.md, line 41).

Three arms ruling (Kevin's own words, quoted): personal writing development, commons extraction and development, and a gift library — each from a work surface of its own; all three belong inside final NESI, which is a world, not a menu of three games (THE_VISION.md section 1B, lines 60-96).
