What this edge is, and — first — what it is not

Edge 12 of 24: a shared convention, observed across two decisions that were made separately.

Group C (Memory/Suspension — Kevin's 2026-08-15 governance suspension of the vendor CLAUDE.md, MEMORY.md, and the machinery listed with them) and Group J (Retired builds — world3d and the corpus's other closed trees) sit on opposite sides of the vector-equilibrium diagram: one is a rule about what may act, the other is a record of what stopped being built. What connects them is not a shared subject — it is a shared form of words for how each decision treats what it sets aside.

FRAMING, STATED PLAINLY BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE ON THIS PAGE: this file draws a parallel between two decisions Kevin made independently — the 2026-08-15 suspension and the 2026-08-14 retirement of world3d.
