These bind the decision-surface chassis only — the same chassis this page is built on (DS_v1). Do not carry them into the game world; § G of the source doc names that split explicitly.

▾
Reading and deciding are two separate acts.
the object of THIS decision renders before its control, always
reading
Never gate content behind the same click that carries the decision. A file link is not a read surface — the text must be physically present in the widget. Recurred four times (2026-07-19 to 07-24) before it held.
§ B — feedback_widget_read_gated_behind_decision.md
▸
The decision list is derived, never padded.
the floor, not the ceiling — see the pacing rule, always room
reading
Don't invent options to hit a count; don't pad below what's actually live. How many appear at once is a separate question, governed by felt-read pacing (always-room), not by this rule.
§ B — chassis law "B3"
▸
A steer control copies to clipboard — never auto-sends.
regressed twice before it froze
reading
Never an in-widget composer either. "SEND/COPY = a mark copies" — the user's real chat input is the only place a decision actually gets typed.
§ B — feedback_click_populates_not_sends.md
▸
Every exit is pre-authored.
a surface that requires typing to leave is a build failure here
reading
Typing stays available — it's just never made the toll for leaving.
§ B — chassis law "B1"
▸
Manifests are pre-filled at authoring time.
never inferred at render
reading
Consequences show face-up before execution, not after — this is why CARDDATA in the real chassis is filled by hand, not computed live.
§ B
▸
The widget is the entire response — no surrounding prose.
scoped to this chassis, not a general interface rule
reading
Most UIs need surrounding text for error/empty states; this chassis's own completion state is built to cover that instead. Trivial tool-status narration is exempt.
§ B
▸
Plain language, not internal jargon.
"delta feels like a military call sign"
reading
"Delta file" → "holding file." The user's own coined terms (gate, tray, compost, mark, membrane, felt-read) stay as-is — only imported technical vocabulary gets translated.
§ B / § D — feedback_plain_language_widgets.md
▸
Never direct the user's physical body from inside a widget.
no "go paint" cards, even warm, even echoing their own plan
reading
Cards stay inside the system's own jurisdiction — marks, files, builds. "You've been applying a force to the user."
§ B — feedback_never_direct_the_body.md
▸
Never prompt for a felt/somatic field.
a blank field is a complete record
reading
"My body read is mine to hold." If volunteered unprompted, place it verbatim, no follow-up.

These bind the game world only. The widget room's rules do not transfer here — a status-overview panel that's correct for a widget reads as "a completion meter with better typography" in-world.

▾
Read the pixels, not the plan.
still an owed repair — see the always room
reading
Measure visibility in real screen pixels, never the data model. A game object once sat at 7.8px — present, data-correct, functionally invisible. No instrument in this corpus formally asserts anything is VISIBLE.
§ A — LEARNED law 5
▸
Ask the object its own state — never infer it from a symptom.
"landed" inferred from stillness, while still falling
reading
A controller once inferred "landed" from 20 frames of stillness while the character fell for 8 more seconds. A full day of screenshots captured 240m in the air, believed to be evidence.
§ A — LEARNED law 23
▸
Progression is physical, unnumbered — for this project specifically.
not general UX law; the accessibility gap is open, not solved
reading
No scores, ranks, bars, streaks. Built substitute: tank fill height, pool size, room brightness. No non-visual equivalent exists — a real accessibility cost this choice hasn't solved.
§ A — "progression as physics, unnumbered"
▸
A blank screen can vacuously pass a rule.
a test passable by absence tests nothing
reading
"No system text on screen" was once satisfied by a world with zero text primitives at all — incapacity mistaken for compliance.
§ A — LEARNED law 4
▸
An ambient element must behave identically regardless of content.
candidate — never ratified
reading
Test: would it act the same for a thousand words of grief as a thousand words of grocery lists? If not, it's a judgment wearing atmosphere.
§ A — "the indicator refusal," CANDIDATE_GRAMMAR.md
▸
Guards refuse the act; they never rewrite what's already rendered.
enforce by refusing, never by correcting the result after
reading
A word cap that truncated on `input` corrupted the player's own typed text mid-stream. Fixed by refusing the keystroke on `beforeinput` instead.
§ C — LEARNED law 24
▸
Social comparison, resistant exits, forced onboarding, dashboards — refused.
one lens's rulings for this build, not general UX claims
reading
Recorded as this project's own calls, not proven law — see the source doc § G for what each substitute actually is.
§ G — counsel/gamecraft
▸
Silent autosave, no "saved" indicator — a held tradeoff, not a free win.
the cost (a cleared store loses the world) is kept visible on purpose
reading
Resolves save-anxiety without a status line; the real cost is named rather than hidden behind the calm UI.

These bind public/authored pages and standalone tools — not the widget chassis, not the game world.

▾
No dark backgrounds on deployed public pages — for this audience.
not general UI law; this page follows the light-default choice
reading
Chosen for a projector/daylight-phone audience. Dark mode is a real accessibility accommodation elsewhere (photosensitivity, migraine) and an OLED battery win — this page has not built that option.
§ A / § E
▸
Demonstrate, don't describe.
cut text that tells the reader what the page will do
reading
Cut "why this matters," "how this works," and "what this page isn't" framing where the structure already makes the point.
§ D
▸
Stay first-person as your own lived instance.
offer the transferable pattern separately
reading
Second-person copy about one person's specific setup reads as projecting it onto the reader as something to copy.
§ D — "it reads as if the reader owns all of this"
▸
A public tool demonstrates by being used, not by being read.
this page follows this rule
reading
Standalone, no external dependencies, nothing graded by an AI, any donation ask stated once and never pushed.
§ D
▸
No generalized negative framing in prose.
open gap: safety/warning copy is an unresolved exception
reading
Negative form belongs only in a lint refusing one named thing, or the edge of a container.
§ D
▸
Voice: direct, no apology, no padding.
soft openings and generic encouragement don't land
reading
Sentences end where the work ends.

No separate governance track exists for "UI decisions" — these five apply to everything this corpus builds, regardless of which other room you came from.

▾
Only the user's own click/mark counts as a decision.
the AI maps both sides, never marks on the user's behalf
reading
The AI cannot read the somatic half of any felt-gate — fail-closed when uncertain.
§ F — feedback_map_both_sides.md
▸
Publishing, deploying, or deleting requires an explicit mark.
never inferred
reading
Editing/drafting/staging inside the workspace is free. Archiving beats deletion even when deletion is authorized.
§ F
▸
Don't build a UI feature unless it removes a real burden.
the load-test build gate
reading
If it disappeared tomorrow and nothing changed for the user, don't build it. Gates what gets built — never how much the user gets.
§ F — feedback_load_test_build_gate.md
▸
Pace to felt-read capacity, not to what could be generated.
one decision surface per turn, not a list of five
reading
If you're listing N options to choose among, you've already cost-shifted.
§ F
▸
No UI/visual claim is verified without a human actually looking.
a green suite is not a verdict — still an owed repair, see the game room
reading
"No WALKABLE without a stranger read" — machine-proved surfaces are unwitnessed until someone looks.
