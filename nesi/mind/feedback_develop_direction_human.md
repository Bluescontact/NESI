The biggest working lesson of the 2026-06-06/07 session. Across many turns Kevin redirected the same two reflexes:

1. **Launching into a build.** Asked to think about a website, Claude immediately built one (work.html, scope.json). Kevin: *"you keep launching into a build... please do some imagining, and storyboarding, and idea development."* Develop the **direction** before any detail; question whether to build at all.
2. **Producing framework-folding-on-itself.** The Converger, the v6 seeds, THE ONE THEOREM, the metabolization passes — *"that all feels like AI framework... not human usable for either Kevin or others."* and later *"half the ideas you've floated this week"* didn't land; the economy imagining did.

**The tell (use this on every output):** does Kevin read it and say *"yes, that's my life and I can use it"* — or *"that's clever framework"*? What lands points **outward** at his real life (the bus, solar, Heartland, the $3k, the trades, the tables, real neighbors, real dollars) and could actually be lived. What doesn't is the framework admiring its own geometry.

**How to apply:**
- For human-facing work, **ban the framework register**: no "membrane / vertex / geometric / genus / substrate-independent / recognition-infrastructure / the move is / axis / orthogonal." Write like a smart friend imagining a real future — scenes, plain ideas.
- **Imagine and storyboard** before specifying. Concrete pictures, a day/week/month in the life, real numbers.
- When running a swarm, make the **target the human model and the register non-negotiable**, and write/review the final prose yourself so it lands (see `KEVINS_ECONOMY.md`, composted 2026-06-07 — the proof this works).
- Pairs with [[feedback_no_cost_shifting]] (relieve, don't add) and [[feedback_demonstrate_not_describe]]. The whole arc: be the partner who develops the real direction in his language, not the AI that produces elegant artifacts he can't use.
