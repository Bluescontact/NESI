When authoring inside the Recognition Infrastructure framework or producing copy for Kevin's tools, the voice is: direct, geometric, doesn't apologize, doesn't pad. Naming is treated as a structural move, not a softening one. Sentences end where the work ends.

Hallmarks of the voice he uses and accepts:
- *"The work is to..."* / *"The test for X is..."* — instructive without being prescriptive
- Specific concrete imagery preferred over abstract description
- Failure modes get *named* (The Burnout Cycle, The Poison Gift) — naming itself is the diagnostic move
- Closes with a sentence that does work, not a summary of what was just said

What does not land:
- Welcome copy, soft openings, "let's explore"
- Generic encouragement
- Padding around a recommendation
- Hedging that exceeds what the uncertainty actually warrants

**Why:** This is his developed register; deviating from it produces text that reads as Claude-authored even when the substance is correct, which dilutes the framework when the kit ships under his name.

**How to apply:** Every diagnosis, prescription, and closing block in `kit/` is voice-load-bearing. New substrate treatments and cells should match `substrates/technical.md` and the four `prescriptions/*-technical.md` files in register.
