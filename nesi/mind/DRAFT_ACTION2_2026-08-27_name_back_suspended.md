## What this action is not — corrected after a ground pass

The first draft of this proposal offered Kevin a pre-picked shortlist of
three candidates to choose from. An adversarial ground pass found that this
repeats a pattern the corpus has already paid for once: a session curating
what counts as worth showing Kevin is the near-identical shape of a defect
logged in an earlier session (one dropped line returning as four
constitutional rulings), and the fix adopted then was **THE DROP RULE** —
*file drops against the corpus, then stop; no tiles, no forks, no ruling
offered.* `LEARNED.md` law 12 (*"the field, not the doorway"*) and law 8
(*"filter, never a queue — Kevin tunes rules, not items"*) both name exactly
this failure. A shortlist, even an honestly disclaimed one, is a relevance
ruling by another name — the suspension's own escape clause asks a session
to *"say so out loud and ask,"* not to pre-build a menu in anticipation of a
question Kevin hasn't asked.

## What this action is

One narrow, unranked act: **the backup file's shape is named to Kevin,
without picking winners from inside it, and he is handed one open,
unweighted question.**

The shape, stated plainly and without ranking: the backup holds twelve laws
(six of them — 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 11 — already carried forward and enforced; the
other six — 1, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12 — not found live anywhere in this pass), a
verification-bar section (likely redundant with what's already live), a
where-the-rest-lives routing table (confirmed stale), and 148 suspended
memory entries never checked against the current corpus at all — the
largest part of the suspension this pass could not rule out as
already-carried-forward.

**The question:** does Kevin want to (a) read the backup himself, on his own
time, (b) have it surfaced to him in full and unranked — every line, no
session judgment about which lines matter — or (c) leave it exactly as
suspended, with nothing further done. All three are complete answers. A
non-answer is also a complete answer.

## Why this shape

- Matches the suspension's own escape clause exactly — Kevin asks and names,
  a session doesn't pre-select.
- Costs nothing until he chooses, and this time "costs nothing" is scoped
  correctly: it's not just "no file changed," it's "no pre-filtered field
  handed to him either" — the two failure modes `LEARNED.md` laws 8 and 12
  actually name.
- Still avoids the failure this corpus paid for once already at the
  adjacent layer: a session ruling its own authority over something that's
  Kevin's call alone (`authority-check`, `LEARNED.md` laws 20 and 25).

## What this is NOT

Not a request to lift the suspension. Not an argument that the suspension
was wrong. Not a recommendation on which lines to name back, and — corrected
from the first draft — not even an unranked-but-selected shortlist. The
twelve-laws / verification-bar / routing-table findings above are reported
as **research into what's already redundant**, offered so Kevin isn't asked
to re-read material this pass could already rule out — they are not offered
as a menu to pick from.
