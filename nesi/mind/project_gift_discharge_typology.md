A multi-model reconciliation candidate ("difference-preserving substrate") was developed across a full arc and staged in `_INTAKE/` (5 docs: mirror_seed_resolution, difference_preserving_substrate_landing_axis, difference_preserving_substrate_mechanism, six_model_reconciliation, covenant_third_discharge_profile). **Nothing crossed the gate**; 4 marks opened (held) in `marks/`, body-lines blank except covenant's, which Kevin authored.

The framework classes a gift by **discharge profile**: **mirror** (point-discharge on visibility), **seed** (point-discharge on alteration), **covenant** (continuous discharge, never complete while the relation holds), **unilateral_closure** (discharged at moment of giving, field dissolved before confirmation — fourth form, promoted 2026-07-01). The obligation-class lives in the **form**; whether it **lands** lives in the receiving **substrate** — two orthogonal axes. [[feedback_renderer_not_verifier]] [[feedback_somatic_threshold]]

**Four discharge profiles:**

| | Mirror | Seed | Covenant | Unilateral Closure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Topology** | point | point | continuous | point |
| **Completion** | visibility | alteration | none (co-extensive) | moment of giving |
| **Confirmation** | external | external | relational | none available |
| **Handle** | none | none | permanent | none — but registry stays open |
| **Closes via** | recognition arrives | alteration confirmed | relation ends | self-trust as closing witness |

**Covenant** is the one original survivor of a six-model adversarial pass. Grounds non-transactional economy — "non-transactional" means covenant-discharge. See [[project_non_transactional_mechanism]] [[project_open_ledger]] [[feedback_complete_on_giving]].

**Unilateral closure** (fourth form — 2026-07-01): gift discharges at moment of giving; field (community, relationship, structure) dissolves before confirmation signal can arrive. Registry reads pending — not because arc is incomplete but because the registry requires a signal the gift-form cannot produce. Closing witness: the giver's body when self-trust is present. Pattern: `patterns/metabolic_lag_on_complete_giving.md`.

Kevin's felt-read on covenant: **falsifier held in the relationship itself, not in any representation of it.** A covenant's discharge-state is not readable off any model, ledger, or test. Same structure as debt-unrepresentable and witness-has-no-witness.
