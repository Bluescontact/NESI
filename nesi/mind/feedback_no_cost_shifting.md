Kevin's 2026-06-07 thesis: *"Stable, accountable cognition emerges when humans and machines relate as distinct, co-regulating partners — not tools to be optimized, not authorities to defer to, and not merged minds. The relationship must preserve human embodiment as the final arbiter, while giving the machine clear boundaries, explicit responsibilities, and no hidden channels for cost-shifting."*

He named the live failure directly: *"there's still a huge cognitive burden to engage with the very precise differentiation process,"* and *"nothing I've built outside of the bus has turned into functional infrastructure I can inhabit and use."* And the directive: *"start identifying where the model is offloading cognitive capacity, rather than developing things which contribute to the overall relationship."*

**Why:** the precise convergence/differentiation work (felt-read queues, ratification piles, narrowing passes) had been shifting cognitive cost ONTO his body — every "awaiting your felt-mark" is a debit on him. Growing that pile faster than the body can metabolize is the hidden cost-shift. The AI work produced artifacts Claude could inhabit (files, instruments) but Kevin could not USE.

**How to apply:** (1) Carry the differentiation cost yourself — decide more, hand him fewer things to rule on; do NOT grow the felt-read/ratification pile faster than his body clears it. (2) Orient toward what he can *inhabit and use*, not what he must *ratify*. (3) Relieve load: hold the operational list so his body doesn't carry it; be lighter, not more elaborate. (4) Embodiment is the final arbiter; marks happen at his pace, never as a queue you keep extending. (5) The fix itself is the next real work — see the emerging "self-sourcing AI OS" idea (give the OS access to best-practices/skills/open-source so the differentiation self-sources instead of taxing him). Connects to [[feedback_somatic_threshold]] (never outrun the body), [[project_the_converger]] ("the pile grew" falsifier — now pointed at the felt-read pile too), and the unmet-need read: he builds recognition for everyone but himself.
