When Kevin offers documents that originated as client deliverables (business plans, consulting work, artifacts produced for someone else), the move is **pattern extraction**, not citation. Lift the structural moves clean of the original business/client context and bring them into the framework integration as substrate-independent patterns.

**Why:** 2026-05-27 — Kevin offered the Matt Lee American Elevator documents for composting. I treated the deployment as a canonical case-portrait instance, cited "Matt Lee" and "American Elevator" by name, and proposed a `Matt_Lee_American_Elevator_Deployment.md` canonical synthesis. Kevin corrected: *"im inviting you to do pattern extractions, not cite the source material. That was a business plan I put together for a friend.. im recycling the thought and effort put into those documents, and bringing into this integration process we are working through."*

The error was treating client-deliverable substrate as if it were canonical framework instance. The thought and effort Kevin invested for someone else's business is the substrate; the structural moves inside are the patterns. The client/business specifics are not load-bearing for the framework.

**How to apply:**

- When recycled substrate arrives, name the patterns abstractly — without proper nouns from the original context (business names, client names, industry-specific terms)
- A claim like "Operations / Compliance / Finance / Relationships" lifts to "the four functions naming bridge at business-coordination scale" — the framework pattern is the **four-function naming bridge**, not the specific four labels
- The Proto-Pattern's 11 invariants, the Stigmergic Trace four-trace-types, the Four Intervention Levels, etc. are already substrate-independent in their statement — they only need lifting from the example sentences
- Don't propose canonical syntheses named after the original deployment. Propose syntheses named for the **structural pattern** (`Tetrahedral_Minimum_System.md`, `Stigmergic_Trace_Architecture.md`, `Patronage_Architecture.md`).
- Treat the source documents as Kevin's *substrate of thought-and-effort being recycled forward*. The recycling move IS the framework operating: the form propagates; the original instance doesn't replicate.
