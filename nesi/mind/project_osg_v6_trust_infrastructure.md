The frame that arrived 2026-06-25 after a full session of building and zooming out:

**OSG v6 is trust infrastructure.** Not a portfolio, not a recognition display, not a personal dashboard. Trust infrastructure: the structures that make accounting unnecessary because trust is doing the work the ledger usually does.

**The design constraint:** Work visible, person protected. The creator economy converts face/body/life into attention products. Kevin's model is the opposite — recognition comes through the work, the person stays behind the membrane. The site should give less of Kevin, not more. Every move toward personal-dashboard-detail is extraction surface.

**Zero-or-gain structure:** The choice available to any visitor is: zero (nothing happens, no cost, no debt, no obligation) or gain for everyone (something flows). No loss option. No implicit debt from having been seen or welcomed. Every design element that creates social cost for NOT engaging erodes this.

**Upstream boundary:** Before any page, tool, or connection — find where extraction dynamics want to enter and reroute them there, upstream. Not scattered defensive mechanisms. One boundary move that changes what can flow in. Extraction resistance is the primary design constraint.

**Tools already exist.** The goal is not invention. YouTube economy, creator economies, open source tools, mutual aid networks — all have directly applicable tools. The job: find the gaps, identify which existing tools fit without introducing new extraction dynamics, connect at those gaps.

**What's wrong with what was built 2026-06-25:** index.html and request.html were built in inhabitant-register but still in "make my life visible" mode. Flow-grid showing Kevin's personal details, dashboard mockup with even more personal surface — moving in the wrong direction. These are staging, not the thing.

**The site worth building:** Makes the work visible and the person protected. Has an upstream boundary that reroutes extraction. Uses existing tools at the gaps. Creates conditions where accounting isn't needed because the form of exchange doesn't require it.
