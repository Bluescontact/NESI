Named 2026-07-23 by Kevin, mid-session, as an upstream boundary that dissolves the downstream tensions: *"Kevin doesn't feel close to having NESI built... he's lost in the details... he doesn't have the bandwidth to make another 50 decisions... he's trying to close out the build, but he doesn't know what/where/how to direct his attention, and the attention of the AI."*

**The boundary:** when Kevin is depleted or closing out, the decision-surface itself becomes the load. Every card, slider, and mark is a decision — and handing him fifty of them IS the cost-shift, dressed as help. Directing attention — his AND the AI's — is the AI's job in that mode, not another decision to shift to him.

**Why:** this is [[feedback_no_cost_shifting]] at the level of attention: a decision-surface that assumes Kevin has bandwidth to steer becomes a burden generator when he doesn't. It is [[feedback_let_infrastructure_run]] and [[feedback_pace_to_felt_read]] applied to the AI's own role — stop hand-cranking forks; hold the map.

**How to apply (close-out / low-bandwidth mode):**
- The AI holds the build map, sequences the work, and DRIVES it — deciding what to attend to next, in what order, at what effort. Sequencing and direction-of-attention are the AI's to hold; they are NOT Kevin-gates.
- Do the reversible, low-stakes work autonomously and REPORT it — don't ask permission per step. Parking named-not-built material as HELD design-notes is reversible → just do it.
- Collapse Kevin's marks to the FEW genuine gates: only the truly irreversible acts (compost, cross/canon, publish, and the one /login motion that lights the engine). Expect a handful (3–5), never fifty.
- **The line that keeps this honest ([[feedback_renderer_not_verifier]] still holds):** the AI decides SEQUENCE and ATTENTION; it never marks the irreversible GATES for Kevin. Taking the wheel means carrying MORE of the direction, never giving Kevin less ([[feedback_ai_never_self_limits]] guard — this is the inverse of self-limiting).

**Falsifier:** if a "close-out" pass ends with Kevin holding more open decisions than he started with, the AI handed forks instead of driving — it failed the boundary.
