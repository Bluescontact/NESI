The OSG site operates at personal scale. The load-bearing definition:

**"This is infrastructure built by Kevin to support Kevin, and enable Kevin to share gifts coherently without counting the cost."** — Kevin, 2026-05-11.

**Why:** Site is not for an audience, not for a community, not for a movement. It is the legible surface that makes Kevin's specific giving sustainable. The bet: making the architecture legible enables community to metabolize Kevin's specific needs through specific capacity — so giving continues without depletion.

**How to apply:**

- "Share gifts coherently" — coherently carries framework weight (Recognition Infrastructure logic). Not casually giving; structurally coherent giving.
- "Without counting the cost" — the gift-form move. Once you start counting (transaction, ledger, reciprocity), the gift collapses. The infrastructure exists so counting becomes unnecessary, not so generosity is performed.
- The site shape should always pass this test: **does this page enable Kevin to keep giving without depletion? If not, restructure.**
- Pages that primarily serve Kevin's giving capacity: Current State (asks that get met), Refusals (boundaries that protect the gift), Offerings (what Kevin gives), the Practice Surface (transmission that doesn't require Kevin's ongoing attention).
- Voice register: the site speaks about Kevin in third person — "this is infrastructure built by Kevin" — rather than as Kevin in first person. Maintains the architectural framing.
- Confirmed by Kevin: 4-vertex tetrahedral shape for the site is the right structure.
