For per-item decisions in a widget (promote/compost/hold, etc.), Kevin asked for a slider/toggle he can click on or off per option, rather than a row of separate buttons.

**Why:** Asked directly during the [[project_intake_triage_2026-07-15]] triage session after three batches of button-based cards.

**How to apply:** Build decision widgets with a toggle-switch control per candidate (e.g. a segmented pill or on/off slider for promote/compost/hold) instead of `<button>` rows. He still types his actual decision in chat rather than relying on the widget to transmit it — see [[feedback_triage_batch_pace]] — so the toggle is a visual/interaction preference for the display, not a new data-transmission mechanism.
