Not the whole corpus — that's unbounded and not one piece. The stage is the
one coherent body of claims this session's own most recent work produced,
treated as a single "draft" in `full-development`'s sense:

- **The held completion-condition table** — the category articulation (A
  closable-by-reading, now done; C needs-a-build-mark; D Kevin's-decisions-
  alone; E permanently-unconfirmable) built from the panel's convergence and

  html`'s Newly Resolved #3 and routing table, and from this document.

Together these are one movement: build a map, read the map five ways, verify
a slice of it. `full-development`'s method exists for exactly this — testing

was, and this stage has never been audited or grounded, only built and read.

## The governing claim this stage rests on, unstated but load-bearing

No file says this in so many words, but every one of the five lens readings
converged on the same shape without being shown each other's output — which
means the corpus is currently *behaving as if* this claim is true:

> **Mapping the corpus onto a geometry, reading it through five independent
> lenses, and verifying a slice of its citations is itself forward movement
> — the correct next thing to have done at this point.**

This is exactly the kind of claim `full-development`'s ground stage exists
to test: confident, structurally reasonable, agreed on by five independent
readers, and never actually attacked. Fuller called it a map that "has not
yet been walked." Game-craft called it "the door out of the map room hasn't
been tested." Cowan called it "the faces... remain implicit." Change-composite
named the sharpest form of it directly: *"movement here is nearly all
mapping... a geometry of relationships, awaiting the load the map says it can
bear."* Five lenses naming the same gap independently is convergent evidence
the gap is real — it is not yet evidence that more mapping was the right
move, versus an increasingly well-organized way of not yet building anything
game2d-facing.

## Stage-by-stage scope

### 1 · Compost and scatter

Break the stage's own claims into separate items, detached from the order
they arrived in (VE24 → panel → citations): every corroborated cross-edge
finding, every lens's "what completes it" line, every named-not-routed gap
in the routing table, every "Kevin's call" item, judged on its own merit —
not on how well the whole arc reads as a narrative of progress.

### 2 · Inventory

For each composted item: what does it assert, what does it depend on, what
depends on it. Candidate decoration already visible without running the
stage: does every one of the 24 edges' "Newly Resolved" findings actually
get used by anything downstream, or do some sit as verified-but-inert facts
nothing currently reads? (The relationship map's own "Would-resolve-if-
combined" rows — 12+15, 12+16 — are exactly this kind of unused-dependency
candidate, named but never acted on.)

### 3 · Audit — adversarial, fan out

Test the governing claim above and its supporting quotes for: **unearned**
(five lenses agreeing is convergence, not proof the map was the right thing
to build); **unfalsifiable** (is "the map is whole, it hasn't been walked
yet" a claim any future state could ever contradict, or does it stay true
forever regardless of what happens next — in which case it's an observation,
not a finding); **circular** (does "mapping now enables future walking"
justify itself only by deferring its own test to a future that never has to
arrive); **metaphor doing argumentative work it hasn't earned** (Fuller's
jitterbug, Cowan's faces, game-craft's door — three physical metaphors for
"more work is needed," none yet cashed out as a concrete next action with a
name and a cost).

### 4 · Ground — fan out

Hunt for the case that kills the governing claim. Candidate counterexample,
named here so the ground pass has a real target rather than starting cold:
**a corpus that keeps building instruments to read itself, and keeps finding
those instruments need more instruments to read them (VE24 needed a
relationships pass; the relationships pass needed a citation-verification
pass; this document is itself a third layer down), can produce an unbounded
sequence of legitimate-feeling next steps that never touches the live build
a player would actually walk through** (`nesi/game2d/index.html`). The ground
pass's job is to find out whether that pattern is already happening here, or
whether this document is itself the fourth instance of it.

### 5 · Diverge — fan out, five real shapes

At minimum: **(a)** stop mapping, spend the next session entirely inside
`nesi/game2d/` on a player-facing feature; **(b)** build the traversal
instrument named as category C (a way to walk the VE24 graph edge-to-edge
rather than read it as 24 flat files); **(c)** Kevin closes the remaining
named decisions (category D) directly and nothing further gets built until
he does; **(d)** a hybrid — one small, concretely-scoped game2d slice this
session, with the VE24 material held exactly where it stands, neither
extended nor abandoned.

### 6 · Dream

Ignore the stage's own account of itself. One sentence: what is the one idea
here worth a player's time, or Kevin's, that isn't "here is a better-organized
map"? Candidate, offered for the actual dream pass to confirm or replace: **a
mechanism that lets a finding from a lens read change something in the live
build the same session it's found** — collapsing the gap every one of the
five lenses named, between naming a gap and a hand closing it, from
"documented, awaiting a mark" to "seen and acted on in one sitting." What
dies if this whole VE24+panel arc loses 40%? Likely candidate for the cut:
the citation-precision layer (item A) — real, careful work, but the kind that
could be automated or skipped without losing what the panel itself found
load-bearing.

### 7 · Converge, 8 · Route and place, 9 · Verify

Not run — these are Kevin's calls (7) or downstream of a build decision (8, 9)
that hasn't been made. Named here only so the manifest is honest about what
it is not yet claiming to have done.
