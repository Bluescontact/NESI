## Ten deposits, collapsed to the load they bear

Ten sources — a game-world constitution, a 21-year wiki, a gift-economy
graveyard, a myth critic, a banker-turned-ecologist, a solo terminal doctrine,
and four more — were read adversarially and processed into 117 cards. Read
across each other, they converge on eight laws none of them named alone. The
spine is the collapse; the deposits are the evidence.

**10** deposits · **117** cards · **8** convergence laws · **4** marked ·
Sweetwater gate · **113** waiting at the gate

Cards order nothing. They wait at the gate. The mark is Kevin's.

### I · The spine — eight convergence laws

A law earns a place here only when two or more deposits arrive at the same
shape independently — different fields, different stakes, no citation between
them. Independent arrival raises confidence, not novelty. Each citation chip
drops to the source card below.

**LAW 1 — The render never promotes itself.** The interface describes the
world; it does not become the world. Fullerton finds it in etymology
(business, the ownable noun, mistaken for commerce, the live verb —
Whitehead's fallacy of misplaced concreteness). The Living World spec writes
it as runtime law at five separate layers: presentation may never substitute
for operational state. Sweetwater runs it diagnostically — "friction is a
signal of incompetence" is the epistemology that ends in compliance
infrastructure. DivineOS proves the enforcement point: police the one act
that changes state, and the conversation stays free. This is Terminal Law
found already working in four unrelated fields.
`fullerton·1` `gov-engines·2` `sweetwater·3` `divineos·2` `gift·anti/seams`

**LAW 2 — Two gates, not one — readiness is not truth.** Entry and exit are
separate validations. The Living World spec is strictest: operational
validation ("could this be attempted") never confirms outcome ("did it
actually land") — execution is an attempt; validated outcome is reality.
DivineOS splits VERIFIED from CLOSED and asks three closure questions of
everything built. The P2P corpus inverts the door: stop predicting worth, vet
the actual output afterwards — which makes every mark provisional. SQLite's
survival asymmetry is the same gate rotated: use is cheap, modification is
expensive. NESI's Escalation Rule gates entry only; the exit gate is the
named gap.
`gov-engines·1` `divineos·4` `divineos·7` `p2p·8` `gift·12` `opencivics·10`

**LAW 3 — Absence must be typed, and failure must be loud.** A check that
cannot run must look different from a check that passed. DivineOS paid seven
silent breaks to learn it (except: pass + exit 0). hledger's balance
assertions make blank a positive, dated record rather than a missing file.
The P2P/Sensorica post-mortem shows the heaviest extractors appearing as
near-absent in a voluntary ledger — a gap is a reading, not missing data.
Shimotsuki's profile disclosure was indistinguishable at the point of contact
from no disclosure. OpenCivics' entire sanction mechanism is a zero-byte file
returning HTTP 200. Trustroots is the lone schema that stores "no" as a value
with an expiry.
`divineos·6` `gift·3` `gift·10` `p2p·12` `shimotsuki·2` `systemed·13`
`opencivics·anti/zero-byte`

**LAW 4 — No cost vanishes — it lands at 90°, on someone.** The smuggle
detectors, converging from physics, accounting, and myth. Fuller's
precession: nothing cancels; the resultant squirts out to the side, which
forbids the sentence "this costs nothing." Fullerton: an externality is a
debt deferred inside the same system, compounding whether or not it's
booked. REA accounting refuses any gain-event without a paired loss at write
time. McNamara's extraction signature asks one typed question — who pays to
hold this connection open? — and his two-node rule makes a card with no
return path extractive by construction. Sweetwater's seven-link compliance
chain shows each link locally defensible and the whole totalitarian. The P2P
corpus names the three smuggle siblings: unnamed cost, unnamed beneficiary,
unnamed purpose.

`gift·2` `sweetwater·6` `gov-engines·7`

**LAW 5 — Escalation is a number, not a mood.** Every working gate in the
corpus routes on an observable, never a feeling. DivineOS computes ceremony
from surface features of the desire as typed, "precisely to avoid
self-assessment circularity." OpenCivics has the corpus's one real
threshold — $2,500 routes to full assembly — and its binary-deliverable rule
removes the need for honesty entirely. McNamara turns Terminal Law into a
checkable ratio: governance must review faster than the plan executes.
Ostrom adds repetition as a trigger regardless of content; the licence
graveyard proves the corollary — membership questions are gateable, magnitude
questions escalate by construction, because in twenty years nobody ever
filled in the percentage.

**LAW 6 — The ledger is a byproduct of the act, never a report.** Any record
that depends on remembering to write it will have the shape of OpenCivics'
three in-house grant rounds: 58% of the money, zero named recipients. The
mark must be emitted by the act of installing — org-mode's state changes log
themselves, with abandonment priced highest (a cancel costs a sentence; a
done costs a stamp). Stigmergy makes each mark carry its own next move. REA
stores events only and derives every total, so no counter can drift. History
and memory stay separate objects — validated record versus per-entity
belief — and the only honest edit to either is a dated append with a stance,
never a silent revision. Two deposits arrived at that append independently.

**LAW 7 — Anything may run the sequence; only Kevin makes the mark.**
Shimotsuki's four stations — origination, interrogation, correction,
ratification — allow the middle two to be released entirely; release either
endpoint and the work stops being yours. Her escalation moves who drafts,
never who ratifies. Fullerton's abduction claim is the same boundary from the
AI side: the leap — does this convergence mean anything, should NESI
change — cannot be delegated to whichever agent wrote the card. Sweetwater's
stage six names the failure mode when it slips: the return that becomes a
mandate, the gift that starts guaranteeing its own correct use. Ruddick
compresses it to fourteen words: routing may be global; redemption stays
local. No imported card arrives pre-marked.

**LAW 8 — Build nothing you will not count.** The warning law, delivered
four times by demonstration. The P2P wiki: 25,661 pages, median inbound links

### III · Anti-nutrient register

What the corpus warns against, mostly by demonstration. Read every deposit's
confidence at the discount its own production earns — nearly all of this is
single-author, unreviewed, and internally self-citing.

**DivineOS · the empty keepers.** Their salvage ritual was immaculate — an
87KB append-only ledger — and its keepers/ folder is empty. The ritual
ported nothing. Import composting instruments, never the ritual alone; and
their doctrine layer couldn't agree whether it had 14, 15, 17, or 18 fired
truths, because nothing watches the prose.

**P2P Wiki · never reward mark frequency.** The one value formula the wiki
ships multiplies logged contribution by a reputation coefficient and
incentivizes frequent logging — both inputs the participant controls
cheaply, with gaming never named as a risk. A streak counter would be this
defect. High mark frequency with low real-world change should trigger Deep
Review. And no page anywhere describes a dispute path — which, given
Sensorica, looks causal.

**systemeducation · the empty toolbox.** A category with no cards under it
is a false gate: it advertises capacity that doesn't exist and spends
attention discovering that. The ontology's quality is exactly what makes the
emptiness invisible. If NESI ships a gate, the cards are in it at ship time.
Corollaries: rendering is not recording (the best taxonomy is a JPEG), and
speech never written down becomes unquotable.

**Gift graveyard · do not write the philosophy page.** Every project with a
strong stated stance exported it to a website now larger and better
maintained than its software. Do not admit an extensible card format —
stated values held privately are indistinguishable from no values once the
author is tired. Do not import seamlessness: a gate that admits smoothly
admits everything. And a restart wearing the costume of a diagnosis is the
highest-frequency move in the territory — when a card stalls, the permitted
output is an obituary, not a re-plan.

**OpenCivics · the zero-byte sanction.** The constitution's only consequence
clause points at a zero-byte file that returns HTTP 200 — a named escalation
pointing at an empty document is indistinguishable from having no sanction.
Audit content, never reachability. A value that cannot be run as a test is
not admitted to the register. And the refusals — most of a gate's actual
output — are the one party with no representation in any document.

**Governing Engines · a machine's doctrine.** Every actor in 129 pages is an
NPC. Nowhere does it ask whether an entity has capacity, consents, or is
depleted — correct for a game engine, a serious omission if imported
wholesale into a system that governs a person's days. Import the validation
architecture; do not inherit its silence on capacity. Uniform rigor is not
uniform importance, and the document audited everything except itself.

**Shimotsuki · the consensus is the hazard.** 137 likes of reassurance
resolved a structural worry socially instead of structurally; the one
respondent who declined to reassure produced the only card. A deposit that
arrives pre-approved should be read twice. Disclosure is cheap; the stations
are the work. And a disclosure that covers everything distinguishes
nothing — the mark must fire on the mediated act specifically, or it becomes
wallpaper.

**Fullerton · a monoculture citing itself.** Six essays, one worldview
restated in different keys — internal consistency is not corroboration.
Quantum entanglement does more metaphysical work than decoherence permits.
At least one recommendation carries an undisclosed commercial stake. The
prophetic register is persuasion technology, not evidence — extract the
doctrine; don't let the delivery's confidence transfer to the claims.

**Mallinckrodt · precision that doesn't survive a second look.** "A
14-month forensic audit of frontier AI platforms confirmed…" — stated in the
same confident register as everything verifiable, with no method, platform,
or auditor named. If NESI voice-matches this register, it must not import
the habit of stating unverifiable specifics in the tone of verifiable ones.
Most of the deposit is secondhand, and its Card 10 is the visible template
for scoring title-only evidence Low.

**Sweetwater · the unaudited baseline.** Campbell's monomyth is imported
wholesale as ground truth — a Western single-author synthesis treated as
neutral cross-cultural fact. The stage-by-stage work holds regardless, but
"one true original hero's journey" is a rhetorically useful frame, not
anthropology. The named-individual psychology (Altman) has real force and
zero falsifiability.
