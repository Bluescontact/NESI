Kevin cannot fully explore/understand widget content without triggering a card-open that is bundled with steer buttons (GO/WARMER/PLAINER/HOLD/DROP). The chassis v4 pattern — one plain line, click header to open to explanation + steering in the same panel — collapses "let me read this" and "make me decide this" into one gesture. He named it directly: "youve gated the decision behind a door i cant see through."

**Why:** He wants to fully explore all content in a widget without being forced to make any decision to see it. A card that only reveals its substance when opened, with steering buttons sitting right there once open, reads as if reading = starting to decide. This is a stronger, more specific requirement than [[feedback_widget_gate_visibility]] (cards with real stakes default open) — it's not about which cards start open, it's about separating the read surface from the decision surface entirely.

**How to apply:** When building a widget that surfaces developed content (substrate briefs, pattern development, anything with real explanatory text) — render the full content in plain, always-visible prose first (no click required, no card metaphor), and only THEN offer a separate, clearly distinct decision-cards section below it for the actual choices. Do not make "see the content" and "steer/mark/decide" live in the same collapsible unit. If a summary-only view is used for space, the full text must be reachable by a plain expand that carries no decision buttons — reading must never be gated by a control that also commits.

**Recurrence, 2026-07-21 (same day, next turn):** violated again immediately after being written — a "pass 2 vs hold" card was rendered with its plain-words explanation still living inside the click-to-open `.card-bd`, gated behind the same control as its steer buttons. Kevin's correction: "remember how the last session i mentioned decisions gated through doors i couldn't see through... same thing here. I want to see everything about the decision before making it." Concrete cost surfaced this time: he intended to hand the widget content to a *different* Claude session to draft the pass-2 prompt, and the gated summary didn't carry enough detail for that handoff to work. **Sharper rule:** any content Kevin might need to relay elsewhere (to another agent, into a prompt, to himself later) must be complete and plain on first render — never assume he'll click before deciding whether the content is even worth extracting.

**Recurrence, 2026-07-23 (Gift Seed pass):** violated AGAIN — the deliverable itself (a 497-word gift prompt) was placed inside a v5 L1 click-to-open card ("Read the seed right here") that also carried a copy-steer slider. Kevin: "i should be able to read the whole output in the widget without a click of decision." **The trap is structural in chassis v5:** L1 cards are closed-by-default, so the natural place to drop developed content IS a card — and that is exactly the violation. **Hard rule for deliverable output:** when the widget's whole point is to surface a produced artifact (a prompt, a draft, a brief, a document), that artifact renders as an ALWAYS-OPEN reading panel above the decision cards — never in an L1 card, never behind a "▸ click to open", never sharing a unit with a slider. The v5 four-layer form governs *decisions*; the produced *output* is not a decision and must not be filed as one. A copy button on the reading panel is fine (utility, not a steer); a slider on it is not.

Kevin: "why cant i read it in the response you offered?" **Sharpened rule:** the always-open reading panel is not just "don't hide it behind a click" — it is "the produced text must be physically present, in full, inside the widget." A file link is not a read surface; embedding it is. When a session produces a document, embed the whole thing (styled `.read` panel above the glance) — do not assume the file link counts as making it readable. Fix landed same turn.
