**Kevin's mark, 2026-08-22** (against `CLAUDE.md` FRAMING, admitted into
`nesi/mind/LEARNED.md` as law 27 the same day):

> *"i think i need to institute a new filter. I want to strip negtive framing

This is the same law, sited on OSG's own working surface rather than

---

## WHAT IT SAYS HERE

The `unrouted-gifts` skill already carries this shape without having named it
as a law: capacity built and never sited isn't a loss, it's *unrouted* —
present, real, and waiting for the deposit that would carry it in. Law 27
extends that same reading to every OSG surface, not only past-build audits:

- **The Field Kit Engine.** A structural audit that finds no clean
  recognition pattern for a civic or consumer dispute is not handed to the
  practitioner-ally as "this doesn't resolve." It's handed back as the
  specific place the gift economy hasn't reached yet — which is itself the
  actionable finding the Field Kit exists to produce.
- **The deploy loop** (`project_osg_deploy_loop.md`). A page that fails the
  5-question integration test, or a push withheld at gate 2, is read as
  where the loop currently doesn't close — not as a failed page. What it
  names is which upstream condition (Kevin's own mark, a missing node
  content field) the loop is still waiting on.
- **The Stage-0 zeros** (`project_osg_v6_build_spec.md`). All four are
  open and Kevin-held by design. Their still-open state is not the build
  behind schedule — it's the exact record of which deposit (his threshold
  line, his self-authored node content, the governor boundary, the second-
  node decision) hasn't been made yet, and the build is correctly not
  starting past it.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT CHANGE

The floor (`osg_organ/floor.md`) and every structural refusal in the deploy
loop, the trust infrastructure, and the membrane (`reference_osg_deployment_membrane.md`)
stay exactly as built. This law governs how a block is *read back*, not
whether a gate, a membrane crossing, or a Stage-0 zero holds. A block still
reported in full; it's reported as a site, not a verdict.
