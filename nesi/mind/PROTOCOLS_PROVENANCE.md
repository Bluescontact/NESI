## Pre-flight Protocol — the skills-wrapper meta-skill

> **The skills-wrapper meta-skill mechanism (built 2026-07-21, Kevin's mark — Branch 3 of the 2026-06-26 pre-flight session, held in the felt-read queue since):** distinct from the behavioral rule above. A heavy skill may declare its own manifest fields as frontmatter metadata (`manifest: {do, touches, size}`) instead of the do/touches/size being freshly derived, in prose, every time that skill runs. When a skill file carries a `manifest:` block, read it directly and render it as the pre-flight strip — do not re-derive from scratch. `membrane-controller` (`nesi/bench/membrane-controller/skill.md`, mirrored vendor-side) carries the first instance, since every membrane crossing needs the same three lines every time. Not yet retrofitted to the other twelve skills — this is the mechanism proven on one exemplar, not a completed pass across all of `nesi/bench/`. Extending it to another skill means adding the same `manifest:` block to that skill's frontmatter, nothing more.

## The Load-Test — the build-gate

> This gates what gets *built*. It is not a per-artifact truth-test run after the fact (that is the falsifier line); it is a build-gate run *before* — a filter on what deserves to exist at all. The invariant it protects: NESI exists to remove load from its keeper without ever removing his authority. A build that adds a room, a surface, or an organ but takes no burden off Kevin's body is architecture masquerading as progress — hold or drop it.
>
> **Falsifier (self-applied):** if adopting this law changes nothing about what future sessions build — if every build would have happened anyway — then it was already implicit and the adoption was ceremony. It earns its place only by stopping a build that would otherwise have shipped.
>
> **Provenance:** surfaced 2026-07-23 as the crown artifact of three external-model reconciliations of the week's NESI work (Return 1, "BUILD THE ORGANISM"); reconciliation at `_INTAKE/external_returns_reconciliation_2026-07-23.md`. The returns claimed this invariant ran under every development prompt of the week without changing once — the load-test is that invariant made into a gate. Adopted to canon on Kevin's mark ("Adopt the load-test build-law into canon"), mark caught in `MARKS_LOG.jsonl`.

## The Locatable Move — the surface-gate

> Five locatables, one expandable map: **Kevin himself** (only he places this — the system holds an empty, honest slot and never fills it) · **the object** in front of him · **the process** it is inside · **the decision** it is asking for · **the field of past decisions** (the caught marks as terrain, not a log). > **Provenance:** named upstream of two convergent failures (2026-07-30): the repeated decision-toggle asks and the self-improvement organ both failed by the same geometry — the AI holding the map and handing Kevin a position. > **Falsifier:** if adopting this criterion never changes a surface's geometry — if every surface would have shipped identically without it — it was ceremony. It earns its place the first time it stops or reshapes a steering-seat build.

> - **L3 · DEPTH** — "▾ the stakes": manifest (do/touches/size) · falsifier · against-case · provenance. Revealed only on Kevin's deeper click.
>
> **Frozen rules (decided once, never re-litigated):**

> - **DISCLOSURE** = the layers ARE the disclosure. Reading lives at L0/L1/L2; the control inside L2; the stakes at L3. Reading is never gated behind the act.

### Guard 1 scoped to interfaces — amended 2026-08-07 (same day as adoption)

> Kevin's ruling, session 5680ec24: *"the law governs interfaces only — amend its wording to say so."*
>
> **What forced it.** Twelve hours after adoption, a dreaming pass filed to `nesi/spec/THE_DREAMING_PASS_2026-08-07.md` ended by stating the payoff of the whole architecture out loud, to the person building it: *"the machine that stands the boundary before anyone can fall, so that no one falls and no one has to pour the floor by hand afterward."* Read against guard 1 as originally written — *nothing may be pointed at* — that is the exact failure the guard forbids, arriving from inside the system on the day of adoption.
>
> **The two readings, surfaced rather than resolved by the author.** (a) The law is general and the pass violated it, so the dreaming cycle itself needs the guard. (b) The law's true scope is narrower than its wording: it governs what an INTERFACE tells Kevin at the moment of acting, and says nothing about what a spec may state to its own author about its own architecture. Kevin ruled (b).
>
> **What the scoping does and does not release.** It binds cards, tiles, button faces, locator lines, manifests, prompts — anything the system puts in front of Kevin at or near the moment of deciding. It releases specs, namings, and dreaming documents doing design work in the open. The distinction is *a lever placed under a hand* versus *design stated where it can be argued with*.
>
> **Falsifier:** if a widget or card starts telling Kevin what an action is really worth and the author defends it by pointing at this amendment, the scoping was read as a loophole and guard 1 goes back to general.
