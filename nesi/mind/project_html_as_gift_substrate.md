## What this closes

The substrate question for the gift-object is answered: **HTML**. Not because
it's the richest format — because it's the dumbest one that still opens
everywhere, for a human or a model, without a runtime dependency. That
"universal, dumb, shareable" quality is the whole of the reason it was
chosen; nothing above it is a rationalization Kevin didn't give.
