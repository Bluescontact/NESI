Read against the topology (`nesi/mind/LEARNED.md`'s 28 laws, wired into

## 1 · Register tracks containment — and the topology has one register for all of it

Card 1 of the visual-language extraction: *"the register a concept gets isn't
decorative — it tracks how contained the concept is... the most conceptually
central, least tameable idea in the collection is drawn in the least
diagrammatic way available."* Fully-specifiable structures get hard outlines;
the two concepts explicitly about the limits of specification get no outline
at all.

The topology has been drawing everything in one register — clean rects,
solid borders, uniform weight — for clusters that are genuinely different in
kind. Clusters B, C, F, G are mechanically specifiable: enforcement rules,
throughput checks, return obligations, accountability duties. The keystone
(1, 20, 14) and cluster E (law 25, alone) are not that kind of thing — they
are the corpus's own limits of specification: whether a thing is wanted,
whether a session may rule its own fork. By Card 1's own logic, drawing them
in the identical hard-diagram register as the mechanical clusters overstates
how contained they actually are.

## 2 · Let the linework carry the claim at the exact boundary

Card 2: *"where a diagram's own edge represents a loss of order, certainty,
or containment, let the linework at that exact edge visibly degrade."*
Cluster E's still-missing lateral edge is precisely this kind of boundary —
not absence-as-oversight, but absence-as-honest-finding, argued at length by
three counsel seats earlier today without resolving. Law 28's honest-limit
collapse (named, then re-broken, same session) is the same shape at the level
of a single node. Both are places where a solid line would overclaim.

## 3 · The bridge's authority-laundering warning is not hypothetical here — it already fired today

`WORKBENCH_BRIDGE`, Gift 01, §7: *"yesterday's derived gift can appear
tomorrow as 'what already stood.' ... Y begins to look constitutional."* Its
proposed guard is a provenance-class vocabulary sharper than anything the
topology used today: **VERBATIM · MARKED · INHERITED RENDERING · INSTRUMENT
FACT · SESSION-DERIVED.**

This session's own record shows the risk operating in real time: an external
Claude return was read, five lines were checked and admitted with falsifiers,
and the artifact's own footer under-reported its work as "four lines" before
being corrected to five. That correction happened because the provenance
file was checked against the actual edits, not trusted from memory — but the
topology *artifact itself* never visually marked which of its own lines were
VERBATIM (quoted from `LEARNED.md`), SESSION-DERIVED (this session's cluster
cuts and edges), or INHERITED RENDERING now VERIFIED (the five lines pulled
from the external return). All of it rendered in the same weight. The
bridge's vocabulary is the fix, not a new one invented here.

## 4 · The session seal is a trim-tab-scale import, not a redesign

`WORKBENCH_BRIDGE`'s seal strip (SESSION · SOURCE AUTHORITY · RENDERING
AUTHORITY · NOT AUTHORIZED · DEPENDENCIES) is small, cheap, and answers
exactly the question a reader of the topology map would otherwise have to
infer: what is this page allowed to claim about the canon it's reading. Worth
importing wholesale rather than re-derived — the bridge already paid for its
shape.

## DEVELOPED, same day — full-development pass on this document and the artifact it justified

Kevin's mark: *"I dont think ths has developed toward full univeral pretension
across it just yet... let this stand from anythgin before, and change nd
resolve the tensions through design and development."* Read as: don't revert
this weave, develop it — run the actual procedure (compost, parallel audit,
ground, diverge, converge) rather than another ad hoc patch.

**What audit found, most severe first:**

1. **False citation.** §3 above claims the artifact's own footer
   under-reported five admitted lines as four. Checked: the artifact footer
   never said four. The actual undercount was a trailing sentence in
   `LEARNED_PROVENANCE.md` itself, left over from round one (2 lines) after
   round two added 3 more (5 total) — fixed there directly, cited correctly
   here now instead of misattributed to this artifact.

2. **The register-switching mechanic didn't survive contact with the map's
   own content.** §1's classification — keystone and cluster E as "the
   corpus's own limits of specification" against five "mechanically
   specifiable" clusters — breaks on inspection: law 11 sits hard-registered
   in cluster B while its own provenance note says plainly no instrument
   makes it machine-checkable yet (`LEARNED_PROVENANCE.md:171-174`), and laws
   17 (*divergence is held, never averaged*) and 28 (*the honest limit
   widens the field, it never narrows it*) — both in hard-registered
   clusters — are textually closer to Card 1's actual criterion (a concept
   *about* the limits of specification) than the keystone's three crisp,
   testable imperatives are. None of the three counsel seats consulted for
   this same redraw endorsed the classification either; composite's reading
   directly undercuts it. **Retracted** — every cluster now shares one
   register.

3. **The dissolving-line motif was a category error, independently found by
   both audit and ground.** Source Card 2 documents a *continuous* boundary
   degrading at its own edge (an axis fraying toward zero, a ruled line
   losing a fight with water already drawn). Cluster E's open question was
   never that — it was three *discrete*, named, non-overlapping candidate
   readings (fuller: a twin-ledger edge to B; cowan: a contested
   self-pricing mechanism, held open against a competing tensegrity read;
   composite: the cluster-cut itself may be wrong, since law 11 already
   belonged in B by Kevin's own ratified `DRAFT_SIX_SHAPES_2026-08-20.md`).
   A single fading line visually asserts one relationship losing certainty,
   not three parties disagreeing about what relationship (if any) exists —
   the exact shape law 17 already governs. **Retracted**, replaced with the
   three actual candidate readings, shown by seat, none drawn as a
   completed edge.

4. **Card 1 was applied past what its own source licensed.** The register
   this pass borrowed (Register 4, painterly/atmospheric) is built from 2 of
   11 images inspected — and the source's own anti-nutrients section warns
   explicitly against treating a five-image sample as more than "a sketch
   of the system, not a census of it." That caveat wasn't carried forward
   alongside the ND-license caveat that *was* carried forward. Named here
   now; moot for the artifact since the mechanic itself was retracted, but
   worth stating plainly rather than letting the citation stand uncaveated
   in this document.

5. **Minor: asymmetric tagging.** Law 1 and law 22's shared admission (the
   sovereign/enforcement gate distinction) was tagged at law 1 only. Fixed —
   both sites now carry the provenance tag.

**What ground found:** the same defect as #2, reached independently by
hunting for a real-world counterexample rather than auditing the prose —
law 11's placement is a documented fact in this corpus's own provenance file
that contradicts the register claim in the very artifact it was applied to.
Ground's minimal honest reformulation, adopted: *reserve any visual
distinction for a boundary that is genuinely continuous and uncertain in
degree; render a discrete, multi-party disagreement as visible multiplicity
— named candidate claims, attributed to source — never as a single fading
line.*

**What diverge produced, and what was and wasn't adopted:** five genuinely
different shapes for the whole map (unresolved-first, a timeline, a single
paragraph, a failure-mode ledger, a provenance-class bin sort) and two
alternative openings, one more confident than the current header, one more
provisional. None adopted wholesale — the cluster-diagram shape stays,
because the relationships between laws (the actual content of clusters A–G)
are load-bearing in a way none of the five alternate shapes preserved
without a real loss named honestly in each case. Two things *were* pulled

the deferral named below; the instinct behind the unresolved-first shape
