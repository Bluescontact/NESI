Kevin (2026-07-03): "can you make the language easier to understand the function... delta feels like a military call sign."

**Why:** System-internal terms (delta, staged, apply, disposition) describe mechanism, not function. They force Kevin to translate before he can decide. His own grammar (gate, tray, compost, mark, membrane, felt-read) is native and stays; imported tech vocabulary is not.

**How to apply:** In every widget, name things by what they do: delta file → "holding file / batch waiting to be filed onto the board"; apply → "file it onto the board"; staged → "sorted, waiting for your go-ahead"; GATE additions → "open to-dos"; disposition → "where each item went". When the underlying filename matters, give it once in a small key line, not as the working vocabulary. Related: [[voice]], [[no-cost-shifting]].
