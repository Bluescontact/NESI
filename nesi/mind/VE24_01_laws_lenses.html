What this edge is

Where the corpus's constraint-plane meets its perception-plane.

On the vector-equilibrium mapping this VE24 project uses, Group RULE (A · Laws, B · Protocols, C · Memory/Suspension, D · Gate/Ledger) is what constrains the corpus; Group READ (E · Lenses, F · Skills, G · Tools/Checks, H · Gift-pipeline) is what perceives and finds. A and E are adjacent vertices of the cuboctahedron — no vertex in a set touches another vertex in its own set, but touches two in each other set — so a Laws↔Lenses edge is one of the twenty-four structurally real relationships, not an invented one. Concretely: nesi/mind/LEARNED.md is the floor every lens reads at boot, and the five lens agents are the instruments that apply it while reading material.
