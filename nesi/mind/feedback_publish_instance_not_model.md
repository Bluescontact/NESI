2026-06-08, landed after three corrections on the OSG "The Model" page. Writing Kevin's economy in **second person** ("you keep what you need, your bus, your solar") — even though that was his own notes-to-self voice in KEVINS_ECONOMY — reads, once *published*, as projecting his specific infrastructure onto the reader as a model to copy. He named it exactly: "it reads as if the reader owns all of this." Wrong frame.

**The fix (two moves):**
1. **First person — his actual instance.** "This is how I sustain myself": my bus, my solar, my water, the work I give. Not an instruction manual aimed at the reader.
2. **The gift is the PATTERN, named separately and held apart from the infrastructure.** "I can't hand you my bus — they're mine, built from my salvage for my ground; what travels is the pattern underneath." The transferable shape (e.g. *own what you depend on · give the work as a gift, support via recognition not price · need less so "no" stays cheap*) is the gift — explicitly NOT his setup. "Same form, different ground. Run it on your own ground, not mine."

**Why:** pattern crosses, instance never does ([[feedback_composting_threshold]], [[feedback_recycled_substrate]]); the gift is the shape, not the parts; his About page already held the principle ("Same form. Different ground."). Pairs with the non-transactional frame ([[project_osg_v5_resynthesis]], [[project_non_transactional_mechanism]]).

**How to apply:** any public-facing presentation of Kevin's life/work — keep his lived instance in first person, call out the abstract transferable pattern as the gift, never as a how-to for the reader to replicate his infrastructure. Watch for the second-person "you" that turns his life into the reader's assumed possession.
