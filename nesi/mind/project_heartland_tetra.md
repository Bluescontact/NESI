The buildable model for the ~25-acre off-grid Camp Far West parcel (the move ground in [[project_heartland_ground]]). Built 2026-06-23/24 from the "Heartland Tetra" SEED, then **twice reframed by Kevin** — the second reframe is the live one. Compiled to a ~32-page illustrated PDF at DSS root: **`HEARTLAND.pdf`** (built from `build_heartland_pdf.py` + `heartland_diagrams.py` + the source `.md` files; 9 inline-SVG diagrams; rebuild = `python build_heartland_pdf.py`). Staged, nothing crossed the gate.

**Reframe 1 — abundance over income (the spine flip).** Kevin: the market/$100k model "misses the whole point." Rebuilt around **material + food abundance + TIME FREEDOM through engineered, layered, automated, self-sustaining cycles built from the literal ground up.** Money is the thin SPILLWAY at the top, not the keystone (`HEARTLAND_ENGINE.md` is the new spine: 7 layers — Water·Soil·Perennial-stack·Intensives·Animals·Automation·Spillway — built in [Yeomans] Scale-of-Permanence order). Backed by: median US farm LOSES money + 92% of small-farm income is off-farm → sell only surplus; complete diet grown on ~0.18 acre at ~12.5 labor-days/yr. This is canon ([[project_single_person_economy]] need-less-not-earn-more; [[feedback_somatic_threshold]]).

**Reframe 2 — WATER CORRECTION (load-bearing).** The SEED's "two lakes, abundant water, strongest vertex" was WRONG. Ground truth: **no lake access; a ~3 GPM well.** Water is now the **binding constraint that sizes the whole farm**, not the strongest vertex. Consequences folded into the model: pond aquaculture REMOVED; aquaponics demoted to a small optional module (water-thrifty but needs 24/7 power → kills fish on outage); the hydraulic ram pump removed (no surface water). Real budget: well ~1,000–1,500 gal/day into a cistern (pump-test = master variable), rain ~22–24"/yr all winter, **the SOIL is the only farm-scale reservoir** (keyline + organic matter banks ~0.7–1.4M gal). **Caps the operation at homestead + market-garden + dry-farmed-orchard scale** — not irrigated acreage, not large herds, not ponds. Protein = eggs/rabbit/small-ruminant + dry-farmed nuts/acorns/legumes.

**The two master UNKNOWNS to ground-truth before any money/scale:** (1) the well's measured recovery rate (multi-hour pump test); (2) SGMA basin status (de minimis ≤2 af/yr exempt; crop irrigation exceeds it → GSA registration). Plus carrying capacity (RDM), fire-vs-density, predator infrastructure, soil test.

The market work (income/two-paths/restoration-contacts/field-sheet/gate-note) survives as the **demoted Spillway appendix** + still-valid legal envelope (tri-county, run APN; mushrooms barred from cottage food; crowd→farm-participants gift-frame). NOTE: `HEARTLAND_TETRA.md` and `_TIMELINE.md` on disk still carry the old money-keystone + lake framing — **superseded by `HEARTLAND_ENGINE.md`**, dropped from the PDF.
