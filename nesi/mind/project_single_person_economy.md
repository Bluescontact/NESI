`KEVINS_ECONOMY.md` (DSS root) — *"Kevin's Economy — One Man, One Life, Real Dollars."* Composted 2026-06-07 by metabolizing the whole active v5 intake (Village Center engine, gift/paid mechanics, Heartland rhythm, the few candidates that were actually about money) **toward the economy** instead of toward more framework. Written in plain human language, grounded in his real life (the bus, the 2.6kW solar, the pump+tank, the $3k, the tables, Heartland) — usable by Kevin AND by a stranger running any one-person trade. A staged draft at root, his to mark; nothing pushed.

**The model — seven parts + the moves:**
- **Ground** (own power/water/shelter/transport via salvage → near-zero burn; a slow month can't sink you; set it up to *idle* not collapse when you step away)
- **Hands** (paid trade work; sell the whole thing not the wood+hours; lead with fast-cheap cash, don't bet rent on the showpiece)
- **Vault** (build once that keeps paying — the pump+tank that an auto-watering system got built on top of is the real flywheel instance; give help that doesn't need you again)
- **Face** (two currencies — cash + your name traveling; plant work where crowds already gather; don't lock into one narrow label)
- **Mesh** (plug into a commons someone else already holds rather than build your own; the place you feed feeds you; keep gift and paid as two clean lanes, never barter; the outside witness)
- **Brake** (work pace is a *safety* question — count held worries on your *worst* day, that's the ceiling; low burn makes "no" cheap; half-day floor; two-on/two-off rhythm)
- **Mind** (the AI back-office carries the bookkeeping so his head stays clear; money is the last thing to show up — that's normal — but keep an honest two-cycle gauge so "last" never becomes "never")
- **The moves:** *need less, not earn more* + *be needed for less, not for everything* ("a business that gets worse the more it runs through your hands is a monument with a man buried under it"); the flywheel; compounding over years; the model itself as a free gift (the playbook IS the introduction).

**New economic ideas the compost surfaced** (beyond the first imagining): the closed circuit (place-you-feed-feeds-you, so gift and business stop fighting); labor-pace-is-safety (held-worries ceiling, measured on a bad day); design-yourself-out as a falsifiable test (count of things-that-need-you must fall each cycle); festival-as-income + the borrowed-crowd risk (keep a portable floor); abundance hides the buried cost; the outside witness as a hard economic function; plug-into-an-existing-commons.
