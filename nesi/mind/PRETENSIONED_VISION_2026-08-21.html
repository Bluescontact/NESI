Nine load-bearing articulations, pretensioned against each other

Not a document you read top to bottom — a structure. Nine of Kevin's own rulings about NESI stand as compression members, never touching. What keeps the whole thing standing isn't any one of them; it's the counter-articulations pulling each one back from overreach. Remove a cable and a strut doesn't fall — it drifts, unchecked, into a claim nobody actually made. Three of the nine were added in a second development pass, adversarially checked before being let in.

1

The centre

"The centre is the game. Everything else serves it."

↻

Nothing can be sent to it — no edge reaches the centre. Any design where a seat delivers something to the centre is geometrically false. The strut stays a strut only because the cable makes it unreachable — the moment something touches it, it stops being the centre and becomes ordinary apparatus.THE_VISION.md §4, Kevin, 2026-08-16

2

Content-blind

"Content-blind, permanently. Nothing reads for meaning."

↻

The reason we can claim content-blindness isn't that we're reading the user's words directly — we're mapping the backend relationships and geometry between and through words. Without this cable the strut collapses into "nothing ever touches the words," which the sort, the aim, and the wireframe all contradict.MARKS_LOG.jsonl, Kevin, 2026-08-20T20:15–20:22

A level is an edge

"A level is an edge — a tetra is a developed object."

↻

Each level consists of a week. Without this cable the definition is pure topology — a level exists the instant it's touched, no duration, no pacing. The week cable is what turns a geometric position into something actually lived.MARKS_LOG.jsonl, Kevin, 2026-08-20T20:45 / 23:35

Game-craft authority

"Game-craft leads with full authority; every other prior ruling is advisory."

↻

"A diagram is not a game." One phrase, and it's the whole of what's actually verbatim — the sweeping grant of authority is the chief-of-staff seat's own order, issued off that one quote, ratified rather than assumed. The cable ties a strut wide enough to govern every other ruling back to the single sentence it was built to stand on.THE_GAME.md, Kevin, 2026-08-12; ratified 2026-08-20

The overwintering

"Nothing you release is wasted; it comes back as ground."

↻

Growth roots only across returned days. No volume of writing can force it. Without this cable the promise is sentiment — pretty, unfalsifiable. The day-gate is the mechanism that makes it a mechanic instead of a mood.THE_GAME.md / THE_VISION.md §3

The heliostat bowl added, 08-21

"The loading screen between levels is the lines filled by completed levels... by level 12 a bowl has been made and it transforms into the full heliostat bowl."

The wireframe draws only completed edges — never an unwalked one, not even faintly. "The 4th of ecological succession, and biodiversity as a side effect of balancing the three cycles in a level."

Day one added, 08-21

"I think the first four stage is the bottom square of the cuboctahedron. And I think that the tank goes to the filter/dam. Both are the same thing."

↻

Checked against solid.js directly: TANK does not sit on the bottom square. FILTER is a direct member of TANK; DAM is one step outside it, adjacent to two of its corners. The cable is what keeps "both are the same thing" a statement about function, not a claim the solid itself would refuse.MARKS_LOG.jsonl, Kevin, 2026-08-19T00:00:00

None of the nine struts touch. That's not a gap in the synthesis — it's the actual shape of a pretensioned structure: every member's stability comes from the tension running through the others, not from any single one being strong enough to stand alone. Pull one cable out — read a strut without its counter-articulation — and it doesn't hold its shape. Three struts were added this pass, and each one only earned its place by surviving the same check: is it genuinely his own words, is it distinct from what's already there, and does it have a real cable — not a decorative one. The vision isn't the nine claims. It's the eighteen, held in the right relationship.
