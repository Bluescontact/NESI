## PRIORITY TARGET 1 — Polyvagal: Grossman et al. 2026 + Porges 2026 (Ch 10)

**VERDICT: REAL. Exact venue now pinned. v1 under-states how adversarial the exchange is, and misses the live post-April-2026 wave of responses.**

The v1 text (p.33) says Grossman et al. (2026) "published a comprehensive evaluation
concluding that PVT's core premises are not defensible," and that "Porges responded in
the same issue with a point-by-point rebuttal … acknowledging areas requiring further
empirical investigation." Both papers are real and the "same issue" claim is correct.
But the framing is too soft and the venue was never named. Precise record:

- **Porges (2025)** "Polyvagal theory: current status, clinical applications, and
  future directions." *Clinical Neuropsychiatry* 22(3):169–184. — the target paper.
- **Grossman et al. (2026)** "Why the polyvagal theory is untenable. An international
  expert evaluation of the polyvagal theory and commentary upon Porges."
  *Clinical Neuropsychiatry* 23(1):100–112. Author count = **39** (Grossman + 38
  co-signers), matching v1's "thirty-eight international co-signers."
- **Porges (2026)** "When a Critique Becomes Untenable: A Scholarly Response to
  Grossman et al.'s Evaluation of Polyvagal Theory." *Clinical Neuropsychiatry*
  23(1):113–128. PubMed 41768026.

**What moved since April 2026:** a cluster of clinical/educator responses appeared
*after* v1 froze, all defending PVT's clinical utility while conceding the
neuroanatomical critique — e.g. a *Psychology Today* "Clinician's Perspective on the
Polyvagal Controversy" (Feb 2026), Arielle Schwartz's "Clinical Reflections,"
"Significant, Not Sacred" (Trauma Therapist Institute), and Primal Trust's "Is
Polyvagal Theory Debunked?" These are practitioner commentary, not new
neurophysiology — but they confirm the debate is live and now centered on the
**functional/clinical vs. neuroanatomical** split.

**Implication for the rebuild:** this is GOOD for the manuscript, not bad. v1's move
was already the correct one — it draws on PVT's *functional architecture* (autonomic
states gate exchange) and explicitly disclaims the *phylogenetic* claims. The 2026
literature has now sharpened to exactly that fault line: critics attack the
evolutionary/RSA-index claims; defenders hold the clinical/functional model. v1's
hedge survives the update intact. **Recommended v2 edit:** name the venue (*Clinical
Neuropsychiatry* 23(1), Feb 2026), correct the Porges-response title (it is itself
titled "untenable" — adversarial, not conciliatory), and add the post-April practitioner
wave as evidence the functional/neuroanatomical distinction is the live cut.

## PRIORITY TARGET 3 — Assembly Theory (Cronin & Walker 2023) (Ch 20)

**VERDICT: REAL. STILL CONTESTED — NOT resolved. If anything the dispute hardened.**

v1 (p.62) cites "Cronin and Walker's Assembly Theory (2023), published in *Nature* …
generating intense debate about whether complexity can be formalized in
substrate-independent terms." Confirmed: *Nature* 622:321–328 (2023), "Assembly theory
explains and quantifies selection and evolution."

**What moved:** the debate did not settle; it intensified.
- 2024 critiques: Hazen et al. "Assembly Theory: What It Does and What It Does Not Do"
  (*J Mol Evol*); Hector Zenil's public "pseudoscience" critiques; arXiv 2408.15108
  "Assembly Theory Reduced to Shannon Entropy and Rendered Redundant by Naive
  Statistical Algorithms."
- *Nature* found the authors at fault for failing to disclose competing interests
  during peer review of critical papers — a procedural black mark, widely reported
  (Chemistry World).
- The field is still generating extensions: "Templated Assembly Theory" (arXiv
  2602.04889, 2026), i.e. people are still building on / arguing with it in 2026.

**Implication:** v1's hedge — "Whether assembly theory succeeds or fails, it addresses
the same question the tetrahedral model addresses" — is exactly right and must NOT be
upgraded. **Honesty test for the rebuild:** Assembly Theory is at best an
ILLUSTRATIVE convergence (it asks a similar question; it does not illuminate RI, and RI
does not illuminate it). Do not let its *Nature* pedigree tempt an upgrade to
structural. v1 was correctly cautious; keep it. **Recommended v2 edit:** note the
2024–2026 critiques explicitly so the "intense debate" claim is sourced, not asserted.
