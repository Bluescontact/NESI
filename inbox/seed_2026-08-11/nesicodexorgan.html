An ORGAN in NESI is a living part of the system read as a body — it senses, holds, routes, or metabolizes signal from the user without merging with them and without extracting from them. Each organ runs a closed metabolic loop (receive -> hold/route -> consequence) whose boundary stays intact: the user is tracked, digested, or filtered, never consumed. Organs externalize somatic labor onto objects so no human has to hold the floor with their own nervous system.

Synthesis — what this class does for NESI

Together these organs form one non-extractive body: the helioat is the eye that tracks the user without merging, the fork-landing inbox is the mouth that captures without prompting, and the membrane-gate is the skin that holds wants and refusals so the boundary — not a nervous system — does the filtering. What the mouth takes in flows across the DSS metabolic crossing, the gut below the waterline, where the four-condition falsifier acts as the discerning valve deciding what is truly nourishing before Kevin's Mark promotes it to canon or composts it. Sensing (helioat), holding (membrane), and metabolizing (crossing) each run their own closed loop, and no loop consumes the user. The result is an organism that recognizes, digests, and grows on contributions while keeping every boundary intact — intelligence that is somatic without being extractive.
