In NESI, INFRASTRUCTURE is the substrate layer other things run on: the engines, protocols, and physical nodes that generate, coordinate, or sustain activity without being the activity itself. It spans both digital engines (pattern extractors, agent loops, trust protocols) and physical off-grid nodes (water, shelter, sanitation appliances). Infrastructure is defined by being load-bearing and reusable — it drifts toward coherence, holds trust in the open, and keeps a camp alive whether or not anyone is watching.

Core Extraction Engine (built, deliberately unwired)

A self-improving pattern-extraction engine kept intentionally decoupled from any live vehicle.

The pattern

Keeping it unwired lets the substrate stabilize under low-stakes load so that when a vehicle is finally attached, the engine is already trustworthy. The reusable move is: mature the substrate in isolation, wire the consumers later.

Four-Agent Tetrahedron Engine

Trust Engine — Five Gears

Five graduated community states advanced by visible deposits, not reputation scores.

The pattern

Trust modeled as discrete states (Stranger -> Recognized -> Contributing -> Circulating -> Architecting) driven by five gears (recognition, deposit, graduation, circulation, repair) makes standing legible without scoring people. Because progression comes from public, auditable contribution rather than likability, the protocol resists popularity capture and gaming. The reusable pattern is a graduated-trust state machine where every transition is an observable deposit anyone can inspect.

Self-Erecting Dome (crown hub + center pole)

One-person dome assembly built in the air on a center pole and rotated into place.

The pattern

Anchoring a structure to a crown hub on a central pole lets one person build edge-to-edge in the air and rotate the work, collapsing a 4-hour multi-person job to 15-30 solo minutes. Standard parts (2020 aluminum, T-bulb gaskets, edge connectors) keep it repairable and reproducible. The reusable pattern is a central-mast self-erecting node that removes the crew as a dependency for shelter.

Synthesis — what this class does for NESI

Together these six form the two-layer substrate NESI runs on: a digital stack (self-refining extraction engine, tetrahedral deliberation loop, graduated trust protocol) and a physical stack (water, shelter, sanitation nodes) that keep bodies alive at a site. The digital layer decides and coordinates while drifting toward coherence rather than entropy; the physical layer makes a place survivable by one person with no grid and no crew. Trust is the connective tissue binding them — it governs who may drive the engines and steward the nodes, all through visible deposits rather than scores. The whole is load-bearing by design: each piece is matured in isolation, wired to its consumers deliberately, and reproducible from standard parts so the settlement's substrate never depends on a single point that can fail.
