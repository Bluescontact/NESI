# GIFT · L4's hidden recognition-log — held at the gate, not built

Filed 2026-08-11 21:5x, chief-of-staff ruling under the NESI.EXE COUNSEL BUILD
ORDER: the L4 seed clause ("build the log now, keep the surfacing hidden until
L4") is withdrawn from the active build. This card names what was withdrawn and
why, so it isn't lost — it goes to the gate, not into code.

**What it was** — When an invited second player takes something across the
shared membrane and marks it as a gift, NESI would write a recognition-log of
that event and later use it to surface a gift FROM THE DEEP — a recognition of
the past gift, before it transferred — kept hidden from the player until Level
4. The mechanic itself, and its concealment, were both part of the build order.

**Why it's held, not built, not even as a stub** — it sits directly inside the
standing-open fork "second user is NOT upstream" (`project_first_user_threshold.md`,
2026-08-02) and the still-open downstream-agency fork the corpus has held on
purpose across multiple sessions. Both are about a second person's agency
inside the game — territory this corpus has repeatedly ruled belongs at the
gate, in Kevin's hands, not defaulted by any seat or session even under a
build-authority override. A hidden mechanic that logs another person's action
without their visibility into it is exactly the shape those forks were opened
to hold open.

**What's needed before this can be built** — Kevin's own ruling on the
second-user/downstream-agency fork itself: what agency an invited second
player actually has, and whether anything about their actions may be logged
or surfaced without their own visibility into that logging. This card doesn't
propose an answer.
