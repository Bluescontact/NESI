# The Subtraction Membrane

*Membrane function four, built 2026-09-01 on the keeper's instruction
("build the subtraction membrane"), after the second mirror audit named it
the item drifting fastest toward its fork: 129 admissions in thirty days,
zero subtraction passes ever run. A membrane with intake and no outflow is
not a membrane; it is a funnel.*

---

## What this closes

The gate's own instrument (05-subtract, Card 13) existed with its
enforcement written and deliberately unreachable, held on the fork *"where
the subtraction quota lives."* The record it carried is why: adding is a
contribution; removing is an accusation — so every list grows monotonically
while its accuracy falls monotonically (the exhibit: a public removal queue
open for five years and never drained). The fork is now closed: **the quota
lives in the gate's own configuration (`gate.conf`,
`subtract_quota_days = 14`), enforced by the instrument as an active
mechanism.** A gate that has not run a subtraction pass inside its quota
does not print green.

## The law of a pass

1. **Its only permitted output is removal or demotion.** A pass in which
   the operator added their best work and took nothing away does not count
   as a pass.
2. **It must remove at least one thing.**
3. **Every removal goes to compost with a cause** — an id and a stated
   reason, appended to the compost record. Nothing is removed to the void.
4. **The unfakeable check:** the target file must be smaller after the pass
   than before it, verified in bytes by the instrument itself. A pass that
   did not shrink the target did not subtract, and nothing is written to
   the ledger. This is the one reason the instrument cannot be satisfied by
   paperwork.
5. **The full original is kept.** Before a pass, the target is backed up
   whole, byte for byte, at a named path recorded in the pass's note.
   Subtraction here is demotion out of the live surface, never erasure of
   the record — the same law as every other crossing.

## Jurisdiction — what a pass may and may not remove

- **May remove, on the builder's own authority:** derived and self-logged
  material — run logs, self-appended verdicts, generated accounting that no
  instrument reads, duplicates of things that live elsewhere. The test:
  removing it changes no instrument's verdict and no person's record.
- **May propose, never remove:** anything that crossed by the keeper's
  mark — patterns, essays, admitted gifts, marks themselves. For crossed
  material a subtraction pass produces a *proposal with a cause*; the
  removal is itself a crossing, and the mark is the keeper's.
- **May never touch:** another person's record (the closed-marks law
  already governs those), or the compost record itself — the membrane's
  outflow ledger is not subject to its own outflow.

## The first pass, on the record

Run 2026-09-01, the day the fork closed, on the gate's own ledger — the
place the second mirror audit pointed: 155 "gate-run" self-logging lines
(the silt had rebuilt after an earlier compaction; six runs landed in one
day) removed with cause, −241,290 bytes, full original kept at a named
backup, the pass and its compost entry on the ledger. The membrane's first
act was to subtract from the meta-layer, not from the work — which is
where the audit said the pile was growing.

*The falsifier this carries: the instrument's own refusal conditions. If
the quota lapses, the gate refuses and says so in the open; if a pass ever
claims removals while the target did not shrink, the instrument refuses to
record it; and if compost entries appear without causes, this document is
being violated in a way the compost record itself will show. The deeper
falsifier: watch whether removal queues drain. If proposals against crossed
material accumulate unmarked the way five-year removal queues do, the
membrane has outflow for silt but not for substance, and this document must
say so.*
