Inst · 1
Define before you delegate

A retained veto without a stated discharge condition is structurally a pump, regardless of the holder's intent. If you retain authority, state the condition under which it passes — before the work begins, in terms the delegate can verify without access to your interior. The definition is the holder's obligation, not the delegate's to discover. If the delegate cannot state the passage condition before submission, the gate is a pump.

Reading

Fires at any delegation moment. The test is the delegate's test: "Can I state right now what passage looks like, in terms you could verify?" If the honest answer is "I don't know" or "it depends on your reaction," the delegation is structurally uncompletable. The failure mode to watch for: fluency as mask — a holder can state a crisp-sounding discharge condition and still veto off-script. Stated definition is necessary but not sufficient. Whether the stated criterion matches the operative criterion is a separate track.

Inst · 2
Track where the cost landed

Delegation moves proximity without moving authority. The delegate absorbs cognitive burden (planning, contingency, sense-making), logistical burden (sequencing, coordination, error-correction), and liability burden (being there when things go wrong) while the holder retains override. The externalization is legible to everyone as something else — autonomy, trust, professional development. The structural effect is identical regardless of how it is named. If you cannot state where the cost went, assume it landed on the person nearest the work who had no authority to refuse.

Reading

Fires when reviewing a delegation arrangement — especially one that has been running for a while. The hidden subsidy: the holder's decision-making feels cheaper than it is because the delegate pre-processed the situation. The accountability inversion: when the system fails, the delegate bears proximate blame while the holder retains structural distance. The only move that closes the gap is reunion — authority and proximity in the same body, or the delegate exits.

Inst · 3
Put it in writing before the room assembles

The written record is the pre-meeting coalition. What arrives in writing before people assemble shapes what the room can see and ratify. What depends on live agreement depends on a lower-trust, lower-recall mechanism — the social dynamics of the room, the fluency of the fastest speaker, the silence that reads as consent. The meeting's "consensus" is often ratification of what was already legible in writing. Write the structural read first, before the room forms its own frame.

Reading

Fires when an important decision, disagreement, or structural observation needs to enter a group context. The pre-write is not advocacy — it is a structural move. What gets written before the meeting constitutes the terms on which the room will deliberate. What doesn't get written relies on real-time construction under social pressure. The record also survives the meeting: live decisions degrade; written records persist and can be returned to.

Inst · 4
Demand the specifiable gap

The manufactured deficit loop requires an indefinite threshold. The loop sustains itself through modification without specifiable content — "not quite, not yet, almost" without naming what is missing. When a verdict arrives without a nameable gap, demand: what, specifically, is absent? State what sufficient looks like in terms someone else could verify without your interior reading. If it cannot be specified, the loop is structural. The modification is not a quality judgment — it is the loop sustaining itself.

Reading

Fires when you are inside a delegation or review structure where threshold-approach keeps producing modification without passage. The diagnostic: chart the approach-instances. Three or more approach-followed-by-modification without passage, without a nameable specific = structural. The holder's experience: the modification feels like accurate discernment. That is the pattern's mechanism — the dread of closure is phenomenologically identical to genuine quality concern. Only the specificity of the gap distinguishes them in real time.
