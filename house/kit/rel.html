Rel · 1
Name the limit inside the work, not before it

A limit stated without shared process reads as a wall. A limit named inside work you've done together reads as a coordinate — "we've reached the edge of what this approach can tell us." The receiver's ability to locate the limit depends entirely on whether they hold the same map. Shared diagnostic process produces a jointly-held map. Without it, the limit has no referent in the receiver's terrain and lands as arbitrary, protective, or as the practitioner's ceiling.

Reading

Fires when you are about to name a limit to another person — especially an epistemic limit (the edge of what you know) rather than a structural one (a prior boundary that precedes the map). The premature-declaration failure: naming the limit efficiently, to protect time, before the receiver has any referent for it. The limit may be accurate and still land as a wall if the shared work hasn't built the map. Two failure modes to distinguish from: premature declaration (genuine process not run yet) and performed process (the process was staged, the limit was predetermined).

Rel · 2
Read reception as territory, not verdict

How a transmission folds in the receiving environment — where it binds, where it deflects, where it partially engages — is structural information about that terrain, not a verdict on the quality of what you sent. Sparse reception from a well-calibrated source is accurate territory description. The somatic distinction: deflection-as-terrain lands as absence of purchase; deflection-as-verdict lands as contraction. Same external event, different location in the body. The body can learn to distinguish them.

Reading

Fires after any significant transmission — an offer, a disclosure, a named limit, a shared observation. Three fold states: full binding (the receiver reorganizes around the message), partial binding (engagement without integration), deflection (silence, routing around, withdrawal). Each is information. Deflection means the receiving environment, as currently constituted, cannot hold this shape. That is accurate topographic data. The gift-stance (send without claim on reception) does not require reading-blindness — you can send without claim and still read what the fold teaches.

Rel · 3
Register fully, absorb without merging

The membrane is what makes full presence possible — not walling off, but not merging either. Witness the signal without metabolizing it as your own survival data. The other's transmission is information you register, not instruction to your nervous system. You can be fully present to the entire signal and still not run it as your own survival response. Merging is membrane collapse — when the other's signal stops being information and becomes a state you run. Full attention is not the risk. Unsupported presence over time, under conditions that don't slow, is.

Reading

Fires in prolonged relational contact where one party is in a survival-transmission state. The double bind that summons this: the other body won't slow, but the situation requires accurate witnessing. The move is to deposit the signal where it will register and route around the defensive reflex — don't reach across the survival response for acknowledgment, which triggers the defense. The close: forfeit the claim. Not just to material recovery but to authorship. If you narrate the forfeit, you haven't forfeited — you've redirected the claim into the explanation of its absence.

Rel · 4
The close holds in silence

The internal decision to stop is completed by what does not enter the shared channel afterward. Any explanation of the close reopens it at the level of explanation. The announcement of silence breaks the silence. An apology for not doing more is more. The limit is enacted, never declared — declaring it is enacting the opposite. One move, in the channel, on record. Then nothing. The failure is always the same shape: labor dressed as restraint.

Reading

Fires when an internal decision to stop, forfeit, or withdraw needs to hold externally. The sequencing condition: a personal claim can be silently forfeited only once the structural protection it was holding open is confirmed to hold without it. Forfeit before the structure stands on its own is not a close — it is abandonment. The close is sound when the silence is the structural fact, not the excuse. What persists is the protection, not the actor.
