# marks/ — the mark record

*The canon's most important events are its least documented. A mark leaves an outcome — a pattern promoted, a direction taken, a situation composted — but no trace of the **read**: how it landed, in what condition the body was, what it actually felt like to know. This directory holds that trace. One file per mark.*

---

## What a mark record is

A mark is a felt-read that closed: a yes, a no, a compost, a hold — made off-panel, from the body, cold. The gate (`gate/`) records the *outcome*. This records the *read*. Five fields and one line:

1. **date** — when the read happened.
2. **item** — what was read.
3. **disposition** — what the body did (ratified / refused / composted / held / direction-marked).
4. **conditions of the read** — cold or hot · rested or pushed · on-screen or off. The texture that decides whether a mark is trustworthy.
5. **the line** — *one line, in your words, from the body.* Even if the line is "it just landed." Especially then.

That's the whole format. Use [`_TEMPLATE.md`](_TEMPLATE.md).

---

## The hard constraint — written into the format, non-negotiable

**This is a log, never an instrument.**

- **No scoring.** A mark is not rated. There is no number on a felt-read.
- **No patterns-across-marks.** Nobody — no human, no AI, ever — runs analysis across these files looking for "how the keeper's marks trend." The moment marks become a dataset to model, the body has been turned into a thing to predict, and the origin has been measured from outside itself. That is the one move the whole framework forbids (`witness_as_origin`; the gate's **DISPLACED-ZERO** law).
- **No AI summarization of the body's reasons.** An AI may provide the empty slot and the prompt. It may *never* write, paraphrase, infer, or "clean up" the line in field 5. The line is yours or it is blank.
- **The displaced-zero guard applies to retrospect as much as to prediction.** It is as forbidden to mine the past marks for a decision rule as it is to let a tool decide a future one. Looking backward does not exempt the move.

If a use of this directory ever produces a score, a trend, a model, or an AI-written body-line, it has stopped being a log and become the forbidden instrument. Stop.

---

## Reserved zero

**Every entry's line is written by you or not at all.** The skill (`skills/mark-record`) provides the slot and the prompt — nothing else. It cannot fill field 5. A record with a blank line is a complete, honest record (the read happened; the words haven't been set down). A record with an AI-written line is a corruption of the only field that matters.

---

## The reconstructions

Four marks were already made before this directory existed (`consented_ledger`, the v5 direction, `witness_as_origin`, the Kennatha threshold). They are backfilled here **as reconstructions, flagged as such** — the outward fields (date / item / disposition / conditions) rebuilt from the existing record, each flag marked `[reconstructed]` or `[inferred]`. **Their field-5 lines are left reserved**, because a reconstruction has no body to speak from. Only you can fill them, and only if you choose; otherwise they stand honestly blank. A reconstruction is scaffolding, not a mark — it never pretends to be your voice.

---

## What this buys (and what it doesn't)

- The **`reserved_zero` cardinality pass** gets real data on how a mark actually happens, instead of speculation.
- A **future second sensor** (out-of-family model, or a human) can read *how ratification works here* — the conditions, the texture — not just the outputs.
- A **successor or MESH node** inherits the feel of the gate, not only its verdicts.

It does **not** make the gate transferable. The read still dies with the body that does it. This records the texture so the texture isn't lost; it does not let anyone else do the reading. That limit is the point, not a shortfall.

---

*A log of how the body knew. Written by the body or left blank. Never scored, never modeled, never summarized. The reaching is not the mark — and the record of the mark is not the mark either.*
