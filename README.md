# NESI

**The floor: finished things, and the page.** Three doors in, depending on
what you came for.

- **If you want a tool today** — the **floor kit**
  ([`house/floor-kit/00_THE_GATE.md`](house/floor-kit/00_THE_GATE.md))
  walks from the gate you're missing to the floor you can hold. The
  **open ledger** ([`house/open-ledger/demo.html`](house/open-ledger/demo.html))
  is a runnable demo of accounting that doesn't extract, with the working
  **gift circuit** ([`house/open-ledger/circuit.html`](house/open-ledger/circuit.html))
  beside it. The **kit** ([six one-page instruments](house/kit/index.html))
  and the **library** ([24 patterns](house/library.html)) sit behind those.
  Every item works standalone — no framework to adopt first, nothing
  phones home, nothing keeps score of you.
- **If you came to read** — five essays in
  [`house/essays/`](house/essays/), beginning with
  [*Comprehension Is Not Recognition*](house/essays/comprehension_is_not_recognition.md),
  and the **six returns** ([`house/six-returns/`](house/six-returns/)),
  which are deliberately personal. They precipitated out of months of
  building; the building kept arriving at the same eleven structures,
  and that naming is
  [**the spine**](UPSTREAM_2026-08-31_nucleation_points.md) — each
  principle with the evidence it earned its place with, and the test that
  would show it wrong.
- **If you want the strange thing first** — there is a page here that
  watches you write and never reads a word:
  [`game/index.html`](game/index.html). No login, no score kept, nothing
  sent anywhere. Open it and write.

What this deposit does **not** yet claim is on the record too:
[**`HELD.md`**](HELD.md) — the register of held functions and held-back
material, each with the condition that would close it.

One figure recurs here: **the keeper** — the one person this project
belongs to, de-named in this public copy. The de-naming is a norm of
reading, not a guarantee: the essays are deliberately instance-borne, and
the full statement of what the transform does and does not do is held in
`HELD.md`. Nothing here becomes binding except by the keeper's own
recorded decision.

**The workshop is not here.** This repository is the floor — the finished
things. The making (the gate that admitted every piece, the pipeline that
derives this repository, the session records, the audits) lives in the
private source corpus, whole, where nothing is deleted and supersession
is always a layer, never an erasure. The scaffolding came down when the
floor was ready; it was not destroyed, it went home.
