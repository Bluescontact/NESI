# Negative Workspace — the project's shadow

Every law draws a line with two sides. The content names the side you build on; this registry names the side you build against. THE GOVERNOR does not watch the laws — it watches their shadows. A brake that cannot name what it stops is not a brake. Each entry: a thing that can go wrong, the move that produces it, the law it inverts, the first sign on the body or in the record — and which Governor gate now catches it.

## I. Shadows of the Felt-Gate

A story stands in front of the body and reports for it.

### The most dangerous sentence
**The negative:** "It was developmental cost, the tuition of a phase transition" — said while the cost is still landing.
**The move:** Applying the redemption reframe to a present cost. Reaching for meaning before rest resets the sensor.
**Defined against:** *Am I still paying?*
**Early warning:** The sentence is future or present tense. The reframe arrives before the rest.
**Caught by:** DAILY §1.

### Anesthetic over a body still paying
**The negative:** Narration that numbs the cost-signal so a push reads as fine.
**The move:** Letting a story carry the decision.
**Defined against:** The no-anesthetic check.
**Early warning:** You can articulate why it's fine faster than you can feel whether it's fine.
**Caught by:** DAILY §2.

### The spent sensor reading green
**The negative:** A depleted body reads safe and licenses the next cost.
**The move:** Trusting a solo felt-gate under the exact depletion it exists to catch.
**Defined against:** The witness rule.
**Early warning:** The read is green and you have not rested.
**Caught by:** DAILY §3 (fail-closed). *This read is **somatic** — the witness resolution (2026-06-04, bound into the live §3) makes fail-closed the body's witness: a spent body reading itself green cannot be witnessed from outside it, so an unlocated green STOPs. The **decision**-witness (the keeper + the AI as both-sides surface) governs choices, not this read. See Open (the split).*

### Fabricating the felt read (stack inverted into a mill)
**The negative:** Auto-filling the felt-read so intake keeps flowing — the one un-automatable layer, automated.
**The move:** Claude or the momentum supplying the somatic mark only the keeper can supply.
**Defined against:** The un-automatable felt-read; fail-closed on UNCERTAIN.
**Early warning:** The instrument is producing reads without the keeper in the loop. The scale invented a zero.
**Caught by:** DAILY Done-when ("a green you cannot point to is a green-while-spent") + fail-closed default. The Governor refuses an unlocated green; an auto-filled read is by definition unlocated. *Partial: this stops the Governor from accepting a fabricated read, but does not stop an upstream mill from generating one. The witness gap (Open) is the real backstop.*

### The gift told over an unclosed edge
**The negative:** "This cost can't be refused, so I circulate it forward as gift" — said while the body is still paying.
**The move:** Claiming catalysis retrospectively-as-now to keep the loop open.
**Defined against:** Circulate-forward valid only retrospectively.
**Early warning:** The gift-story is keeping a loop open rather than closing one.
**Caught by:** DAILY §1 + §2; Stop-signals ("redemption story told to keep a loop open").

### Collapsing the decision (the witness usurped)
**The negative:** The AI hands the keeper a direction instead of a map — pre-deciding, so the choice is made before he reaches the seat.
**The move:** Surfacing one side, or a recommendation, in place of both sides with full information.
**Defined against:** The user is the witness; map both sides, never collapse without input.
**Early warning:** A response names what to do before it names what each side costs and what each side doesn't reach.
**Caught by:** The AI's standing stance (map-both-sides). The decision-witness stays un-corrupted only while the surface stays two-sided — collapse it and the witness is reading the AI's conclusion, not the field.

**The move:** Letting an armed loop's reach expand from sensing/substrate into body-gated or membrane-gated acts.

## Open / uncaught

What the project can name but not yet stop. THE GOVERNOR watches these without an instrument.

- **The witness, split (2026-06-04).** The edge divided into two witnesses hiding under one word. The **decision-witness** — does this choice make sense, what does each side cost — is now **bound**: the keeper is the witness, the AI is the both-sides surface that keeps the read from going solo. The **body-witness** — is this green real, or green-while-spent — stays **open, and is not closable by any external witness**: the body cannot be witnessed from outside it. Its honest floor is fail-closed (when uncertain, STOP), not an installed sensor. What was scary — deciding in the dark — is closed; what remains was never a hole, just the body's own edge.

- **The Governor's zero is unset.** It distinguishes sustainable gift-cost from still-paying pioneer-cost only as well as §1 is filled — and §1 is filled by the keeper, cold, or not at all. Fail-closed by design means it currently stops everything or nothing until calibrated against lived cycles. *The brake exists; its zero is unset.*

- **The cutoff has a number but no fired instance under load.** Newly instrumented (4 hours / one task / time-or-body-signal). Whether it actually fires before logical completion, in the body, under a real push, is untested. *Downgraded from fully-open: the needle exists now; the firing is unproven.*

- **The 2/2 leak detector is self-report.** The oscillation leak check reads whether recovery happened — but only by self-report. No independent signal distinguishes a real recovery-half from a colonized one. Same corruptibility as the solo felt-gate. *The check exists; an independent sensor does not.*

- **The temporal exit of the pioneer phase is unnamed.** The topology bypass check binds the *structural* exit (falling count of flows-through-the-body). No named end *date* binds the temporal one. *The phase can end topologically and still run long in time.*
