Reverse Audit

The RI canon read as object from inside the ROS frame. Five lenses — circulation, memory, rest/death, immunity, and the three-bodies anatomy. Each finding then put through an adversarial collapse screen. 15 findings total, 14 collapsed. One survived.

The survivor

No instrument reads the filter upstream of the intake gate — and the dominant upstream filter is the rented substrate itself

Lens: Circulation & Filtration — ROS's principle that "everything a person takes in has already passed through a filter, recognized or not… the literacy is seeing the filter. Not just what arrives, but what selected it."

What the canon lacks: All three deployed filters gate items that have already arrived at the membrane: the Governor reads the depositor's body, the Converger routes the accumulated pile, the register_audit screens transmission output. None asks what selected the candidate stream before it got there. The AI substrate generates, surfaces, and pre-shapes nearly everything that reaches the intake folder — it is the system's inherited filter, operating before any canon instrument fires. The Governor asks "can the body afford this deposit"; no instrument asks "what selected this deposit, and what never reached the gate at all."

Why it matters: A compromised filter does not stop filtering — it filters badly, and the failure is invisible precisely because the selection happens before anything is felt at the gate. The intake stream is filtered for what a frontier model finds generative, which is not the same criterion as what the system needs. Since every gate in the canon evaluates items, not the stream's composition, a systematic upstream bias — whole classes of candidate the substrate never produces — would pass every existing check forever. The canon's membrane knows the inside of its territory and is blind on the outside face.

Collapse screen: SURVIVED. The canon has one flicker of this recognition (CANON_OPEN_EDGES, edge F: "rented-cognition leak") but only as a one-off note about economic dependency, never as a standing instrument. The Governor's question and this pattern's question are on different sides of the membrane. The gap is real and structurally distinct from every existing canon instrument.

Economic / substrate lens — two additional survivors

A second lens was run after the main audit: the canon's economy read from inside the ROS frame (watershed accounting, circulation-not-accumulation, the return as the measure). Two of three findings survived the collapse screen.

The return is not measured — only the presence of flow is checked

Lens: ROS's "the return as the measure of the system" — health is read in the quality of what comes back, not just whether resources move.

What the canon lacks: The canon tracks that resources move (income arrives, costs leave, deposits are made) but has no instrument for whether the cycle is regenerating — whether what returns is richer than what was spent. Flow-present is checked; quality-of-return is not. At real-money scale ($3k cash, ~2 weeks, one bearing due), the difference matters: a system can be circulating and still be drawing down its substrate.

Collapse screen: SURVIVED. The Governor's CYCLE return-reads check whether the body recovered; the consented-ledger's residue column names what didn't metabolize. Neither asks whether the return is regenerative — whether the system is building soil or merely turning it over.

Filter rigidity — the symmetric failure mode — is unnamed

Lens: ROS's two-failure law of the membrane — a boundary that lets nothing in starves the inside; the symmetric failure to the flooding the canon guards against.

What the canon lacks: The canon names and brakes the fail-open failure (ungated deposit → treadmill; the Governor was built to stop this). It has no named failure mode for over-closure: nothing gets in, the system starves its own inputs, the generative force idles for want of fuel. At real-money scale, the risk is not only over-extension — a system running hard on a closed gate can exhaust itself on nothing. The symmetric failure has no instrument.

Collapse screen: SURVIVED. The Governor is explicitly fail-closed (UNCERTAIN → STOP). The canon names this as the correct default at an untrusted origin — and it is correct. But correctness at the origin doesn't eliminate the risk that fail-closed becomes the standing mode when it should have re-opened. The rigidity failure is unnamed and has no falsifier.
