Contradictions — the live edges

These four verdicts triangulate into one finding: ROS fails open, the RI canon fails closed. Each framework makes an incompatible claim about a boundary's correct default state when its selectivity cannot be trusted. This is not a flaw in either — it is the most useful thing a counter-origin delivers.

Contradicts
Lossless torus with ungated intake
"Nothing is lost in this cycle; everything becomes something else" — the cycle is closed and lossless, and intake is ungated: receive it, let it land.
Canon counterpart: consented_ledger + the_governor

The RI canon holds the lossless-cycle claim to be literally false and load-bearingly so — "a closed ledger is a thermodynamic claim no organism can satisfy." Leaking is mandatory and must be consented by name. Ungated deposit is the realized treadmill the Governor was built to stop. ROS's watershed has no intake brake and no consented-leak column.

Two-failure law of the membrane
A boundary fails symmetrically — too permeable or too rigid — and both failures are equal: "the result is not safety, it is starvation."
Canon counterpart: the_governor (fail-closed) / witness_as_origin (trusted-or-fail-closed)

The canon prescribes closure as the correct default when the selector is degraded: UNCERTAIN defaults to STOP. ROS holds that a membrane sealed under failure is itself the pathology, equal to flooding. Incompatible claims about the correct default state. ROS would read the canon's deliberate brake-bias as the rigidity failure; the canon reads ROS's symmetric law as the exact failure mode that builds a treadmill out of virtue.

The ever-present witness (the stillness)
Stilling reliably reaches "the awareness that was always present behind the doing, the way the sky is always present behind the weather" — the solo inner witness is continuously available and trustworthy.
Canon counterpart: witness_as_origin + consented_ledger (witness requirement)

The canon holds the solo felt-gate "fails silent at the lethal end" — a clear read is indistinguishable from a dead sensor. The origin is trusted-or-fail-closed; a mandatory second origin reads for divergence. From inside, you cannot verify you have settled into the witness rather than into a dim sensor reporting clear. ROS's claim that the witness is always reachable by settling is exactly what the canon's second-origin doctrine was built to guard against.

ROS lands hard on the unresolved side of the one genuinely open cross-cluster frontier.
