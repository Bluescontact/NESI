# External extraction

Project-scoped override. This file takes precedence over the bundled
`anthropic-skills:external-extraction` skill for this repository — no local
copy of that skill's full instructions exists here to inherit from, so this
file states the procedure as it has actually run in practice (confirmed
against filed decks, e.g. the 2026-08-24 Aakash Gupta "Product Builder"
extraction) plus one addition named below.

## The procedure

### 1. Inventory

List what the source actually contains — its parts, its structure, its
claims — without judging any of it yet. Name the method used to reach the
source (crawl, paste, fetch) and record where it was blocked, not just where
it succeeded.

### 2. Audit

Separate what survives extraction standard from what doesn't. A claim passes
when it's a genuine structural fact about the source (an explicit sequencing
claim, a named taxonomy, a verbatim attributed quote). A claim is cut when
it's the source's own rhetorical or credibility-laundering move — flag it as
seen, but don't card it as a found nutrient.

### 3. Cards

File what survives audit as nutrient cards, one per finding: a Capacity /
Effort / Confidence rating, the verbatim quote it rests on, and a routing
note connecting it to NESI material — cited, not adopted, unless a mark says
otherwise.

**Compression constraint (added 2026-08-24, Gift 5 of the routing pass on
this deck):** a card must stand as a complete, meaningful unit from its one
verbatim quote and its rating alone, without needing its source link to still
resolve. Confirmed against this project's own extraction, whose 25 linked
guides were unreachable (`ROBOTS_DISALLOWED`, paywalled) yet whose cards
survived that intact — because the quote carried the claim, not the
citation. If a drafted card can't stand as one sentence without its source,
it isn't compressed enough yet; tighten it before filing.

### 4. Routing Note

Close with a routing note, not a decision. Cards order nothing — they wait
at the gate for the keeper's mark, per this project's standing rule that no law
becomes a lever to give the keeper less and no session marks on his behalf.
