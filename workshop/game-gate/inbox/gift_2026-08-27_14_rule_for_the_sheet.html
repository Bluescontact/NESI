GIFT — a rule for the sheet, not a solver

  • pattern: a tension membrane whose shape is a rule, not a simulation: each point of a sampled grid is displaced from the pull by a pure exponential falloff — one exp() per point, no solver, no iteration — and strain is simply the pull's reach. Pull past the reach threshold and the sheet tears; a torn membrane holds nothing. It heals only by elapsed real time, against a persisted timestamp — never while watched, never on demand, and nothing asks you to wait.
  • source shape: built and finished in a sibling project's station, sibling to the burn station and governed by the same persistence law.
  • capacity: a cheap, non-physics way to render a responsive, tactile-feeling surface, plus a distinct kind of consequence from a permanent scorch: a temporary but real cost the player cannot rush, because the countdown runs on the wall clock whether or not the page is open. Tension obeyed, not simulated.
  • why such value goes unrouted: a finished station never crosses a project boundary, and the pattern has a precondition — a surface or connector rendered as sampled points — that the receiving build may not have. Where no consumer exists, the honest disposition is deferral, not inventing a surface to force the gift in.
  • routing shape: a way in → a surface rendered as sampled points → an act → displace each point by exp(-distance/k) scaled by pull length; past the reach constant, flip a persisted torn state with a close-timestamp → a visible consequence → while torn the surface is inert, and it un-tears only once real time has actually elapsed, even across a reload.
