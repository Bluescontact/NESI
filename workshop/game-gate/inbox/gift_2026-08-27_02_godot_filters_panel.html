GIFT — the hand-sorted filter panel

  • pattern: a complete, playable filter surface — a tray of small material fractions, already separated by their own physics, moved by hand-drag into several real destinations, one of which is "set it down" (no destination, no confirmation, nothing drawn). Nothing sorts, scores, classifies, or suggests; which fraction goes where is entirely the hand's, and the panel has no opinion about it.
  • source shape: fully built in a sibling project on a different engine.
  • capacity: a finished interaction design — tray, drag mechanics, real exits, and its refusals proven by construction — that a filter sub-game can port instead of inventing one under time pressure. The discipline (no machine opinion, hand does the separating) is in the shape itself, not in a policy layered on.
  • why such value goes unrouted: value strands across a project boundary. A complete answer built in one project, on one engine, never crosses to the sibling that needs it — not struck, not superseded, simply never carried over.
  • routing shape: a way in → a sub-game needing a real interaction design → an act → port the panel's exact shape (tray, drag-to-exit, unlabeled, no confirmation on set-down) rather than designing from nothing → a visible consequence → the sub-game ships with a design already proven against its own laws.
