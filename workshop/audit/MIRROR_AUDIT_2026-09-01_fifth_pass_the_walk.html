Mechanics

1 · Seat-taking (twelve seats, first-person gestures) Jurisdiction: invented by the operator, installed for a player; no metric exists — a seat is a single string in local state. Reduction: not validation/completion/prizes; a declaration ("a declaration, not a commitment"). Cash: the player's orientation. Giraffe: take CAST purely to say "I GIVE IT AWAY" aloud and never open a run — nothing notices, nothing pends, no seat is better; absorbed. Verdict: TRANSFORMS — choosing a stance is the act, and the geometry makes it consequential later (which runs leave it) without ranking it now.

2 · The position card (carry / need / refuse) Jurisdiction: the player's alone. Reduction: free text, saved on input, read by nothing, blank complete, gating nothing. Cash: structurally nowhere — the rare mechanic that cannot be cashed even by its owner's surface. Giraffe: refuse "giraffes below the fourth vertebra"; absorbed without residue. Verdict: TRANSFORMS — this is the page's cleanest object precisely because nothing downstream requires it.

3 · The run cards and the state-words ("nothing settled yet · not yet aimed · the third day is in") Jurisdiction: operator-built, player-fed. Reduction: this is a three-item quest checklist per edge, rendered in deficit grammar — "nothing settled yet," "not yet aimed," "the week not begun" — and "the third day is in" is a running count in ordinal clothing. WEEK_DAYS exists to say numbers without digits. The page's own standing law, printed below: no number reaches you — no tally, count, rank, or bar that fills. A card that reports which of three requirements remain open, updated per act, with an ordinal day counter, is a tally and a progress readout; removing the digits removed the numerals, not the number. Cash: the player — but what it feeds is the closure loop, not the writing. Giraffe: leave a card at "the fifth day is in" forever — unpunished, absorbed; the deficit grammar still names it a deficit every load. Verdict: DECORATES (folded with entry 6, the same object at two renderings) — the mechanic-as-built falsifies the constraint-as-displayed, the exact shape of first-pass entry 1.

4 · The water — keep a line, blank allowed and counted Jurisdiction: operator-built; content-blind, verified. Reduction: the writing itself is unreduced — no length, no quality, no count of lines shown. Cash: the player's own kept text, and eventually the gift. Giraffe: keep a blank line seven days running. It is counted as settled AND it touches the day — so the blank keep is simultaneously the water act and the attendance token. "Blank is complete" is genuinely anti-validation for content: nothing distinguishes a blank from a paragraph anywhere, which is the law working. But because a keep is also a day-touch, the act's floor collapses to one click, and the closure machinery (entry 6) cannot tell an act from an attendance tap. Blank-is-complete does not hollow the act; wiring the act to the day-counter does. Verdict: TRANSFORMS on its own terms, with its seam discharged into entry 6 where the hollowing actually happens.

5 · The light — aim down or aim out, either completes Jurisdiction: operator-built. Reduction: the aim is stored, read by nothing, and the two options are identical in every consequence — same closure credit, same map, same gift, same everything. The page says so honestly ("Nothing favors one. The aim is the act."). An unread declaration is lawful here — entry 2 is one. But entry 2 gates nothing; the aim is one of three requirements for closing a level. A mandatory act with no differential consequence, where every input wins, is a completion checkbox with two labels — Kelly's second item wearing first-person prose. The honest disclosure does not redeem it; by this deposit's own doctrine, a frame held by prose is a broken frame — a real aim would have to change something, anything, downstream. Giraffe: aim down, then out, then down, forty times — "the last aim stands," nothing notices; absorbed, but absorbed the way a form absorbs any value, which is the tell. Verdict: DECORATES — a fake choice validating completion.

6 · The growth — the seven-day gate and the root-segment display Two objects, ruled together. The gate: seven distinct real dates, non-consecutive allowed, absence never subtracts — this is genuinely not a streak, and it is the first mechanic in this corpus that structurally cannot be closed the same day it ships. That property is real and named. The display: seven fixed slots, filling left to right, monotone, toward a known target, completion rewarded (gold + walkable). What distinguishes seven slots that fill from "a bar that fills as you go"? Nothing structural — no digits is the only difference, and the law forbids the bar, not the numeral. The root imagery is a progress bar wearing a root's clothes. Giraffe on gaming it: open the page seven days, click "tend it today," write nothing, aim once. The level closes — verified in source: one blank keep satisfies the settled clause while also touching a day. A fully hollow week closes a level. Who cashes a hollow close? No platform, no operator — the ledgers never see this page. The hollow close cashes into the map's gold and the player's own sense of a run "worked to its close." That is the Duolingo shape minus the shareholder: an attendance loop whose reward vocabulary ("this run holds," "the week is in") asserts that holding occurred when only clicking occurred. In a single-player local system this is self-deception headroom rather than extraction — but the mechanic manufactures it: all three closure requirements are click-satisfiable, so the level's win condition measures attendance and only attendance, while the surface's language credits the player with settling, aiming, and growth. Verdict: DECORATES — the display falsifies the page's printed law, and the closure it feeds decorates calendar attendance with the vocabulary of work.

7 · Closed runs drawn gold; walking gated on closed runs Jurisdiction: operator-built. Reduction: a persistent map where completed edges accumulate in gold is a collection display, and with all members visible the full-gold solid is a latent completion state no text names but every eye can see. Against that: the gold is load-bearing, not commemorative — a closed run is the only path a position can move along, so the map reports the game's actual traversability rather than trophying it (the same structural redemption that cleared the seat display in the first pass). Weaker than that precedent in one respect: seats could be un-picked freely; a run never un-closes short of total reset. Cash: the player's mobility. Giraffe: click a far seat to be told, gently, the solid has no such member — the refusal teaches geometry, doesn't punish; absorbed. Verdict: BOUNDARY — transforms as movement infrastructure today; becomes a completion board the day anything addresses the set of closed runs as a set. Re-run if any surface ever reads closed runs in aggregate. It inherits entry 6's taint passively: what it draws gold may be hollow, and it cannot tell.

8 · The door out — the CAST—TANK gift Jurisdiction: operator-built; the assembled text leaves only by hand-copy, nothing sends itself, verified (no clipboard API, no network, no download). Reduction: does assembly create a harvest incentive over the writing? The gift concatenates every non-blank kept line from every edge — so more writing means a fuller gift, which is incentive-shaped. But the incentive's beneficiary is whoever the player chooses to hand the copy to, outside the system; the machine keeps no copy-count, displays no gift-size, and a walk of all-blank keeps yields the page's single best sentence ("a complete gift is still a gift") rather than a shame state. The blank-gift line closes the harvest loop at its own root: an empty harvest is ruled complete. Cash: outward, by hand, to a recipient the system never learns. Giraffe: close the door-out run on blanks and ceremonially gift nothing to a friend; the page endorses it in advance. Absorbed by design. Verdict: TRANSFORMS — the one place the corpus's give-it-away gesture is enacted rather than accounted.

9 · Set it down One line, as an anti-mechanic: full clear, one confirm, "quitting loses nothing that mattered" — honest about what it does delete. The giraffe's permanent exit, built in. No verdict row.
