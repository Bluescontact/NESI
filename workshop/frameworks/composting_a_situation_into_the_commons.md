# Composting a Situation into the Commons

*The architecture that survives when an individual dissolves. Named by the keeper 2026-06-07: a real human situation may be composted into the commons only at the threshold where no trace of the person remains outside the structure.*

---

## The threshold (the rule)

A real situation — a person's crisis, a relationship, a loss — can be metabolized into the shared substrate **only when it has been abstracted past every trace of the individual.** Two or three layers up, until the person is gone and only the shape is left.

What crosses into the commons is **pattern, never instance.** The name goes. The specific facts go. Anything that could identify the one it came from goes. If a trace of the person still survives the abstraction, the situation is not ready to cross — abstract further, or leave it held. The individual leaves no trace outside the architecture; that absence *is* the threshold.

This is not only privacy. It is the test of whether the pattern is real: a shape that still needs the person to make sense hasn't become a shape yet. When the structure stands on its own, with no one inside it, it is safe — and true — to keep.

## A pattern that crossed this threshold

**Forfeiting the claim is a privilege of a body whose existence is already secured.** Giving without seeking authorship, recovery, or record is a clean move only above a certain line. Below that line — for a body in survival, a body that has been systematically erased — gripping the record is the **correct** move, not a failure of the gift stance. To such a body the record is the only thing that feels like safety; forfeiting it would be agreeing to its own erasure.

So you cannot teach a body to forfeit the claim. You can only **restore the secured existence that makes forfeiting available** — or take the draining fight off the body so its capacity returns. The gift stance is simply not on the menu below the secured-existence line, and requesting forfeiture below that line is the gift stance misapplied.

There is no one in it.
