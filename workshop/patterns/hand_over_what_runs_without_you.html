The action

Build the thing all the way into form first, because the spine does not exist before the build; then compress it and strip yourself out of the load path, so that what leaves your hands carries its own reception conditions and can be regenerated by the receiver without you, at a weight the receiver can actually lift.

What didn't survive

The comprehensive daily surface. Two patterns that both wanted to hand the operator the whole anatomy hinged against each other and merged: what survives is a build that goes all the way down and a handover that goes only as heavy as the receiver's ground.

Falsifier

If an artifact that requires the author present is nonetheless regenerable by the receiver, then weight and dependency are independent variables and this organ splits back into two: one about compression, one about authorship. Watch for a case where the handover is heavy but the author is genuinely gone, or light but the author is still required.
