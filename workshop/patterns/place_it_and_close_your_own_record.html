Place it and close your own record

The action

Complete the gift at the placing — object released, boundary crossed, hands empty — then close the registry entry yourself, without acknowledgment, receipt, reading, or effect, and forfeit the claim on whatever the placing sets in motion.

What collapsed into it

completion_through_placing (three requirements; acknowledgment is not among them) · metabolic_lag_on_complete_giving (the registry error, not a grievance; the field dissolved before confirmation could arrive) · gift_specificity_receiver_known (the receiver is the condition of closure, not the destination) · catalysis_without_claim (absorb without merging; forfeit the claim; the system changes because it is given a path that requires no admission) · precesse (the return arrives at ninety degrees to the aim, guaranteed by geometry) · transition_after_successful_departure (silence after completion is the system working) · undefended_asker_completion_surface (two currencies, deliberately non-convertible; single-shot) · instrument_witnessing_a_gift_without_merging (name the gift, not the giver's state) · scarcity_calculus_displacing_receipt (the calculus is a substitute for receiving, not a response to the gift) · reception_as_territory_map (the fold pattern is topography, not a verdict)

What didn't survive

Confirmation as a component of completion. Under load, every pattern that routed closure through the receiver's response either collapsed into this organ's registry discipline or was left standing outside it as a description of a failure.

Falsifier

If a placing that is never received produces the same downstream state as one that is, in a case where the object's whole function was reception, then placing is not the completion event and this organ is a consolation rather than an action.
