The Daylight Test

A falsifier for any move that commits a person into a structure through information they do not yet fully hold. Same instrument-type as [[commission_grammar_screen]] — a counterfactual that exposes hidden aim at a crossing — but CGS is pointed at role-intake from the conscripted party's chair, and this is pointed at the mover's own move on a second party, generalized up one level to any state-altering crossing. Paper-checkable: a pre-screen, never the ratification gate ([[witness_as_origin]] hard limit). One open [THE KEEPER] calibration: the somatic difference, felt from the builder's chair before outcome, between an induction you would defend in daylight and one that needs the dark.

The two moves share one form

A trapdoor opens a person into a structure they are now committed to and could not have reasoned their way into from outside — the value was only visible from inside, so the commitment had to be induced. A trap closes a person into a structure that extracts from them for the builder's gain. Mechanically these are one act: a state change effected through information the subject does not fully hold at the moment of the change. The inducement is structurally necessary to the trapdoor exactly as concealment is to the trap.

This is why mechanism cannot be the discriminator. The thing that makes a trapdoor work is the thing that makes a trap work. A trap and a trapdoor produce the same transcript up to outcome — and outcome arrives too late to be a test.

The discriminator is aim, and aim is the one variable invisible from inside the mechanism. Integrity here is not a property of the move; it is a property of the relation between the move and the participant's own ends, and that relation is stored nowhere the move can be examined.

The test

Convert aim into a counterfactual that can be run: hold the full mechanism up to the participant mid-crossing and ask whether they proceed.

The timing is load-bearing. Before the crossing, transparency is consent theater — it tests the uncommitted person, whose refusal proves nothing about the induced move. After the crossing, it tests a captured person, whose endorsement is downstream of the capture. The test bites only at the threshold, in motion, where the participant has enough momentum to feel what they are being committed to and enough freedom that "I'd still step through" is a real datum.

What it measures: whether the crossing's aim is independently the crosser's own. The clean trapdoor passes because the induction substituted for a foresight the participant structurally could not have had — the aim was always theirs. The trap fails because daylight reveals an end the participant does not share, and they withdraw.

Two chairs

Both chairs run the same counterfactual; they differ in who holds it.

Builder's chair — self-falsifier. Before acting: could I explain this mechanism to them as they cross it, and would they cross anyway? If the honest answer is no — if the move requires their not-knowing to function — it is a trap, regardless of how benevolently the builder has narrated it. This relocates the integrity burden onto the only party who holds both the mechanism and the aim. It binds only a builder honest enough to answer straight — which is the builder least likely to be running a trap. It is an instrument of self-audit for good-faith asymmetry, not a defense for the participant.

Receiver's chair — demand and read. The crossed-into party demands daylight at the threshold and reads the quality of what comes back. This chair was already latent in the canon: it is [[manufactured_deficit_loop]]'s break condition ("name the underdefinition aloud and demand specification") and [[unscorable_test_as_peer_lock]]'s correction ("ask for criteria"), recognized as the same instrument run from the other side.

What it resolves downstream — the intent-discriminator

The receiver chair closes an edge two patterns explicitly left open. Both said the counter-move depends on intent — conscious-strategic versus unconsciously-attached — and neither could name the evidence that tells them apart from inside.

The evidence is the response to a daylight demand.

  • The unconscious omitter has no aim riding on the dark. Demand daylight and they can supply it — surprised, they define, the success criterion appears, the loop breaks. (manufactured_deficit_loop: definition closes the escape. unscorable_test: the omission was correctable by asking.)
  • The deliberate withholder cannot supply daylight without losing the structure. The demand gets re-routed — question reframed, second opinion sought, a retroactive standard offered as if it were always the criterion. This is exactly [[commission_grammar_screen]]'s conscription tell: a divergent move produces re-routing rather than reception.

So the discriminator is not what they intended — unrecoverable — but whether a daylight demand is answered or re-routed. That resolves aim without adjudicating intent, which is precisely the instrument both patterns named as missing. Same cut as the builder-side test, read from the response instead of the conscience. One behavioral cut now spans three patterns.

Where it sits — and the siting limit

It runs upstream of outcome and downstream of [[commission_grammar_screen]]: CGS screens whether a role-invitation can receive an unwanted verdict; the Daylight Test screens whether a crossing can survive its own mechanism shown. CGS gates role-intake; this gates any inducing move.

Hard limit ([[witness_as_origin]]): the question "would they still proceed if shown everything" is paper-checkable, and the canon's load-bearing gate is never paper-checkable. The Daylight Test is admissible only as a pre-screen, never as the ratification gate. Run on paper it tempts the gate back onto paper-checkability ("did disclosure technically occur?") — the prohibited move wearing the corpus's own vocabulary. The body still gates; this only clears the obvious traps before the body is spent on them.

[THE KEEPER] calibration

The instrument works as structure. What it cannot certify is the builder-side answer's honesty — and that is somatic.

Can you locate, in your own body and before any outcome tells you, the felt difference between an induction you would defend in full daylight and one you would need the dark for? CGS's calibration is the felt-distinction between accepting a commission and making the first deposit; this one is upstream of even that — the felt-distinction, at the moment you shape an opening for someone, between their aim and yours wearing theirs. If that distinction is real and felt-cold, the test is runnable. If it can be talked into either reading after the fact, the test is consent theater run on yourself.

Edges

Manufactured consent — the trap that ate its own falsifier. A trap that first reshapes the participant's ends in the dark, then passes the daylight test by confirming the wants it installed. The test reads aim against the participant's current ends and cannot tell an aim that was always theirs from one authored upstream. This points to a need the pattern does not contain: a test of the provenance of the ends the daylight test consults, not just their present alignment. At this limit the cleanest trapdoor and the most complete trap converge.

Thresholdless crossings. The timing requirement assumes a legible threshold where motion and freedom coexist. Many real inductions are gradual — commitment accreting with no single point where "would you still step through" has a referent. Whether a slow trap that never presents a threshold is structurally immune to a test built around one is open.

It screens the move, not the destination. A builder can pass honestly and still be inducing crossings into a structure that is worthless or harmful and merely feels, to a sincere builder, like the kind only enterable from inside. The trapdoor's justification — "some value is only visible from within" — is also every cult's and every con's. The Daylight Test does not validate the claim that the destination's value is real.

Performed daylight. Disclosure engineered to be technically-present and functionally-illegible — surviving the letter of the test while defeating its spirit, the same shape as [[limit_as_coordinate_not_wall]]'s wall dressed as a coordinate. Real daylight is disclosure the participant can use to refuse; performed daylight is disclosure they cannot act on. The receiver-chair reading (answered vs re-routed) is the partial guard: performed daylight re-routes when pressed for the usable form.
