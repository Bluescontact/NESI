## The action

When a node is carrying what it cannot carry, change the container's topology — add the second node, take the weight off whatever is attackable, return the function to the mandate that funds it — rather than modifying the behavior of the node.

## What collapsed into it

`load_off_the_vulnerable_node` (whatever is attackable carries none of the weight) · `carrier-topology-as-infrastructure` (the exit condition is container change, not carrier change) · `second_node_changes_topology` (one to two is categorical, not gradational; ten in the wrong configuration do nothing) · `ejection_immunity_personal_node` (you cannot be ejected from something you instantiate yourself) · `migrating_single_point_of_failure` (during copying, the load-point is not stationary) · `keystone_held_by_least_authority` (removal test: collapse or redistribution) · `distributed_veto_architecture` (1+2, not three peers; and no veto reaches the agenda) · `bilateral_route_formation` (route = A participating AND B participating; conjunctive, not additive) · `interdependence_membership_threshold` (bidirectional load transfer, tested by removal) · `conditions_over_direction` (set conditions and step back; direction competes with emergence) · `relocation_release_amnesty` (release and amnesty as one operation) · `tetrahedral_agent_protocol` (one job per vertex, and name what it must resist) · `written_record_as_pre_meeting_coalition` (two positions in one channel collapse serial isolation without coordination) · `refusal_as_sealed_escape_route` (declining the role removes the surface the cost was routed onto) · `repetition_event_to_institution` (the interval is the deposit; the third instance is the threshold) · `coercive_care_form` (sincerity of care and structure of the container move independently)

## What didn't survive

Capacity-building as an answer to a carrier singularity. Nothing survived that fixed a topology problem by making the carrying node stronger.

## Falsifier

If a behavioral change at a single node ever produces a durable exit from a load pattern with no change to the container, then topology is not the independent variable and this organ is one instrument among several rather than the governing act.
