The Locating Move

The pattern

Locate, never steer.

A system serving a person does not hand them a position on its map — it keeps the ground true under their feet and lets the route derive itself where they stand. The decision tree is the routing. The decisions are the topology. Routing is locally derived from elevation.

Five things must be locatable in one expandable field:

  1. The person — where they stand right now. Only they can place this; the system holds an empty, honest slot and never fills it.
  2. The object — the thing in front of them.
  3. The process — what stage of what metabolism the object is inside.
  4. The decision — what the object is asking for.
  5. The field of past decisions — everything already marked, rendered as terrain, not a log.

Perfect alignment with zero merging: the system never blends into the person's judgment, never pre-digests, never steers. Location is the whole service. The move happens where the person is standing, and only they make it.

What it replaces

Any mechanism whose geometry puts the machine in the steering seat — authored decision trees, pre-cut option branches, self-scored improvement ledgers, sympathetic statements about the person's inner state. These fail by geometry, not by build quality: a branch the person didn't cut is a corridor, and every corridor that isn't theirs ends in a wall. The failure shows up as lens misalignment — the person cannot locate themselves in the surface, because there is no place in it to stand.

The gate form

Before any surface ships, one question: does this make something locatable, or does it steer? Runs beside the load-test (which gates whether a thing deserves to exist); this gates its geometry. Both run before building.

Falsifier

If adopting this pattern never stops or reshapes a steering-seat build — if every surface would have shipped identically without it — it was ceremony. It also fails if the "person places themselves" slot ever gets filled by the machine, however gently: a mirror-sentence about where the person is, what they are ready for, or what state they are in is the steering seat wearing empathy.

Provenance

Named upstream of two convergent failures (2026-07-30): repeated decision-toggle asks and a self-improvement tracking organ both failed the same way — the AI holding the map and handing the person a position. Adopted to session canon the same day (PROTOCOLS.md, beside the load-test), then proven in the first build it governed: THE_TERRAIN.html, where all past decisions became sculptable ground (four faces: time, relationship, jurisdiction, discharge) and the person raises or lowers the elevation themselves. Crossed to library on the keeper's mark as "the channel naming itself" — the pattern was recognized as the name of the arc that produced it.
