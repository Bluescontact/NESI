The action

Hold the verdict — the settling-read that a thing is ready — at the origin, refuse to let any container, clock, or fluent second reader perform it, fail closed when the object is past the instrument's reach, and let nothing pass the door carrying an unmarked confidence or a stripped provenance.

What collapsed into it

witness_as_origin (the zero from which all witnessing is measured; trusted or fail-closed, never witnessed) · externalize-container-not-verdict (the container is licensed in full; the verdict and its timing stay home, because whatever times the read has already decided whether) · the_door_as_stack_layer (the crossing is its own layer with its own law) · render_without_canonizing (rendering is a function, canonization is an act; three-node minimum) · ai_constitutional_stack (threshold, keeper, decision surface — entry, retention, marking) · untagged_provenance_defaults_to_true (the defect is in what the interface will not transmit; skepticism is the first cost optimized away) · cascade_failure_across_layers (confidence must fall as the claim approaches the decision) · second_reader_problem (a validator must be causally downstream of the object; fluency is internal consistency) · learning_type_boundary (structural learning reads the domain; behavioral learning models the human) · proximity_instrument_horizon (past the horizon the instrument does not degrade, it goes blind while appearing to function) · translated_origin_failure_mode (the sensor keeps operating from a shifted zero and passes every check) · the_daylight_test (hold the full mechanism up mid-crossing; mechanism cannot be the discriminator) · source_check_before_giving (many states, one observable; instrument the source, not the output) · framework_as_perceptual_instrument (direct read off observable shape, no disclosure required) · attractor_currency and holding_mode_ladder and future_image_as_pull (the direction read, and the rung that determines whether it can run at all)

What didn't survive

The second opinion. Every candidate for an inside-the-frame validator — a peer with the same history, the same body later, a fluent renderer — dissolved here and reappeared, transformed, in the next organ, STAKE THE READ.

Falsifier

If an external instrument ever performs the settling-read and the loop does not atrophy, the verdict is externalizable and this organ is a preference about sovereignty rather than a structural law. Also fails if a shifted zero is ever caught from inside — that would remove the need for the organ that follows.
