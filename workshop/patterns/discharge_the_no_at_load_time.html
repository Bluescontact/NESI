The action

Do the refusing now, before the encounter, in the build — then delete the route around the brake, so that at execution nothing has to be recalled, resisted, or decided.

What collapsed into it

load_to_form (tier one is attention-held; tier two is the default output of a correctly-operating instance) · structural_incapacity_over_prohibition (could a fully compliant system still have produced the harm?) · brakes-only-path (delete the second edge; do not strengthen the brake) · exclusion_before_offer (unconditionality is a temporal product — the conditioning ran at T-minus) · configure_before_demand (prestress: the paths exist before the load arrives) · pressure_masquerade_detection (enumerate the illegitimate inputs at rest, not under pressure) · pre_funded_debt_unrepresentable (the time axis a ledger needs has zero length inside the form) · commission_grammar_screen (the doorway is an event with a structure; a pre-closed commission makes every downstream check theatre) · surface_without_no (a decline counts only if it mutates state that gets read before the next offer) · actor_vs_dynamic_naming (the pointing vector is set once, at founding) · falsifier_travels_with_pattern (mark-time capture; the disproof is welded on at authoring) · no_completion_function (the instrument's stopping-place written into its law, not decided live)

What didn't survive

Willpower as a mechanism. Under compression, every pattern that ran on remembering the rule at the moment of pressure collapsed into a pattern that removes the need to remember. Nothing survived that required the operator to hold a line live.

Falsifier

If a rule paid for at load time still degrades under peak pressure at the same rate as an attention-held rule, the two tiers are one tier and this organ is a preference about design taste rather than an action.
