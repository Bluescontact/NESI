The workshop

This is not an archive — it is the shop where the rest of the house gets made, and it runs a loop you can walk. Everything below is read live from the shop's own ledgers, the same files the mechanisms write.

inbox → the gate → the spine → the loom → the gauges → the compost

1 · The inbox — the receiving bench

Gifts arrive here as cards — capacity found in past work, written up so it can be judged. Nothing on this bench is part of the house yet.

how the pipeline works

2 · The gate — the crossing bench

Nothing enters the house except by a recorded decision. Each crossing below is quoted from the ledger the gate itself writes — the decision, verbatim, with its date. The mechanism that refuses bad crossings is code, not policy.

latest crossings · full record: MARKS.jsonl

3 · The spine — the seating

Everything admitted gets seated under one of the twelve principles the work kept arriving at. The principles, with their evidence and the test that would show each wrong, are the spine.

4 · The loom — declared relations

Relations between pieces are declared by a hand at a prompt, never inferred by a machine. Each strand: two ends, the relation in the hand's own words, and its receipt. The woven whole is the weave.

latest strands · full record: DECLARATIONS.jsonl

5 · The gauges — the shop's own health

Instruments that read these same ledgers and report, in their own words, whether the shop is working — including the ones that report it isn't. A held gauge is waiting on a named open question, and holding is a lawful state here.

showing the most recent run only — every run is on the ledger · the instruments: source · independent audits of these mechanics, including of this page: the audits

6 · The compost — the outflow

A shop that only accumulates rots. Removal runs on an enforced schedule (the subtraction membrane); everything removed lands here with its cause, and the full original is always kept.

Honesty note: this page is the shop seen through its own glass. The levers — admitting, declaring, subtracting — are command-line mechanisms that run where the work lives; a web page can watch the ledgers but cannot pull a lever, and does not pretend to. The rest of the shop, room by room: the pipeline · the gate room · the patterns · the audits · the intake · everything else. And the game this shop exists to make is playable: the page, and the walk.
