Crystallized from a dated working record. The instance layer — machine paths, session narration — stays in the source corpus; what crosses is what the record still teaches.

The pattern: a working corpus does not live inside a sync boundary

A cloud-sync folder is a second writer with its own opinions about every file: partial hydration, background reverts, lock contention with tooling that expects a plain disk. A corpus whose ledgers are append-only and whose checks assert byte-facts cannot share its ground with a writer it does not control. The repository was relocated whole to a plain local path, and the sync boundary now contains nothing the system depends on.

The discipline the move followed

Diagnosis before motion — the symptom traced to the boundary, not patched around. The move made whole, then verified by its own instruments rather than asserted: the check suite run from the new ground, the ledgers byte-compared, the relocation recorded as a dated layer rather than a silent change. Any future session opens from the new path; the old tree stands as the predecessor record, not a second truth.

What it left behind as a lesson

A falsifier that points at the wrong ground checks nothing — one instrument kept validating the abandoned tree until a later audit caught it. A relocation is not finished when the files have moved; it is finished when everything that names the old ground has been found and repointed, and the finding is an audit's job, not memory's.
