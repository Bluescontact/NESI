Watchers

Standing flags on crossed patterns whose distinctiveness or safety rests on something not yet proven out. Not a task list. Not a bug tracker. An entry here does not get resolved, closed, or auto-cleared by any organ — it stays visible until the keeper removes it himself, on his own read, whenever that comes.

Status vocabulary: unresolved tension is the only status these carry. Nothing escalates it, nothing downgrades it. If a watcher's condition later gets addressed, the fix belongs in the pattern file itself (a revision, a cross-link, an edge closed) — and only then does the keeper decide whether the watcher entry is struck or kept as history.
