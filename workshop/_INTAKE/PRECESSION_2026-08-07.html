PRECESSION

The keeper, 2026-08-07: the secondary actions of each process or exchange become more impactful than the direct output.

Fuller's term, and the right one. The honeybee is after nectar. Pollination is the inadvertent side effect at right angles to what the bee intended, and it is the effect that regenerates the plant world. The bee is not trying.

It is already the law here — it just had no name

This is not a mechanic to add. It is what the system has been doing at every scale, described from the outside for the first time.

The geometry is exact

Fuller's precession is the resultant at ninety degrees to the line of action.

In this world the line of action is vertical — the fall, the spire, the descent, the column. And every precessional effect listed above lands on the horizontal: moisture diffuses across the triangle, held ground is a region, deposit spreads, the base of the tetra is a plane.

Direct output runs along the axis. The precessional effect spreads across the plane at right angles to it. The tetra already has both — three edges from apex to base, and a base face. Nothing had to be adjusted.

What it forces

A build gate, of the same kind as the four physics. A station, process, or exchange is not designed until its secondary effect is named — and if the secondary is not the more consequential of the two, something is wrong with the design, not with the law.

Right now: the filters have no named secondary. The membrane has no named secondary. The four station minigames were specified with two outputs, which are both direct. Their precessional effects are unwritten.

The constraint this puts on play, and it is a hard one

Precession only works if the actor is not aiming at it. A bee that knew about pollination and went for pollination would stop being a bee and start being a farmer, and the effect would collapse into an intention.

So: the direct action has to be genuinely worth doing for its own sake. The player must want the nectar. If the direct output is busywork and the player is told the real value is elsewhere, they are performing a ritual for an effect they cannot see and cannot aim at, and the game becomes a chore with a moral.

This is the sharpest design problem in the system and it has not been faced. Every station needs a direct output the player actually wants, and that direct output must not be the point.

What it tidies up

  • Why the dam's output is above it. Not a curiosity. Precession.
  • Why nothing may be pointed at. The system cannot flag a deep, cannot recommend, cannot say "you keep returning to X." Under precession that stops being a restraint and becomes a requirement: pointing at a secondary effect destroys it.
  • Why the far gate opens from the other side. The giver's direct act is giving. The gift is the precessional effect and it is not in their hands.
  • Why there is no scoring. A score measures direct output. Under this law direct output is the least informative thing about any act.

Open

  • Whether precession is stated as a binding law or held as a description.
  • The named secondary for each of the four stations.
  • Whether a process can have more than one precessional effect.
  • Whether the operator ever learns what a secondary effect was — and whether being told would destroy it.
