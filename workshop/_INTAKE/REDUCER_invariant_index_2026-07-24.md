1. **Family members are NOT redundant to their invariant — they are its facets.** *Gift Specificity — Receiver Known* is not redundant to `gift`; *Ratification by Live Hands* is not redundant to `proximity`; *Absence Has No Error Coordinate* is not redundant to `limit`. The difference-preserving guard forbids it — and here it fires on essentially every family member, not a few.

2. **There is no true redundancy in the canon to cut.** A near-duplicate scan (Jaccard over word-sets) finds **0 real duplicates**: only 11 pairs above 0.35, the maximum is 0.50 (half-shared), the median pattern's closest neighbor is 0.19. **So "subtract the redundant" has no safe referent here.** Honoring your gate on every subtraction means reporting that the gate has nothing to open: the canon is already differentiated; cutting it would destroy, not reduce.
