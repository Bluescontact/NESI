## THE NUTRIENTS — shelf grammar: a name · the relation · what it composes with

A nutrient is a small named relation discovered by doing, which composes with others
(cowan_workshop.html, the shelf's own definition). Each of these was found by reading
the standing build through Fuller's instruments; each names only what is already there.

**1 · The tension is continuous, the stations are islands.**
The four stations never touch each other; what makes them one object is the water
routed between them by the operator's hand — discontinuous compression, continuous
tension. Composes with: tensegrity · the-hand-is-the-anti-optimum (Cowan shelf) ·
law 5. Tether: BUILD_RECORD "every routing is a drag by the operator; no computed
pass" — the tension member is literally the drag gesture.

**2 · The seam is one closed great circle, and that is why it holds.**
The two-cupola split was chosen because its cut is a single closed great circle, not
a scatter of edges — one continuous tension line around the whole solid. Composes
with: the-seam-is-the-tension-member (Cowan shelf) · the four-diagonals-interchangeable
ruling · edge-effect. Tether: PROTOCOLS geometry block, verified 2026-08-09, "the only
split whose cut is a single closed great circle."

**3 · The dam is the trim tab.**
One lever, held or released, and the whole downstream world answers — ground, lake,
lens, light. The smallest surface in the build carries the largest consequence.
Composes with: one-output-many-depositings (Cowan shelf) · law 1's three outputs ·
light-as-the-return-flow. Tether: BUILD_RECORD "the dam panel's release is the only
governed act; shutting the panel is not an action."

**4 · Light is the precessional return.**
The player routes water DOWN; what comes back is light, at right angles to the effort
— room brightness = 1 − exp(−cycles/9), and the watered lenses send light up. The bee
is not trying to pollinate; the writer is not trying to light the room. Composes with:
light-as-the-return-flow (Cowan shelf) · the Second Output gate's interface guard ·
damage-ripples-back. Tether: BUILD_RECORD sentence-lens section — "the return flow now
runs through the player's own sentences."

**5 · Removing the clock was ephemeralization, not amputation.**
The 100-word interval was deleted, not replaced with a lighter counter — the sentence
itself became the arrival, and the file now counts nothing. More with less until the
mechanism is doing everything with nothing. Composes with: derive-don't-store-a-count ·
law 2 · the-load-test-build-gate. Tether: BUILD_RECORD supersession section, "Nothing
in the file counts words — which is also the cleaner reading of law 2."

**6 · The tank is the tetra seen small.**
The minimum system — four faces, the fewest that enclose — appears at every scale:
the tank in the room band IS the writing tetra, opened by a click into the full net.
The whole is inside the way in, which is the entry-tetra line made walkable. Composes
with: the 4·6·4·1 form (vertices never named here) · T1–T6 · verbatim-is-place.
Tether: BUILD_RECORD writing-tetra section, ruling T1.

**7 · Worked-ness is structural, not judged.**
An unworked stone hangs at the writer's node and will not carry past it — the gate is
geometry (behavior as the carrier), never a score, never a prompt. Synergy read
correctly: the whole refuses without any part saying no. Composes with: behavior-
before-color (Cowan shelf) · law 10 · held-is-habitable-latency. Tether: BUILD_RECORD
T4, "no text refuses him... Unworked forever is lawful; nothing prompts."

**8 · Declining to break a symmetry is a decision.**
The four cupola nets are congruent under the solid's 24 rotations; the seam axis stays
unfixed BY RULING, not by neglect — the first time this wall was answered from inside
the geometry. Composes with: the-slot-stays-empty · held-not-asserted · the entry-tetra
collision (recorded, unreconciled). Tether: PROTOCOLS, Kevin's mark 2026-08-09, "leave
it unfixed — the four are interchangeable... This is a decision, not a deferral."

**9 · The stone is a verb.**
A sentence is not a thing sitting; it is something happening — banked live at the
keystroke, worked by the hand, descended by a drag, settled as ground, watered as a
lens, returned as light. "I seem to be a verb," carried by the player's own words.
Composes with: verbatim-is-place (Cowan shelf) · law 4 · the sentence-lens (Kevin's
articulation). Tether: BUILD_RECORD, "Sentence → fractions → spire → sediment → lens,
with nothing blurring whose words went where."
