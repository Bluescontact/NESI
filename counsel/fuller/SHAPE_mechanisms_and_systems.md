## SYSTEMS — how the mechanisms compose, and what emerges

**S1 · The tension–leverage circuit (M1 + M2) — standing, built, walkable.**
Tensegrity supplies the load path (hand-routed water binding untouching stations);
the trim tab supplies the release point (one lever at the dam). Composed, they make
the build's one complete circuit: effort distributed across the whole net, consequence
concentrated at the smallest surface. What emerges is the game's actual feel —
everything you do is light, and one thing you do matters — without any part of the
system saying so. This is synergy in the strict sense: no station, alone, predicts
the circuit. LAWFUL-NOW; it is a description of what is already verified in
BUILD_RECORD, offered so the counsel can defend it against additions.

**S2 · The precessional return (M2 + M4) — standing, built.**
The release drives water DOWN; the truss of settled sentences catches it and sends
LIGHT back up, at right angles to the intent — the writer lights the writing room as
the bee pollinates. Composed, the two mechanisms close the only upstream flow the laws
allow (light rises; water and deposit fall). What emerges: the game's reward is the
medium, never a report — which is the Second Output gate's interface guard implemented
as physics. LAWFUL-NOW.

**S3 · The derived world (M5 + the containment) — routable behind one guard.**
If the rivers ride the great circle, then containment (cuboctahedron), surface (the
two-cupola net), seam (the closed circle), and watercourse become ONE derivation —
the world stops having drawn geography and starts having consequent geography.
What emerges: ephemeralization at the world scale; the drawing is deleted and the
solid does the work. NEEDS-KEVIN only if a specific diagonal must be fixed (the guard
in M5).

**S4 · The passage engine (M3 + S1 + S2) — named, held, NOT proposed.**
If the world ever changes phase, the jitterbug carrier is the only lawful skeleton
(twice verified), the tension circuit is what would flex, and the precessional return
is how a phase would be FELT rather than announced. What would emerge is the called
reading — the world is the passage — as conduct. Every element is tethered; the whole
is an unruled fork. NEEDS-KEVIN, and the counsel carries it silently until a build
tries to invent a state machine — at which point this card is the cheaper, already-
verified geometry to put on the table.
