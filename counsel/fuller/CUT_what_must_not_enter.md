# CUT — the Fuller material that must NOT cross into NESI

The CUT vertex, 2026-08-11. Fuller's seat refuses additions; this file is the
knife. Part one: Fuller material barred at the membrane, with the law that
bars it. Part two: the additions the other vertices are most likely to
propose, pre-refused here, each with its lighter substitute.

---

## Part one — barred material

**Domes as aesthetic.** The geodesic dome is Fuller's most recognizable
artifact and the least transferable one. NESI's geometry is already ruled:
two stacked tetrahedra (3D lineage), the two-cupola net unfolded flat (2D
ruling). A dome anywhere in NESI is decoration wearing structure's clothes —
it imports Fuller's brand, not his instrument. Barred by the 2D ruling and by
"the added complexity of a 3D world is the depletion move."

**Dymaxion-map cartography.** The Dymaxion map exists to show landmasses
without distortion. NESI is not a map and carries no landmasses — PROTOCOLS
says this in so many words when holding the net. Importing Dymaxion
projection logic, icosahedral re-nets, or "less distortion" arguments reopens
the 1954 abandonment against a standing mark. Barred by the net ruling.

**Geodesic ornament.** Triangulated struts, hex-pent panels, space-frame
texture on surfaces that have their own law (the triangle world, the glass
strip, the tetra net). Every face in NESI already means something; ornament
that quotes Fuller adds a second voice to surfaces that must carry only the
player's material. Barred by the refusals: nothing in the world may point at
anything as worth attention, and ornament points.

**The techno-utopian register.** "Spaceship Earth," "humanity's final exam,"
comprehensive-anticipatory anything as COPY. NESI's world speaks no text to
the player at all (refusal three); the teaching ruling permits museum
captions that name mechanics, never a worldview. A single line of visionary
register is the world editorializing. Barred by the silence law and the
teaching line: names the world, never advises.

**Any numeric efficiency readout.** Energy accounting, throughput ratios,
performance-per-pound — the entire Dymaxion habit of quantified performance.
Law 2 is absolute: no number reaches the player. This includes derived,
"harmless" forms: a percentage of tank used, a cycles counter, water-routed
totals, comparative flow between spires (law 2 also bars comparing the
spires, even as a stub). Fuller measured everything; NESI renders everything
and counts nothing to the player's eye. Barred by law 2 and the refusals'
first line.

**World-game telemetry.** Fuller's game ran on total world inventory — data
gathered from everywhere. NESI reaches nowhere (law 11): no telemetry, no
analytics, no accounts, no sync. Any "inventory of the world's resources"
instinct stops at the membrane. Local files only.

## Part two — additions the other vertices will likely propose, pre-refused

**1 · A leverage map / trim-tab dashboard** ("show which intervention moves
the world most"). Pre-refused: that is a ranking of acts, a numeric read one
step removed, and it defaults Kevin's forks by ordering them.
*Lighter substitute:* the world already shows consequence as terrain —
release at a dam and watch the ground. The downstream IS the readout; no
overlay needed.

**2 · A synergy view** ("the whole is unpredicted by the parts — render the
whole-system state on one screen"). Pre-refused: a system dashboard is a
summary, and the world may never summarize (refusal six); it also points at
the whole as worth attention.
*Lighter substitute:* the glass floor. The room already stands above the
world with both zones always visible. Standing there and looking down is the
synergy view, unlabeled.

**3 · Tensegrity structures in the world** (compression islands, tension
nets, as new furniture or a new station). Pre-refused twice over: the FULL
FREEZE bars new organs, and the sentence-lens already carries the structural
reading — sentences as load-bearing nodes with attachment points is Kevin's
own articulation, built.
*Lighter substitute:* read the EXISTING sentence-lens field through the
tensegrity instrument at counsel, on paper, and say what it rules. Reading
costs nothing; the freeze permits it.

**4 · Jitterbug animation** (the VE flexing through icosahedron to octahedron
as a transition, a loading motion, or the tetra opening). Pre-refused: the
fold-back is ruled a wordless triangle — the room keeps its shape; and the
order-12 subgroup finding is corpus geometry for READING, not a screensaver.
Animation of the doorway makes the doorway a spectacle.
*Lighter substitute:* the net opens and closes as already built. The flex
stays in the mathematics where it is verified.

**5 · Naming the seam axis or the spires to "complete the geometry."**
Pre-refused by two standing marks: the four diagonals stay interchangeable
(*"leave it unfixed"* — a decision, not a deferral), and the spires carry no
names (GROWN/GIVEN/WOVEN was a fork offered, never ruled; R2 closed spire-
mapping). A build needing a specific diagonal says so out loud and puts the
question to Kevin.
*Lighter substitute:* none needed — unfixed is the standing state, and it
costs nothing to keep.

**6 · An anticipatory-design pass** ("comprehensively anticipate what the
player will need next session and pre-stage it"). Pre-refused: that is the
re-engagement hook wearing Fuller's vocabulary. Held is lawful (law 7); the
world stops when you leave (law 8); nothing prompts a return.
*Lighter substitute:* nothing. The fallow field is the design working.

**7 · "More with less" refactors during the freeze** (consolidate stations,
merge stores, one elegant replacement for three plain things). Pre-refused as
generation: the freeze's lawful verbs are sift, sort, compost, deposit —
subtraction into the one form, never substitution by a new form. The
supersession of the 100-word clock shows the lawful shape: the clock was
REMOVED and nothing new was authored to stand where it stood.
*Lighter substitute:* delete, and record the deletion on the ledger
(RETIRED.jsonl exists for exactly this).
