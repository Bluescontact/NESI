## 1 · The two-cupola net — Fuller abandoned it in 1954; Kevin held it

**The collision.** Fuller dropped the cuboctahedron net *because* of it: mixed
square-and-triangle faces make seam alignment the weak point — edge distortion
at the six-edge seam, constrained unfolding options — and he moved to the
icosahedron. Kevin read that counter-evidence face-up and held the net anyway
(mark 2026-08-09, *"hold the two-cupola net"*). The ruling governs and is not
reopened here.

**What Fuller's objection still predicts will hurt** — so the build can watch
for it, not so the ruling moves:

- **The seam is where distortion concentrates.** Anything laid out ACROSS the
  six-edge seam (a station straddling it, a river crossing it, text flowing
  over it) inherits the alignment problem Fuller named. His objection predicts
  the seam will keep demanding special-case code — every "just this once"
  seam-handling patch is the 1954 failure arriving in installments.
- **Square-triangle adjacency has no uniform metric.** A square face and a
  triangular face meeting at an edge do not tile a shared grid; any mechanic
  that assumes one coordinate system across a square-triangle boundary will
  drift. Watch for off-by-slivers at those edges.
- **The unresolved cost is already on record**: the cupola split cuts BOTH
  inscribed tetrahedra transversely (1+3, not 4+0), so the net does not
  deliver the entry-tetrahedron whole. PROTOCOLS holds this as a live
  collision, unreconciled, no reconciliation inferred. Fuller's objection
  predicts this is not cosmetic — the seam cutting the entry-tetra is the
  same class of weakness he fled.

**Who governs:** Kevin's mark. The net stands.
**Falsifier to watch:** a session that quietly special-cases the seam a third
time, or a build that needs the entry-tetra whole and does not put the
question back to Kevin out loud.

## 2 · VE-as-doorway vs a game contained inside it

**The collision.** Fuller's instrument reads the vector equilibrium as the
condition nature never rests in — a doorway, not a place. NESI is declared
fully contained INSIDE a cuboctahedron: a world you occupy, inside the one
arrangement that cannot be occupied.

**The ruling.** *"THE WORLD IS THE PASSAGE"* (Kevin's mark 2026-08-09). The
collision reading is dropped; pattern 4 stands unamended; NESI being inside
exactly that arrangement is the point, not a defect.

**What the ruling FORBIDS a builder from doing:**

- Building any state of rest at the VE itself — no hub, home base, lobby,
  save-point, or "center room" the player settles into. The center is
  un-nameable and unoccupiable; the corpus already holds THE SLOT STAYS EMPTY.
- Rendering the cuboctahedron as a destination the player travels toward, or
  as an achievement of balance. It is the containing frame, unrendered
  (BUILD_RECORD g2 sites on this correctly).
- Treating equilibrium as a win-state: any mechanic where "balanced" is the
  goal converts the doorway into a place and breaks the passage ruling. The
  world is moved THROUGH; balance is transit, never arrival.

**Who governs:** Kevin's mark. **Falsifier:** any build where standing at the
most symmetric point is rewarded, marked, or made comfortable.

## 3 · Ephemeralization vs the freeze's no-additions

**The collision, examined:** these are ALIGNED at the surface — both refuse
weight — but they are not the same law, and the difference bites. Fuller's
ephemeralization is generative: do more with less, which licenses building the
lighter replacement. The freeze forbids new generation of any kind; only
sifting, sorting, composting, and the ONE deposit are lawful. A Fuller-minded
session could propose "replace these three heavy systems with one elegant
light one" — an ephemeralization move that is nonetheless NEW GENERATION and
therefore frozen.

**Who governs:** the freeze, ratified as written 2026-08-07, lifted only by
Kevin's own words. Ephemeralization inside NESI is lawful only as
SUBTRACTION and composting — removing, dissolving, depositing into the one
form (the supersession of the 100-word clock is the lawful shape: removed,
not replaced with a new organ).
**Falsifier:** a proposal that arrives wearing "more with less" as its
justification but whose diff adds a file, an organ, or a mechanism. The
justification does not exempt it.

## 4 · World-game's 100%-of-humanity vs a first-person single-player interior

**The collision.** Fuller's world-game makes the world work for all humanity
through spontaneous cooperation. NESI is one player, no network, no account,
no second player, no outward reach (law 11); the second user is explicitly
NOT upstream (first-user threshold ruling, 2026-08-02).

**Who governs:** the refusals and law 11. The single-person economy IS the
corpus's answer: infrastructure by Kevin for Kevin, and the world-game's
"without the disadvantage of anyone" is satisfied at n=1 without importing
its scale. Fuller's instrument survives only in its ethical floor — nothing
in NESI wins by someone losing — which the no-comparison laws already carry.
**Falsifier:** any feature justified by "when others play" or "sharing" —
that is the world-game's scale smuggling in against law 11. Also the softer
form: telemetry "to understand players," plural.

## 5 · Comprehensivity vs the vertical-slice law

**The collision.** Fuller: start with universe; specialization is a trap;
read every part against the largest whole. NESI's build law: one vertical
slice per session, eight files or fewer (advisory), never end a session
having produced only specs or architecture — and this repo already has far
more specification than game.

**Who governs:** the slice law. Comprehensivity is lawful as a READING
posture (the whole system first, at counsel) and unlawful as a BUILD posture
(the comprehensive plan document is exactly the output the slice law
forbids). Fuller's own instrument concedes this: "the OS becomes real by
becoming runnable," not by becoming complete.
**Falsifier:** a session that opens with the whole tetrahedral system map and
closes with a document instead of a walkable change. The corpus has measured
this failure at 316 offered, 1 answered.

## 6 · Trim-tab thinking sliding into optimization-thinking

**The collision.** The trim tab — the smallest surface with leverage — is one
comparison away from being an efficiency metric: "which move yields most per
unit effort" is a ranking, and ranking is what the anti-build laws exist to
prevent. The step from "find the one-gesture move" to "score the candidate
moves by leverage" is short and always feels like diligence.

**Who governs:** the refusals (no numeric read, ever) and the anti-run-ahead
laws. The trim tab is lawful as a SELECTION discipline — prefer the smallest
move, one at a time — and unlawful the moment leverage becomes a computed or
displayed quantity, a ranked list of interventions, or a reason to tell Kevin
which of his forks is "highest leverage" (that is defaulting his fork by
another name). The inbox's H/M/L capacity-x-ease tags on the gift cards are
the closest standing thing to this line; they order nothing and each card
waits for his mark — that is the boundary holding, and the thing to keep
watching.
**Falsifier:** any surface, in-game or in-counsel, that presents Kevin's
options with a leverage ordering attached as if the ordering were a fact
rather than a seat's read. Precession's interface guard is the same law from
the other side: never tell the actor what their act is really worth while
they decide.
