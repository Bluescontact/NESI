## 1 · The returning edge
**Instrument:** great-circle thinking — on a sphere (or a folded solid) every straight path comes home; nothing falls off a world.
**Tether:** THE_FLOOR_2D as quoted in the workshop's homework: *"The outer triangle is the fourth... the boundary that holds the other three and returns what reaches it"* (workshops/cowan_workshop.html, "The entry geometry — CLOSED").
**Change:** the outer triangle stops being a picture frame and becomes the fourth face doing its stated job: water, a wandering suspended disc, or a dragged stone that reaches the world's outer edge is *returned* — re-enters along the boundary, on the adjacent inner face, exactly as the fold-structure dictates. The net behaves as the solid it is. No new object, no new rule; an already-ruled sentence made physical.
**Smallest slice:** one fraction type (the wandering disc) wraps at one edge. Watch it cross.
**Status:** LAWFUL-NOW — it is the floor spec's own clause, currently unenacted.

## 2 · Rivers as geodesics of the fold
**Instrument:** the geodesic principle + dymaxion projection — the shortest path on the solid looks bent on the flat map, and the bend is the truth.
**Tether:** BUILD_RECORD g2: *"the flat projection of the solid's three inner faces, the outer triangle the fourth. One tetra containing the whole at every level"*; the workshop: *"'fall' must be reread as adjacency across faces and across the seam."*
**Change:** river paths are computed on the fold, not the flat — a river crossing a face boundary continues on the adjacent face at the fold's true angle. The player who watches long enough can *discover* the world is a solid, from the water's conduct alone, with nothing telling him. Behavior as the carrier (law 10) applied to geometry itself.
**Smallest slice:** re-path one river's polyline through one fold seam.
**Status:** LAWFUL-NOW.

## 3 · The octet ground
**Instrument:** the octet truss — alternating tetrahedra and octahedra, the strongest structure per unit of material; strength from packing, not from members.
**Tether:** BUILD_RECORD, THE SENTENCE-LENS: *"a sentence as an anchor holds up the vertex of an upward-facing tetra"* — every settled sentence already renders as an upward tetra profile with endpoint attachments.
**Change:** when two lenses settle adjacent, their nearest attachment points *share* — the profiles join at the vertex, and dense ground literally trusses: upward tetras alternating with the octahedral voids between them. Ground density becomes visible as structure instead of as shading — more with less, and no count anywhere. Third-order dream, tethered: this is only drawing what the lens articulation already implies.
**Smallest slice:** two adjacent lenses share one attachment point.
**Status:** LAWFUL-NOW (rendering only; touches no store).

## 4 · Tensegrity in the pool — lines instead of merges
**Instrument:** tensegrity — islands of compression, a sea of tension; if the parts touch everywhere it is a pile.
**Tether:** BUILD_RECORD T5: *"the line (double-click two stones on the table ties them; a hand-made relation)"* — built; and R4's merge, which fuses stones into one.
**Change:** the line becomes load-bearing at descent: stones tied by lines can be sent *as a constellation* — they descend together, settle apart, each verbatim, the relation kept as the spacing between their landings. A merge is compression (one body); a line is tension (many bodies, one structure). The player gains the choice Fuller's structures always offer: fuse, or hold apart under tension. Today only the fuse exists.
**Smallest slice:** two line-tied stones descend in one gesture and settle as two sediments whose lens attachments carry the line.
**Status:** NEEDS-KEVIN — descent semantics are downstream-order semantics (T6: "descent order IS the downstream order"); a constellation-send extends that ruling rather than reading it.
