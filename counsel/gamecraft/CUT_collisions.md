## C1 · Reward loops vs law 7 — held is lawful

**The collision.** The field's deepest-trained instinct: close every loop with a
return. Act → consequence → reward → appetite for the next act. Every good game
the field has shipped tightens this cycle; the craft literature calls it the
compulsion loop when honest and "engagement" when not. NESI's law 7 severs the
cycle's third beat on purpose: *"Unprocessed water, an unassigned spire, a dry
ground triangle, a fallow session — all correct states. Never nag, never prompt
a return, never build a re-engagement hook."* The hand that knows how to make a
player want to come back is forbidden from making the player want to come back.
That is not a small ask — it is the deletion of the field's primary tool.

**Who governs today.** Law 7, vendor `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` ("Laws that break the
build if forgotten"), reinforced by the refusals file:
*"Notifications, reminders, daily-login rewards, decay timers, urgency
mechanics, or any outward reach. The game never contacts the player."* —
`nesi/world3d/.claude/rules/refusals.md`. Enacted in the build: *"Hold is the
default and costs no click"* and *"a full tank, a held pool, an untouched field:
no nag anywhere"* — `nesi/game2d/BUILD_RECORD.md`, twelve-laws check, item 7.

**Falsifier to watch.** The observable cost would be abandonment-without-return
that Kevin himself names as a loss: the game sitting unopened for weeks and
Kevin saying, in his own words, that he wanted to be in it and the world gave
him no thread back. Not low session counts — nothing counts sessions — but his
own report that the silence read as the world not wanting him. If that report
ever comes, law 7 paid a real cost. Until it comes, the fallow field is the
design working.

## C2 · Progression systems vs law 2 — no number reaches the player

**The collision.** Progression is how the field makes time in a game feel
spent rather than lost: levels, meters, unlock trees, "you are further than you
were." The craft truth underneath is real — a player needs to FEEL the world
answer accumulated effort. Law 2 bars every instrument the field uses to say
it: *"No scores, counts, ranks, percentages, progress bars, streaks, or word
counts on screen. Nothing compares the three spires to each other, ever, not
even as a stub."* The hand's instinct to show the climb has nowhere legal to
put a rung.

**Who governs today.** Law 2, vendor `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`; refusals item 1:
*"Scoring, ranking, XP, levels, percentages, or any numeric read of the player.
Including 'helpful' ones: word streaks, depth stats, completion percent,
session counters."* — `nesi/world3d/.claude/rules/refusals.md`. The build's
substitute is already standing and is craft, not compromise: *"tank is a fill
height, pools are ellipse size, ground is density, light is brightness; no
digit, bar, or count renders"* — `nesi/game2d/BUILD_RECORD.md`, law check 2.
The room literally brightens as cycles accumulate (light = 1 − exp(−cycles/9)):
progression as physics, unnumbered.

**Falsifier to watch.** The cost shows if Kevin cannot tell, by looking, that
the world has changed since last time — if ground density and room light are
too subtle to carry the "I have been here, and it mattered" signal, and he says
the world feels the same no matter what he pours into it. That would be the
analog channel failing at the job the digital channel was barred from. The fix
would be louder physics, never a number — but the failure itself must be
watched for, because analog readouts saturate and numbers don't.

## C3 · Tutorialization vs the silence law — as amended by the teaching ruling

**The collision.** The field's law: the first minute is a lesson no one notices
(World 1-1). NESI's original silence law made even the lesson unlawful — and
the first playtest hit exactly the wall the field's whole tutorial tradition
exists to prevent. Kevin's own first-walk report: *"I dont understand anything
you've built or how it functions... i cant walk through it without the symbols
having labels."* The collision fired in the wild, on the only hand that
matters, and it was real.

**Who governs today.** Kevin's ruling of 2026-08-11, on the record: *"The game
can teach the user how to use it"* — `nesi/game2d/BUILD_RECORD.md`, "The
teaching" section, with the boundary drawn in the same breath: *"teaching names
the world and its mechanics; it never advises, never scores, never reads or
mentions what he wrote. A museum caption, not a coach."* This is a standing
ruling and is not reopened here. What remains COLLIDING is the line's exact
position: the field's craft says captions-on-hover is discovery-gated teaching
(you must already suspect a thing is interactive to learn it is), and World 1-1
teaches by ARRANGEMENT — the first goomba walks at you whether you hover or
not. Teaching-by-arrangement without teaching-by-pointing is craft the ruling
permits and the build has barely begun to use.

**Falsifier to watch.** A second walk-report of the same shape as the first —
Kevin, or the first user (who per the standing memory is NOT upstream), unable
to find an act the captions technically cover — would show the caption strip is
teaching's floor, not its ceiling. Conversely: if a caption ever drifts one
word past naming into advising ("try releasing the dam"), the museum has hired
a coach, and the refusals' own falsifier fires.

## C4 · Difficulty-as-punishment vs fail-as-physics

**The collision.** The field punishes to teach: fail states that cost progress,
sharpen attention, gate mastery. The roguelike school refined this to its
honest form — loss must be legible as fair — but the instrument is still
punishment. NESI's fail states refuse the frame entirely: the rim slops over
(RIM 1.0), the receiver scorches shut at 3.4 s of continuous beam, the membrane
tears past reach 150 and heals in its own 7 s. Nothing is taken FROM the
player; the material obeys its own physics and the consequence persists. There
is no death, no retry, no penalty — and the field's instinct whispers that
without stakes, care evaporates.

**Who governs today.** The built constants and their design, carried from the
world2d authority code: *"fail-state constants from the world2d code (RIM 1.0 ·
BURN_AT 3.4 s · REACH 150 · RELAX 7 s)"* and *"the lean persists... scorches
the receiver shut — persists, nothing reopens on its own"* —
`nesi/game2d/BUILD_RECORD.md`. The Cowan gate-check already recorded the
adjacent collision and Kevin's spec governed: *"the table gives no warning line
and no permitted-range marker; you read the lean"* — BUILD_RECORD, Cowan
gate-check, "RECORDED, NOT FIXED." That ruling stands; this file only holds
its craft-side twin open: no-warning + no-punishment together mean the ONLY
teacher of the rim is crossing it, and the only tuition is a persistent scorch.

**Falsifier to watch.** A scorched receiver or persistent lean that Kevin
experiences not as physics-obeyed but as the world being spiteful — his own
report that a fail state felt like punishment rather than consequence — would
show the fairness-legibility the roguelike school demands has not survived the
removal of warnings. Watch also the opposite failure: if fail states are so
consequence-light that the hand never learns the rim exists, the physics isn't
teaching either.

## C5 · Juice and feedback vs law 6 — set-it-down has no confirmation

**The collision.** Game feel doctrine: every input deserves an acknowledgment —
the screen-shake, the tick, the settle-sound. A player action that produces
NOTHING reads, in every playtest the field has ever run, as a broken button.
Law 6 builds exactly that on purpose: *"Set-it-down has no destination, no
animation, and no confirmation. The absence is the feedback. Do not add a
toast, a chime, or a counter."* The bare plate is a deliberately dead button,
and the field's entire feel literature says dead buttons get reported as bugs.

**Who governs today.** Law 6, vendor `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`, verbatim above;
enacted: *"the bare plate; the function does nothing else — no destination, no
animation, no confirmation, no counter"* — `nesi/game2d/BUILD_RECORD.md`. Note
the law is narrower than the instinct fears: it governs set-it-down
specifically. Elsewhere the build is rich in lawful feel — fractions sink,
hang, film, clump; the tray leans; water visibly rises. The silence is sited at
one act, and that siting is the design.

**Falsifier to watch.** Kevin, or the first user, pressing the bare plate
twice — the second press is the observable event. A repeated set-down of the
same object is the hand reporting "I don't believe the first one worked." One
occurrence is noise; a pattern of doubled set-downs is the absence failing to
read as feedback, and it is the cheapest possible telemetry-free falsifier
because it is visible in the act itself.

## C6 · Save-game conventions vs law 12 — quitting mid-sentence loses nothing, and nothing says so

**The collision.** The field's hardest-won trust lesson runs in two halves:
never lose the player's work, AND TELL THEM IT'S SAFE. The saved-icon, the
"progress saved" toast, the checkpoint chime — the second half exists because
players who don't know they're safe play defensively and quit anxiously. Law 12
adopts the first half as law and bans the second: *"Quitting mid-sentence loses
nothing and nothing on screen says so."* The build keeps the promise silently:
autosave at 2 s + blur + close, *"no saved indicator, no last-saved field."*

**Who governs today.** Law 12, vendor `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`; the game-craft
agent definition itself names it *"the field's hardest-won lesson, stated as
law"* — `.claude/agents/game-craft.md`. The Cowan gate-check already named the
real edge and it stands RECORDED: *"the risk that a cleared browser store loses
the world is real and is the cost of law 11... Named here so the cost is not
invisible to YOU, the operator of the build"* — `nesi/game2d/BUILD_RECORD.md`.
And the eight-edit build logged an actual breach in this territory (the scratch
door failing toward live) — proof the persistence seam is where this world's
real dangers live.

**Falsifier to watch.** Two distinct events. (1) Trust-side: Kevin re-typing
something he already wrote because he didn't believe it survived a quit — work
doubled by unannounced safety. (2) Substance-side: a browser store actually
cleared and a world actually lost, the recorded Cowan cost coming due. The
first would show the silence half of law 12 costing; the second would show the
local-only half of law 11 costing. Both are on the record as accepted; the
watch is for the day the acceptance is tested.

## C7 · Accessibility conventions vs law 10 — behaviour, not colour, and not only motion either

**The collision.** Law 10 IS the field's accessibility lesson, adopted as law:
*"Colour is never the only carrier of a distinction."* The build honors it —
*"diamond sits, disc hangs and wanders, ring is empty; shape and motion carry
the distinction."* The collision is one layer down, and it is the field's own
refinement of its own lesson: motion is also not a universally readable
channel. A distinction carried by "hangs and wanders" versus "sinks fast" is
invisible in a still glance and hard for anyone who reads motion poorly. The
field's mature answer is redundancy across THREE channels minimum. The build
has shape + motion + colour, which meets that bar — but the caption strip (the
teaching ruling's instrument) is the only text channel, and it is hover-gated.

**Who governs today.** Law 10, vendor `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`; build check 10 in
`nesi/game2d/BUILD_RECORD.md`. The teaching ruling (2026-08-11, same file)
governs what text may do: name, never advise. Nothing in this collision asks
to reopen either; the open question is whether naming-on-hover reaches a hand
that cannot read the motion channel, and that question belongs to a walk, not
to a ruling.

**Falsifier to watch.** A player (Kevin or the first user) misrouting a
fraction — bedload to a spire believing it suspended — because the behavioral
difference didn't land before the routing act. A misroute traceable to
channel-illegibility rather than to intent is the observable cost. The lawful
fix already exists inside the teaching ruling: captions may NAME the fractions'
behaviors. No new law needed; only the watch.

## C8 · Onboarding distance vs the blank field — the front door is empty on purpose

**The collision.** The field counts the front door in seconds and gestures:
distance from open to first meaningful consequence. NESI opens on a blank
writing field — *"opens in a writing field, nothing precedes"* is acceptance
criterion 1 — and the first consequence (a sentence banks as a stone, water
rises) now arrives at the first finished sentence, since the supersession
ruling killed the 100-word clock. That is genuinely fast — one sentence — but
the doorway is blank, and the field knows blank pages are the highest-friction
door there is. Every writing tool the field has studied loses most hands at
the empty field. NESI cannot prompt (law 7, refusals: nothing points at
anything as worth attention), so the door must be crossed unassisted, every
session, forever.

**Who governs today.** The supersession ruling, Kevin verbatim: *"the 100 words
increment is superseded by the intake and writing surfaces made in the nesi
integration session"* — `nesi/game2d/BUILD_RECORD.md`, which made the sentence
the arrival and shortened the door to one sentence. The refusals bar every
prompt-shaped assist. The floor-check finding is already held open on the
record: *"the floor-check finding (the blank field as unread clause) is NOT
solved here... stays open and is not smuggled closed"* — BUILD_RECORD,
provisional calls. This file adds only the craft-side reading: the blank field
is simultaneously the genre's worst door and this game's entire thesis, and
that is a collision to hold, not to fix.

**Falsifier to watch.** Sessions that open and close with nothing written —
the file opened, the field faced, the field left blank, repeatedly. Not
measurable by the system (nothing counts) but visible to Kevin in his own
record: the page he returns to unchanged. If the blank door stops him at the
threshold on days he came wanting in, the thesis is paying rent. The ghosted
world beneath the sheet (the 80% patch) is the one lawful lure already built:
the world visible through the writing is arrangement doing invitation's work.

## C9 · Playtest instrumentation vs law 11 — nothing reaches outward

**The collision.** The field's only truth is the playtest, and the field's
playtest apparatus is instrumentation: heatmaps, funnels, session recordings.
Law 11 bars all of it: *"No telemetry, no analytics, no sharing, no accounts,
no cloud sync. Local files only."* The build cannot know how it is played.
Every collision above ends in "watch for X" — and the only watching instrument
that exists is Kevin's own report, given in his own words, at his own pace.

**Who governs today.** Law 11, vendor `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`; build check 11
(*"no telemetry, no fetch, localStorage only"*). Also the standing memory law:
never declare Kevin's state — system facts plus his verbatim words only. The
playtest channel for this game is structurally identical to every other
channel in the DSS: Kevin's mark, or nothing.

**Falsifier to watch.** Every falsifier in this file depends on a report Kevin
is never prompted to give (law 7 bars the prompt). If collisions C1–C8 sit
unfalsifiable for months because the reporting channel is itself governed by
the silence, that is law 11's real cost surfacing: a design that can only be
playtested by the one hand it must never solicit. The observable event is a
defect discovered LATE that a single walk-report would have caught early —
the first-walk labels wall was exactly this shape, and it was caught only
because Kevin volunteered the report.
