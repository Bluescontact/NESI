## F. THE DREAM, ALONE — each stated as a gesture a hand does inside the first minute

**1 · The page is the map.** You write; the sentence completes and goes grey on your own page (already built). The dream: the grey line's *left margin* carries a two-pixel mark in the tone of the ground where its water actually landed. Nothing labels it. Weeks later you scroll your own page and the margin reads as a coastline. *You are reading terrain in the margin of your own handwriting.* *Feeds:* the world returning what it takes; witnessed change. *Grows from:* `world.html:433-452` plus `:1029-1046` — the wire exists in one direction.

**2 · The door is the day, and travel replaces the menu.** You open the file and you are on the page, writing. You press Escape and the sheet lays aside; the valley is under it and always was. You walk down the water to the dam, you climb the light to the seats. No map, no node, no level number, and **no screen in this game that is only a way to get to another screen.** *Recognizable:* from boot, zero clicks to the first keystroke, and Escape never shows a menu. *Feeds:* one continuous world with one camera; the catalog's *chambers joined by meaningful travel*. The answer to STOPs 1 and 8 that costs the least new invention — `world.html` already is one world with one camera, and `ascent.html`'s twelve mechanics are each under twenty lines.

**3 · The sounding pays out at the ground's own speed, and shows the seam.** Thin ground answers almost at once; ground fed across many returns makes your hand wait as the line pays out. When it lands, ground that was merged comes back as **two sentences shown as two, in written order, verbatim** — the seam the store already keeps drawn as the gap between them. *Feeds:* depth read as patience rather than as a value; the Overwintering made legible without a date, a count, or a word.

**4 · The cast carries terrain, not counts.** Open the emitted file: the silt array, the flood-mark levels, the seat bearings — the shape of a valley. You cannot tell how many times anyone did anything, how many days they came, or whether they set anything down. A valley never shaped exports a flat line. Two unrelated writings that shaped the same ground export identical bytes. *Feeds:* `NESI_VISION_as_it_stands.md:30` as written, and law 2 at the one place the build breaks it. **This dream deletes rather than adds.**

**5 · The paper you write on is the progress report.** Tomorrow the sheet is the same sheet; the sky behind it is not. Light the valley has earned raises the ambient floor permanently and the paper takes the tone of ground that has been fed. No bar, no dot, no count, no comparison — **you read what you built in the colour of the page you write on.** A month in the room is warm and nothing ever said so.

**6 · The hand can never aim at a socket, anywhere.** There is nothing you can click to set. You set the gate, the bearing, the pull, the place you let go — and the physics finds every consequence. *Feeds:* law 5, law 2 and the seating law turn out to be one law. **And the shape it must take:** not a check. **A parameter that does not exist** — earned state reachable only through a function that takes no index, the way `own()` takes no string.

- **Dream 4** strains nothing and removes something — the cheapest correction here, closing a live law-2 leak. **But it touches STOP 5.** It can be done as *subtraction only* (stop exporting the counts) without ruling whether a set-down leaves a record; two different acts, only the first is this seat's to describe.
