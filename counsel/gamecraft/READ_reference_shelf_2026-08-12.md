**Shelf source:** `sacreddollarsknowledge.html:177-185`. Stripped of every word of its content: **every surface has a back, and the back is reached by a motion of the hand, never by a mode change and never by a message.**

Consequence: Esc again brings the same surface back with everything exactly where it was; nothing announces either state; the motion is the whole feedback.
**Smallest playable slice:** extend the built Esc grammar to **one** station panel — the dam, since it carries the only governed act — so its close is a lay-aside rather than a dismissal. One class toggle, one transition, no store change.
**Thirteen-law check:** law 6 untouched · law 2 clean (a motion, no readout) · law 7 clean · law 12 / BUILD LAW 13 clean · law 10 clean.

Generalizing the built keystroke is the only face-layer move on this shelf.
**TAG: LAWFUL-NOW** for the mechanic; the *scope* question is F1.

## 6 · FORKS OPENED

**F1 · Does the lay-aside grammar become the world's one reveal verb, or stay the writing sheet's own?**
*Branch A — one verb everywhere.* Gain: the face-layer answer — every door opens the same way; a stranger learns one motion instead of nine dismissals. Cost: each room then has two ways out, and BUILD LAW 13's *lossless close* contract was written for one.
*Branch B — Esc stays the sheet's verb.* Gain: the panels' own close controls stay the single exit per room. Cost: the world keeps nine different ways of getting out of things — precisely the shape of "the face is missing."
**TAG: design/mechanic — rulable by session under MANDATE.md.** Note the adjacency: the *salience* half is already at Kevin's gate and this fork must not be used to answer it sideways.
