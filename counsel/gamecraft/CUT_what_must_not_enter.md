# GAME-CRAFT LIBRARY — CUT: what must not enter

Written 2026-08-11 by the game-craft agent (THE PLAYER'S HAND), CUT vertex of
the counsel swarm. Companion to `CUT_collisions.md`. Precedent:
`counsel/fuller/CUT_what_must_not_enter.md` — barred material named, and where
one exists, the lighter substitute already lawful in this world.

This file is the field's memory of every way games have failed players,
pre-refused BY NAME so no future session smuggles one in under a craft word.
The refusals are the product, not constraints to negotiate
(`nesi/world3d/.claude/rules/refusals.md` is the source; the game-craft agent
definition carries the same line as its own hard limit). Nothing here is a
work queue; it is a standing list of doors that stay shut.

The falsifier over the whole file, from the refusals source:
*"If a player finishes a session comforted by the system rather than by their
own material, this list has failed."*

## Part I — the field's dark patterns, refused by name

### 1 · Grind
Repetition demanded before the world answers. Volume-gated anything.
**Why the hand refuses it:** grind converts the player's time into the
designer's retention metric; it teaches the hand that acts are currency. In
NESI it would convert Kevin's WRITING into currency — the worst possible
version, because the material being ground is his own words.
**Lighter substitute, already lawful:** one sentence is an arrival (the
supersession ruling, `nesi/game2d/BUILD_RECORD.md`). The world answers the
smallest possible act. There is nothing to grind because there is no quantity
gate anywhere.

### 2 · Streaks
Consecutive-day counters; anything that makes a missed day a broken thing.
**Why the hand refuses it:** a streak manufactures loss out of absence. Law 7
rules the opposite: *"a fallow session — all correct states."* The refusals
name streaks explicitly: *"Including 'helpful' ones: word streaks."*
**Lighter substitute:** the room's light. Cycles accumulate as brightness
(1 − exp(−cycles/9)) and NEVER decay — absence subtracts nothing, so return
is always to a world at least as warm as it was left.

### 3 · FOMO and limited-time anything
Expiring content, seasonal windows, decay timers.
**Why the hand refuses it:** FOMO is urgency manufactured from scarcity the
designer invented. Refusals: *"decay timers, urgency mechanics."* Law 8: the
world stops when you leave — nothing can expire in a stopped world.
**Lighter substitute:** the membrane's 7-second heal is the only clock in the
game, it runs only while you are present, and it runs TOWARD you (healing),
never against you. Time in NESI only ever repairs.

### 4 · Daily-login shapes
Login rewards, daily quests, "welcome back" bonuses — any structure that pays
attendance.
**Why the hand refuses it:** paying attendance makes presence transactional,
and this is the one world built explicitly as non-transactional (the standing
frame: NESI = non-transactional game). Refusals: *"daily-login rewards...
The game never contacts the player."*
**Lighter substitute:** nothing. The absence is the design. The world's
greeting is that everything is exactly where it was left (law 8, verified:
*"quit and reopen, everything where it was"*).

### 5 · Juice-as-reward-cue
Celebration effects wired to compliance: the ding on task-complete, confetti
on submission, escalating fanfare for volume.
**Why the hand refuses it:** juice aimed at the act's COMPLETION rather than
the act's PHYSICS trains the hand toward the cue instead of the material.
Law 6 is the extreme case, stated as law: the absence is the feedback.
**Lighter substitute:** juice as physics, which the build is full of and which
is lawful: fractions sink fast / hang / film / clump, the tray leans, the
water rises as you type (T3), the beam scorches. Feel that reports what the
material is doing is craft; feel that congratulates is a leash.

### 6 · Variable-ratio reward schedules
Loot-box logic; randomized payout on a fixed act; any slot-machine curve.
**Why the hand refuses it:** the single most addictive structure the field
ever built, and the most studied. It works by decoupling act from consequence
so the brain chases the schedule. Every NESI law couples act to consequence
deterministically: the same lean slops at the same rim, the same beam scorches
at the same 3.4 s. The world is honest physics precisely so nothing can be a
slot machine.
**Lighter substitute:** the sounding. Click ground, one settled sentence
returns VERBATIM — surprise supplied entirely by the player's own forgotten
material, never by a random table. The delight of rediscovery without the
schedule: the payout is his own words, and the "randomness" is his memory.

### 7 · Completionism meters
Percent-explored, collection grids with empty slots, checklists of the
world's content.
**Why the hand refuses it:** a completion meter converts a world into a chore
list and an absence into a nag. Law 2 bars the number; the refusals bar the
pointer (*"Nothing in the world may point at anything as worth attention"*).
FK4 rules the deep version: *"gaps only — the form stays discovered"* — the
six unbuilt edges stay UNDRAWN until earned. The world never renders its own
empty slots as demands.
**Lighter substitute:** terra incognita as built — an unnamed region owns its
absence as a small dashed empty box, and a region gets ONE naming line only
after something lands there (R5). Absence rendered as room, not as debt.

### 8 · Social comparison
Leaderboards, shared worlds, friend activity feeds, "N players also..."
**Why the hand refuses it:** comparison is scoring outsourced to peers. Law 2's
deepest clause already bars comparison INSIDE the world — *"Nothing compares
the three spires to each other, ever, not even as a stub"* — and law 11 bars
the channel comparison would need. There is no second player by design
(*"no second player, no network, no account"* — the vendor brief's first
paragraph), and the standing memory holds that even the first OTHER user is
not upstream.
**Lighter substitute:** nothing, and lawfully nothing. The three spires
staying incomparable is the substitute: difference without ranking.

### 9 · Retention hooks
Cliffhangers at quit, "are you sure?" exit friction, one-more-turn bait,
notifications after close.
**Why the hand refuses it:** law 7 (never prompt a return) and law 8 (the
world stops when you leave) close both ends. An exit that resists is a wall
disguised as a door — the same failure class as B1's typing-toll, applied to
leaving a world instead of a widget.
**Lighter substitute:** law 12's silent safety. The exit costs nothing, loses
nothing, and says nothing. The lightest possible door in both directions IS
the retention design: a world cheap to leave is cheap to re-enter.

### 10 · Difficulty-as-paywall analogues
The free-to-play shape with the money removed: friction installed so its
relief can be sold — here, sold for attention, volume, or compliance instead
of cash. Grind walls relieved by writing more; fail states relieved by
returning sooner; capacity gates relieved by session length.
**Why the hand refuses it:** the shape is extortion regardless of the
currency. Refusals: *"Achievements, unlocks gated on volume, or difficulty
curves."* The tank's own capacity was explicitly ruled OUT of this shape:
*"a rendering ceiling, not a banking limit. Nothing is ever swallowed"*
(supersession, BUILD_RECORD).
**Lighter substitute:** fail-as-physics with self-relief on the world's own
time: the membrane heals in 7 s whether or not you write another word. Nothing
in the world withholds relief pending payment in any currency.

### 11 · Onboarding funnels and forced tutorials
Locked features until lessons complete; the guided first session; tooltips
that fire unbidden.
**Why the hand refuses it:** a forced tutorial is the world grabbing the
player's hand — the inverse of law 5 (the operator's hand runs the filter).
The teaching ruling (Kevin, 2026-08-11) draws the exact line: *"A museum
caption, not a coach."* Captions name on the hand's own approach (hover);
nothing fires unbidden, nothing locks pending comprehension.
**Lighter substitute:** already built and governing — the caption strip, the
panels that name themselves, teaching-by-arrangement (the world ghosting
through the writing sheet at 80%, discoverable without being told).

### 12 · Sentiment mirrors and material-reading "features"
Mood tracking, theme detection, "your writing suggests...", word clouds,
AI reflections on the player's text.
**Why the hand refuses it:** the field's newest dark pattern and this world's
oldest refusal. Refusals: *"Any system that summarizes, interprets, scores,
validates, reassures, or advises on the player's own written material."*
Law 4: verbatim only. The morning-pages guard states the general law: the
pages feed the system; the system never reads the writer. Violating this is
named a build failure in the same class as adding an approve button to the
GATE.
**Lighter substitute:** the sounding and the sediment — the world hands his
words back CHARACTER-FOR-CHARACTER, arranged in space, never interpreted.
Arrangement that lets a shape become visible, as long as nothing labels the
shape (refusals, allowed list).

## Part II — additions the other counsel seats may plausibly propose, pre-refused

The other three libraries (fuller · cowan · kevin-lens) read this world through
their own instruments. Each instrument has a failure mode that lands, from the
hand's side, as one of the patterns above wearing a better coat. Named here in
advance, with the lawful substitute, so the counsel can move fast past them.
These are pre-refusals of PATTERNS, not of the seats — each seat's standing
work is cited with respect, and nothing here reopens any of it.

### F1 · From the Fuller seat: efficiency readouts and the trim-tab metric
The Fuller library already pre-refused *"any numeric efficiency readout"* on
its own side (`counsel/fuller/CUT_what_must_not_enter.md` per its INDEX), and
named the live risk *"trim-tab decaying into optimization."* The hand seconds
it from the play side: a leverage point that renders its own leverage becomes
a high-score. If the dam's one governed release ever displays throughput,
ratio, or yield, law 2 is breached through the geometry door.
**Substitute, already lawful:** the release's consequence rendered as world —
ground density, lake size, lens water, room light. The trim tab's power stays
felt in the hand, never read on a dial.

### F2 · From the Fuller seat: the world-game's dashboard instinct
Fuller's world-game ran on total information display. Scaled to n=1 (a
collision the Fuller library itself holds open), the instinct becomes a
status-overview surface — the world summarized on one screen.
**Why the hand refuses it:** a world-state dashboard is a completion meter
with better typography. The world IS the display; law 9 even keeps one whole
region (the deep) permanently off it.
**Substitute:** the glass floor. The room already looks down on the world —
overview as a PLACE you stand, reading ambient state (the allowed list:
water level, deposit density, a dry channel), never as a panel that
summarizes.

### F3 · From the Cowan seat: cost-visible-at-choice as warning UI
The Cowan floor rule (cost visible at the moment of choice) already collided
with the water-table's no-warning-line law, and the spec governed — RECORDED,
NOT FIXED, on `nesi/game2d/BUILD_RECORD.md`. The plausible next proposal is
the same rule arriving at another station: a tear-proximity indicator on the
membrane, a heat gauge on the heliostat.
**Why the hand refuses it:** a warning line is a guard rail, and guard rails
are the field's way of not trusting physics to teach. The dashed sheet before
tear and the visible beam before scorch are precursors the world already
shows AS BEHAVIOUR. Adding a marker converts read-the-material into
read-the-UI.
**Substitute, already lawful:** louder precursors in the material itself —
more visible strain in the sheet, the receiver visibly heating — physics
amplified, never annotated. (And the standing ruling stands: where the spec
refuses even the precursor, the spec governs and the collision stays on the
record.)

### F4 · From the Cowan seat: nutrient-cycle readouts and ecological accounting
An ecological-design lens wants flows visible: what entered, what settled,
what the system metabolized. As a design method, lawful and valuable. As an
IN-WORLD surface, it is law 2 breached by mass balance — an accounting of
Kevin's words.
**Why the hand refuses it:** any ledger of the material is a numeric read of
the player one derivation away.
**Substitute:** the world's own body as the only ledger — ground that
thickened, a lake that widened, lenses that filled. The accounting exists;
it is written in terrain and read by walking, exactly as the sounding reads
the archive by dropping a line.

### F5 · From the kevin-lens seat: the floor indicator entering the game
The DSS widget law puts a derived floor line ("N marks · M gates open") on
every decision surface — correct THERE, where Kevin must never guess whether
a mark took. Carried into NESI it becomes a count on screen: held stones
enumerated, open acts listed.
**Why the hand refuses it:** the game is not a decision surface, and law 2
does not have a jurisdiction seam the widget law can cross. The two chassis
laws serve different rooms; FK2's own wording already made the translation —
*"all held stones visible — depth-zero governs"* — VISIBLE, as objects,
never a count (*"the held stones lie VISIBLE through the glass at each dam
as objects (never a count)"*, eight-edit build).
**Substitute:** exactly that standing ruling. Depth-zero enters the game as
everything-showable-by-looking; the number stays on the DSS side of the
membrane.

### F6 · From the kevin-lens seat: the mark-ledger instinct — logging play
The DSS runs on durable ledgers (MARKS_LOG, DECISIONS_OFFERED, gates). The
instinct, applied here, proposes session logs, play journals, an in-game
record of acts taken.
**Why the hand refuses it:** a play log is telemetry stored locally — law 11's
letter kept, its spirit broken — and one derivation from a session counter.
The world's memory must be the world.
**Substitute:** stones as terrain (eight-edit build: *"the set-down position
IS the record, persisted"*). Where a thing lies is when-and-that it happened.
The ledger is the ground.

### F7 · From any seat: the "gentle" versions
The final door, and the one the field guards hardest, because everything in
Part I re-arrives wearing care: the streak that only celebrates and never
shames · the soft prompt that "just wonders" if you want to return · the
single unobtrusive stat "for your own insight" · the optional weekly
reflection on your writing. The field's record is unambiguous: every dark
pattern shipped first as a kindness, and the refusals' falsifier names the
mechanism — comfort supplied by the SYSTEM displaces comfort found in the
MATERIAL.
**Why the hand refuses it:** gentleness changes the pattern's tone, not its
structure. A re-engagement hook that is polite is a re-engagement hook.
**Substitute:** the world's actual gentleness, which is structural and
already built — nothing is ever lost (law 12), nothing ever decays (law 8),
everything waits exactly where the hand left it, and the light only ever
warms. A world incapable of disappointment does not need to be nice.
