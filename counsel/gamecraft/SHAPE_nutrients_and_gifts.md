## THE NUTRIENTS — shelf grammar: a name · the relation · what it composes with

A nutrient is a small named relation discovered by doing, which composes with others.
Each of these is craft the whole field already knows, found ALREADY OPERATING in the
built game (nesi/game2d/nesi.html, per BUILD_RECORD.md) — named, never added.

**1 · The first screen is a lesson no one notices.**
Boot opens the tetra on SEQUENTIAL — a writing field, nothing precedes it — and the
first period banks a stone live at the keystroke. World 1-1 without a single word of
tutorial: the core verb (write) is taught by being the only thing there is to do, and
the first consequence (the tank rises) arrives inside the first sentence. Feeds:
onboarding measured in seconds and gestures, not features. Composes-with: the
caption strip (nutrient 2) · the ghosted sheet (nutrient 8) · law 2 (the rise is
height, never a number). Tether: BUILD_RECORD supersession + writing-tetra, "the
program still opens in a writing field, nothing precedes it."

**2 · The caption is a museum placard, not a coach.**
The teaching ruling ("the game can teach the user how to use it") landed as the one
form the refusals permit: a strip that names what the cursor rests on — the thing,
its act, its fail state — and never advises, never points, never mentions his
material. This is the field's hardest teaching discipline (Ico/INSIDE school)
executed exactly. Feeds: discoverability without prompts; the silence law kept whole
while the world becomes walkable. Composes-with: nutrient 1 · the fail-state
legibility (nutrient 3) · the refusal on tooltips-that-advise. Tether: BUILD_RECORD
teaching section, "A museum caption, not a coach."

**3 · Every fail state is the physics obeyed, and every one persists.**
Slop past the rim → the deep; 3.4 s of beam → scorch, receiver shut, nothing reopens
on its own; pull past reach → tear, heals on wall clock in its own time. No guard
rail, no punishment screen, no retry prompt — the roguelike law (loss must be legible
as fair) met by making loss BE the material's behavior. Feeds: difficulty as honesty;
trust that the world has real edges. Composes-with: nutrient 4 (hang) · the scorch
legible from outside the room (eight-edit item 3) · law 10. Tether: BUILD_RECORD
constants RIM 1.0 · BURN_AT 3.4 · REACH 150 · RELAX 7 s, carried from world2d
authority code.

**4 · The hang is the tutorial.**
An unworked stone hangs at the writer's node and will not carry past it. No text
refuses him; the weight does. This is game-feel doing law's work — the hand learns
worked-ness the way it learns a heavy door, and unworked-forever stays lawful with
nothing prompting. Feeds: teaching-through-feel; the T4 gate as body-knowledge.
Composes-with: nutrient 3 · worked-is-an-act-of-the-hand (T4) · law 7 · law 10.
Tether: BUILD_RECORD T4, "an unworked stone hangs at the node... nothing prompts."

**5 · Hold is the game's negative-space verb.**
Water arriving at a dam simply stays; holding costs no click; shutting the panel is
not an action; the bare plate does nothing else. The strongest verb in the build is
the one with no input at all — restraint as a mechanic, the Journey/INSIDE school at
its extreme. Feeds: pacing owned by the player; the anti-grind. Composes-with: the
dam release (the trim tab, Fuller shelf) · set-it-down (law 6) · held-is-lawful
(law 7). Tether: BUILD_RECORD, "Hold is the default and costs no click."

**6 · The drag is the decision.**
Descent (T6) is one gesture — drag a worked stone down through the writer's own
position and release; no confirmation, no undo dialog. Input equals commitment, the
cleanest grammar the field knows: the hand that let go is the record. Feeds: every
routing in the build (fractions to spire mouths, stones to the plate, the merge in
the pool). Composes-with: nutrient 9 (order) · law 5 (the hand runs the filter) ·
the merge-in-written-order (R4). Tether: BUILD_RECORD T6, "the drag is the decision."

**7 · Persistence is the promise kept without saying so.**
State lands the moment the hand lets go; quit mid-sentence loses nothing; nothing on
screen says saved. The save system as trust (law 12, the field's hardest-won lesson)
— and the mid-station charge-loss defect was found and FIXED, so the promise is
verified, not asserted. Feeds: writing without vigilance; the fallow session as a
safe state. Composes-with: law 8 (stops when you leave) · the watermark (deletion
never un-banks) · law 7. Tether: BUILD_RECORD law-12 check and Cowan gate-check fix.

**8 · The ghosted sheet is diegetic wayfinding.**
After Kevin's first walk, the sheet dropped to 80% — the triangle, dams, and ground
became discoverable BENEATH the writing, without being told. Wayfinding carried by
layering, not by a map or a waypoint: the world seen through the work is the
invitation to walk it. Feeds: load-is-navigation; the front door's second room
visible from the first. Composes-with: nutrient 1 · nutrient 2 · the room band
always visible. Tether: BUILD_RECORD patch, "the sheet now ghosts... discoverable
without being told."

**9 · The machine picks nothing, and that is the depth.**
Descent order IS the downstream order — the machine no longer picks even by arrival;
merge joins in WRITTEN order regardless of pick order; the region name opens once for
his hand. The Zachtronics law fully honored: the physics is built, the solution space
is the player's, and there is no optimum to screenshot because nothing is measured.
Feeds: systemic depth from ordering alone — sequencing stones is a real skill with
no score watching it. Composes-with: nutrient 6 · law 5 · R4 · the no-comparison law.
Tether: BUILD_RECORD T6, "the machine no longer picks even by arrival."

### GIFT GC-1 · The unwalked drag — the build's own named feel-risk

**What it is** — The build record's own confession that the one thing built most
directly for the hand has never met one: the descent drag was verified by emulating
its release condition, and the four station acts ran through functions, not fingers.

**Where it came from**
> "the net's drag-feel — the hang of an unworked stone in the hand — is unwalked,
> and it is the part built most directly for the hand."
— BUILD_RECORD, the writing-tetra edge note, 2026-08-11

**Latent capacity** — Playtesting is the only truth; no design survives contact with
a hand. The field's whole craft of drag-feel — pickup threshold, hang weight, release
tolerance, hit-target size — is waiting at exactly the gestures the record names:
the descent drag · the fraction chips at the stations · the pool merge · the two-stone
line. Ranked by consequence: descent first (it carries the whole order-choosing
skill of nutrient 9), fraction routing second (it carries the fail states).

**Shortest routing** — Way in: Kevin opens the file and drags. Act: his hands, not a
harness. Consequence: the feel-risks stop being a list and become one session's
report in his own words. This gift is a RANKING of where to stand when he walks,
nothing more; the walk is his.

**Reading** — capacity H · routing effort L (it is one play session) · confidence H
that the risk is real — the record itself ranked it.

### GIFT GC-2 · The scar as landmark — persistent fail states are the world's memory

**What it is** — The built fact that scorch, tear, and lean PERSIST (law 8: as they
were), and that a scorched receiver is legible shut from outside the room, on the
station's own face in the band.

**Where it came from**
> "a scorched receiver is legible shut from outside the room, on the station's own
> face in the band"
— BUILD_RECORD, the eight-edit build, item 3

**Latent capacity** — The field knows persistent scars as the strongest wordless
narrative device there is (the roguelike death-site, the Shadow of the Colossus
body): a player returning after days reads their own history off the world's face
with no log, no journal, no timestamp. The build already does this; the craft
finding is that it is a LOAD-BEARING feature to defend — any future "cleanup,"
"reset," or "repair" convenience would delete the world's only memory of the hand.
A defense card, not a build.

**Shortest routing** — None; it is standing. The routing is the counsel carrying it
against subtraction-of-the-wrong-thing.

**Reading** — capacity M · routing effort none · confidence H — the mechanism is
built and verified; the reading adds nothing.

### GIFT GC-3 · The front-door count — seconds and gestures from open to consequence

**What it is** — A craft audit instrument, applied once, held here: the distance
from opening the file to the first meaningful consequence, counted in the field's
units. As built: open → the field is there (0 gestures) → type one sentence → stone
banks at the period (first consequence, ~1 sentence of time) → tank visibly rises.
First ROUTED consequence (ground gained) requires: open tetra face or find station →
drag fractions → dam release — roughly four learned gestures deep.

**Where it came from**
> "Onboarding is the first player's whole experience... Count it in seconds and
> gestures, not features."
— the game-craft lens itself, read against BUILD_RECORD's verified walk order

**Latent capacity** — The count is the honest instrument for the one wall Kevin
actually hit ("I dont understand anything you've built"). The caption strip answered
naming; this card holds the SEQUENCING question — whether the four-gesture distance
to first ground is the intended slow arrival (the restraint school says maybe yes)
or a front-door length worth reading. No number ever reaches the player; the count
is the author's, exactly like the Second Output gate.

**Shortest routing** — Way in: Kevin's next walk. Act: nothing — the counsel just
knows where the door's length is when he reports how it felt. Consequence: his
felt-read lands on a mapped door instead of a vague one.

**Reading** — capacity M · routing effort none (it is already counted above) ·
confidence M — whether door-length is a problem or the point is his call, uncalled.

### GIFT GC-4 · Run-spacing as the one rhythm the world already keeps

**What it is** — The built fact that runs (Enter on an empty line closes one) are
invisible everywhere except the dam panel, where held stones group by sitting as
spacing — no label, no number.

**Where it came from**
> "Runs are invisible everywhere except the dam panel, where held stones group by
> sitting as spacing — no label, no number."
— BUILD_RECORD, the supersession, 2026-08-11

**Latent capacity** — Spacing is the field's oldest wordless rhythm instrument
(the measure, the beat-map, the breath in a text). The dam panel already renders the
player's own sittings as visible phrasing — which means the merge gesture (R4) can
already be performed WITH the phrasing in view: joining within a sitting versus
across sittings is a real compositional distinction the build supports today and
nothing announces. A found technique, not a feature — the kind of depth that exists
only because two built things share a surface.

**Shortest routing** — None to build. The routing is naming it to the counsel so no
future refit of the dam panel flattens the spacing as "just layout."

**Reading** — capacity M · routing effort none · confidence H that the spacing
renders as described; M that the technique matters — that is the hand's to find.
