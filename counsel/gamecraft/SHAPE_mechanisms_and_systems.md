## MECHANISMS

### M1 · The sentence as arrival — LAWFUL-NOW (built, verified)

**Loop:** type → the punctuation cluster completes → the stone banks live at the
keystroke → the tank rises → keep writing (or Esc, or leave — all lawful exits).

**Gesture:** the period. The smallest input in the whole game is its core verb's
commitment point — the hand feels the sentence END and the world answer, in the same
keystroke, with a rise it can see and no sound, number, or toast. Minute-one test:
passed by construction; this IS minute one.

**Fail state as skill:** there is none, and that is the design — deletion never
un-banks, the watermark moves past the page, and learning that the water already
left teaches the one real skill here: the sentence is spent when it ends, so the
ending is where the writing happens. The cost lives at a named boundary
(BUILD_RECORD: "the one place a cost could hide... the world shows it").

**Refusals-check:** tested against no-counting (nothing counts words — the clock is
dead by supersession), never-rewrite (the stone is verbatim), never-prompt (nothing
asks for a sentence). Passes: the arrival is the player's own punctuation, read by
the band's cut, character-for-character from the D5 regex.

### M2 · The hang-gate — worked-ness as weight — LAWFUL-NOW (built) · feel unwalked

**Loop:** a stone banks unworked → the hand works it (edit · merge · line · break) →
it will now carry past the writer's node → drag it down through the writer's own
position → release → it enters the descended water.

**Gesture:** picking up a stone that will not go. The hand pulls; the stone hangs at
the node; nothing says why. Then the hand edits one word, picks it up again, and it
carries. The difference between those two drags is the entire lesson, delivered as
weight — the door that was heavy is now light, and the hand knows what its work did.

**Fail state as skill:** the "fail" is the hang itself, and it is the system's own
physics: an unworked stone is not refused by text, it is unsupported by structure.
Unworked forever is lawful; the skill is discovering worked-ness by hand, never by
being told. (The hang's actual FEEL is the build's own ranked unwalked risk —
GIFT GC-1 in the sibling file.)

**Refusals-check:** tested against no-score (worked is a fact of acts, never a
quality judgment — T4: "never time, never a glance"), no-prompt (nothing nags an
unworked stone), colour-never-sole-carrier (the gate is behaviour, law 10). Passes;
this mechanism is the refusals' own showcase.

### M3 · The lean, the beam, the stretch — tension read by deformation — LAWFUL-NOW (built)

**Loop:** open a station → the hand loads the material (lean the tray / turn the
mirror / pull the sheet) → the material deforms toward its limit → route before the
limit, or cross it → past the rim/3.4 s/reach, the world takes it (the deep, the
scorch, the tear) → the consequence persists; the hand leaves knowing its range.

**Gesture:** holding something at the edge of too far. The tray lean, the beam's
dwell, the sheet's stretch are all the same felt question — how much further? —
answered by the material's behaviour alone, with no warning line (the spec's own
refusal, recorded in the Cowan gate-check collision).

**Fail state as skill:** slop, scorch, and tear are the tension member let go too
far, and each persists exactly as law 8 leaves it. The skill is calibration — the
roguelike honesty law met in miniature: loss is always legible as the physics
obeyed, and the scar (M2's cousin: the shut receiver readable from outside) is the
record the hand learns from.

**Refusals-check:** tested against no-warning-markers (holds — deformation signals
load, labels do not), no-punishment (the fail is material, not moral), held-is-lawful
(an empty tray is a state you can stand in — patched after Kevin's first walk).
Passes. Constants RIM 1.0 · BURN_AT 3.4 · REACH 150 · RELAX 7 s carried from the
world2d authority code, not invented here.

### M4 · The release and the plate — the two silences — LAWFUL-NOW (built)

**Loop:** water arrives at a dam → it stays (no input, no cost) → EITHER the hand
clicks release → ground, lake, lens, light answer downstream → OR the hand drops a
stone on the bare plate → nothing else happens, ever → either way, out is one step.

**Gesture:** the click that opens, and the drop that ends. Release is the build's
one governed act and the hand feels its leverage (the whole downstream world moves);
the plate is its exact opposite — a drop with no destination, no animation, no
confirmation, where the absence IS the feedback. The pair teaches the deepest thing
in the game: not-acting is an act, and it costs nothing.

**Fail state as skill:** none exists, by law 7 — hold is lawful forever. The skill
is timing without feedback pressure: learning when a release is wanted with no
meter, no fullness warning, no nudge. The plate's skill is subtler: trusting that
set-down really is nowhere (the field's players are trained to suspect hidden
inventories; this world keeps its word).

**Refusals-check:** tested against law 6 (no destination/animation/confirmation —
the plate does nothing else, verified), law 7 (no nag anywhere), law 1 (three
outputs always on screen), FK2's ruling (all held stones visible, depth-zero
governs). Passes as built.

### M5 · The sounding — memory touched, never served — LAWFUL-NOW (built)

**Loop:** click ground → a line drops → one settled sentence shows, verbatim → the
line withdraws → the ground is unchanged.

**Gesture:** reaching into what settled. Player-initiated only; a sounding that
finds nothing shows nothing. The hand feels the difference between a world that
REPORTS its history and one that lets you touch it — the sounding is the only read
of the past and it costs a deliberate act each time.

**Fail state as skill:** the empty sounding. Nothing marks where sediment lies; the
skill is remembering your own routing well enough to find your own words — the
world's memory is the player's memory, prompted by nothing.

**Refusals-check:** tested against never-rewrite (verbatim slices, law 4),
never-summarize (one sentence, never a digest), no-prompt (player-initiated only),
the deep-never-renders (a sounding reaches ground, never the deep). Passes.

## SYSTEMS — compositions of what exists; one new interaction, never a new system

### S1 · The order circuit (M1 + M2 + the station tray) — LAWFUL-NOW, standing, defend it

Descent order IS the downstream order (T6: the machine no longer picks even by
arrival), and the charge is the stone (the supersession). Composed, the three built
facts make sequencing a real playable skill: the order the hand releases stones from
the node is the order the stations present them, is the order sentences settle as
ground. What emerges is authorship of the world's stratigraphy with no score
watching it — Zachtronics' law with the optimum removed. This system already exists;
the craft finding is that it is FRAGILE against convenience: any future "sort
oldest first" or "auto-queue" helper deletes the skill. Carried as a defense, like
GC-2. Refusals-check: nothing compares orders, nothing measures them; passes.

### S2 · Phrasing at the pool (run-spacing + the R4 merge) — LAWFUL-NOW, found, not built

Two built things share the dam panel's surface: held stones group by sitting as
spacing (the supersession), and the merge joins stones in written order (R4). Their
one interaction — merging WITHIN a sitting versus ACROSS sittings, with the phrasing
in view — is a compositional technique the build supports today and nothing
announces. What emerges is rhythm as a playable material: the writer's own sessions
become the units of composition. New code required: none. Refusals-check: spacing
is never a count, the grouping never labels; passes as standing. (Card GC-4 holds
the defense side.)

### S3 · The scar in the caption (M3's persistent fails + the caption strip) — LAWFUL-NOW at reading strength

The caption strip names a station's act AND its fail state; the scorch is legible
shut from outside the room. Composed as built, the world already teaches its edges
before the hand crosses them — hover the receiver, read "scorches shut," and the
3.4 s limit is knowable as a fact of the world without a warning line ever
appearing at the moment of action. What emerges is the resolution of the recorded
Cowan collision (cost-visible-at-choice vs. no-warning-marker) at a DIFFERENT
surface than the one the collision names: cost is visible at the caption, at rest,
on the player's initiative — never at the lever, under load. The collision itself
stays on the record exactly as BUILD_RECORD holds it; this composition is offered
as a reading of why the built state may already be whole, not as a ruling.
Refusals-check: the caption never fires during the act, never advises, never
mentions his material; passes.

### S4 · The lit sounding (M5 + the watered lenses) — NEEDS-KEVIN

The only composition here that would change conduct: a sounding dropped in a region
whose lenses hold water would show its verbatim sentence in that region's own light
— the brightness the player's routing already earned, carried into the moment of
touching the settled word. Both systems exist (the sounding; lens-water and the
light return); the interaction — the sounding's rendering reading the region's lens
state — does not. It adds no number, no prompt, no rewriting; light stays behaviour.
But it is a NEW interaction under the freeze, it brushes the never-read-his-writing
boundary's neighborhood (it renders his sentence differently based on world state —
the state is his routing, not his meaning, which is why it is arguable rather than
dead), and whether the sounding should have any register at all beyond the flat
verbatim line is his fork. NEEDS-KEVIN, held silently by the counsel until a build
ever tries to decorate the sounding with something worse — this card is the lighter,
already-lawful-shaped geometry to put on the table that day.
