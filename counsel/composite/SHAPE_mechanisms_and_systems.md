## MECHANISMS

### M1 · Stillness-as-mechanic — hold is the strongest move — LAWFUL-NOW (built; this is a reading)

**The loop:** water arrives at the dam → nothing is clicked → the pool stands visible →
the world waits without asking → the release, when it comes, is chosen from stillness
rather than from pressure.

**Register word:** presence (Tolle) — the Now as the only field where anything real
happens; the game's deepest verb is the one with no input.

**Fail state as skill:** there is none, and that is the finding — held is lawful
forever (law 7), and the skill is tolerating a world that will never prompt. The
player who waits for a nudge learns, by its absence, that the move was always theirs.

**Law-check:** law 7 holds (no nag, no re-engagement hook) · refusal on
notifications/urgency holds · law 12 holds (quitting mid-hold loses nothing). Tether:
BUILD_RECORD, "the dam panel's release is the only governed act; shutting the panel is
not an action." This mechanism exists to be DEFENDED: any future "gentle reminder,"
idle animation that beckons, or held-state highlight breaches it.

### M2 · The gift circuit — every act completes on giving — LAWFUL-NOW (built; this is a reading)

**The loop:** write → work the stone by hand → descend it through your own position →
hold or release → ground/lake/plate receive → nothing returns as credit, debt, score,
or acknowledgement → the world is simply different.

**Register word:** gift (Eisenstein) — the transfer that creates ties without account.
Three outputs at every station (law 1) are three ways of giving; set-it-down is the
gift with no recipient, and it is lawful.

**Fail state as skill:** the "fail" a new player feels is the missing receipt — no
toast, no counter, no thanks. The skill is learning to read the world's changed state
as the whole of the answer, which is the non-transactional container doing its work
on the person.

**Law-check:** law 6 holds exactly (set-it-down: no destination, no animation, no
confirmation) · law 2 holds (no number anywhere in the circuit) · refusal on
achievements holds. Tether: BUILD_RECORD T6 and the bare-plate furniture (eight-edit
build, item 5). NEEDS-KEVIN only at the edge g4 already names: what "finished" means —
complete-on-giving stays his to rule, and this mechanism does not close it.

### M3 · The presencing U — the built circuit read as Theory U — LAWFUL-NOW as reading · NEEDS-KEVIN for anything added

**The loop (all built, nothing proposed):** sensing (the writing field, verbatim) →
letting go (working the stone: edit, merge, break — the raw arrival released by the
hand) → the bottom of the U (descent through the writer's own unnamed position, T6) →
letting come (the hold, then the release at the dam) → crystallizing (sediment, the
sentence settled as ground) → the emerging future arrives as light.

**Register word:** presencing (Scharmer) — and, held DOUBLE per Kevin's seating,
Schwermer's moneyless economy: the entire passage runs without a number, a price, or a
score, an economy lived rather than argued. Both resonances stand until his word;
neither is resolved here.

**Fail state as skill:** an unworked stone hangs at the writer's node and will not
carry past it (T4) — the U cannot be skipped by throughput. The skill is that working
the words IS the letting-go, an act of the hand, never of time or a glance.

**Law-check:** every arc of the loop is tethered to BUILD_RECORD (T3, T4, T6, the dam
law, the sentence-lens). Law 4 holds — the U never rewrites or interprets the words;
law 5 holds — the hand runs every stage. NEEDS-KEVIN: any move that would make the U

Second Output interface guard and the never-declare-his-state law — the reading is for

### M4 · The deep as the honored unconscious — descent without surveillance — LAWFUL-NOW · any retrieval NEEDS-KEVIN

**The loop:** bedload sinks fast → exits the frame → the deep receives it → nothing
renders, nothing counts it → a sounding, player-initiated only, may later touch bottom
somewhere — or find no bottom, and the system never marks which.

**Register word:** depth (Jung) — the psyche's unrendered remainder given a real place.
A world with no deep would claim the player is fully legible; law 9 is the refusal of
that claim.

**Fail state as skill:** losing a fraction to the deep (slop past the rim, the scorch,
the tear) is permanent and unannounced. The skill is Jung's own: relating to what has
gone down without demanding it back — and the game's refusal to retrieve is what makes
the relation real.

**Law-check:** law 9 holds (the deep never renders) · the sounding sits on the
refusals' allowed list (the player's own instrument) · "a deep... never marked by the
system" (world3d vocabulary). NEEDS-KEVIN: anything that ever returns material from
the deep, renders it, or lets it be fished — no tether exists, law 9 stands against
it, and the composite does not propose it. Carried so no future build invents
retrieval as a feature.

### M5 · Crisis-as-weather — the fail states as non-pathologizing witness — LAWFUL-NOW (built; this is a reading)

**The loop:** the hand overreaches → the world answers physically (scorch shuts the

**Register word:** emergence over pathology (Blackwell) — intensity treated as process,

**Fail state as skill:** the fail state IS the mechanism. The skill is range-finding by
consequence — learning reach, lean, and beam-time from a world that deforms instead of
labeling (law 10: behavior as the carrier).

**Law-check:** law 7 holds (the torn membrane never asks you to wait) · law 8 holds
(scorch/tear persist as they were; nothing advances in absence) · refusal on any system

## SYSTEMS — how the mechanisms compose, and what emerges

**S1 · The witnessing world (M1 + M4 + M5) — standing, built.**
Stillness (nothing prompts), depth (the deep receives without rendering), and
non-pathologizing weather (fail states persist unlabeled) compose into the game's care
model: a world that witnesses everything and comments on nothing. What emerges is the
frame Kevin named — "the upstream work of making sense and meaning of their own
experience without anyone telling them they are right or wrong or need to change"
(THE_APEX_AND_THE_SPIRES §0, verbatim) — as physics rather than as policy. LAWFUL-NOW;
a description of what is verified in BUILD_RECORD, carried so the counsel can defend
it against helpful additions.

**S2 · The whole-person circuit (M2 + M3) — standing, built.**
The gift circuit supplies the economy (every act completes on giving); the presencing U
supplies the passage (raw arrival → worked → descended → released → light). Composed,
they answer the composite's seat-question — what does this do to the human being who
walks it? — with a testable shape: the person who walks the circuit practices, in
miniature and without being told so, presence (M1), release (T4/T6), gift (M2), and
relation to their own depths (M4). LAWFUL-NOW as a reading. The guard is absolute:
this composition is FOR THE COUNSEL; naming any of it to the player at the moment of
acting breaches the Second Output interface guard, and the composite refuses movement
that depends on the player being told what walking is doing to them.

**S3 · The aimed stillness (M6 + M1) — standing.**
Hill and Tolle, the composite's own internal tension, compose rather than collide here:
the definite aim (one output) and the stillness under it (held is lawful, the freeze
forbids generation) are the same posture at two scales. What emerges is the composite's
resistance stated as system: no movement without presence — drive that cannot sit still
is run-ahead, and the corpus has already measured where that goes. LAWFUL-NOW; nothing
to build, one thing to keep refusing.
