## THE NUTRIENTS — shelf grammar: a name · the relation · what it composes with

A nutrient is a small named relation discovered by doing, which composes with others
(cowan_workshop.html, the shelf's own definition). Each was found by reading the standing
build through the composite's eight instruments; each names only what is already there.

**1 · Held is the Now.**
The build's strongest state is the one where nothing happens: hold costs no click, a full
tank is lawful and unannounced, the blank field is a complete state. Tolle's stillness is
not layered onto the game — law 7 already is it; the dam holding is presence rendered as
water. Composes with: the trim-tab (Fuller shelf) · held-not-asserted · STILL as the
strongest move. Tether: BUILD_RECORD, "Hold is the default and costs no click — water
that arrives at a dam simply stays."

**2 · The gift completes on giving, and the world is built from that clause.**
Descent is one gesture through the writer's own position, no confirmation; set-it-down
has no destination and no return; nothing comes back as debt. Eisenstein's gift economy
is not a theme here — it is the physics: the only acts are givings, and none of them
open an account. Composes with: complete-on-giving → compost · law 6 · the three outputs
of law 1. Tether: BUILD_RECORD T6, "No confirmation; the drag is the decision."

**3 · The return arrives at right angles, so no one can aim at it.**
The writer routes water down; light comes back up through his own sentences — and the
Second Output gate forbids the interface from ever saying so at the moment of acting.
Hill's aim, Eisenstein's gift, and Scharmer's emerging future all require exactly this:
a consequence that cannot be gamed because it is never pointed at. Composes with:
light-as-the-return-flow (Fuller shelf) · the-bee-is-not-trying · the interface guard.
Tether: BUILD_RECORD sentence-lens section, "the return flow now runs through the
player's own sentences."

**4 · The deep is the honored unconscious.**
Bedload sinks fast and exits the frame; the deep never renders; a sounding is
player-initiated and a sounding that finds nothing shows nothing — the system never
marks a deep. Jung read structurally: the build gives the not-yet-conscious a real
place and refuses to floodlight it. What descends is not lost; it is withheld from
rendering, which is a different thing. Composes with: law 9 · the sounding as the
player's own instrument (refusals, allowed list) · the-slot-stays-empty. Tether:
world3d vocabulary table, "a deep — a sounding that finds no bottom; never marked by
the system."

**5 · Intensity is weather, never diagnosis.**
Scorch, tear, slop — each persists, each heals in its own time or stays as it was, and
nothing names it a mistake, warns before it, or reads it back to the writer. Blackwell's
non-pathologizing witness is already the fail-state design: the world receives the
overload and continues, exactly as a body in crisis needs a field that does not call it
malfunction. Composes with: fail-state-as-skill (Fuller SHAPE) · never-declare-Kevin's-
state · law 7. Tether: BUILD_RECORD, "heals in its own time, 7 s, and nothing asks you
to wait."

**6 · Walkable is the truth condition.**
The verification bar — walked from spawn, PNGs actually read, "mechanism works" split
from "does what you needed" — is Peace Pilgrim's vow in build discipline: nothing is
claimed that has not been walked, and the unwalked edge is named as the real one
(BUILD_RECORD names its own: "the drag feel is unwalked"). Composes with: Definition of
Done · the placement doctrine · WALKABLE/BLOCKED as the session's last word. Tether:
vendor brief, "can it be walked from spawn?"

**7 · The teaching names the world and never the writer.**
Kevin's ruling ("The game can teach the user how to use it") drew the composite's own
line exactly: captions name the tank, the stations, the acts — and nothing speaks about
his material. Care that informs without reading the person is the whole social-change
register in one sentence: a museum caption, not a coach. Composes with: the Recognition
Law · refusals ("tooltips that advise") · morning-pages guard (the system never reads
the writer). Tether: BUILD_RECORD teaching section, "A museum caption, not a coach."

**8 · The mastermind holds no account.**
Hill's mastermind — minds in harmony creating what none holds alone — enters this corpus
filtered: the counsel of four, the swarm passes, the workshop are masterminds whose
output is deposited as gift, never accumulated, never scored against each other. The
acquisitive register did not cross the door; "riches" reads as capacity throughout.
Composes with: the counsel structure itself · non-transactional container · the freeze's
"many depositings, one output." Tether: THE_APEX_AND_THE_SPIRES, "I'm exporting the
roles of village into a non transactional game container."

**9 · The centre is drawn and unnamed.**
The writer is the bottom node, drawn and unnamed (T3); the centre-of-the-form stays
un-nameable (T2); the slot stays empty. Jung's Self — the centre that is not the ego —
is held here by refusal rather than by symbol: the build keeps the place and never
fills it, which is the only way a centre survives being built. Composes with:
4·6·4·1 (the un-nameable centre) · the-slot-stays-empty · declining-to-break-a-symmetry
(Fuller shelf). Tether: BUILD_RECORD T2, "the centre-of-the-form stays un-nameable."

**10 · The presencing U runs through the dam.**
Write (sensing) → work the stone (letting go of the raw arrival) → descend through your
own position (the bottom of the U) → hold → release (letting come) → the world answers
in light. Scharmer's U is not proposed for the game; it is a reading of the circuit
already built — and Schwermer's resonance is held beside it: the whole passage runs
without a number anywhere, an economy lived moneyless. Both resonances stand until
Kevin strikes one; the double is face-up here as everywhere. Composes with: the
trim-tab circuit (Fuller SHAPE S1) · law 2 · the descent gesture T6. Tether:
BUILD_RECORD T6 and the dam-hold law, read together.
