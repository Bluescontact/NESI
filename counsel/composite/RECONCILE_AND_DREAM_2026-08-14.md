**F2 · THE SLACK MIRROR, GENERALIZED — the whole world runs on earned permanence.** A channel your hand cuts, a mirror you aim, a beam you hold — all hold while your hand is on them and **keep** only where something was fed. **F3 · THE SECOND HAND'S WATER, WITHOUT A SECOND PLAYER.** Another person's words enter only the way they already can: pasted by your own hand, the *who* kept verbatim and never generated.
