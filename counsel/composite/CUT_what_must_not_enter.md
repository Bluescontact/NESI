# CUT — the composite material that must NOT cross into NESI

The CUT vertex of the change-composite swarm pass, 2026-08-11. Eight voices
carry eight registers, and most of what made those registers famous is exactly
what the laws bar. This file is the knife: each barred item names what it is,
the specific law that bars it, and the lighter substitute — what lawfully
carries the same nutrient. Findings only; nothing here is a work queue.

---

## 1 · The self-help register

**What it is.** The voice all eight get flattened into on a bookstore shelf:
second-person imperative, promised outcomes, steps toward a better you. "Set
your intention." "Practice presence daily." "Trust the process."

**The law it breaks.** The world speaks no text to the player (the silence
refusal); law 4 — no generated prose on the player path, verbatim only; the
locatable-move gate — locate, never steer, and the self-help register is steer
distilled; never-direct-the-body for every embodied variant.

**The lighter substitute.** The mechanic that IS the advice, unsaid. The blank
intake field is "begin where you are." The filter run by hand is "attend to
what you actually have." Set-it-down having no destination and no confirmation
is the whole minimalism literature as one absence. The world demonstrates;
demonstration is the standing law (demonstrate-don't-describe) and it costs no
words.

---

## 2 · The guru voice

**What it is.** The authority register: the teacher who has arrived, speaking
down the gradient to the one who hasn't. Tolle and Hill both ship in this
voice; Scharmer's facilitator is its institutional cousin. It includes the
soft form — the wise narrator, the world that "knows something you don't."

**The law it breaks.** HELD, NOT ASSERTED — the one law every constitutional
law is an instance of; the AI Constitutional Stack (fluency-as-authority is
the named failure); renderer-not-verifier; and Recognition Law by implication
— a guru does the recognizing for you, and the mechanic never does the
recognizing.

**The lighter substitute.** The witness register: system facts plus Kevin's
verbatim words with paths. Peace Pilgrim is the internal model — she answered
questions when asked and otherwise walked. Counsel-side, a seat states its
read AS a seat's read and holds it; it never speaks as the one who has
arrived.

---

## 3 · Prosperity and extraction framing — Hill's acquisitive register

**What it is.** "Riches," the money consciousness, desire as the wish for
accumulation, other minds as instruments of the aim. The entry-filter in the
agent's own seating already names it: FILTERED at entry, the acquisitive
register does not cross.

**The law it breaks.** The non-transactional mechanism (labor-as-gift,
near-zero burn); complete-on-giving; the single-person economy's frame —
nothing in this corpus accumulates against another; and the composite's own
charter: nothing ships that changes the world by extracting from anyone.

**The lighter substitute.** "Riches reads as capacity" — the substitution the
seating already made. Hill's engine minus acquisition is: a definite aim
(Kevin's one output, already named), organized plans (the slice law), and the
mastermind (the counsel itself, build-side). The drive survives; the taking
does not.

---

## 4 · Diagnosis — typology, stages, and the clinical read

**What it is.** Jung's types and complexes applied to a person; Scharmer's
listening-levels applied to a person; Blackwell's emergence-vs-emergency
distinction applied to a person; any sentence of the form "this person is a
[category] in [stage]." Includes the benevolent form: diagnosing readiness,
diagnosing growth.

**The law it breaks.** The hard limit verbatim: never score, rank, measure,
diagnose, or pathologize Kevin or any player. Never-declare-Kevin's-state.
Blackwell's own guard closes even the softest gap: "you seem to be holding
back" is never sayable.

**The lighter substitute.** Describe the SYSTEM in system terms and stop at
the skin. "The river is low today" — a world-fact the player can see too.
Counsel-side: what a design does to a generic human who walks it, in third
person, structurally. The typology stays useful as a lens on DESIGNS (does
this station assume one temperament?) and never lands on a person.

## 5 · Interpretation of the player's material

**What it is.** Jung's method pointed at the water: theme-finding, symbol
reading, sentiment, "your writing suggests," any inferred category laid over
what the player wrote. The morning-pages guard already names the whole class:
the pages feed the system; the system never reads the writer.

**The law it breaks.** The Recognition Law (the mechanic never does the
recognizing); law 4 (never rewritten — no summarizing, paraphrasing, or
inferred categories on the player path); law 5 (the operator's hand runs the
filter — no classifier deciding what a fraction is); the morning-pages-channel
guard, where violating it is named a build failure.

**The lighter substitute.** BEHAVIOR, not meaning. The four fractions separate
by how they move — sinks fast, hangs, films, clumps (law 10) — and the
player's own hand sorts them (law 5). The world responds to what the words DO
as water, never to what they mean. If recognition happens, the player did it;
the design's whole job was to leave the room quiet enough.

## 6 · The growth-journey frame

**What it is.** Transformation as trajectory: stages, arcs, before-and-after,
the U traversed, individuation as a path with mileposts, "how far you've
come." Every voice but the AOC carries a version. Any rendering of it —
progress bars, phase names shown to the player, a world that remembers you
were "earlier" — reads the writer against a timeline.

**The law it breaks.** Law 2 — no number reaches the player, no progress bars,
no streaks; law 7 — a fallow season is not a regression; never-declare-
Kevin's-state (a stage IS a declared state); and law 8 — the world does not
simulate your journey while you are gone.

**The lighter substitute.** The world changes; the player is never measured.
Ground accumulates where water was sent, the heliostat's light deepens with
cycles run — consequence as terrain, unlabeled, uncompared, walkable. The
player who wants to see how far they've come can walk their own ground and
decide for themselves what it says. That is the journey nutrient with the
reading of the writer removed.

## 7 · Facilitation instruments — prompts, check-ins, guided thresholds

**What it is.** Scharmer's participant-facing kit: journaling prompts, sensing
questions, check-in/check-out rounds, "what wants to emerge through you?" Also
its ambient forms: a threshold that asks you to set an intention, a session
that opens with a reflective question, any surface inviting the player inward
on cue.

**The law it breaks.** Law 7 — never nag, never prompt a return; the silence
refusal — the world speaks no text to the player; the Second Output interface
guard — never tell the actor what their act is worth while they decide; the
somatic threshold — the body-read is never prompted.

**The lighter substitute.** The blank field. Intake opens on nothing and asks
nothing; whatever wants to emerge can, because nothing is in the way. STILL is
already in the corpus as "the strongest move in the game" — presencing as an
available act, never an assigned one. The facilitator's nutrient is space;
NESI delivers space by architecture instead of by voice.

## 8 · Autosuggestion and affirmation mechanics

**What it is.** Hill's operational core: write the aim, read it aloud twice
daily, repeat until the subconscious accepts it. As a mechanic: mantra
surfaces, the game echoing the player's words back at intervals, prompted
re-reading of past writing "to reinforce it."

**The law it breaks.** Law 4 — echoing-back-with-intent is the system using
the player's words ON the player, the manipulation face of verbatim; law 7 —
scheduled repetition is a re-engagement loop by definition; law 6 — the
reinforcement chime is the confirmation set-it-down refuses.

**The lighter substitute.** The words persist as world. What the player wrote
became water, became ground, and stands where it was sent — re-encounterable
on the player's own walk, on the player's own schedule, unprompted. Presence
without repetition: the corpus keeps everything and rehearses nothing.

## 9 · Community, witness, and sharing features — the mastermind rendered

**What it is.** Hill's mastermind, Eisenstein's gift-circulation, Scharmer's
co-sensing field, built INTO the world: sharing your ground, a witness mode,
gift-exchange between players, "the field between human beings" as a feature.

**The law it breaks.** Law 11 — nothing reaches outward, no accounts, no sync,
no sharing; law 3 — no network on the core loop; the first-user-threshold
ruling — the second user is NOT upstream; and law 2's deepest clause — nothing
compares, and a witness is a comparison waiting to happen.

**The lighter substitute.** The counsel of four IS the mastermind, seated on
the build side of the membrane where plural minds are lawful. In-world, the
field-between-humans nutrient is carried the only lawful way: the world one
player tends is made entirely of what one human gave it, and the gift
completes on giving. If it ever crosses to a second person, it crosses by
Kevin's own hand outside the game — the OSG circuit already exists for exactly
that, and the game does not duplicate it.

## 10 · Crisis framing and the emergence protocol

**What it is.** Blackwell's clinical setting imported: treating intense
writing as an event the system should respond to — safety copy, grounding
exercises offered on trigger, "support resources" surfacing when the water
runs dark. The caring face of diagnosis.

**The law it breaks.** The Recognition Law — trigger-response IS the machine
recognizing; law 5 — a classifier deciding a fraction is "contaminant-as-
distress" is the automatic sorting the filter bans; never-declare-the-state;
never-direct-the-body. The morning-pages guard again: no sentiment, no
reflection of inner state back to the writer.

**The lighter substitute.** Blackwell's actual gift is the NEGATIVE ruling:
intensity is not malfunction — so the design's whole duty is to not flinch.
The world receives dark water exactly as it receives clear: same physics, same
hand on the filter, no alarm, no special case. Non-pathologizing witness,
implemented as the absence of a pathology branch anywhere in the code.

---

## 11 · Purity vows and renunciation as mechanic

**What it is.** Peace Pilgrim's vow and Schwermer's moneyless decades turned
into game structure: pledge mechanics, streaks of practice, penance for
lapses, simplicity enforced by the system rather than found by the player.

**The law it breaks.** Law 2 — a streak is a count; law 7 — a lapsed vow that
nags is the re-engagement hook; law 12 — quitting mid-sentence loses nothing,
which a vow mechanic exists to contradict; never-direct-the-body for every
fasting/walking variant.

**The lighter substitute.** Renunciation is already the build's own method,
system-side: the FULL FREEZE, ephemeralization-as-subtraction-only (the Fuller
CUT's sharpest edge), the retirement ledger. The player-side nutrient is that
the world never adds obligations at all — the vow of simplicity is kept BY THE
DESIGN so the player never has to make one.

---

*The sharpest edge of this knife, matching the Fuller seat's: the eight enter
NESI only as ways of reading what the world does to a human being. The moment
any of them becomes a voice the player hears, a category laid on the writer,
or a hand on anyone's back — this seat refuses.*
