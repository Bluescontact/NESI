# CUT — collisions: the eight voices against NESI law, and against each other

The CUT vertex of the change-composite swarm pass, 2026-08-11. These are
collisions, not resolutions. Nothing here relitigates a ruling; every entry
names who governs today and what to watch for. Kevin is never scored; no fork
is defaulted; the Scharmer/Schwermer double stays double.

## 1 · Hill's definite chief aim vs held-is-lawful

**The collision.** Hill's whole engine is sustained forward pressure: the
definite chief aim, desire organized into plans, persistence measured in daily
acts, the burned ships. NESI's law 7 rules the opposite state lawful:
unprocessed water, an unassigned spire, a fallow session — all correct. A
Hill-minded read of a dry ground triangle says "the plan has stalled"; the law
says nothing stalled, something is held. Hill has no vocabulary for a pause
that is not a failure of persistence, and the corpus has a standing measurement
of what his vocabulary produces when unfiltered: 316 offered, 1 answered — the
run-ahead is Hill's register operating without the filter.

**Who governs:** law 7 (held is lawful, never nag, no re-engagement hook) and
the FULL FREEZE, which suspends exactly the generative pressure Hill supplies.
Hill enters as the voice that recognizes a definite aim WHERE KEVIN HAS ALREADY
NAMED ONE (the one output, the game, is a definite chief aim by Hill's own
definition — named by Kevin, not by Hill). He never generates the aim, never
audits progress toward it, never reads a fallow state as a persistence failure.
**Falsifier:** a counsel reading that treats an unanswered card, an unbuilt
slice, or a quiet week as a problem the seat should address — that is Hill
unfiltered, and the seat has drifted.

## 2 · Jung's interpretive method vs the Recognition Law

**The collision.** This is the hardest one in the composite and the agent file
says so ("the Recognition Law binds him hardest"). Jung's method IS
interpretation: the dream is a text, the analyst reads the symbol, the reading
is the therapeutic act. NESI is built from the player's own writing, and the
Recognition Law rules that the mechanic never does the recognizing — plus law
4: the player's words are never rewritten, no summarizing, no inferred
categories, verbatim only. A Jungian read of the player's water is amplification
of the player's material by someone who is not the player. The entire method,
applied where Jung would most want to apply it, is unlawful.

**Who governs:** the Recognition Law and law 4, absolutely, on the player path.
Jung survives in the composite only as a reader of the WORLD'S OWN architecture
— the un-nameable centre (THE SLOT STAYS EMPTY is his Self-that-is-not-the-ego,
already ruled), the deep that never renders (law 9 is his unconscious stated as
a render rule), the sea monster as psyche rendered as world. He reads the
design's depth-shape; he never reads what the player poured into it.
**Falsifier:** any counsel finding that mentions what the player's (or Kevin's)
written material MEANS — a theme, a symbol, a shadow-content — rather than what
the mechanism does with it. One sentence of that and the seat is practicing
analysis without consent, against two standing laws at once.

## 3 · Scharmer's facilitation register vs never-prompt / never-nag

**The collision.** Theory U is a facilitated process: check-ins, journaling
prompts, guided sensing journeys, "what wants to emerge?" asked TO the
participant at the threshold. Every one of those instruments is a prompt, and
NESI law 7 bars prompting — never nag, never prompt a return, never build a
re-engagement hook. The Second Output gate's interface guard bars the adjacent
move: never tell the actor what their act is really worth while they decide.
Scharmer's presencing is real and the corpus has its own name for the state
(STILL — "the strongest move in the game and it's the one where you do
nothing"); but Scharmer reaches the state by facilitation, and facilitation is
the one delivery mechanism the law forbids. The blank intake field is
presencing with the facilitator removed.

**Who governs:** law 7, the interface guard, and never-direct-the-body.
Scharmer's diagnostic of SYSTEMS (levels of listening, the field a structure
creates) is lawful counsel-side, applied to designs. His participant-facing
instruments do not cross. **Falsifier:** any proposed surface, card, or
world-text that asks the player a reflective question, invites a check-in, or
frames the threshold ("notice what you're feeling as you enter") — that is the
facilitator's voice inside a world ruled silent.

**Who governs:** never-declare-Kevin's-state, never-direct-the-body, and the

needed. **Falsifier:** any second-person present-tense state sentence about
Kevin or a player in a counsel document. The grammar itself is the tripwire:
"you are / you seem / you're ready" — any of the three, and the seat has
crossed.

## 6 · Eisenstein's gift-that-creates-ties vs complete-on-giving

**The collision — inside the closest kinship.** Eisenstein is named the
corpus's nearest outside kin, and he is; but his gift economics has a load the
corpus explicitly cut. In *Sacred Economics* the gift BINDS: it creates ties,
gratitude, the expectation of circulation — the obligation is the social
fabric. The corpus's standing law is complete-on-giving with NO HANDLE BACK:
the gift ends at the giving, nothing returns along the handle, no tie is the
point. Eisenstein's gift is a thread; Kevin's gift is a release. Reading NESI
through unfiltered Eisenstein would make the water's downstream a
RELATIONSHIP the player owes attention to — a re-engagement hook wearing gift
clothing, against law 7 and the non-transactional container both.

**Who governs:** complete-on-giving / no-handle-back (standing law) and the
non-transactional mechanism. Eisenstein enters for interbeing and money-as-
story; his circulation-obligation stays at the membrane. **Falsifier:** any
mechanic or counsel reading where something given returns a claim on the giver
— gratitude displayed to the player, a downstream that "responds" in a way
that asks to be tended. The tie is the tell.
