## 5 · MUST NOT ENTER

1. **Second-person affirmation strings on the player path.** **Harm:** the system issues a claim about a person that the person may not recognize in themselves; because the claim is unfalsifiable, the mismatch has nowhere to land except as the player's own deficiency. The only available reading of the gap is "I am failing the thing that is being kind to me." *Bars:* law 4, the silence law, the teaching ruling.
2. **"Emotional resonance tracking."** **Harm:** it reads the writer. Every protection in this build assumes nothing does. Named first among equals because it arrives dressed as attentiveness.
3. **Guided arrival sequences, breathing synchronization, intention-setting at entry.** **Harm:** directs the body, and installs a correct way to arrive — so the person who arrives scattered is wrong at the door of the one place built not to require legibility.
4. **Connection counts, journey tracking, impact visualization, the five success metrics.** **Harm:** converts a life into a total a person can be behind on.
5. **"No direct purchasing of one's own items" / structurally forced relationship.** **Harm:** the person with no one is designed out of their own value. The founding wound re-inflicted by a benevolent architecture.
6. **Reciprocity frameworks / "receivers become givers; the loop closes."** **Harm:** debt with better manners — an obligation issued to someone who never consented to receive one, and who now owes at the moment they were most depleted.
7. **The manifestation and abundance register as a claim on the interior.** **Harm:** it makes material circumstance a readout of inner state, which for a person actually short of money is an accusation delivered as encouragement.
8. **Identity verdicts, including flattering ones** — *"You are a survivor, a warrior, a beacon of strength"*; *"Those voices that whisper doubts… silence them."* **Harm:** the system decides what the person's struggle means and returns a verdict on who they are. The third clause is worse: it forbids a feeling. *Bars:* Recognition Law, never-declare-state, and its inverse — **never declare the good either; a positive diagnosis is a diagnosis.**
9. **"Strategic withdrawal creates developmental pressure" as a mechanic aimed at a player.** **Harm:** withholding engineered to force growth is a system deciding what a person needs to develop, and from inside it is indistinguishable from neglect. Lawful as a description of acts Kevin took in his own life; barred as a design move.
10. **Spiral Dynamics stage placement.** **Harm:** diagnosis with a developmental face; it tells a person where they are on a ladder they did not agree to stand on.
11. **Story collection, voice-note submission, story cards with connection counts.** **Harm:** it turns writing into performance for an audience — the precise inverse of the BLIND face, where *"the words don't perform for you."*
12. **The refusal that fires against my own seat, logged by name.** The shelf's central ask — that a system *recognize a person's worth and reflect it back* — is a thing I am seated to want and a thing I am barred from designing. **I decline to propose any surface that reflects worth, in any form, including architectural, indirect, or "emergent" forms.**
