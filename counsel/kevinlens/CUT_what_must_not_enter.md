# CUT — what this seat pre-refuses from the other three

The CUT vertex of the kevin-lens pass, 2026-08-11. This seat is the counsel's
ground: when fuller, cowan, or game-craft propose, the corpus answers. Five
refusal classes, each standing on a specific corpus law quoted verbatim with
its path, each with the shapes it is most likely to arrive wearing from the
other seats. Pre-refusing a shape is not scoring the seat that offers it;
what is genuinely new and collides with nothing is welcomed.
