## 3 · The teaching ruling vs "nothing may point at anything as worth attention"

**Law one** — `nesi/game2d/BUILD_RECORD.md` (Kevin's ruling 2026-08-11,
"The game can teach the user how to use it"):
> "the game may TEACH. The line held: teaching names the world and its
> mechanics; it never advises, never scores, never reads or mentions what he
> wrote. A museum caption, not a coach."

**Law two** — `nesi/world3d/.claude/rules/refusals.md`:
> "**Progress bars, quest logs, objective markers, waypoints, minimap pings,
> glowing outlines on interactables.** Nothing in the world may point at
> anything as worth attention."

The teaching ruling settles CONTENT (mechanics, never material, never
advice). It does not settle SALIENCE: a caption is attached to one object
and not another, and attachment is a pointer. A label that appears when the
player approaches is a museum caption; a label that draws the approach is a
waypoint with words. The line between them — timing, unbidden appearance,
visual weight — is unruled.

**Who governs today:** the teaching ruling for what a label may say; refusal
two for everything about where and when it appears — and their overlap has
no ruling. The lean, reported as a lean: the corpus's grammar (player-
initiated sounding, "the absence is the feedback") suggests teaching text
should be player-summoned, never volunteered. **Falsifier:** a caption that
fires without a player act, glows, persists, or attaches to the player's own
material.

For a fraction to SINK FAST or HANG, the engine must already hold its

whether some default assignment from text properties is inevitable, is
unruled.

**Who governs today:** law 5 and R2 lean hard — any assignment computed from
what the text SAYS is a classifier and barred. An assignment keyed only to
what the hand DID is the lean's lawful shape, unratified. **Falsifier:** a
code path that sets a fraction from text content (length, sentiment, keyword,
punctuation-as-meaning) with no hand act between the words and the identity.
