## THE NUTRIENTS — shelf grammar: a name · the relation · what it composes with

A nutrient is a small named relation discovered by doing, which composes with
others. Each of these is drawn from Kevin's own patterns and standing laws;
each carries its verbatim line and path. The names are the corpus's own slugs
and namings — none is minted here.

**1 · precesse — the return arrives at right angles.**
The result the world gives back is never the one the action aimed at; it is
structurally guaranteed by the action's geometry, not granted afterward.
Composes with: the Second Output gate · light-as-return in the 2D build ·
`attractor_currency`. Tether, verbatim: "Result arrives orthogonal to intended
action... the consequence that was not aimed at, produced as the unavoidable
structural outcome of the action's geometry." —
`patterns\precesse.md` (Canon 49).

**2 · completion_through_placing — placing is the completion event.**
A gift is done when it crosses into the right container and the hands are
empty — not when received, read, or acknowledged. Composes with: law 6
(set-it-down has no confirmation) · the gift grammar (completes on giving, no
handle back) · `the_silent_close`. Tether, verbatim: "A gift is complete at
the moment it is placed in the right container. Not when received. Not when
read... The placer has already finished." —
`patterns\completion_through_placing.md` (Canon 56).

**3 · limit_as_coordinate_not_wall — the edge is positional data.**
A limit named from inside shared work lands as a coordinate on a jointly-held
map; the same words from outside shared work land as a verdict. Composes with:
the Definition of Done rule 5 (name the edge of what was checked) · fail
states in the build that persist rather than warn. Tether, verbatim: "When the
process is private, the limit can only be delivered as a verdict from inside a
map the receiver cannot see." — `patterns\limit_as_coordinate_not_wall.md`.

**4 · gate-as-f4 — retained authority owes its discharge condition.**
A veto without a stated passing condition is structurally a pump, whatever the
holder intends; the definition is owed before the veto fires. Composes with:
laws 5 and 7 (the hand runs the filter; held is lawful) · `veto_pattern_
drift_detection`. Tether, verbatim: "A retained veto without a stated
discharge condition is structurally a pump, regardless of the holder's
intent. Definition plus held control equals a gate." —
`patterns\gate_as_f4.md`.

**5 · tended_void_paradox — self-operating is not substrate-free.**
An organ that runs itself is running on a vascular system someone built and
someone tends; the tending is invisible precisely when it works. Composes
with: law 8 (the world runs while you are in it) · the builder/player split ·
`thermal_mass_effect`. Tether, verbatim: "The 'self' in self-operating names
autonomy of function, not independence from substrate." —
`patterns\tended_void_paradox.md` (CANON 2026-07-01, membrane mark: Kevin YES).

**6 · held, not asserted — the one law the others are instances of.**
The pattern is universally recognizable, but only in the reader's own
material, and only when held. Composes with: the Recognition Law · the
Locatable Move · every no-score law in the twelve. Tether, Kevin verbatim,
2026-08-03: "I have this vision naturally.. it's not a somatic reality most
could ever imagine ... yet its something everyone can recognize in their own
patterns when its held rather then asserted." —
`C:\Users\KMEAR\.claude\projects\C--Users-KMEAR-OneDrive-Desktop-DSS-content\memory\feedback_held_not_asserted.md`.

**7 · the slot stays empty — one locatable only he places.**
What is upstream of the center is never named, never offered candidates; an
offer to fill it is a fill. Composes with: the Locatable Move's five
locatables · `feedback_never_declare_kevins_state` · the counsel's own
conduct. Tether, Kevin verbatim, 2026-08-04: "The slot stays empty. Do not
fill it, do not offer candidates, do not ask again." —
`C:\Users\KMEAR\.claude\projects\C--Users-KMEAR-OneDrive-Desktop-DSS-content\memory\feedback_the_slot_stays_empty.md`.

**8 · carrying is not asking — a tile does two jobs, and they must split.**
Disclosing a live decision and asking for a mark are different acts; a carry
renders and stays markable without depositing a new demand. Composes with: B3
(derived, never authored) · the rate brake · law 7 (never nag). Tether: "a
tile whose reading and option texts are IDENTICAL to a live unanswered offer
on the same surface is a CARRY... it does not deposit — so it never reaches
the brake." — `nesi\mind\PROTOCOLS.md`, CARRYING IS NOT ASKING, Kevin's mark
2026-08-07.

**9 · at its level — stillness is a complete state.**
A thing that does not move is not failing; where there is no fall beneath it,
resting is the correct physics, not a stall. Composes with: law 7 (held is
lawful) · R1's third output · `completion_through_placing`. Tether, verbatim
(THE_VISION_WHOLE §5, quoted in the ruling record): "A thing that does not
move is not stuck, refused, or failing. It is at its level. There is no fall
beneath it right now, and that is a complete state." —
`nesi\spec\THE_FOUR_RULINGS_2026-08-07.md`, R1.

**10 · the drop rule — a drop is not a request for a decision sequence.**
Material arriving with no ask gets: what it confirms, what it collides with,
where it already exists — then quiet. Composes with: the FULL FREEZE · the
authoring test (fork or finding?) · this library's own conduct. Tether, Kevin
verbatim, 2026-08-09: "file drops against the corpus, then stop." —
`nesi\mind\PROTOCOLS.md`, THE DROP RULE.
