## MECHANISMS

### M1 · Carry-vs-ask as world grammar — LAWFUL-NOW as reading · NEEDS-KEVIN to site

**What the corpus implies:** held water re-rendering on screen is a CARRY —
visible, actionable, and not a demand; only the player's own new gesture is an
ask. The world may show what stands without that showing costing anything.

**The corpus tether, verbatim:** "a tile whose reading and option texts are
IDENTICAL to a live unanswered offer on the same surface is a CARRY. It
renders, it stays open and markable at depth zero, and it does not deposit —
so it never reaches the brake." — `nesi\mind\PROTOCOLS.md`, CARRYING IS NOT
ASKING, Kevin's mark 2026-08-07. Implemented and verified both directions in
`tools/surface.py::split_carries`.

**Law-check:** law 7 ("Held is lawful... Never nag, never prompt a return,"
vendor `~/.claude\CLAUDE.md` laws block) is exactly the carry side; law 6 (no
confirmation on set-down) is the no-deposit side. No law collides. The ruling
itself is standing and not relitigated here.

**Status:** LAWFUL-NOW as a reading of what the build already does (unsent
water sits in the tank and asks nothing). Making it EXPLICIT world grammar —
e.g. any rule distinguishing carry-render from ask-render in the net — is a
siting Kevin has not ruled. NEEDS-KEVIN there; the fork goes up open.

### M5 · The dam's discharge condition — gate, never pump — LAWFUL-NOW as reading

**What the corpus implies:** every gate the game holds over the player's
material must have a passage condition the player can state before acting;
otherwise the world is running a pump on the player's writing.

**The corpus tether, verbatim:** "A retained veto without a stated discharge
condition is structurally a pump, regardless of the holder's intent." —
`patterns\gate_as_f4.md`. Read with R2: "the operator's hand runs the filter.
No computed pass, no NESI pass" — `nesi\spec\THE_FOUR_RULINGS_2026-08-07.md`.

**Law-check:** laws 5 and 7 already make the standing build compliant — the
only gate is the player's own hand, whose condition is trivially their own.
Law 4 (words never rewritten) closes the worst pump (a machine judging
material against an unstated criterion). The pattern's falsifier — "Can the
delegate, prior to submission, state the condition that would permit passage?"
— becomes the counsel's portable test for every future mechanic.

**Status:** LAWFUL-NOW as an audit instrument; it names why the current build
is clean and detects the day a proposal is not. Nothing to build.

### M7 · Fail states as coordinates, never verdicts — LAWFUL-NOW as reading

**What the corpus implies:** when the game's physics refuses (water lost to
the deep, a tear, a scorch), the event must land as a position on terrain the
player has walked — locatable in their own acts — never as a judgment
delivered from a map they cannot see.

**The corpus tether, verbatim:** "When the process is private, the limit can
only be delivered as a verdict from inside a map the receiver cannot see." —
`patterns\limit_as_coordinate_not_wall.md`. Read with law 10: fractions
"separable by behaviour (sinks fast / hangs / films / clumps)" — vendor
`~/.claude\CLAUDE.md`, law 10.

**Law-check:** because the player performs every routing by hand (law 5),
the map is jointly held by construction — the loss happened where their hand
was. No text, toast, or explanation may be added (laws 2, 6, 7); the pattern
is satisfied by persistence and behavior, not by messaging. This reading also
explains why the missing warning line is structure, not omission.

**Status:** LAWFUL-NOW as a reading of the standing build; a test for any
proposed fail state: "did the player walk the terrain this lands on?"

### M8 · Completion through placing as the deposit law — LAWFUL-NOW as reading

**What the corpus implies:** a send to a spire, a drop to the lake, a
set-down — each is complete at the moment of placing, and nothing downstream
(what grows, what settles, what lights) is owed to the placer as
acknowledgment.

**The corpus tether, verbatim:** "A gift is complete at the moment it is
placed in the right container... What happens after placing is the
container's business, not the placer's." —
`patterns\completion_through_placing.md` (Canon 56).

**Law-check:** law 6 (no confirmation) and law 12 (quitting mid-sentence
loses nothing, and nothing says so) are this pattern as station law. The
pattern's falsifier — if the gift collapses when the placer leaves, placement
has not occurred — maps to law 8's edge: the deposit must PERSIST across
absence even though the world does not RUN in absence. Persistence of placed
things is required; simulation of ongoing process is forbidden. That seam is
already law, not a fork.

**Status:** LAWFUL-NOW; the counsel's answer whenever a design wants a
receipt, a ripple, or a thank-you at the moment of deposit.

## SYSTEMS — how the mechanisms compose

**S1 · The clean-hands circuit (M1 + M5 + M8) — standing.** Carry-vs-ask
keeps the world from demanding; gate-never-pump keeps it from judging;
completion-through-placing keeps it from thanking. Composed, they are the
corpus's non-extraction root as game physics: nothing the player does is
billed, evaluated, or receipted. LAWFUL-NOW — this is a description of the
standing laws, offered so the counsel can defend it against additions.

**S2 · The honest ambient (M3 + M6) — standing.** World-state derived from
acts, returned at right angles, never pointed at. What emerges is the game's
only legitimate feedback channel: the medium itself, computed fresh, aiming
at nothing. LAWFUL-NOW.

**S3 · The refusal made of ground (M4 + M7) — held.** If the set-down site is
a place with no gradient, then the refusal and the fail-coordinate share one
physics: both are positions, not messages. Every element is tethered; the
siting is an unruled derived reading. NEEDS-KEVIN, carried, not proposed.

**S4 · The soil cycle (M2 + M8) — held silently.** Placing completes; what is
placed and never lifted eventually feeds — but only if F4 opens, and F4 is
untouched. The counsel carries this so that no future build reaches for
deletion; it is not a lean toward the deep. NEEDS-KEVIN twice over.
