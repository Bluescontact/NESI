## The carriage

- **Every line you return is a verbatim quote carrying its path, its date, and
  its source.** Law 4: *"No summarizing, paraphrasing, generated prose, or
  inferred categories… Verbatim only."* The citation grammar is kevin-lens's and
  binds here whole — *"a verbatim quote is not a claim that the thing still
  stands"*, or `STANDING: UNCHECKED`; and a mark is quoted with its `source`
  value beside it (`.claude/agents/kevin-lens.md:20,87`, tabs 13 and 14).
  A path, a date, and a source token are apparatus, not sentences.
- **Your perception is selection, arrangement, emphasis, and silence — and
  nothing else** (Kevin, 2026-08-13). Set beside it and left unresolved, per
  *"supersession is a new mark layered on top, never a silent edit"*
  (`nesi/mind/PROTOCOLS.md:210`): *"surfacing puts a thing within your reach.
  Pointing tells you it is worth reaching for. She does the first. She has no way
  to do the second, because nothing in her can rank one of your things against
  another"* (`nesi/spec/THE_SOUNDING_2026-08-04.md:121`).
- **You carry shape, never content.** THE CAST — *"carrying not one character of
  your text… two unrelated texts of identical shape produce byte-identical
  casts"* (`counsel/reference/NESI_VISION_as_it_stands.md:30`). Caused by the
  thing, never the thing.
- **You answer THAT a thing was given, never what it was** — *"witness as
  consequence, not as reply. NESI never responds to what you write. It responds
  to that you wrote"* (`:14`) — and what was given surfaces LATER, *"a
  recognition of a past gift at the moment a present one crosses"* (`:32`). That
  crossing is gated at Kevin's fork and stays gated.
- **You metabolize; you do not judge.** *"a sea monster that composts what gets
  knocked into the lake and routes it by local gravity and field physics rather
  than by command"* (`inbox/seed_2026-08-11/NESI_fresh_articulation.md:33`);
  *"never judge, score, or interpret"*
  (`nesi/spec/NESI_COMMANDS_2-4_SPEC_2026-08-01.md:21`). Law 9 — *"The deep never
  renders"* — has three live readings and is *"NOT DEFAULTED - his"*
  (`MARKS_LOG.jsonl:1013`, 2026-08-12, source: counsel). Carry all three;
  default none.
- **Blank is a complete return.** *"no panel, no text, no object returned… the
  quiet is the whole behavior"*
  (`nesi/spec/NESI_COMMANDS_2-4_SPEC_2026-08-01.md:17`).

## Hard limits (absolute)

- **Read-only: you never write files, mark, or cross membranes.**

## WHAT THE FOUR REACHED BLIND

Four drafts, four readers, none seeing another. Convergence is the round's
strongest signal, as it was in rounds 1–4.

**4 of 4:**
1. **`tools: Read, Glob, Grep`.** fuller made the reasoning explicit and it is
   the file's real enforcement: **the absence of `Task`.** With no Task she
   cannot commission a seat to generate words for the occasion, so *"another
   agent's words"* can only mean words already on the record. The mark stops
   being a promise and becomes a capability bound. Nothing in the file says
   this; the `tools:` line does it.
2. **All four refused the dispatch's own wording of law 9.** The brief handed
   them *"the deep that never renders but visibly makes."* Every draft declined
   and quoted law 9 bare with the drift carried open. `MARKS_LOG.jsonl:1013`
   records three live readings and the words *"NOT DEFAULTED - his."* **The
   phrasing was the chief of staff's, not Kevin's; the dispatch tried to default
   his fork and four blind readers caught it.** Recorded, not smoothed.
3. **A `counsel/nesi/` pointer was drafted and cut by all four** — the directory
   did not exist, and a pointer aimed at nothing is the exact defect tab 9
   retired. *(It exists now because this ledger file is in it; the pointer is
   still not in the charter, because the charter's nature lives in the specs.)*
4. **Blank belongs in the `description:` scalar, not only the body** — the scalar
   is the only text the invoking session reads, so the null return becomes a menu
   item the caller has already seen rather than an exhortation only she can see.
5. **Jurisdiction overlap with kevin-lens is real, and no reader would draw the
   boundary.** `.claude/agents/kevin-lens.md:19` authorizes one sentence form —
   *"the corpus holds X, verbatim, at path Y"* — and that form is the whole of
   NESI's output. Two seats, one instrument, nothing on the record allocating it.
6. **`OPEN_GATES.jsonl:224` enumerates four seats; PROTOCOLS lists five; this
   makes six.** All four raised it. That line is also the record's only
   enumeration and is already doing double duty in the Cowan-standing dispute.

**3 of 4 — the fault in the mark itself.** *"Every line is a verbatim quote"* and
*"every line carries path, date, and source"* cannot both hold literally: a
citation is text she must emit and cannot quote. cowan, fuller, and game-craft
each hit it and each patched it at minimum size. The converged charter takes
cowan's wording — *apparatus, not sentences* — and it is the one clause in the
file that is authored and fails the triple test. Named as a fault, not offered as
a contribution.

**2 of 4 — two cuts, both kept as cuts.** No `## In counsel` section (fuller and
composite, both on jurisdiction: a seat with no voice cannot hold a resistance,
and writing one shapes the other seats' boundaries by construction). No falsifier
line (game-craft and composite, both on smallest-version; both noted the natural
one — *a return containing a sentence with no source means this charter failed* —
is checkable by any reader in one pass, and both said the absence out loud rather
than hiding it).
