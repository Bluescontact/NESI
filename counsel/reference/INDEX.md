## The reading rule for this shelf

1. **Reference, never ruling.** A line from this shelf may inform a seat's read.
   It may never be cited as authority for a build decision on its own. The
   authority chain is unchanged: Kevin's marks → PROTOCOLS.md → the seat's own
   library → this shelf.
2. **The register gap is the point.** Five of these six documents are written in
   a devotional / affirmational / paradigm-declaring register. NESI's own laws
   bar most of that register from ever reaching a player (no numbers, no
   prompts, no system-issued naming, words never rewritten, the world never says
   what your material means). **A seat that imports the voice has failed; a seat
   that imports the structure has done the work.** Cowan's own cut already names
   this shape: *Cowan's tests enter, Cowan's surfaces do not.*
3. **Nothing here reopens a closed fork or closes an open one.** Same as always.
