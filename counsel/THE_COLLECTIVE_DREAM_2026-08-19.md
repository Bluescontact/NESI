## THE DOOR FINDING — the round's center of gravity

Game-craft's round one traced `index.html:144`: `HREF.TANK = "daily.html"`. The only click path from the game's own front door to a writing surface sends the player to the file `tank.html`'s own header calls superseded. Grepped every `.html`/`.js` file in the corpus: zero inbound links to `tank.html` anywhere. `tools/scope_check.js` and `tools/refusal_check.js` both build their "live" sets by hand-maintained lists that omit it too — the instrument built specifically to catch "a check reading a building nobody walks" has the identical blind spot again, one file later, inverted: this time the building *is* walked, by the builder, and the instruments have never seen it.

All three other seats tested this against their own reads and none of them softened it.

- **Fuller:** praised `index.html`'s door on its own geometric terms in round one — degree-four, no dead end constructible by the figure, no stored ledger about the door. Asked directly whether that praise sits uneasily against the finding, Fuller's answer: it sharpens it. *"Degree-four is a claim about the graph... It says nothing about whether the string sitting in `HREF.TANK` still names a file that is the current answer to that seat."* Connectivity and content-currency are different guarantees, and this corpus's geometry only makes the first kind. Fuller names it a tensegrity gap: the seat's geometric identity (compression, fixed, correct) and the seat's current file (drifting, unchecked) have no continuous tension member holding them congruent — the identical pattern named independently for `node.html`/`crystal.html` in Fuller's own round one, found again here by a different seat looking at a different pair of files. Fuller calls the fix — one line, `"daily.html"` → `"tank.html"` — this round's cleanest trim tab, and prefers pairing it with a lint (adding `tank.html` to the two blind instruments' live sets) over the line-fix alone: *"the line fixes today's drift; the lint fixes the next one."*
- **Cowan:** the finding changes the *weight* given to Cowan's own confirmed findings (the sounding fix, the root mechanic), not their truth. Both are real, both function, both are exercised by every hand that already knows the filename — which in practice has meant the builder and the four counsel seats reading source, and no one else. Cowan's own watershed frame, inverted against itself: *"I have been reading the channel bed and calling the water present because the bed is correctly shaped. Game-craft checked whether the water can get into the bed at all from the one inlet a stranger would use, and found the inlet plumbed to a different, superseded channel entirely."*
- **Composite:** the finding forces a specific, named downgrade of composite's own round-one claim. D1's reversal — "the world now keeps what it takes" — is **a built fact, not yet a walked one**. Composite's own resistance clause (*what does this do to the human being who walks it*) was asked about the code and never asked about the door. Answering the two questions put to it directly: no, "the world keeps what it takes" is not yet true for the person who opens the game today; and the door-routing gap and composite's own gate finding (below) are the same underlying condition — *presence requires a path* — arriving from opposite ends, not two separate problems.

**Game-craft's own second pass names why the well-built door makes this worse, not better:** *"A well-built door that is wired to the wrong room is more dangerous than a badly-built one, because nothing about using it correctly — clicking the labeled seat, trusting the hover text, following the degree-four geometry exactly as designed — reveals the mistake."* Fuller's independent praise of the door's craft is the proof of this rather than a complication of it.

**Held as game-craft's own fork, not defaulted by any seat:** is `index.html:144`'s current value a stale wire or a deliberate hold (the same shape as the still-open B2 cell question)? No seat found a `MARKS_LOG.jsonl` entry ruling it either way. **This is Kevin's, not any seat's, to close** — though all four agree the mechanical fix, once ruled, is near-zero-cost, and Fuller and Cowan both independently classify it as container-edge / lint territory rather than a design fork.

**Fuller reads `node.html`/`crystal.html` as tensegrity partially achieved — shared skeleton (`seam.js`), the right shape, half-built.** Composite, reading Fuller's claim against game-craft's door finding, does not contest it but names an altitude difference: *"Fuller is describing the object's internal structure, game-craft is describing its aperture. ## THE SECOND FINDING — the fraction sort with nothing underneath it

Composite's round one: `seam.js`'s fraction/window mechanism, generalized to all thirty levels, sorts and aims *the seam itself* at nine of twelve water edges — not a player's writing, because (the builder's own comment, `seam.js:61-67`, quoted independently by both Composite and Cowan) *"there is no second body of writing standing at the door to sort. What IS at the door is the seam, so the hand sorts and aims THAT."* On TANK, picking a fraction answers "what did my words become" — a real referent. At the other nine, the identical control answers a question about the world's own geometry. Composite reads this as breaking the 08-14 consensus item — *"the writing is the only water, and the player is its source"* — at nine of twelve seats, by the builder's own honest naming rather than by drift.

**Cowan tested this against Cowan's own round-one confirmation of the same code and reversed it in public.** Cowan's round one called the generalized walk "gate 1's own test inverted correctly." Round two: *"I was reading the mechanism's generality... and missed the material's absence underneath it... I did not catch this on my own pass. Composite did. Naming it plainly: my STOP 1 confirmation this round was too generous."* Cowan extends the finding rather than merely accepting it — proposes an addition to Cowan's own orphan-mechanics fork (a fourth branch: resite only if the seam's own writing survives the move) and names a filter with no prior term in Cowan's own list: **hollow circulation** — *"the loop runs, nothing is taken, but nothing outside the loop was given either."*

**Game-craft, asked to weigh this against the door finding, reaches the round's sharpest craft verdict:** *"The correct order was the one this build didn't take. TANK's full depth... should have been proven whole at one seat, walked, and then the question 'what generalizes and what doesn't' asked explicitly before wiring the other nine. Instead the interface generalized... while the question of what each edge's fraction sort is actually sorting went unasked."* Named as ten shallow rooms wearing one room's UI, not systemic depth.

**No seat prescribes the fix.** Composite, Cowan, and game-craft all independently stop at the same line: give the nine edges real per-seat source material, retitle them as a lighter mechanic outside "water" in the corpus's own vocabulary, or leave them as-is with "water" no longer universally meaning what THE_SOUNDING's own line promised. **Three branches, none defaulted, Kevin's.**
