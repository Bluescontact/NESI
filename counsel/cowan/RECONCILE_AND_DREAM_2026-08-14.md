**4 · NO LEVEL IS AN ISLAND.** The twelve stop being twelve rooms with twelve scratch states (`ascent.html:182`, `L={t:0}` per level) and become one continuous fall-and-rise. **5 · THE CAST COSTS THE WORLD WHAT IT GIVES.** Pulling the fruit past its resistance actually removes from the world what the fruit was grown from. **6 · THE APPARATUS REMEMBERS THE HAND.** The mirrors, the membrane, the gate — the *apparatus*, never the ground — acquire slight differences from how often a hand has worked them. A healed tear leaves a hairline and gives sooner along it. Nothing announces it; you learn it by working two and feeling the difference. *Feeds:* solutions grow from place — no optimal configuration can be screenshotted when the tolerances are facts about a site's own history. **NEEDS-KEVIN, and this seat's hard limit fires:** *may a site's physics differ by its own history, knowing that per-site physics stands one step from per-site comparability, which law 2 bars?* **The ground is not in this dream and must not be** — heterogeneous persistence at ground scale was closed on his own canon.
