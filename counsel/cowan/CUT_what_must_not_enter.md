# CUT — the Cowan material that must NOT cross into NESI

The CUT vertex of the Cowan swarm pass, 2026-08-11. Cowan's seat is the most
dangerous of the four to guard, because his vocabulary and NESI's are already
the same vocabulary — barred material arrives here pre-translated, wearing the
world's own words. Part one: Cowan-adjacent material barred at the membrane,
with the law that bars it. Part two: the additions the other vertices are most
likely to propose, pre-refused, each with its lighter substitute.

---

## Part one — barred material

**Sustainability-metric dashboards and eco-scoring.** The whole quantified-
sustainability apparatus his field swims in — footprint scores, impact
metrics, health indices, a watershed rendered as KPIs. Law 2 is absolute: no
number reaches the player, and no comparison ever, including "green" ones. A
virtuous number is still a number, and an eco-score is the one-score anti-
pattern Cowan's own filter fires on — his lens bars it before NESI's does.
Barred by law 2, refusal one, and the seeks-balance filter itself.

**Smart-cities telemetry and the sensor register.** He was Chief Scientist of
the Smart Cities Council; that register — instrument everything, sense
everything, optimize from the data exhaust — stops dead at the membrane. NESI
reaches nowhere (law 11): no telemetry, no analytics, no accounts, no sync,
local files only. The instrumented-city instinct also breaks refusal two
inward: a world full of sensors is a world that points at things as worth
attention. Barred by law 11 and refusal two. The only lawful sensor in NESI is
the player's own body walking somewhere and looking.

**The Capital Institute financialization register.** Multiple capitals,
blended value, regenerative *capitalism*, $2.8B of community investment — the
finance vocabulary that carried Cowan's ideas through institutions. NESI has
no currency, no capitals, no conversion between fractions, no yield. One
resource bar is the modeling error his own filter names; four fractions with
distinct behaviors and no exchange rate is the standing answer. The word
"capital" applied to anything the player holds is the first step toward a
balance. Barred by the wealth-holistically filter read in NESI's direction,
law 2, and the non-transactional frame (the game is a gift circuit, not an
economy).

**Any measured-Kevin instrument.** The latency table in the source reading §6
is Cowan's instrument built from Kevin's own eighteen days — four months of
silence to one day, a real curve from a real filing. As biography and as
lens-evidence it is canon. As a *template* it is barred absolutely: no
instrument may ever apply that measurement back at Kevin — response-latency
tracking of his sessions, channel-scoring of his correspondence, a dashboard
of how fast his life answers him. The line is the same as the game's: the
system never takes a numeric read of the person. Barred by the counsel hard
limits (no number reaches Kevin as a score), never-declare-Kevin's-state, and
the coordination skill's own ceiling.

**System-issued naming of the player's material.** The Reliable Prosperity
move — the framework names the patterns and hands you the deck — inverts at
NESI's membrane. Any card, codex, badge, or discovered-relation UI whose noun
the player did not author is the world doing the recognizing. Barred by the
Recognition Law and refusal six. (See collision 9; the grammar itself is
lawful counsel-side, where it names things *for Kevin's reading* and never
renders in-world.)

**The regenerative register as copy.** "Regenerative," "living systems,"
"thriving," "flourishing" as on-screen language — the visionary voice of the
field arriving as theme, which the source reading itself names as the
documented failure mode. NESI's world speaks no text to the player (refusal
three); a single line of ecological uplift is the world editorializing, and
worse, reassuring — the falsifier's exact wording is a player comforted by the
system rather than by their own material. Barred by refusal three, refusal
six, and the falsifier.

**Anticipatory stakeholder process.** Charrettes, participatory design
sessions, community engagement — the everyone-is-a-designer machinery at
institutional scale. There is one resident and no network; the second user is
NOT upstream (standing ruling). Any structure built to host a future plural
imports law 11's breach as scaffolding. Barred by law 11 and the first-user
threshold.

## Part two — additions the other vertices will likely propose, pre-refused

**1 · A downstream-entry ledger panel** ("gate 2 wants the cost visible — put
an entry surface at the dam"). Pre-refused, and already ruled: the named entry
is refusal six, the quantified entry is law 2, and Kevin's 2026-08-11 mark
filed the ledger as existing — the sounding plus the ambient allowed-list IS
the ledger.
*Lighter substitute:* nothing to add. Walk there and drop the line; the entry
is the act of looking. Already built.

**2 · A latency readout / channel-response indicator** ("make the delay
legible — show how long each spire takes to answer"). Pre-refused: a latency
display is a number, and a per-spire one is the spire comparison law 2 bars
even as a stub.
*Lighter substitute:* the heliostat, already built — delay carried as the
quality of the light the writer writes by, no number, no bar. The gap is the
medium, never a report.

**3 · Pressure on the held water** ("gate 3 — no closed state; held water
should push back, seep, accumulate"). Pre-refused: closed on Kevin's own
canon (*"nothing banks — holding as a present-tense state"*); a hold that
builds pressure punishes staying, which is urgency, which law 7 forbids.
*Lighter substitute:* the hold as legible standing state — the level visible,
the un-crossed downstream visible as what is not crossing yet. Alive as
state, never as process.

**4 · An autopoietic tick** ("the world should do something when nobody's
there — even a little regrowth"). Pre-refused three times: law 8 (the world
stops when you leave), the closed persistence ruling (ground is pure record;
processing, not time, is the only remover), and Cowan's own warning that the
regrowth timer is worse than nothing.
*Lighter substitute:* none needed. The stage-one done is already ruled in the
opposite grain: write on Monday, return on Friday, and the ground is
different — because of what YOU sent, with nothing on screen having told you
so. The world's stillness between sessions is the design working.

**5 · A pattern deck in the world** ("vocabulary should accumulate — give the
player the cards"). Pre-refused: system-issued names are the world
recognizing the player's material (Recognition Law, refusal six), and the
FULL FREEZE bars the new organ besides.
*Lighter substitute:* vocabulary accumulates in the player. If a name is ever
held anywhere, it is one the player typed, verbatim, placed by their own
hand — authorship of the noun is the whole test.

**6 · Edge-content construction** ("the edge-effect filter trips — the seam
carries nothing; build something on it"). Pre-refused: the freeze bars new
generation, and a build commissioned to satisfy a filter is theme wearing a
layer's clothes.
*Lighter substitute:* placement. The collapse is already depositing existing
material into the one form; setting existing weight down toward the seam and
the boundaries generates nothing — and even that placement is Kevin's, edge
by edge, by recognition (R2), never authored in advance.

**7 · A "who bears the cost" annotation** ("watershed logic — mark which
ground carried each choice"). Pre-refused: attribution text is the world
interpreting the player's acts (refusal six) and pointing at things as worth
attention (refusal two).
*Lighter substitute:* the glass floor, already built. The downstream is
literally visible underfoot at the moment of the send. Standing there IS the
accounting; nothing labels it.

**8 · A floor-rule disclosure tooltip** ("the blank field is the unread
clause — warn the writer their words become permanent terrain"). Pre-refused
as a tooltip: the world speaks no text (refusal three), and a warning at the
field is the system advising. The floor check itself stands — this is the one
entry where the concern is real and the obvious fix is unlawful.
*Lighter substitute:* the cost must be legible as *behavior* before commitment
scale is reached — the first hundred words become water while the writer
watches, and the world's response to a small deposit teaches what a large one
means. Show, in the medium, at small stakes. If that is ever judged
insufficient, the question goes to Kevin as a design fork; it is not solved
here.

---

*The sharpest edge of this knife: Cowan's tests enter; Cowan's surfaces do
not. Every gate he brings, NESI already passes by behavior — and every panel,
score, deck, or readout proposed to prove the passing is the failure the gate
was written against. The seat admits the question and refuses the dashboard.*
