## THE NUTRIENTS — shelf grammar: a name · the relation · what it composes with

A nutrient is a small named relation discovered by doing, which composes with others.
Each of these names only what is already in the build; discovery is not unlocking.

**1 · The sentence is the arrival.**
When the clock died, the medium's own unit became the pulse — a stone banks at the
keystroke that finishes it, and nothing anywhere counts. The flow gauges itself by what
it is made of; no meter stands beside the river. Composes with: latency-is-content ·
law 2 · ephemeralization-as-audit (Fuller shelf). Tether: BUILD_RECORD supersession,
"Nothing in the file counts words — which is also the cleaner reading of law 2."

**2 · The watermark is the cost, kept visible.**
Banked text stays on the page and the watermark moves past it — the water that already
left remains legible exactly where the commitment was made. The floor rule (cost visible
at the moment of choice) met with no tooltip, no warning, no number: the page itself is
the disclosure. Composes with: the-floor-rule · verbatim-is-place · law 12. Tether:
BUILD_RECORD, "This boundary is the one place a cost could hide, so it is named here
and the world shows it."

**3 · The caption is a museum, never a coach.**
Teaching names the world and its mechanics; it never advises, never scores, never
mentions what he wrote. Make-nature-visible and the silence law reconciled by register:
the world may speak about itself, and only about itself. Composes with:
make-nature-visible (gate 5) · the Recognition Law · behavior-before-color (Cowan
shelf). Tether: BUILD_RECORD teaching section, Kevin's ruling "The game can teach the
user how to use it" — "A museum caption, not a coach."

**4 · The descent runs through the writer.**
T6: a worked stone reaches the world only by being dragged down through the writer's own
position, and descent order IS downstream order — the machine no longer picks even by
arrival. In a one-resident watershed, every headgate is the same body; the crossing
makes that literal. Composes with: the-hand-is-the-anti-optimum (Cowan shelf) · law 5 ·
the one-body frame (workshop, "the same person who is upstream"). Tether: BUILD_RECORD
T6, "the drag is the decision."

**5 · Later means it arrives.**
The standing water: intervals banked above the brim queue unseen and flow in as the tank
drains — nothing swallowed, nothing announced, the vessel simply keeps filling on a full
writing day. Holding capacity overflowed becomes latency, not loss. Composes with:
held-is-habitable-latency (Cowan shelf) · law 7 · gate 3's negotiation read. Tether:
BUILD_RECORD standing-water section, "banked so nothing bursts later read as LATER MEANS
IT ARRIVES."

**6 · The place is named once, by the hand that landed there.**
A region is nameless until something lands; then one naming line opens for his hand,
once; blank stays nameless lawfully and nothing re-asks. Solutions grow from place made
mechanical: the name is a consequence of arrival, never a label authored in advance.
Composes with: solutions-grow-from-place (gate 1) · never-name-over-Kevin's-naming ·
terra-incognita (R5/FK4). Tether: BUILD_RECORD R5, "ONE naming line opens for his hand,
once."

**7 · Source is a property of the water, not a place.**
Self smooth, given banded, fetched stippled — provenance rides the material as texture,
colour redundant, while the spires stay unnamed and uncompared. The one lawful way to
carry where something came from without making channels into categories that rank.
Composes with: the channel-corollary trap (workshop layer 6) · law 2 · law 10. Tether:
BUILD_RECORD R2, "mapping them would make spires comparable categories."

**8 · The set-down position is the record.**
A stone placed in the tetra keeps its place; where the hand left it IS the persisted
entry. The ledger written by conduct — making an entry visible is the act itself, and
no second bookkeeping object exists to drift from the world. Composes with:
ecological-accounting (gate 2) · the-sounding-is-the-ledger (Cowan shelf) ·
derive-don't-store-a-count. Tether: BUILD_RECORD eight-edit build (4), "the set-down
position IS the record, persisted."

**9 · Recovery is a property of the site.**
Three fail states, three persistence regimes, already built: the scorch never reopens,
the tear heals in its own wall-clock time, the lean persists as left. Heterogeneous
persistence — the reading's hardest ask — exists in miniature at station scale, while
the ground runs pure record. Which is which is a fact of the site, not of the act.
Composes with: autopoiesis (the persistence fork, still Kevin's at ground scale) ·
latency-is-content · law 8. Tether: BUILD_RECORD station constants — scorch "persists,
nothing reopens on its own" · tear "heals in its own time, 7 s" · "the lean persists."

**10 · The door must fail toward scratch.**
The breach: the ?scratch=1 door was silently stripped by the browser and the machine
wrote the live store — a floor that failed for the operator who could not see it. The
fix is the relation: an isolation door is only a door if its return is CHECKED before
any write; absence of the door must halt, never pass through. Composes with:
the-floor-rule (run on the build's own tooling) · fail-close (membrane-controller's
standing law) · Definition-of-Done rule 3. Tether: BUILD_RECORD breach, "arriving
through a door that failed toward live."
