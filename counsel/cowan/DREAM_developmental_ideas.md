## 7 · The constants become properties of the site
**Gate/filter:** solutions grow from place — *"could someone screenshot the optimal setup and post it? if yes, the gate fails"* — and the reading's own line: knowledge should transfer *partially* between sites.
**Tether:** *"fail-state constants from the world2d code (RIM 1.0 · BURN_AT 3.4 s · REACH 150 · RELAX 7 s)"* (BUILD_RECORD).
