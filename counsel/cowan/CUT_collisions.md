# CUT — collisions between Cowan's instruments and NESI's standing law

The CUT vertex of the Cowan swarm pass, 2026-08-11. These are collisions, not
resolutions. Nothing here relitigates a ruling; every entry names who governs
today and what to watch for. Kevin is never scored; no fork is defaulted.
The Cowan lens is unusually dangerous here precisely because it fits: work this
compatible arrives wearing NESI's own vocabulary, and half the collisions below
are the lens agreeing with the world loudly enough to smuggle something in.

---

## 1 · The ledger (gate 2) vs refusal six and law 2 — RULED, held as precedent

**The collision.** Cowan's gate 2 wants a surface showing what a choice cost and
who carried it. The source reading's version — "an entry appears naming what the
change cost" — trips two laws at once: an entry that *names* a cost is the
system interpreting the player's material (refusal six), and any quantified
entry is a number reaching the player (law 2).

**The ruling.** Kevin's mark 2026-08-11 (*"run the trim tab — file the ledger as
existing"*): the ledger already exists under another name — **the sounding plus
the ambient allowed-list** (water level, deposit density, a dry channel). Both
of gate 2's commitments are met in the lawful direction: the ledger starts
incomplete (nothing shown unbidden), and making an entry visible is itself an
act in the world (walking there, dropping the line). Source reading §2.2 is
annotated, §10 is superseded, the document composts. Not reopened here.

**Why it stays on this list although ruled:** it is the *template* collision.
Every remaining Cowan instrument will arrive in exactly this shape — a gate
whose test NESI already passes and whose obvious implementation NESI forbids.
The ruling established the discipline: satisfy the test, refuse the surface.

**Who governs:** Kevin's mark. The sounding is the ledger.
**Falsifier to watch:** any future session that builds a cost display "because
gate 2 wants it" — the gate's test was already passed without one, so a
display is not gate-compliance, it is refusal-six breach wearing Cowan's coat.

## 2 · Channel-latency (the corollary) vs law 2's no-comparison clause

**The collision.** Cowan's sharpest instrument: **the channel determines the
latency** — different signals return at different speeds, and learning *which
channel answers* is a skill (his own evidence: four months → 10 days → 2 days
→ 1 day). NESI has three channels: three spires. But learning their different
return speeds is one step from *comparing* them — and law 2 bars comparing the
three spires to each other, ever, not even as a stub. All three grounds are
visible through one glass floor. This is live, not hypothetical.

**Who governs:** law 2, absolutely. Per-spire latency may *exist* — the world
may genuinely answer one spire faster than another — but the world may never
juxtapose the three, annotate the difference, or make the comparison for the
player. If the player learns which channel answers, they learn it the way
Cowan's own record shows it being learned: by living through the latency, one
channel at a time, with nobody tabulating the curve for them. The table in the
source reading (§6) is the instrument's own biography, not a UI spec.

**Falsifier:** any surface, readout, or arrangement whose purpose is to make
the three spires' response speeds legible *side by side* — including a
"harmless" one like three rivers rendered to a shared clock. Also the counsel-
side form: telling Kevin which spire "answers fastest" as if it were a fact
the world should surface.

## 3 · Gate 1 (solutions grow from place) vs the geometry that dissolved its site

**The collision.** Gate 1 is the anti-build law: no screenshot-able optimal
configuration. The source reading sited this on per-spire dam variation — the
correct dam setting depends on the ground below, ground differs per site. But
the 2D ruling (2026-08-07) and the two-cupola net unseated that site: the dam
scripts are "built and unsited" per the world3d record, and on the flat net
"below" is adjacency across faces and a seam, not a fall. Gate 1's proposed
mechanism no longer has a place to stand.

**Who governs:** the 2D ruling and the freeze — and the gate *still passes*,
by a route the reading did not credit. Laws 4 + 5 (verbatim only, the
operator's hand runs the filter) make place-dependence free: the material IS
the site, no computed pass exists to optimize, no configuration is independent
of the words in the tank. The workshop already found this; it stands. What
must not happen is rebuilding dam-ground variation to "restore" gate 1 — the
gate is satisfied, the freeze bars the build, and the dam's siting question
belongs to Kevin whenever the net's mechanics reach it.

**Falsifier:** a session that reads gate 1's test as failing (because the dam
variation is gone) and proposes construction to fix it. The test is the
screenshot test, and verbatim material already defeats the screenshot.

## 4 · Autopoiesis vs law 8 — the world stops when you leave

**The collision.** Cowan co-founded a company named after self-production; his
instrument asks: *does the world do anything when nobody is there?* A world
changed only by action is "a machine with an operator." NESI's law 8 answers
in the other direction, deliberately: the world runs while you are in it and
stops when you leave — no offline progression, no catch-up simulation on load.
The two positions are not adjacent; they are opposite.

**Who governs:** law 8, and now also the closed persistence question. The
2026-08-11 library homework closed heterogeneous persistence on Kevin's own
canon: *"processing, not time, is the only remover"* · *"return after four
months and nothing has been lost and nothing has been noted"* · ground is pure
record. Cowan's own caveat concedes the danger — the global regrowth timer is
"worse than nothing, because it teaches the player that consequence is
temporary" — and NESI's answer removes the whole class: nothing regrows,
nothing decays, nothing ticks unwatched. Between sessions the world is not
dormant; it is *complete*. What remains genuinely open is only the in-session
question (the live hold), and that too closed on Kevin's canon: a hold is a
standing level doing no work, alive only as legible state. Nothing transforms
unwatched, inside a session or out.

**Falsifier:** any mechanic justified by "the world should feel alive" that
computes elapsed real time, session count, or absence — including the soft
forms: weather seeded from the clock, an indicator creature that "has moved
since last time." Absence must cost nothing and change nothing (law 7, law 8,
and Build Law 13's empty-state rule all say this once each).

## 5 · Gate 5 (make the delay legible) vs law 2 — the gap without a number

**The collision.** Gate 5: rendering is a rule; show flow, accumulation, and
above all **the delay** — the gap between act and consequence legible AS a
gap, not smoothed away. Every ordinary implementation of "legible delay" is a
number: a timer, a countdown, a cycles counter, an age on the deposit, a
"3 sessions ago" stamp. Law 2 bars every one of them. The gate demands
visibility of exactly the quantity the law forbids quantifying.

**Who governs:** law 2 — and the built world already shows the lawful shape:
**the heliostat.** Light quality depends on how many times the cycle has run;
delay is rendered with no number and no bar. The gap is carried as a *quality
of the medium the player works in*, not as a measurement of it. That is the
whole discipline in one object: latency-as-content survives law 2 only when
the delay is something the world *is*, never something the world *reports*.

**Falsifier:** any timestamp, age indicator, cycle count, or "since you last"
construction reaching a player surface — and the counsel-side leak: describing
delay to Kevin in session-counts as if the world should also know them.

## 6 · Gate 4 (everyone is a designer) vs single-player, no network, no outward reach

**The collision.** Gate 4: whoever lives below the intervention can modify it;
a watershed where only one person may touch the water "has adopted the
aesthetics of the field and inverted its content" — the source reading's
heaviest charge against NESI. Against it: law 11 (nothing reaches outward, no
accounts, no network), the refusals' no-second-player ground, and the
first-user threshold ruling (2026-08-02): the second user is NOT upstream.

**Who governs:** the standing law and the frame finding. NESI's frame answer
(the workshop's, standing): the one resident occupies **both banks** — the
player is downstream of themselves, and gate 4's downstream agent exists, as
the same person arriving later. The library homework sharpened it: *"the first
user is Kevin"* and no guest exists. Gate 4's charge lands only if downstream
is a different person; at n=1 the person below the dam can already modify what
the person above it built. The **downstream-agency fork** (can anything other
than the player modify the built — an indicator, the water itself, the deep)
remains one of the standing open forks and stays open; nothing here defaults
it in either direction.

**Falsifier:** any feature justified by "when others play," any sharing or
multi-author surface — that is gate 4 smuggling law 11's breach in as ethics.
And the inverse falsifier: a session that closes the downstream-agency fork by
treating this entry as if it had.

## 7 · Gate 3 (no closed state) vs held-is-lawful — the hold that does no work

**The collision.** Gate 3: the medium is not a resource; holding is
negotiation, not control; "the dam should not have a 'closed' state that
fully stops the system" — held water accumulates pressure, seeks another
path. Law 7 says held is lawful — unprocessed water, a fallow session, all
correct states, never nagged. Gate 3 wants the hold to push back; law 7 wants
the hold to be a place you may stand indefinitely at no cost. A hold that
accumulates pressure is a hold that punishes staying, which is a re-engagement
mechanic wearing hydrology.

**Who governs:** Kevin's canon, closed 2026-08-11 via the library homework:
*"nothing banks — holding as a present-tense state"* · the dam's level clamps
sill-to-crest and spilling "is not an event" · a held gate has a downstream
"legible as what is not crossing yet." The hold is a standing level doing no
work, alive only as legible state. Gate 3's test is passed in the narrow sense
(no state fully stops the system — the water is still there, still legible,
still upstream of an un-crossed downstream) and refused in the pressure sense.
The live-hold fork the workshop surfaced was closed by this; it does not
reopen here.

**Falsifier:** any pressure, seepage, film, settling, or state-drift added to
a held tank or dam "for realism" — that is gate 3 relitigating a closed
question, and its second output is urgency, which law 7 exists to forbid.

## 8 · Damage-ripples-back vs law 9 — the deep never renders

**The collision.** Cowan's right-relationship filter: damage to any part
ripples back; the upstream actor is eventually downstream of someone. NESI's
contaminant fraction runs to the deep, and the deep never renders (law 9). One
flow in the whole watershed has no legible return — by design. The filter
half-fails and the law is the product.

**Who governs:** law 9, unambiguously — with one already-canon pressure valve
recorded, not resolved: THE_SOUNDING's sea-monster passage (*"she may,
sometime, surface something from one... a thing, brought up, set on the Shore,
unexplained"*) against stations.gd's *"nothing reads it, nothing returns it,
nothing counts it."* That is the H1 fork, HELD by Kevin 2026-08-11; SPEC 1
stands written and unbuilt. If the deep ever answers, it answers there, on his
word, and law 9 still holds — a thing brought UP renders on the shore; the
deep itself never does.

**Falsifier:** any rendering, sound, tremor, or discoloration attributed to
the deep's contents; any "toxicity" state that leaks upward as feedback. Also
the counsel form: arguing the filter's half-fail is a defect to fix rather
than a recorded cost of law 9.

## 9 · The pattern deck vs the Recognition Law — who does the recognizing

**The collision.** Reliable Prosperity as content architecture: a deck of
named relations the player discovers by doing; what accumulates is vocabulary.
Deeply compatible in shape — and the reading's version has the *system*
issuing the names, which puts the mechanic on the wrong side of the
Recognition Law (the mechanic never does the recognizing) and refusal six (the
world never surfaces a theme in the player's material). A deck that names what
the player's writing contains is the world commenting on the writing.

**Who governs:** the Recognition Law and refusal six. The nutrient grammar
survives only inverted: **the player supplies every name.** Vocabulary
accumulates in the player, not in a system-issued deck. (Counsel-side, the
grammar is fully lawful and already in use — the workshop's nine nutrients are
named by the lens for Kevin's reading, never rendered into the game.)

**Falsifier:** any in-world card, codex, glossary, or discovered-name UI whose
text the player did not write. The test is authorship of the noun.

## 10 · Edge-effect abundance vs where the built weight actually sits

**The collision.** Cowan's filter: the greatest creativity occurs at edges —
boundaries between systems, where influence ends, the line between held and
released. As built, NESI's weight is at centers: four stations, the tank, and
every act sit in the control room; the seam — the one closed great circle, the
six-edge tension line, the most edge-like object in the whole geometry —
carries nothing. The filter trips, and under the freeze the trip converts to
zero work: a finding about where the pile sits, not a build request.

**Who governs:** the freeze. Nothing may be built at the edges to satisfy a
filter; the lawful verbs are sift, sort, compost, deposit. But the finding has
a lawful use: as the collapse *deposits* into the one form, deposition is
placement, and placement toward the seam and the boundaries costs no new
generation. The R2 ruling already points the same way — the six roads take the
six edges, bindings landing by recognition, edge by edge, never authored in
advance.

**Falsifier:** a session that reads this entry as license to build edge
content ("Cowan says the edges are starving"). It is not. It is a note about
where to set existing weight down, and even that is Kevin's placement, not the
machine's.
