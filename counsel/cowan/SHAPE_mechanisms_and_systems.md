## MECHANISMS

### M1 · Pulse-gauged flow — the sentence is the arrival — LAWFUL-NOW (built)

**The loop:** type → the cut fires on the finishing keystroke → one stone banks live →
the tank rises as height → nothing anywhere counted.

**Physics word:** pulse flow — a river gauged by its own discharge events, not by a
meter standing beside it. The medium's intrinsic unit (the sentence) replaced the
imposed unit (the hundred-word interval), so the channel now determines its own latency.

**Fail state as skill:** bare punctuation is not a sentence; a period inside a number is
not an ending; an unfinished thought banks only when Enter takes it. The skill is
learning that the water moves on the sentence's completion, not on effort — writing more
is not sending more until something finishes.

**Law-check:** law 2 read cleaner by the supersession's own record ("Nothing in the file
counts words"); law 4 intact (composition never derived from the words); law 12 intact
(the stone banks at the keystroke — quitting mid-sentence loses only what never
finished, and the page keeps even that). Tether: BUILD_RECORD, the supersession,
Kevin's ruling verbatim.

### M2 · The watermark transfer — departed water stays legible — LAWFUL-NOW (built)

**The loop:** a stone banks → the watermark advances past it → the page keeps the text →
editing behind the watermark changes the page only → the stone's water shows in tank
and ground.

**Physics word:** recession line — the mark a water body leaves at its former stage.
The transfer between the writer's two positions (upstream author, downstream holder) is
recorded at the exact surface where it happened, by the surface itself.

**Fail state as skill:** deletion never un-banks. The skill is the felt distinction
between the page (his record, always editable) and the water (already in the world) —
learned by editing behind the watermark once and watching the world not change.

**Law-check:** law 4 holds (the page is verbatim his; the stone is verbatim his); the
floor rule is MET here rather than collided with — the one place a cost could hide is
the one place the world shows it; law 2 holds (the watermark is a position, not a
count). Tether: BUILD_RECORD, "The stone is the water that already left; the page
remains his record."

### M3 · Bank storage — the standing water — LAWFUL-NOW (built)

**The loop:** a heavy writing day banks past the brim → the excess queues unseen → the
tank drains through work at the stations → the standing water flows in → nothing was
swallowed, nothing announced.

**Physics word:** bank storage / aquifer recharge — flood-stage water absorbed by the
banks and returned to the channel as the stage falls. Holding capacity exceeded becomes
delay, not loss.

**Fail state as skill:** there is no fail — the skill is trust in latency: learning that
a full tank on a full day means later, not gone, with nothing on screen saying so. The
gap between writing and workability is legible as a gap (gate 5) purely through the tank
that keeps refilling.

**Law-check:** law 7 (held is lawful, silently) · law 2 (the queue never renders as a
number) · law 8 (the standing water moves only in-session, as the tank drains under the
hand) · law 12. Verified end-to-end at 2000 words: "all 20 sentences settled exactly
once." Tether: BUILD_RECORD, the standing water.

### M4 · Site-specific recovery — three regimes at the stations — LAWFUL-NOW at station scale · NEEDS-KEVIN at ground scale

**The loop:** overwork a station → its own fail arrives (slop past the rim · scorch at
3.4 s · tear past reach) → each persists by its site's own law: the scorch never
reopens, the tear heals on wall clock, the lean stays as left → the operator returns
and reads the difference.

**Physics word:** recovery regime — resilience as a property of the site, not of the
disturbance. The same class of act (overreach) meets three different persistence
answers, and which is which was never chosen per-act.

**Fail state as skill:** this mechanism IS fail-state-as-skill: losing a receiver
teaches permanence, watching a tear heal teaches patience, finding the lean where you
left it teaches that the world remembers the hand. No warning line exists (the spec's
own refusal); the reading of lean/beam/stretch is the whole craft.

**Law-check:** law 8 holds (the tear's heal is wall-clock, fixed on the record so a
saved tear reads correctly, and nothing else advances while away — the collision between
a real-time heal and "stops when you leave" was surfaced in the Cowan gate-check and
stands as designed, recorded) · law 10 (each fail is behavioral) · law 7 (a scorched
receiver is a lawful state; nothing nags). NEEDS-KEVIN only if extended to ground —
that is the persistence fork, Branch A provisional, carried as GIFT C-1. Tether:
BUILD_RECORD station physics + Cowan gate-check.

### M5 · The headgate is the writer — descent through the one body — LAWFUL-NOW (built; one edge unwalked)

**The loop:** work a stone by hand (edit · merge · line · break) → drag it down through
the writer's own drawn position → release → descent order becomes downstream order →
the stations draw only from descended water.

**Physics word:** headgate — the single control structure through which a watershed's
diversion runs. In a one-resident watershed both banks are one body; T6 makes the
geometry honest: nothing reaches the world except through him, in the order his hand
chose.

**Fail state as skill:** an unworked stone hangs at the node and will not carry past
it — no text refuses him, the behavior is the refusal (law 10 as the carrier). The
skill is the hand learning the gate: worked-ness is an act, never time, never a glance.
Unworked forever is lawful.

**Law-check:** law 5 (the hand runs everything; the machine no longer picks even by
arrival) · law 7 (hanging is lawful, nothing prompts) · law 4 (working a stone is his
edit, his words replacing his words). Edge named on the record: the drag-feel — the
hang in the hand — is unwalked; the mechanism is verified, the feel is not. Tether:
BUILD_RECORD T4–T6.

### M6 · The museum caption — the world names only itself — LAWFUL-NOW (built)

**The loop:** the cursor rests → the caption strip names the thing, its act, its fail
state → the cursor leaves → the caption clears → nothing was suggested, scored, or read.

**Physics word:** interpretive signage — the trail marker that names the watershed
without directing the walker. Gate 5 (make nature visible) and the silence law
reconciled by register: teaching names the world and its mechanics; it never advises
and never mentions his material.

**Fail state as skill:** the caption never fires on his words — the one thing it cannot
name is the content. The skill it builds is orientation without steering: the walker
learns what a dam IS and still owns entirely what to do at one.

**Law-check:** born from Kevin's own amending ruling ("The game can teach the user how
to use it"), so it relitigates nothing; law 2 (names, never numbers) · law 7 (names
states, never prompts returns) · the Recognition Law (nothing about his material) · the
Second Output interface guard (a caption names what a thing is, never what acting on it
is worth). Tether: BUILD_RECORD, the teaching.

## SYSTEMS — how the mechanisms compose, and what emerges

**S1 · The one-body watershed (M1 + M2 + M5) — standing, built, walkable.**
The pulse banks the water (M1), the watermark keeps the transfer legible at the point of
commitment (M2), and the headgate routes every drop through the writer's own position
(M5). Composed, they answer the frame question the workshop opened — "does the system
let the one person who is both parties see the transfer between his own two positions"
— with built conduct: he sees the water leave (watermark), holds it (tank), and carries
it across himself (descent). What emerges is a watershed with no externality anywhere:
every cost lands on the chooser, visibly, at the moment of choice — the floor rule
satisfied by geometry rather than disclosure. LAWFUL-NOW; a description of the verified
build, offered so the counsel can defend it against additions.

**S2 · The taught watershed (M4 + M6) — standing, built.**
The captions name each station's act and fail state (M6); the sites then teach their own
persistence by behavior (M4). Composed: the world explains its physics once, in the
world's own register, and the consequences do the rest of the teaching — permanence at
the heliostat, patience at the membrane, memory at the table. What emerges is a
curriculum with no coach, no score, and no progress: what accumulates in the player is
vocabulary about sites, which is the nutrient grammar running as gameplay. LAWFUL-NOW.

**S3 · The lawful ledger, end to end (M2 + M5 + the sounding + position-as-record) —
standing; one evidence edge named.**
Gate 2's two commitments — the ledger starts incomplete, and making an entry visible is
itself an act — are now met at every scale: the watermark (what left), the set-down
position persisted as the record (where his hand ruled), the sounding (one settled
sentence, verbatim, only where he walks and drops the line). No bookkeeping object
exists apart from the world; the ledger IS the terrain. What emerges: ecological
accounting with zero numbers, the reading's heaviest layer running entirely on conduct.
LAWFUL-NOW as reading. The one unevidenced edge, carried honestly from the workshop:
whether ground below each spire is reachable and readable by sounding on the current
net has not been walked — an evidence gap, not a build request.

**S4 · The heterogeneous ground (M4 extended past the stations) — NEEDS-KEVIN.**
If the persistence fork ever opens, M4 is the built grammar Branch B would compose from
— recovery as a site property, in-session only, behavior-carried — while the ground's
standing Branch A (pure record, landed never lifts, "Processing, not time, is the only
remover") is the canon's own shape per the homework findings. Both branches stand with
ground under them; this file builds neither, argues for neither, and notes that the
canon findings lean A. The counsel carries it so the fork, if opened, is ruled against
running physics instead of a hypothetical. NEEDS-KEVIN, entirely.

**S5 · The scratch discipline (M-tooling, from the breach) — LAWFUL-NOW as machine
