NESI

a house, a workshop, and a game
If you want a tool today. Start with the floor kit — four short files that walk from the boundary you can't hold to the one you can. The open ledger is a runnable demo of accounting that can't compute debt; the gift circuit runs beside it, and its governance lives in the membrane — how disputes get handled, who holds what power, what leaves this project and in what condition, and how things get removed. The genesis seed is a document you paste into any AI session to change how it works with you, shipped with its own tests. Behind those: the kit — six one-page instruments — and the library of 24 patterns. Everything works standalone; nothing phones home, nothing keeps score of you.
If you came to read. Five essays, beginning with Comprehension Is Not Recognition, and the six returns, which are deliberately personal. The ideas load-bearing under all of it are collected in the spine — twelve principles this work kept arriving at, each with its evidence and the test that would show it wrong.
If you want the strange thing first. There is a valley here that keeps what you give it: ten minutes — write, and it rains; hold the dam and let it go; sink what you release, and the deep answers on a later day. Nothing is read, nothing is scored, nothing leaves your machine. Behind it: the study — every sentence you have ever given the world, with its instruments — and the walk, where you hold a position and work it, a week to a level.

The workshop

Everything above was made somewhere, and the making is public too: the workshop — the gate that admitted every piece, the build pipeline's source, the check suites, the working records, and the audits — labeled as the making, never passed off as the thing. The weave maps the declared relations between the pieces.

What this project does not claim

The held register — open questions this project has named but not proven, each with the condition that would settle it.

A single person owns this project; the pages call them the keeper, because their name is removed from this public copy — a reading convention, not anonymity: the removal says honestly what it does and does not do. Nothing here becomes a rule except by the keeper's recorded decision, and old decisions are kept underneath new ones — layered, never erased.
